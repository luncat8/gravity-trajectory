## deviations and fixes reached during validation

1. **Deep reward is applied after the cap**: `reward = min(pay, deliveryMax) × 1.6`, i.e. a deep
   offer can reach 7.20 > `evacFee 6`. The plan's words are "on top of the ordinary reward", so
   the bonus rides the capped ordinary reward, not the raw pay (fragile doubles through
   `FRAGILE_PAY` *before* the cap — different mechanic, uncapped pay). `career_bench`'s
   "deliveries pay below the evacuation fee" invariant was relaxed only for `t.deep` offers
   (`t.reward < evacFee || t.deep`) — the invariant still guards ordinary and fragile offers.
2. **`career_bot_bench` friendly-rail R&D failed differently after the fee change** — not a fee
   defect: `careerFuelOffer` computed the keep-a-full-cap shot using `career.forceScale`
   (undefined for the player career; the world's ×1.5 lives on the sim), sizing the fuel-conversion
   reserve for a weaker rail than fires, so the conversion drained the pad below the planned
   speed. Fixed to take the forceScale from the live sim (both call sites pass `r.sim`). The
   failure is back to the baseline assertion. Pre-existing bug, unrelated to fees, but required
   for "no new failure name" honestly.
3. **The armed commercial button printed the SPEED_MIN fee even when a faster shot was armed**
   while the `launch` button next to it printed the true fee. Now the button prints the armed
   shot's fee (`launchFee`) when armed, and the highlight tracks it.
4. Plan's "`3.53` full force" corrected to **`3.52`** (`(2.00 + 1.50 × 1.0161).toFixed(2)`);
   plan's file table corrected to the files that actually carry the changes
   (`gui_book.js` for the deep tag, `gui_hud_text.js`, `gui_shop.js`, `gui_tables.js`).
5. `commercialMinFee()` (reserve, fuel baseline) is the dry 10 t ship at `SPEED_MIN` on the
   starter rail → `2.16`; the bot reserve rule "one way off any planet" is now priced honestly.
6. `exitCostAt` quotes the foreign guest tax at `SPEED_MIN` × `rail.tier` (same re-derivation as
   `launchFee`), and the commercial fallback at its energy fee.
7. `experiments/logs/0.6.10-map-statistics.txt` restored to HEAD after the STATS run so the
   artefact the 0.6.4–0.6.6 plans read stays the 0.6.10 run.

## open questions carried into the worklog

- `COMMERCIAL_KWH = 1.50` makes full-force commercial shots cost `3.52`. Against `HOLD-24`
  deliveries (up to `4.50`) a loaded commercial hop can be cheaper than building a rail; the
  plan anticipated this and made `COMMERCIAL_KWH` the A/B knob. The bench A/B above used the
  starting value. Raising the knob so a commercial hop can never fund a `HOLD-24` delivery is
  an open tuning call (the plan's own fallback: record that the note's gate is a `HOLD-8` rule).
- Medic trips / patients home collapsed 4 → 0 in seeds 1–12 under the new fees — deterministic
  consequence of the rival route changes, worth an eye on `POLITICS=1` when 0.6.4–0.6.6 read this
  table, but within the bench bar (bench unchanged at exit 0).
