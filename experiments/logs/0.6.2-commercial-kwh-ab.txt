# 0.6.2 — the `COMMERCIAL_KWH` A/B: does a `HOLD-24` commercial hop undercut a pad build?

The one open tuning number of `0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md` §2 (deferred in
`archive/0.6.2-worklog.md`). Three arms, each run as:
`STATS=1 PAIRED=1 SECONDS=1200 SEEDS=1-12 node experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`
with `COMMERCIAL_KWH` set to `1.50` / `2.00` / `2.50` in `gravity_game.js`
(2026-11-14, node v22.22.3). Raw stdout per arm:
`0.6.2-commercial-kwh-150.txt` / `-200.txt` / `-250.txt`.
`experiments/logs/0.6.10-map-statistics.txt` was restored to HEAD after the runs,
so the artefact `0.6.4`–`0.6.6` read stays the 0.6.10 table.

A loaded `HOLD-24` (38 t) caps at 47 px/s on the commercial rail and burns the
full 1.0161 kWh there, so any loaded hop that can actually reach another system
pays the full-force fee — the mid-speed fees (0.41–0.93 kWh at 30–45 px/s) cannot
leave the planet. Bench guard holds at every arm: a full FT-1 (`3.20`) >
`commercialMinFee()`.

## decision

**`COMMERCIAL_KWH = 2.50` ships.** The question was whether a `HOLD-24` commercial
hop stays cheaper than a pad build: at `1.50`/`2.00` a max-paying `HOLD-24`
(`4.50`) still funds a full-force commercial hop (`3.52`/`4.03`), so a company
profits without ever building a rail — the note's gate fails exactly where the
plan said it would. At `2.50` the loaded hop costs `4.54`: above the `4.50`
delivery cap (not fundable) and above the `3.04` `EMC-1` build, so the gate "no
profit without a built catapult" holds for **every** hold, not only a `HOLD-8`.
`2.00` satisfied neither criterion and was rejected. The bench bar (exit 0, no
new failure, fuel/purse invariants) passes at all three arms, so the knob is a
price decision, and the shape is untouched: the commercial rail never claims the
planet, never blocks a build, is always charged and always offered — only the
price moved (`README.md`, `drafts/career-economy.md`, `0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md`
and `gui_tables.js`'s dynamic help text updated to `2.00 + 2.50 per kWh`,
`2.27`–`4.54`).

Cost of the knob at bench level (vs `1.50`): purse −29.70 (−6.0 %), profit
0.025 → 0.024/s, standing 34.40 → 32.26; in exchange dead pads 2 → 0 (the
launch reserve `commercialMinFee()` is honestly pricier), property 16.30 →
16.48, mean reach 4.37 → 4.83, and the medic collapse flagged in the 0.6.2 A/B
partially recovers (medic trips 0 → 2, patients home 0 → 8). All within the
bench bar.
