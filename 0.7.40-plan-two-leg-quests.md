# 0.7.40 — Two-Leg Quest Valuation & Quest Planner Helper

Source notes: `drafts/politics-quests.md` §3.5 (`helpers for quests`), building on `0.5.15` (one landing table & helper buttons) and `0.6.11` (quest valuation & medical payout).

## Goal

Close the single-flight horizon gap in quest valuation and give players a dedicated two-leg quest helper.

Today `careerLandingFacts` (`gravity_game.js`) values the first leg of a two-leg contract (`at → pickup` for an accepted delivery, or `Capital → rebel` for a medical mission) using a flat `LEG_CREDIT = 0.35` discount without checking whether leg 2 (`pickup → dropoff` or `rebel → Capital`) is physically launchable with the loaded mass, fragile-safe, affordable after leg 1's fee/wear, or inside the contract deadline. On the player side, `quest scan` (`gui_helper.js`) only scans one flight from `career.at`.

## Design

### 1. Shared two-leg contract evaluator (`gravity_game.js`)

Extend `launchFee(career, planet, speed, mass)` with an optional `mass` override (matching `commercialFee`) so leg-2 rival/commercial launch fees price `postMass` without mutating `career.ship.mass`. Share the rules-layer `careerRefundEscrow`, `careerArrivalMass`, `careerArrivalCharge`, and `careerDeparture` helpers from `gravity_game.js` (with `GravityBot.prototype` delegating to them while preserving `this.careerExitSpeed` for bot caches) so `gravity_game.js` never depends on `gravity_bot.js`.

Add a preallocated, zero-allocation two-leg evaluator beside `careerLandingFacts` (sharing the single-table promise from `0.5.15` so UI helpers and `GravityBot` never disagree):

`careerLegPlan(out, career, market, sim, leg1, runStep, opts)`

Given a candidate leg 1 (`origin → body` taking `steps1`, `visitPay1`, `fee1`, `wear1`, `arrivalCondition1`, optional visit log `(logPlanet, logStep, logDwell, logCount)`, and optional prospective board `slot`):

1. When leg 1 already completes the contract (`LEG_DELIVER`, `LEG_PATIENTS`, `LEG_COMPETE`, or no two-leg work is open): `out.feasible = departure1.ok`, `out.hasSecond = false`, `out.total = firstNet + firstCredit` (identical to single-leg scoring).
2. When leg 1 reaches the intermediate stop of a two-leg contract (`LEG_LOAD` at `task.from`, or `LEG_MEDICS` at `task.to` when `aboard === 0`):
   - Compute post-leg-1 ship state at `step2 = runStep + steps1`:
     - `postMass`: `OrbitTech.shipMass(career.ship, task.tons)` for delivery load, or `OrbitTech.shipMass(career.ship, pickedPatients)` for medical pickup (`Math.min(task.medics, market.patients[body], career.ship.hold)`).
     - `postCondition`: `arrivalCondition1 === 0 ? 1 : arrivalCondition1` (with `mandatoryRepair1` reserved from cash).
     - `postPurse`: `purse + acceptRefund - repair0 + leg1Cash - fee1 - mandatoryRepair1` (respecting `CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY = -3.00` when `body === capitalPlanet` and `+3.00` `CAPITAL_REFUND` only when `task.state === TASK_OFFERED` and accepted at Capital; `careerRefundEscrow` remains locked before completion and clears on `goal2`).
   - Query `career.launchBook` (if `bookReady` and matching `(body → goal2)`) plus a preallocated mass-independent static cache keyed by `(fieldTag, stop1, goal2)` retaining up to 6 diverse impact representatives (minimum launch speed, fastest and richest `<= FRAGILE_SPEED`, fastest overall, medium-rail, and richest overall). Reject any leg-2 candidate where:
     - `speed2 > cap2` on `body`'s post-leg-1 rail/charge at `postMass` (`own`, `rival`, `commercial`, `rebel`, or `imperial`),
     - `task.fragile && speed2 > FRAGILE_SPEED` (leg 2 carries the fragile load; leg 1 is unloaded per `0.6.3`),
     - `step2 + steps2 > effectiveDeadline`,
     - `postPurse < fee2`, or post-leg-2 arrival at `goal2` fails `careerDeparture` solvency (with `completesEscrow = true` and `endMass = dry` on `goal2`).
   - Discount leg-2 flyby visits against post-leg-1 `lastVisitStep` so shared intermediate flybys are never double-counted; include `CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY` (`-3.00`) on `goal2 === capitalPlanet` exactly once alongside contract payout (`task.reward` or `pickedPatients * MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT`).
   - If **no** feasible leg 2 exists, `out.feasible = false` and `out.credit = 0` (the helper refuses doomed pickups; the bot does not chase or accept an unreachable leg 2).
   - If a feasible leg 2 exists: `out.feasible = true`, `out.hasSecond = true`, `out.leg2` holds the winning `(origin: body, angleU, speedU, body: goal2, steps, time, length, cash, fee, wear, exit, net, log*)`, and `out.total` equals the exact two-leg net (`(leg1Cash - fee1 - wear1) + leg2.net`, never double-counting `task.reward` or `stop1`'s exit cost).

### 2. Quest helper upgrade (`gui_helper.js`, `gui_book.js`, `index.html`, `gui_tables.js`, `README.md`)

- **`quest scan` (existing button):** Keeps its sliced frame-budget scan from `career.at`, ranking two-leg quest candidates by `careerLegPlan` and refusing pickups whose leg 2 is infeasible. Completion notice reports both legs when applicable:
  `quest shot · P7 (+0.45) → deliver P9 (+2.80) · net +3.25`.
- **`plan quest` (`#helperPlanQuest`, new career button beside `quest scan`):**
  - Sliced under `HELPER_BUDGET_MS` just like `quest scan` (cancellable on world/scale/aim change).
  - Plans an already-accepted contract (`career.contract >= 0`) **or**, when `career.contract < 0`, evaluates open `TASK_DELIVERY` / `MEDIC_SLOT` offers on `career.tasks` that `canAccept` allows from `career.at` (capping leg-1 launch speed to post-accept mass when cargo/medics board at `career.at`, and reporting which board offer it planned without auto-accepting or spending purse behind the player's back).
  - When a full route verifies:
    1. Saves leg-1 and (when `hasSecond`) leg-2 traces into `career.launchBook` via `GravityGame.recordLaunch` **and** emits `bookTrace` 13-tuple actions (`gui_book.js`) for both rows so `CareerActions` replay (`0.7.21` / `0.7.39`) stays 100 % deterministic across leg-2 book reuse and bot handovers.
    2. Arms leg 1 on the current pad; leg 2 appears immediately in `launch list` (dimmed with `shot leaves from P#` until landing at `stop1`, then live and top-ranked at `stop1` with zero re-scan).

### 3. Bot parity (`gravity_bot.js`)

- Route `GravityBot` contract pickup scoring (`careerScoreProbe`, `careerScoreStored`, `careerScoreFacts`) and board acceptance (`careerDeliveryNet`, `careerMedicNet`) through `careerLegPlan` so bots reject gravity-trapped, fragile-impossible, or deadline-impossible pickups and score two-leg contracts on the exact same basis as the player helper.
- Keep all bot scratch structs preallocated on the `GravityBot` instance (zero allocations per step).

## Verification

1. Accepted delivery where pickup `P_heavy` cannot lift `ship.mass + tons` on its EMC-1 rail is rejected (`feasible === false`) in favour of a feasible pickup or fragile-safe speed.
2. Fragile delivery (`0.6.3`) allows high-speed unloaded leg 1 (`at → from`) while restricting leg 2 (`from → to`) to `<= FRAGILE_SPEED`.
3. Capital entry (`-3.00`), `CAPITAL_REFUND` (`+3.00`), and `careerRefundEscrow` settle consistently between `careerLegPlan` and actual two-flight execution.
4. Bot score and helper score agree within `1e-9` on the same candidate.
5. Clicking `plan quest` writes two `launchBook` rows (`at → from` and `from → to`), arms leg 1, records `bookTrace` actions that replay cleanly in `CareerActions`, and makes leg 2 immediately fireable upon landing at `from`.
6. `node experiments/run_fast.js` and `experiments/career_gravity_bench.js` (`PAIRED=1` on seeds 1–12) pass with zero per-step allocation regressions.
