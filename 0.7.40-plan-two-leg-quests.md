# 0.7.40 Plan — Two-Leg Quests & Specialized Quest Helper

> **Roadmap position (Pre-FFT `0.7.*` · Core single-ship career):** Builds on exact `estimateProfitLog` (`0.7.39`) and lands two-leg `careerLegPlan` valuation plus the `plan quest` two-row book helper before sport competitions (`0.7.41`), map expansion (`0.7.43`–`0.7.44`), or multi-ship fleets (`0.7.45`).

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/politics-quests.md` §3.5 (`add helper that is specialized on quest
solving`).

---

## Problem

`quest scan` currently ranks single landings: it aims half of its 34 candidates
at `careerGoalBodies(career)` and scores each with
`money.net + HELPER_QUEST_CREDIT * money.credit`. That works for a one-leg
delivery (`TASK_LOADED`, `at → to`) or returning patients (`aboard > 0`,
`at → Capital`), where `careerLandingFacts` puts the payout into `cash`.

It fails on **two-leg** quests:
- **Unloaded delivery (`TASK_ACCEPTED` with `at !== from`, or `TASK_OFFERED`):**
  leg 1 flies `at → from` (loads `task.tons`) and leg 2 flies `from → to`
  (delivers for `task.reward`).
- **Medical mission (`TASK_MEDIC`):** once accepted at Capital (`aboard === 0`),
  leg 1 flies `Capital → rebel` (loads `min(medics, patients)`) and leg 2 flies
  `rebel → Capital` (`delivered * MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` minus `-3.00`
  Capital entry); or when standing at Capital with `MEDIC_SLOT` still
  `TASK_OFFERED`, accepting boards medics immediately (`+3.00` `CAPITAL_REFUND`)
  and opens the same `Capital → rebel → Capital` pair.

Today leg 1 gets flat `task.reward * LEG_CREDIT` regardless of whether a viable,
affordable, fragile-safe leg 2 even exists from `from` within `task.deadline`
under the post-pickup ship mass! Neither the helper nor rival bots can distinguish
a pickup pad with a fast, cheap delivery shot from a gravity trap where the
loaded ship cannot lift off.

---

## Design

### 1. Rules-layer two-leg valuation (`gravity_game.js`, `gravity_bot.js`)

Add a preallocated, zero-allocation two-leg evaluator beside
`careerLandingFacts` (sharing the single-table promise from `0.5.15` so UI
helpers and `GravityBot` never disagree):

```
careerLegPlan(out, career, market, sim, leg1Facts, runStep)
```

Given a candidate leg 1 (`origin → body` taking `steps1`, `visitPay1`, `fee1`,
`wear1`, `arrivalCondition1`):

1. **When leg 1 already completes the contract** (`LEG_DELIVER`, `LEG_PATIENTS`,
   `LEG_COMPETE`, or no two-leg work is open): `out.hasSecond = false`,
   `out.total = firstNet + firstCredit` (identical to today).
2. **When leg 1 reaches the intermediate stop of a two-leg contract** (`LEG_LOAD`
   at `task.from`, or `LEG_MEDICS` at `task.to` when `aboard === 0`):
   - Compute the **post-stop ship state** at `step2 = runStep + steps1`:
     - `postMass`: `ship.mass + task.tons` for `LEG_LOAD`;
       `ship.mass - task.medics + Math.min(task.medics, market.patients[task.to])`
       for `LEG_MEDICS` at the rebel world (`OWNER_REBEL` commercial rail).
     - `postPurse`: `purse + leg1Cash - fee1 - mandatoryRepair1` (respecting
       `CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY = -3.00` if `body === capitalPlanet`, `+3.00`
       `CAPITAL_REFUND` only when `task.state === TASK_OFFERED` and accepted at
       Capital, and keeping `careerRefundEscrow` locked until leg 2 completes!).
   - Look up the best feasible leg 2 (`body → goal2`) from a small on-demand
     per-turn cache keyed by `(fieldTag, body, goal2, postMass, fragile, deadline)`:
     - Candidate set: existing `career.launchBook` entries from `body` plus a
       compact deterministic fan of static-field probes from `body` toward
       `goal2`, capped by `padCapMass(padAt(career, body), postMass)`.
     - Reject any leg-2 candidate where:
       - `step2 + steps2 > task.deadline`,
       - `fragileBreaks(task, speed2)` is true (`0.6.3`: leg 1 is unloaded so
         fragile never blocks leg 1, whereas leg 2 carries the fragile load!),
       - `postPurse < fee2`, or post-leg-2 arrival at `goal2` fails
         `careerDeparture` solvency (with `completesEscrow = true` on `goal2`!).
   - If **no** feasible leg 2 exists before `deadline` (or the loaded ship cannot
     physically lift off `body` with available rail/fuel), `out.feasible = false`
     and `credit` for leg 1 drops to `0` (the helper refuses it for quest
     planning; the bot does not chase a doomed pickup).
   - If a feasible leg 2 exists: `out.hasSecond = true`, `out.leg2` holds the
     winning `(origin: body, angleU, speedU, body: goal2, steps, net)`, and
     `out.total` replaces the heuristic `reward * LEG_CREDIT` discount with the
     real two-leg net (`leg1.net + leg2.net`, never double-counting `task.reward`).

### 2. Specialized `plan quest` helper & `quest scan` upgrade (`gui_helper.js`, `gui_book.js`, `index.html`)

- **`quest scan` (existing button):** Keeps its sliced frame-budget scan from
  `career.at`, now ranking two-leg quest candidates by `careerLegPlan` and
  refusing pickups whose leg 2 is infeasible. Completion notice reports both
  legs when applicable:
  `quest shot · P7 (+0.45) → deliver P9 (+2.80) · net +3.25`.
- **`plan quest` (new career button beside `quest scan`):**
  - Sliced under `HELPER_BUDGET_MS` just like `quest scan` (cancellable on
    world/scale/aim change).
  - Supports planning an already-accepted contract (`career.contract >= 0`) **or**,
    when `career.contract < 0`, evaluating the open `TASK_DELIVERY` / `MEDIC_SLOT`
    offers on `career.tasks` that can start from `career.at` (telling the player
    which board row it planned if not yet accepted, without auto-spending purse
    behind their back).
  - When a full two-leg route (`at → stop1` and `stop1 → goal2`) verifies:
    1. Saves **both** traces into `career.launchBook` via `GravityGame.recordLaunch`
       **and** records `bookTrace` actions (`gui_book.js`) for both rows so
       `CareerActions` replay (`0.7.21`) stays 100 % deterministic when leg 2 is
       later armed from the book or handed over to a bot.
    2. Arms leg 1 on the current pad; leg 2 appears immediately in `launch list`
       (dimmed with `leaves from P#` until landing at `stop1`, then live and
       top-ranked at `stop1` with zero re-scan).

## Verification

- `experiments/two_leg_test.js` (wired into `experiments/run_fast.js`):
  1. Accepted delivery where pickup `P_heavy` cannot lift `ship.mass + tons` on
     its EMC-1 rail is rejected (`feasible === false`) in favour of a feasible
     pickup or fragile-safe speed.
  2. Fragile delivery (`0.6.3`) allows high-speed unloaded leg 1 (`at → from`)
     while restricting leg 2 (`from → to`) to `<= FRAGILE_SPEED_MAX`.
  3. Capital entry (`-3.00`), `CAPITAL_REFUND` (`+3.00`), and `careerRefundEscrow`
     settle consistently between `careerLegPlan` and actual two-flight execution.
  4. Bot score and helper score agree within `1e-9` on the same candidate.
  5. Clicking `plan quest` writes two `launchBook` rows (`at → from` and
     `from → to`), arms leg 1, records `bookTrace` actions that replay cleanly
     in `experiments/career_actions_test.js`, and makes leg 2 immediately
     fireable upon landing at `from`.
  6. `node experiments/run_fast.js` and `experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`
     (`PAIRED=1` on seeds 1–12) pass with zero per-step allocation regressions.
