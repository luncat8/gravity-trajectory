# 0.3.2 — money mode: a launch costs 1, a visit pays for the time it took

Standalone spec. Every number below is either a rule or a measurement; the
measurement section carries the raw per-shot data so the tuning can be redone
without re-playing. The two measured tours are `rule=tour` runs; a `route` run
earns the same way but without the 120 s cap, and its link is
`#game=1&seed=…&rate=…&rule=route&shots=…&end=…`.

## 1. rules

A run starts with a **purse** `P0`. Each launch costs `C = 1.00`. The purse is
the only new state; everything else is the existing tour.

1. **launch** — `purse -= 1`, refused when `purse < 1`.
2. **first entry** into a system in this run — `purse += max(FLOOR, k · Δt)`,
   where `Δt` is the field-time in seconds since the previous first entry (or
   since the launch, for the first one). `k = 0.10`, `FLOOR = 0.05`.
3. **landing** on a planet — `purse += 0.25`. A landing is what unlocks the next
   launch, so this is the "stevedoring fee" that lets a competent player keep
   flying.
4. **full tour** (26th system entered) — `purse += 3.00`, once.
5. Revisits, aborts and leaving the field pay nothing. Money never goes below
   zero; a refused launch is the run's end.

Landing fee and completion bonus are *not* in the original one-line sketch
(`0.1 × time from last visit`); they exist because the pure time reward makes a
tour lose money against a 1.00 launch cost (see §3). Both are single constants
so a fork can set them to `0` and get the sketch back.

## 2. why time pays at all

The reward is deliberately tied to flight time rather than to distance: a long
gravity slingshot is the thing this game is about, and a slow sweeping route
through several systems earns more per launch than three straight hops that
each burn a launch fee. It also keeps the formula independent of zoom and of
the world size.

## 3. measurement — what the present game actually earns

Greedy bot, seed 31, rate 3, 60 Hz, 26 systems visited (see
`experiments/game_bench.js` for the shot chooser):

| play | shots | systems | flight | Σ k·Δt | landings | +3 tour | cost | net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 s horizon | 10 | 26 | 51.3 s | 5.13 | 2.50 | 3.00 | 10 | **+0.63** |
| 60 s horizon | 6 | 26 | 60.2 s | 6.02 | 1.50 | 3.00 | 6 | **+4.52** |

Per-shot raw data for the 12 s play (`systems / flight s`):
`9/8.0 · 5/10.6 · 2/3.5 · 4/7.5 · 2/4.4 · 1/1.3 · 1/7.6 · 1/1.9 · 1/3.3 · 0/3.3`.
Earnings per shot under the draft sketch alone (`0.1·Δ`, no base, no landing):
`0.80 1.06 0.35 0.75 0.44 0.13 0.76 0.19 0.33 0.33` — the first two shots pay
for themselves, the last eight do not. That is the shape the mode wants: a good
opening pays, a drifted endgame bleeds.

Consequences for the purse:

- **P0 = 12** makes the greedy 12 s tour end at `12.63` (+0.63) and the long-shot
  tour at `16.52` (+4.52): a competent run survives, a lazy one does not.
- 26 single-system hops cost 26 and earn `26·0.05 + 26·0.25 + 3 = 10.8` — the
  purse runs dry after ~12 launches unless the player learns to chain systems.
- Retune without replaying: the two tables above are the raw data. Set `k`,
  `FLOOR`, the landing fee and `P0` against them.

Difficulty presets (only `P0` changes, so links stay comparable):

| preset | P0 |
| --- | --- |
| tight | 6 |
| standard | 12 |
| relaxed | 20 |

## 4. determinism and the replay link

The purse is a **pure function of the shot list** — no timers, no wall clock —
so a replay re-derives it shot by shot and the existing `verified ✓` verdict
extends to it for free. Nothing money-related goes into the link:
`#game=1&seed=…&rate=…&shots=…&end=…` already carries everything.

`end` keeps `visited` and `steps`; add the purse to the verification readout by
recomputation, not by encoding it.

## 5. UI

- A `purse` stat in the game box: `12.63 · last +1.06` (green when positive,
  red when the last shot lost money).
- The launch button shows the fee: `launch · 1.00`, disabled with `purse 0.40 ·
  need 1.00`. The existing "leaves system" warning already covers that case.
- A per-shot toast where the photo cards appear: `P14 entered +0.52 · landed
  +0.25 · purse 12.63` (reuse the postcard slot, 2.5 s, no image).
- The score line gains the purse as a tiebreaker after time, so two identical
  tours are separated by the money left on the table.
- Abort keeps its cost: an abort pays nothing and the launch fee is gone.

## 6. balance knobs, in order of effect

| knob | default | effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `k` (per second) | 0.10 | the main dial; 0.05 makes even a good tour bleed, 0.20 makes it a formality |
| landing fee | 0.25 | rewards the "land and stage" rhythm; 0 removes it |
| completion bonus | 3.00 | makes finishing attractive when the purse is thin |
| `P0` | 12 | difficulty; 6 / 12 / 20 presets |
| launch cost | 1.00 | keep at 1 so the purse reads as "launches left" |

## 7. acceptance

1. `experiments/game_bench.js`: replaying a money run reproduces the purse
   exactly; a run that ends with `purse < 1` refuses further launches and the
   link still replays and verifies.
2. The two measured tours above are asserted as regression rows (with a ±0.05
   tolerance), so a physics change that shifts earnings is caught.
3. `dom_smoke`: purse stat, launch fee label, refusal at < 1, toast text.
