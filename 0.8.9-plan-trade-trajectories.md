# 0.8.9 Plan — Background Trade Modes (`off` / `lines` / `curves`) & the Physical Evacuation Tow

> **Roadmap position (`0.8.*` · Gameplay-completion group, before calibration):** Placed
> after Periodic FFT (`0.8.0`), Max-Distance modes (`0.8.1`) and Rival Characters
> (`0.8.2`), and before the final bot/economy calibration (`0.8.3`): `curves` changes
> the trade world's flight law and this plan retires the evacuation ride's hardcoded
> durations, so both must be settled before `0.8.3` benchmarks the rule set. Runs on
> the classic bounded map and on the `0.8.0` torus (seam rules below); the field-grid

## 0. decisions

1. **`off` and `lines` are presentation; `curves` is a law.** Stopping the trade
   *simulation* would starve colonies (ship deliveries feed colony stock and
   growth) and drift expansion timing — career composition, the `planetEmpty`
   reads and the profit pills depend on that state. So `disabled` means the
   traffic **layer** stops rendering and its per-frame position work stops while
   the event simulation keeps the world clock honest — the same split lite
   already makes (`orbit_view.js` still frame: "a run still advances `sim`
   itself"). `curves` replaces how ships fly, so it is run identity (§5).
2. **Curves arrival times emerge from the arc, not from a fitted rendezvous.**
   Today `Sim.prototype.depart` calls `solveRendezvous` and the ship lerps
   start→aim over the scheduled span. In curves the ship departs on a ballistic
   arc and `KIND_ARRIVE` fires when the arc closes on the destination — the
   draft's «no need to artificially fit journey time». Trade hops are short
   (`MAX_LINKS = 6`, `EXPAND_RADIUS = 420`, nearest-partner links), so every arc
   family a link can ever need is small enough to precalculate in background
   idle slices at map start (the draft's «precalculate all trajectories … at
   start of map»), and new links precalc lazily when a colony founds them.
3. **The evacuation tow rides the same cached arcs, planet to planet.** Route =
   shortest chain over the cached hop graph (A→B→C→HQ); the tow's fee buys
   **speed along the cached path**, not a hardcoded duration. The ride stays a
   scripted career-ship ride (no visits, no wear, no rot, settlement unchanged);
   only its path and duration law change. `CAREER.evacFreeSeconds` /
   `evacFullSeconds` retire; tuning targets are stated in §4 and validated on a
   seed sweep, not in a dialog.
4. **The seeded random stream is untouched.** Speed classes quantize the
   existing per-port `shipSpeed` draw; departure phases quantize the port's
   orbital phase at departure. No new `rand()` call anywhere, so with `curves`
   off every fingerprint is byte-identical, and with it on the world differs
   only in flight paths and event times.

## 1. the setting

One radio row in the `backgroundSettings` details (beside `pause`, the rate
slider and the layer checkboxes — `dom_smoke` asserts that block's contents by
inclusion, adding a row is safe):

	trade   ( ) off   (•) lines   ( ) curves

- **off** — trade ships, builder flights, route dashes and tow lines are not
  drawn and `updateShipPositions` / `drawRoutes` / `drawBuilders` are skipped;
  the `ships` stat keeps reading the sim (the traffic exists, it is not
  rendered). The event sim continues under the lines law.
- **lines** — today's behaviour, the default.
- **curves** — the law of §2/§3. Unavailable (dark, tooltip) while `gravity
  positions` is `ports`: the field must be static («in case of gravity bodies
  do not move») and moving ports never satisfy that (`0.8.0` §3 keeps the
  direct sum there). Switching the radio rebuilds the world the way the mode
  radios do — a mid-run law switch restarts the seed.
- **lite** pins rendering to off: the radio row is dark in lite, no traffic is
  drawn, and the run's law stays whatever started it (a `&bt=curves` link in
  lite runs the curves simulation with nothing drawn).
- `off` / `lines` / `curves` are one stored choice like the layer checkboxes;
  only `curves` is run identity (§5).

## 2. the arc cache (`trade_arcs.js`, new)

New classic script `trade_arcs.js`, node-safe (`module.exports` guarded), no
DOM, loaded by `index.html` after `orbit_sim.js`. It owns the shooter, the
cache and the chain search. One law for every integrator: the shooter samples
the field through the **same accessor the live sim uses** (`fieldAt`'s law —
direct sum today, the `0.8.10` grid when that checkbox is on), so cached arcs
and live physics never disagree about the field.

	TradeArcs.solve(sampler, { from, to, speed, t0, bounds }) → arc | null
	arc = { x, y, t: Float64Array, len, time, endAngle }   // samples every dtArc
	TradeArcs.cache   — key `i:j:class:phase:fieldRev` → arc | null (negative too)
	TradeArcs.chain(planetFrom, planetTo) → [arc, …] | null

- **Units.** The trade world runs on trade-minutes; the field is px/s² of game
  seconds. One named projection, `TRADE_MIN_IN_GAME_SEC = 2` (a trade-minute is
  2 game seconds at the default backdrop rate), converts: acceleration in trade
  units is `a · TRADE_MIN_IN_GAME_SEC² · TRADE_GRAV_SCALE`. `TRADE_GRAV_SCALE`
  (initial `0.03`) is one constant, tuned from benches, not a slider — the
  `0.8.0` `PERIODIC_STRENGTH` precedent. Tuning rule: median curves flight time
  within 0.6–1.6× of the lines flight time for the same link, so the trade
  cadence (deliveries, migrants, expansion rhythm) stays recognizable.
- **Speed classes** replace the continuous per-ship `shipSpeed` (16–25) during
  flight: quantize to three classes (16 / 20.5 / 25 px per trade-minute) whose
  representative launch speed is the class value. A ship's own `shipSpeed`
  stays drawn in readouts; its flight uses its class's arc. `phase` quantizes
  the departure port's orbital phase into 8 buckets; the ship departs from the
  bucket's mid-phase dock position (dock angles are cosmetic today and
  `shipDockAngle` is already randomized on arrival).
- **Shooter.** Integrate at `dtArc = 0.25` trade-minutes from the dock position
  with launch bearing swept around the straight-to-target bearing (bounded
  bisection on the cross-range miss, ≤ 12 integration runs per arc). Target =
  first sample inside the destination port's orbit band and closing. Failures
  (max flight 2.5× the lines time, re-entry of the source planet, escape past
  1.5× the launch distance) are cached negative per `(i, j, class)` — not per
  phase — so a link that has no ballistic connection at a class falls back to
  the lines law permanently and cheaply.
- **Bounds.** On a seam world the shooter wraps positions (`0.8.0` §4's wrap
  rule), stores wrapped samples, and the chain weights use minimum-image
  lengths; rendering splits seam-crossing polylines like every other trail.
- **Precalc scheduling.** At world build, enqueue both directions of every
  initial link × 3 classes × 8 phases; a founded colony or expansion enqueue's
  its new links. The queue drains in budgeted idle slices (the `gui_helper` /
  bot `prepareStep` slice pattern, low priority — below seat search and bot
  planning, ≤ 4 ms per frame, nothing while a flight is live-critical). Until
  an arc is cached, that dispatch flies the lines law — curves degrades
  gracefully per link, never stalls a departure.
- **Cost & memory.** ~1–2 s of idle work total on a 26-planet map; a cached arc
  is ≤ ~200 samples ≈ 2.4 KB, worst case ≈ 5 MB across all links. Bound the
  cache to the current world (flush on seed / universe / field change — the key
  carries the field revision, so stale entries are unreachable) and measure
  retained size in the bench log.

## 3. curves dispatch and render

- `Sim.prototype.depart`: in curves, look up `(from planet, dest planet, class,
  phase)`; on a cache hit, schedule `KIND_ARRIVE` at `depart + arc.time` and
  store the arc reference on the ship (`shipArc`, plus a sample cursor). On a
  miss, the existing `solveRendezvous` path runs — identical event mechanics,
  straight flight. `arrive()` keeps its docking logic; the `maxAimError` stat
  records the arc-end-to-port distance (different meaning under curves; bench
  tables keep the two modes in separate columns).
- `orbit_view.js` `updateShipPositions`: on a cached arc, position =
  time-parameterized sample lookup with linear interpolation between samples,
  heading from the segment direction; docked ships unchanged. `drawRoutes`
  draws the arc polyline (precomputed points — no per-frame integration)
  instead of the straight dashed chord; `drawBuilders` stays straight (builder
  flights target empty planets outside the link cache — out of scope, §Scope).
- The economy feels the timing drift and nothing else: `arrive()`'s stock,
  migrant and return-leg logic, the growth TICK law, `LOAD_RETRY`, masks and
  `portInPlay` are untouched. In `ports` gravity positions the roster timing of
  moving sources shifts with arrival times — that interaction is already an
  accepted backdrop drift and curves is recorded identity when it happens.
- `tradePause` / `tradeSpeed` keep their semantics unchanged: the event heap
  and `advanceTo` chunking invariance hold because curves only changes *when*
  `KIND_ARRIVE` fires, not how the clock advances.

## 4. the physical evacuation tow

- **Route.** `TradeArcs.chain(from, evacTarget)`: Dijkstra over the cached hop
  graph, edge weight = the arc's path length (the tow rides one speed along
  the whole chain, so shortest length is shortest duration at any fee; the
  edge uses the shortest cached arc available for that pair). By the
  time a player can evacuate, minutes of play have warmed the caches; if the
  trade graph does not reach the target (isolated component), the chain falls
  back to straight segments along the same planet sequence, and if even the
  sequence is unreachable, to today's direct lerp — the duration law below is
  identical in every fallback, only the shape degrades.
- **Fee buys speed.** The tow rides the chain at a continuous speed
  `TOW_SLOW + (TOW_FAST − TOW_SLOW) · fractionPaid` (px per game second along
  the precomputed path length; `fractionPaid` is the existing
  `evacuationCost` / fee ratio, so `pay ×` and the purse cap keep working).
  Duration = `Σ hopLen / speed`, converted to steps — no constant duration
  table anywhere. Tuning targets, validated on the seed sweep: a typical free
  two–three-hop tow lands ≈ 180–250 game seconds, a full-fee tow lands in the
  band today's `12 s` full price occupied. `TOW_SLOW` / `TOW_FAST` are two
  named constants tuned once on the sweep.
- **Mechanics preserved.** `evacuate()` keeps its signature and headless path
  (`agreedSteps` override included — replays pin the recorded steps, which are
  now the derived ones). `advanceEvacuation` walks the chain: a monotonic
  cursor over concatenated hop samples with linear interpolation, ship position
  written to `sim.cargo` exactly as today, `sim.relocate(NO_BODY)`, no visits,
  no wear, settlement and `checkEnded` unchanged. `career.evacLastSteps` stores
  the derived total (replay format unchanged).
- **Save/restore.** The chain is serialized with the run capture (≤ 4 hops ×
  ~200 samples, bounded KB) so a restored run rides the identical path even
  with a cold cache; document the save-format addition.
- **Draw.** `gui_draw_econ.js` (rival tows) and `gui_draw_world.js` (the
  player's tow) draw the remaining chain as a dashed polyline instead of the
  straight dash; seam-split rules apply on the torus.
- **Readers.** `comms_facts.js` `evacSeconds` reads `evacuationSeconds` from
  the rules layer and updates itself — H's no-digit-literals invariant already
  guarantees no copy change is needed. `career_evacuation_test`,
  `career_actions_test` (recorded steps) and `career_bench`'s free-duration
  assert move to the new band checks; `bot_career_shop.js`'s rescue call site
  is signature-compatible and re-timed by benches.

## 5. identity

`curves` is law, so it travels with the run like `&fft=1` does:

- `encodeLink` / `decodeLink` and the replay hash carry `&bt=curves`; replays
  lock it. `off` and `lines` are not carried.
- Saved start maps and full run captures record the trade law; restore matching
  law only, the way topology is matched, and `liteRecords` filters saved
  openings by law so a lite boot never seats a lines opening into a curves run.
- Opening/seat/departure/leg caches never read trade ships — verify that audit
  (grep ship/stock reads in `game_opening.js`, `game_seat_*.js`) and record it
  in the worklog; only world-level rebuild flushes the arc cache.
- Benches pin lines (default) and stay green; `BT=curves` env runs the curves
  variant columns (`career_gravity_bench`, `career_bot_bench`): delivered
  tonnage, colonies founded, stage-ups, purse — reported side by side with
  lines, never replacing the fixtures.

## Validation

- `trade_arcs_test.js`, headless: every cached arc's samples are finite, start
  inside the source port orbit and end inside the destination's, with a minimum
  clearance from every planet core along the way (no ship ever flies through a
  planet); same seed + field → identical arcs (byte-equal sample tables);
  negative caches bound the shooter's work per `(i, j, class)`; chain search
  finds the seeded shortest chain and refuses cycles; seam worlds wrap cleanly
  and chain weights use minimum-image lengths.
- Trade: `KIND_ARRIVE` fires exactly at `depart + arc.time`; `advanceTo` with
  a 60-minute jump and step-by-step play produce the same world; `tradePause`
  and the rate slider change wall-clock only; cold-cache links fly straight and
  warm later; masks/universe changes flush the cache; the seeded random stream
  is consumed identically to lines mode (same draw count, same values).
- Cadence parity: on seeds 1–12, median curves flight time within 0.6–1.6× of
  lines per link; delivered tonnage and colonies-founded columns reported for
  both modes (`BT=curves`), with the chosen `TRADE_GRAV_SCALE` and the measured
  table recorded in the worklog.
- Evac: free > paid duration on every seed (monotonic in `fractionPaid`);
  typical free tow inside the 180–250 s target on the seed sweep (per-seed
  table in the worklog); full-fee tow inside today's full-price band; headless
  `evacuate` still arrives synchronously; restored run rides the serialized
  chain; `comms_facts` `evacSeconds` equals the live ride's remaining steps.
- Identity: `&bt=curves` round-trip through link, replay hash and saved map;
  lines fixtures untouched (default benches green by name).
- Perf: idle precalc slices ≤ 4 ms, no slice while a flight is live-critical;
  per-frame curve lookup ≤ the lines lerp (`gravity_bench` reports background
  fast-forward cost for both modes).
- `node --check` on every touched script, `git diff --check`, fast tier green.

## Scope boundary

No player-visible trade gameplay: no new goods, no trading actions, no ship
interaction or collision with trade traffic. Builders keep straight flights.
No arcs for moving-port fields. No per-frame integration of trade ships —
everything flies precomputed samples. `off` never stops the economy clock. The
player's own cargo physics is untouched here (that is `0.8.10`'s grid); the
field the shooter samples simply follows the same accessor. No GPU work (the
`0.8.11` engine may later accelerate the shooter, not required). No change to
colony growth laws, board rules or sport timing.
