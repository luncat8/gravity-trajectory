# 0.6.5 plan — a quest helper that plans the whole contract

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/politics-quests.md` §3.5 (`add helper that is specialized on quest
solving`). The statistics that show whether the helper helps are their own plan,
`0.6.10-plan-map-statistics.md`.

## problem

`quest scan` ranks single landings: it aims half of its 34 candidates at the
bodies `careerGoalBodies` wants next and scores each with
`net + 10 × credit`. That is right for a one-leg contract and wrong for every
two-leg one: a delivery needs `load at A` then `deliver to B`, a medical mission
needs `medics out` then `patients home`, and the best *first* shot in those cases
is not the one with the best immediate landing — it is the one that leaves the
ship in a good position for the second leg. Nothing in the game prices the second
leg today, so neither the helper nor the bot can prefer it.

## 1. the two-leg price (rules layer)

Add one function to `gravity_game.js`, beside `careerLandingFacts`:

	careerLegPlan(out, career, trade, sim, step)
		out.first    the landing facts of this shot (the existing table)
		out.nextFrom planet the ship stands on after this shot
		out.nextBest best single-landing facts available from `nextFrom`
		             for the rest of the open contract, or `LEG_NONE`
		out.total    `first.cash + nextBest.cash` when a second leg exists,
		             else `first.cash + first.credit` (today's single-leg price)

`first.credit` is already the reward times `LEG_CREDIT` — the discount for a
payout that is not in the purse yet — so the second leg's cash *replaces* it
rather than being added to it, and the reward is never counted twice.

`nextBest` is not a search inside the scoring call: it reads a **cache** the
career already needs for the rivals — `careerGoalBodies` gives at most two target
bodies, so `nextBest` is the cached `careerLandingFacts` of "fly from
`nextFrom` to that body" priced with a *nominal* shot (the helper's own 34-aim
candidate set is reused, one fill per `nextFrom`). The cache is a small
`Float64Array(PLANETS × PLANETS)` fill on demand, not per frame; it is only
filled for the shipped goals, so its size stays bounded.

- No new allocation in the frame loop: the scratch lives on the career.
- The single-leg price is unchanged, so every existing bench result stands unless
  the second leg actually exists.
- The bot reads the same function (`careerScoreFacts`), so `quest scan` and the
  rivals cannot disagree about a two-leg contract — the 0.5.15 promise.

## 2. the helper

`quest scan` keeps its button and its 34 aims; what changes is the score:
`careerMoney.net + HELPER_QUEST_CREDIT × careerMoney.credit` becomes
`careerLegPlan.total`, and a candidate that *cannot* reach the second leg (no
affordable departure from `nextFrom`) is refused rather than ranked. The notice
still names one landing, now with the whole plan: `quest shot · P7 · +2.00 ·
deliver P9 (+1.30)`.

**A second, explicit helper** is what the note asks for, so add a
`plan quest` button beside `quest scan` (career only, same gate):
it runs the same 34 aims **from each landing that could start the open leg**,
and writes the winner into the **launch book** as two rows (`A → load` and
`next → deliver`), so the player can `use` the first row and read the second
before flying: a row that leaves from another planet is already listed dim with
its reason (`shot leaves from P#`) until the ship stands there. No second kind of
trace is invented; the book already stores a shot per start planet.

## Validation

- New `experiments/two_leg_test.js`: for a delivery accepted at `A`, the second
  leg of a `B` landing is priced above a same-cash landing that leaves no
  departure; the helper prefers the former; the bot's score and the helper's
  score agree to `1e-9` on the same candidate; `plan quest` writes two book rows
  and `use` arms the first.
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`: known failure names unchanged; no new
  allocation asserted by the existing allocation checks.
- `career_gravity_bench`, seeds 1-12 at 1200 s, with the two-leg price off and
  on: deliveries completed, medic trips and purse per rival-game (`PAIRED=1`).
  The contract share of income is read from the `STATS=1` table once
  `0.6.10-plan-map-statistics.md` has landed; this plan does not wait for it.

## Scope boundary

No change to what a contract pays, no new task kind, no change to the board's
offer rules, and no statistics readout (`0.6.10-plan-map-statistics.md`).
