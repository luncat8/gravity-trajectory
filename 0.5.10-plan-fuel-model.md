# 0.5.10 plan — fuel model: battery-derived fuel, pricing and strategy

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone.

## Goal

Model fuel as produced from the owner's battery charge or bought at cost, priced
above EMC but profitable in selected cases: stay in space to avoid landing on a
heavy planet without the owner's EMC, or reach a very profitable planet that is
hard to reach on EMC alone. Keep the balance tunable and let the AI and player
weigh fuel against EMC.

## Data model

- `ship.fuelKg` (exists), `ship.fuelCap` (tank). Constants: `FUEL_KG_PER_KWH`
  (battery → fuel conversion), `FUEL_PRICE` (money/kg, > EMC launch fee per
  equivalent Δv in baseline cases), `FUEL_BUY_STEP`.
- Fuel has mass: it already feeds `OrbitTech.impulseFuel(dv, engine, mass,
  fuelKg)`; no new mass term beyond the existing one.

## Rules

1. **Production.** At an owned pad, convert battery charge to fuel:
   `fuelKg += kwh * FUEL_KG_PER_KWH`, `chargeKwh -= kwh`. No money cost; drains
   the pad battery; clamped to `fuelCap`.
2. **Purchase.** At any pad, including commercial: `fuelKg += kg`,
   `purse -= kg * FUEL_PRICE`, clamped to `fuelCap`.
3. **Refuel on landing.** `refuelShip` is unchanged (tops up from the tank). With
   no tank, fuel stays 0 and burns stay disabled (already guarded).
4. **Scoring.** `CAREER_BURN_FUEL_COST` stays the opportunity cost in bot
   scoring; purchase price is separate and higher, so the bot only buys when the
   burn's gain clears it.

## Strategy notes

- Fuel is the expensive option: use it when the burn's landing gain clears
  `FUEL_PRICE`, not as a default.
- Typical wins: avoid a heavy well without the owner's EMC, or reach a high-value
  planet that EMC alone cannot reach in one launch.
- Never let fuel buys make the purse negative; clamp and gate on affordability.

## Integration

- `gravity_game.js`: fuel constants, production/purchase helpers, clamping.
- `gravity_bot.js`: `careerBetweenFlights` tops up fuel (prefer battery
  conversion when owned and charged, else buy) when a burn would be profitable;
  route planning weighs fuel vs EMC (fuel price > EMC in baseline, but long or
  high-value detours can win).
- `gui_career.js` / `gui_shop.js`: fuel gauge in the pad block, "buy fuel" and
  "charge → fuel" buttons, disabled without a tank.
- `gui_predict.js` / `gui_hud_text.js`: fuel cost vs alternatives, fuel in the
  live HUD.

## Validation

- Conversion and purchase keep `0 <= fuelKg <= fuelCap` and battery charge ≥ 0.
- Fuel price > EMC launch fee per equivalent Δv in baseline cases.
- Some fuel detours to high-value planets remain profitable; avoiding heavy
  wells without own EMC via burns is viable when profitable.
- Bot careers with a tank end with fuel ≥ 0 and no negative purse from fuel buys.
- `dom_smoke`, `career_bench`, `game_bench`, `bot_bench`, `gravity_bench`,
  `route_bench`, `career_departure_test`, `career_guidance_test`,
  `gravity_seed_test`, `balance_lab_test` green.

## Scope boundary

No change to burn physics or launch book. Tuning `FUEL_PRICE` is a balance pass,
not this plan.
