# 0.5.10 plan — fuel model: battery-derived fuel, pricing and strategy

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone.

## Goal

Make tank fuel a finite economic resource. Fuel is produced from the owner's
pad battery charge or bought with money. Its replacement cost is higher than a
commercial EMC launch in the baseline, but a burn can still pay when it keeps a
ship out of a heavy well without the owner's EMC or reaches a very profitable
planet that EMC alone cannot reach in one launch. The player and the bot weigh
fuel against the rail; the numbers stay tunable.

## Current model and data

`career.ship.fuelKg` is the stored fuel; `career.ship.tank.fuelCap` is the cap.
`OrbitTech.impulseFuel(dv, engine, mass, fuelKg)` already spends fuel by the
rocket equation, and `GravitySim.burnLimit` caps one burn by fuel and thrust.
There is no `ship.fuelCap`. Launch energy uses the existing dry ship mass; this
plan adds no second mass term.

Today `refuelShip` fills the tank for free on every launch, landing, refit and
pad build. That is the defect: fuel has no cost. This plan removes those
automatic fills and replaces them with explicit transactions.

Start constants, all exposed for a balance pass:

- `FUEL_KG_PER_KWH = 20` — battery energy converted into fuel mass.
- `FUEL_PRICE = 0.08` money/kg — a full FT-1 (40 kg) costs `3.20`, just above
  the `3.00` commercial EMC fee.
- `FUEL_BUY_STEP = 10` kg — one shop button.
- `CAREER_BURN_FUEL_COST` is replaced by `FUEL_PRICE` in bot scoring, so a
  planned burn is charged at the same price as replacing its fuel. The old
  `0.008/kg` opportunity cost would make every purchase look profitable.

## Rules

1. **No free refill.** Remove `refuelShip` calls from `careerBeginFlight`,
   `careerEndFlight`, `installPart`, `placeShip` and `buildGear`. A ship carries
   exactly the fuel it had until the player or bot refuels it. Tanks, engines
   and hold changes preserve the stored fuel, clamped to the new cap.
2. **Produce from own battery.** `produceFuel(career, planet, kg)` is allowed
   only while landed on a pad the career owns. Available charge is
   `chargeKwh - reservedKwh`; `kwh = kg / FUEL_KG_PER_KWH`. Convert only the
   affordable whole amount, add it to `fuelKg` clamped to `fuelCap`, and deduct
   the used kWh. No money changes hands. Queued-launch reservations are never
   consumed. Commercial and rival-owned rails cannot be drained for fuel.
3. **Buy fuel.** `buyFuel(career, kg)` works on any pad, including commercial.
   Use `min(request, cap - fuelKg, floor(purse / FUEL_PRICE))`, deduct the money
   and add the fuel. Never let the purse go negative. Without a tank or engine,
   both actions are disabled.
4. **Scoring.** `careerBurnScore` uses `FUEL_PRICE * fuelKg` as the marginal
   cost. `careerPriceBurn` and `careerRescueBurn` model the fuel the next launch
   will actually have after the planned top-up, not an unconditional full tank.
   The launch's battery wait is still priced by `CAREER_ENERGY_WAIT_COST`;
   converting charge into fuel also consumes charge that could have launched
   later, so its opportunity cost is `FUEL_PRICE * kg` plus the extra recharge
   wait.
5. **Bot top-up.** `careerBetweenFlights` runs one scratch burn forecast. It
   prefers own-battery conversion while affordable, then purchase, but only
   when the burn's modeled landing gain clears the replacement cost and the
   extra wait. It never tops up merely to sit. Personalities may keep a reserve
   for the next commercial launch fee.
6. **UI.** The pad block shows `fuel current/cap`, the kWh or money price of
   one `FUEL_BUY_STEP`, and `charge → fuel` / `buy fuel` buttons. Buttons are
   disabled without an engine/tank, at a full tank, with no free/reserved
   battery, or with too little purse. Predicted net and live HUD text use the
   same cost. README's "tanks refill automatically on built pads" sentence is
   replaced by the transaction rule.

## Strategy notes

- Fuel is the expensive option, not the default. A commercial EMC launch is
  cheaper for an ordinary hop.
- The two intended wins are a one-burn trajectory that stays in space instead
  of landing on a heavy planet without the owner's EMC, and a detour to a
  high-value planet that EMC alone cannot reach in one launch.
- `FUEL_PRICE` must satisfy `FUEL_PRICE * TANKS[1].fuelCap >
  CAREER.commercialFee` in the baseline, while at least one seeded high-value
  detour still has positive net score. Both are bench assertions, not guesses.

## Validation

- `0 <= fuelKg <= fuelCap`, `chargeKwh - reservedKwh >= 0`, `purse >= 0` after
  every transaction; no implicit refill anywhere.
- Full FT-1 fuel costs more than a commercial EMC launch; at least one seeded
  fuel detour remains profitable and at least one ordinary hop prefers EMC.
- Bot careers with a tank finish with non-negative fuel, battery and purse;
  `ENGINE=1` burn A/B reports purse, earned, flights, burns, bought/produced
  fuel and detours.
- `dom_smoke`, `career_bench`, `game_bench`, `bot_bench`, `gravity_bench`,
  `route_bench`, `colony_bench`, `career_departure_test`,
  `career_guidance_test`, `gravity_seed_test`, `balance_lab_test` green.
- `node --check` on touched `.js`, `git diff --check`.

## Scope boundary

No change to burn physics, replay encoding, launch-book format, special
planets, corruption or task kinds. Tuning `FUEL_PRICE`, `FUEL_KG_PER_KWH` and
`FUEL_BUY_STEP` is a balance pass, not a new mechanic.
