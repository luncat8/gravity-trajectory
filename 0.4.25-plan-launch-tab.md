# 0.4.25 plan — launch list tab, rail facts, book-seeded helpers

## Goal

The launch book works but the page hides it inside the upgrades drawer, only
shows the shots of the planet under the ship, and never says why a saved shot
cannot fly. Rework the page around it:

1. **One tab per job** — `upgrades` (pad, task board, shop, evacuate), `shots`
   (the whole launch list) and `science`. The old `operations` tab is renamed,
   because it only ever held upgrades.
2. **The list states its gate** — every row that cannot fire now prints the
   reason under it (no rail, cap below the saved speed, fee above the purse,
   shot leaves from another planet, moving field), and a stale book is named as
   stale instead of "empty".
3. **One rail source** — `railText` / `railBlock` move into `GravityGame`, so
   the list, the use button and the bot decision log print the same facts
   (charge → cap, fee, purse, exit).
4. **Helpers use what is already known** — `best profit` and `profit + EMC`
   price the book first with no trace, seed the scan with the winner, and arm
   the saved shot when no fresh aim beats it.

## Review of the 0.4.23/0.4.24 launch list

- A book whose tag no longer matches the field is reported as `empty`; the
  rows vanish without a word and the next save silently drops them.
- A disabled `use` button carries its reason only in `title`. Disabled buttons
  do not show tooltips in Chrome/Safari, so on most machines there is no
  reason at all.
- `refreshLaunchList` keys on `book.version`, `career.at`, field mode and the
  clock. A gravity edit or a purchase changes the tag, the fee or the purse
  without changing that key, so rows can keep a stale verdict and a stale net.
- `useBookEntry` never checks the tag.
- The row price (`rankLaunchList`) and the helper price (`traceMoney`) are two
  hand-kept copies of the same money terms.
- `helperScore` ranks a shot by money but never checks the purse against the
  launch fee, so a scan can arm a shot the launch button then refuses.

## Rail facts (`gravity_game.js`)

```js
railText(career, sim, planet)   // 'P4 · own EMC-1 · 8.20/9.00 kWh → 62 px/s (min 30) · fee 0.00 · purse 4.20 · exit 0.00'
railBlock(career, sim, planet, speed) // '' or the one reason the shot cannot leave now
```

- `railText` is the line the bot log printed as `careerRailText`, moved next to
  the career model it describes; `careerRailText` in `gravity_bot.js` is
  removed and `actorStatus` calls the shared version.
- `railBlock` returns `ship in space`, `shot leaves from P#`,
  `no rail under the ship`, `cap 33 < 61 px/s` or `fee 0.60 > purse 0.20`.
  `careerCanFire` becomes `railBlock(...) === ''`, so the launch button, the
  list row and the bot's "no shot" line cannot drift apart.
- `railOwnerText(career, planet)` is exported too: the bot's queue line names
  the rail owner with it.

## Page (`index.html`, `gravity_gui.js`, `style.css`)

Tabs are a flat table (`upgrades`, `shots`, `science`) with one button and one
panel each; `#careerOperations` is renamed `#careerUpgrades`, `#careerShots`
holds `#careerLaunchList`. Default tab: `upgrades`.

Launch list content:

1. the rail line (`railText`) for the planet under the ship, broken before the
   fee into two drawer-width lines, or the evac line while the ship is in
   space;
2. `paused · gravity bodies move` when the field is moving — rows still list,
   each blocked by `moving field`;
3. `stale · N saved in another field` when the static tag does not match, with
   a `clear` button (`data-act="bookDrop"`) first on the line — the paths no
   longer hold, so the rows are not offered;
4. otherwise every entry of the book, best live money first, fireable rows
   ahead of the rest. A row prints `kind`, `landing · time · net`, a `use`
   button when it can fire, and the block reason on its own dim line when it
   cannot — `cap …`, `fee …`, `shot leaves from P#`, `no rail under the ship`,
   `ship in space`, `moving field`, `bot controls the ship`, `a shot is already
   queued`, `the clock is settling a wait`.

`useBookEntry` checks the tag first, then `railBlock`, and reports the same
words the row shows. `refreshLaunchList` keys on book version, tag, career
planet, flight state, purse and the clock, so a gravity edit or a purchase
re-renders.

## Helpers (`gravity_gui.js`)

- `traceMoney(probe)` and `storedMoney(entry)` share one `moneyFacts()` core
  (harvest + contract + competition − fee − exit, plus the starter-EMC flag),
  and `moneyFlyable()` holds the two live gates (fee covered, exit affordable).
  Row ranking, the scan score and the seed all call the same pair.
- `helperBookSeed(career, origin, goal)` prices the book at the live market
  without a trace, skipping entries above the live cap or behind a rail gate,
  and returns the best one.
- `helperRun` takes the seed: it becomes `helperState.best` before the first
  candidate, so the scan must actually beat the known shot. `helperTick`
  reports `launch list · P7 · +1.24` when the seed wins and `best money
  shot · …` when a fresh aim does.
- No skip-the-scan threshold: a saved shot is free to price, so the helper
  always scans and keeps whichever is better.

  `railBlock` returns the cap, fee and other-planet reasons; `careerCanFire`
  agrees with it.

- `dom_smoke`: three tabs with `upgrades` first and `shots` holding the list;

  reason; a gravity edit makes the book stale with a clear button; `best
  profit` arms a saved shot that beats every fresh aim, and still arms a fresh
  one when the scan wins.

- No regressions in `game_bench`, `bot_bench`, `route_bench`, `gravity_bench`,
