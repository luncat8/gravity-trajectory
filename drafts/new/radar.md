

## radar item

used to know other players trajectory and position at the time of next landing - fantom ico with time like +35s on his trajectory and +41 on my trajectory at the time of landing.
Radar cost balanced for mid-game, i suggest 6cr (more then EMC-1, less then IMP + fuel tank) - so better firstly build EMC-1 before spend money for radar.

## bots use radar

advanced bots with radar may wait and look where other players move to not sit on same planet later right after another bot got visit profit or build EMC and captured planet. This is also for space-start mode and for colonies found may be useful.
