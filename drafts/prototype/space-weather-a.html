Try it out

Change the wind to see the wake turn; the live band keeps its course and later bands enter from the new edges. Generate a band on demand, or wait for the next rare event. Set speed to zero to pause both motion and generation.

Under the surface

Each panel has its own low-resolution weather texture. Every simulation step backtraces the wind, fades existing weather, and adds new source material. Changing the wind never clears the fields: the weather keeps drifting the new way, the live band stays anchored to the frame it was born in, and fresh pixels are generated only on the newly exposed inflow edges.
