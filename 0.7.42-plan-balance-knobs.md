# 0.7.42 Plan — Experimental Balance Multipliers (`pads ×`, `rot ×`, `pay ×`)

> **Roadmap position (Pre-FFT `0.7.*` · Calibration levers at `×1`):** `wear ×` (`#expWear` / `spaceWearScale` / `&ws=`) is already shipped (`0.7.2` / `0.7.6`); this plan adds the remaining three experimental career multipliers (`pads ×`, `rot ×`, `pay ×`) at default `×1` with zero baseline balance drift, giving `0.7.43`–`0.7.45` and `0.8.3-plan-bot-balancing.md` clean orthogonal economy levers.

Source notes: `drafts/career-economy.md` §2.9, `drafts/time-scale-units.md`.

---

## Why dedicated multipliers (and not the orbit `rate` slider)

`0.7.1` decoupled career solar recharge and political clocks from the background
orbit `rate` slider (which moves orbital ports and their gravity when `ports` is
selected). When testing or playing custom career variants, players and benchmarks
still want explicit **balance** levers — pad charge/cost pace, passive rot rate,
and visit payout regrowth — declared in the `experimental` panel in `×` units
alongside `g field`, `catapult ×`, and `wear ×`, never disguised as a clock.

---

## Design

### 1. The three new career multipliers (`gravity_game.js`, `orbit_econ.js`, `gravity_bot.js`)

Follow the exact module-state + link/replay pattern already shipped for
`spaceWearScale` (`#expWear`, `&ws=`):

| UI label & ID | State / URL param | Range (default `1`) | Exact rule call site & orthogonality invariants |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pads ×` (`#expPads`) | `padScale` / `&ps=` | `0.25 .. 4` (`1.00`) | Scales solar recharge & battery leak step span (`padSeconds` / `padMinutes` in `careerAdvance`, `waitCareer`, and `GravityBot.careerEnergyWait` / `careerPadGear` yield). Does **not** alter `DT` or `clockSteps`. |
| `rot ×` (`#expRot`) | `rotScale` / `&rs=` | `0 .. 3` (`1.00`) | Scales continuous political rates in `stepPolitics`: `passiveCorruptionRate(...) * rotScale` and `CORRUPTION_REPORT_RATE * rotScale` (or scales `politicsSeconds` / `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` inverse-proportionally if epoch pace is included — pin passive + report rate scaling so `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS = 60 s` stays fixed and `rot ×0` cleanly freezes passive rot!). **Never** scales one-time `bribeCorruption(money)` (`2 + 0.1·money + 2.5` from `0.7.16`), so bribes cannot calm a world under low `rot ×`. |
| `pay ×` (`#expPay`) | `payScale` / `&ys=` | `0.25 .. 3` (`1.00`) | Multiplies visit harvest payout (`planetProfitAtStep(...) * payScale` in `planetProfitAt` and `estimateProfitLog` — non-special worlds only; Capital `-3.00` and A.R.F. `0` stay fixed). **Orthogonality invariant:** `stepPolitics` passes the **unscaled** neglect value `planetProfitAtStep(market.reportStep[p], runStep, market.k)` to `OrbitEcon.passiveCorruptionRate(...)` so `pay ×0.25` (which caps scaled payout at `1.00`) does **not** secretly disable passive corruption! |

*(Note: `wear ×` / `#expWear` / `&ws=` in `0 .. 3` is already live and remains unchanged.)*

### 2. Forecast, opening-cache & launch-book consistency

- Changing `pads ×` updates `GravityBot.careerEnergyWait` and `refreshCareerPanel`
  (pad recharge readout).
- Changing `pay ×` immediately updates `estimateProfit` / `estimateProfitLog`
  (aim line, planet profit pills & `+rate/s` HUD text, `storedMoney` in
  `gui_book.js`, helper scans, and bot scoring) without invalidating
  `career.launchBook` geometry tags (`launchBook` stores `logPlanet`/`logStep`/
  `logDwell`, not baked money, so `refreshLaunchList(true)` re-prices every saved
  shot in place!).
- Include `padScale` and `payScale` in `seatPlanKey` (`gravity_game.js`) so
  `prepareCareerStarts` does not reuse a `×1` cached opening seat plan when a
  career starts at `pay ×0.25` or `pads ×0.25`.

### 3. Sharing, saved maps & deterministic replay (`gravity_game.js`, `gui_predict.js`, `saved_map.js`, `gui_career_replay.js`)

Wire `ps`, `rs`, `ys` everywhere `ws` (`spaceWearScale`) is wired today:

1. **Links (`gravity_game.js`, `gui_share.js`):** `encodeLink` / `decodeLink` and
   search params emit/parse `scalePart('ps', ...)`, `scalePart('rs', ...)`,
   `scalePart('ys', ...)` when `rule === RULE_CAREER` and value `!== 1`.
2. **Saved maps (`saved_map.js`, `gui_maps.js`):** Include `ps`, `rs`, `ys`
   (defaulting to `1` on older records) in `mapRunInfo`, `startRecord` /
   `mapSameStart`, and `runRecord` / `applyState`.
3. **Action & movie replays (`gui_predict.js`, `gui_career_replay.js`, `career_replay.js`):**
   - `careerOpening()` and `CareerReplay` snapshots record `ps`, `rs`, `ys`
     (default `1` when absent in legacy v3 payloads).
   - Slider handlers call `cancelHelperForChange(...)`,
     `recordCareerAction('scales', { g, f, h, w, p, r, y })`,
     `refreshExperimentUi()`, `refreshLaunchList(true)`, and
     `careerRecordingCapture(true)`.

## Scope & non-goals

- At default `×1.00`, every number is bit-for-bit identical to `0.7.41`.
- Never separates moving-port positions from their gravity clock (`rate` stays one
  disclosed orbit+gravity speed in `MODE_PORTS`).
- `wait` button span stays `60` game seconds.

---

## Verification

- `experiments/balance_knobs_test.js` (wired into `experiments/run_fast.js`):
  1. At default `ps = rs = ys = 1`, `captureState` over 600 steps matches baseline
     bit-for-bit.
  2. `pads ×2` doubles solar kWh gained and battery leak rate per career step, and
     halves `careerEnergyWait` identically in preview and live play.
  3. `rot ×0` holds passive corruption flat while a bribe still adds the full
     `0.7.16` `bribeCorruption` jump; `rot ×2` doubles passive accumulation rate.
  4. `pay ×0.25` quarters non-special visit payouts and `estimateProfitLog`
     (`|estimateProfit − stepHarvest| < 1e-12` in both `HARVEST_ENTRY` and
     `HARVEST_TICK`), while passive corruption on neglected worlds still accrues
     normally (`pay ×` orthogonal to `rot ×`) and Capital stays `-3.00`.
  5. Link round-trip (`&ps=&rs=&ys=`), `SavedMap` snapshot restore, and
     `CareerActions` mid-run `scales` replay all pass.
  6. `node experiments/run_fast.js` stays green.
