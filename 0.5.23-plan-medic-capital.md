# 0.5.23 plan — the medical mission has to be worth the leg that reaches the Capital

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this file alone.

## problem

0.5.16 (merged) closed the hold gate: every rival now ends on HOLD-8, so a
rebellion with patients produces a standing medical offer — 8478 offer-seconds in
24 rival-games at 1200 s (29 % of the time), up from 3621. The mission is still
taken once in those 24 games (`experiments/medic_offer_probe.js`, SEEDS=1-12).

The offer is not the gate and the hold is not the gate. `canAccept` is:

	if (task.kind === TASK_MEDIC) {
		return career.capitalPlanet >= 0 && career.at === career.capitalPlanet &&
			task.medics > 0 && task.patients > 0;
	}

— the only task in the game that must be accepted *on a particular planet*. Every
other offer is taken where the ship happens to stand, so its first stop becomes a
goal (`careerGoalBodies`) the moment it is accepted. A medic offer names no goal
at all, and a rival lands on the Capital only by accident: 20 visits in 24 games,
767 s of 28 800 rival-seconds (2.7 %).

## measured and rejected: naming the Capital as a goal (varD)

Adding the Capital to `careerGoalBodies` while a medic offer stands with no
contract taken, and letting `careerTarget` return it although nobody can pad the
Capital, changes **nothing**: the probe's offer-seconds, at-capital seconds and
visits, accepts, boardings and patients home are byte-identical to 0.5.16's varA.

The reason is in what a goal does. `careerTarget` is only read by
`careerNextShot`, where it sets `requiredBody` — and only while a contract is
*open* — plus a `targetAngle` bias and `CAREER_TARGET_OFFSETS * 2 + 1` extra aims
around it. The shot is still chosen by money (`careerScoreFacts`), and a landing
on the Capital pays `CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY`, nothing else. The mission's reward —
`patients * OrbitEcon.MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT`, two legs away — belongs to no
shot score, so the biased aims lose to the best money shot every time.

## change (to measure)

Give the flight that *reaches the offer* the value of the offer. The bot already
prices a task it does not hold in one place — `careerAccept` adds
`CAREER_MEDIC_VALUE` on top of the reward — and the human helper already prices an
untaken leg (`gui_helper`'s quest scan ranks by cash + `10 ×` the leg credit).
The bot's shot score needs the same term:

- in the landing score the bot uses (`careerNextShot` → `careerScoreFacts` /
  `careerScoreFlight`), add the mission's value when the landing body is
  `career.capitalPlanet`, `career.contract < 0` and
  `career.tasks[MEDIC_SLOT]` is a live offer with `medics > 0`;
- the value is the offer's own reward (patients × the per-patient pay), gated by
  `medicLoad` (the half-hold that can actually board) and discounted by the two
  legs the mission still needs — start from the reward × a fraction, measure the
  fraction;
- keep the same number the helper shows, so the board's promise and the rival's
  behaviour agree (one constant, two readers).

Do **not** relax `canAccept`: the medics board at the Capital by design, and
`acceptTask` loads them there in the same call.

Also worth measuring, in this order:

1. the same term seen from the *rebel* planet: a rival sitting on a rebellion
   with patients has to come back to the Capital first, and the offer's deadline
   (`CAREER.deliverySec * 2`) is the clock it is spending;
2. whether the offer should be cleared when the ship cannot pay for the two legs,
   so the slot stops advertising work the company will never fly.

## validation

- `node experiments/medic_offer_probe.js` (SEEDS=1-12 SECONDS=1200): accepts,
  boardings, patients home and at-capital seconds against the 0.5.22 varA line
  (accepts 1 · boardings 1 · patients home 12 · at-capital 767 s / 20 visits ·
  offer-seconds 8478).
- `SEEDS=1-30 SECONDS=1200 PAIRED=1 node experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`:
  the purse total must not fall beyond its paired standard error against varA's
  1575.06 (+3.07 ± 2.02 against base) — the mission is worth flying only if it
  pays for the detour. Report medic trips (varA: 8) and patients home (varA: 20).
- `career_bench` (3 known failures), `career_bot_bench` (10 known failures),
  `career_guidance_test` (asserts `careerGoalBodies` exactly, including `[]` for an
  empty board), `career_departure_test` (one pre-existing failure, seed 7 P3),
  `politics_test`, `politics_gui_test`.

## watch out

- `careerGoalBodies` is a test contract (`career_guidance_test`) and a helper
  input (`gui_helper`): the goal list is the wrong place, but a *score* added in
  the bot must not move what the helper shows for a human.
- `careerAccept` runs from `careerBetweenFlights`, i.e. once per landing; the
  offer is live for hundreds of seconds, so a rival that reaches the Capital with
  a contract already taken still misses it — the score has to be worth more than
  the delivery it is carrying, or the pickup has to wait for a free slot.
- The Capital can never be padded (the Empire owns the rail), so `careerTarget`'s
  "only a planet where we own a pad" filter and every "can we leave again" check
  have to keep using the Capital's *imperial* rail, which launches free.
