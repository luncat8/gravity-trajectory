> **Roadmap position (Pre-FFT `0.7.*` · Map-law & endgame expansion):** Reconciles the
> earlier `0.6.9` `seedColony` wiring with the human's design in
> `drafts/found-colony-land.md`. Lands on the shipped `0.7.47` composition rule

---

## Goal

Give late-game career progression a high-stakes territorial and engineering
payoff: **uninhabited heavy worlds** (`+20 %` extra bodies, moving the default
career universe from `13` to `16` worlds while keeping old seed positions) with
steep gravity (`g ∈ [1.5, PlanetGravity.MAX]`), **no commercial EMC**, **zero
visit payout**, and **excluded from auto-helpers, bots, and sport targets**
until a company buys heavy single-use **colonization equipment** (`> HOLD-8`
tier-1 cargo hold, `cost 20..40`), lands on an empty world, and founds a new
colony (`trade.seedColony`) that turns it into a settled planet with commercial
EMC.

---

## Design

### 1. Empty planets in the career universe (`orbit_sim.js`, `gravity_game.js`, `planet_gravity.js`)

- **World count (+20 % empty worlds)**:
  - Standard career universe moves from `13` settled worlds to `16` total worlds
    (`13` settled `COLONY_ACTIVE` + `3` uninhabited `COLONY_EMPTY`; in general,
    `emptyCount = Math.max(1, Math.round(settledCount * 0.2))` capped by
    `PLANETS`).
  - Settled worlds keep their exact opening rules (HQs, Capital, ARF, commercial
    EMC pads, opening seat planner). Empty worlds are **never** picked as
    starting HQs, Capital, or ARF.
- **High gravity & zero infrastructure on empty worlds**:
  - Empty worlds draw their gravity in `[1.5, PlanetGravity.MAX]` (`1.5..3.5`,
    or `1.5..5.0` with `0.7.43`), making an unassisted return impossible without
    serious propulsion or a newly built heavy pad after founding.
  - `galaxy.commercial[emptyPlanet] = 0` — **no commercial EMC catapult** exists
    on an empty world (`OWNER_EMPTY`)! Landing there without founding a colony
    (or without enough impulse fuel / `return-tow`) strands a careless ship,
    while flying *past* an empty world uses its steep well for free
    high-deflection slingshots!
- **Excluded from contracts & sport targets while empty**:
  - Task generators (`makeDelivery`, `MEDIC_SLOT`, `0.7.40` two-leg quest cache)
    and `0.7.41` `galaxy.sport` skip `market.empty` worlds completely until
    founded.
  - Market profit on an empty world is `0` (`planetProfit = 0`) until founded.

### 2. Single-use Colonization Kit (`orbit_tech.js`, `gravity_game.js`, `gui_shop.js`)

- Add **Colonization Kit** (`career.ship.colonyKit = false | true`, or single-use
  cargo equipment item in the shop):
  - **Endgame price**: `COLONY_KIT_COST = 28` credits (tunable inside `20..40` in
    `0.8.3`, scaled by `0.7.42` `pads ×` if desired) — priced clearly above mid-tier
    gear so early/mid-game play focuses on pads, engines, and science.
  - **Heavy mass (`> HOLD-8` tier-1 cargo hold)**: `COLONY_KIT_MASS = 12` t
    (`> 8` t capacity of `HOLD-8` tier 1, requiring `HOLD-24` tier 2 cargo hold
    and adding `12` t to `refitMass` / live ship mass while carried), so a
    capable catapult + tank/engine is needed to deliver and survive landing on a
    `g >= 1.5` world.
  - Only purchasable at a settled port (`Capital`, `HQ`, or owned pad) when the
    ship has sufficient free hold capacity (`hold.capacity >= COLONY_KIT_MASS`),
    the map still has at least one `COLONY_EMPTY` world, and
    `!career.ship.colonyKit`.

### 3. Founding action (`foundBlock` / `foundColony` in `gravity_game.js`, `orbit_sim.js`, `gui_shop.js`, `gui_predict.js`)

- Expose `foundBlock(galaxy, market, career, trade)` and
  `foundColony(galaxy, market, career, trade)`:
  1. `foundBlock` checks: ship landed (`career.at >= 0`) on an unpopulated
     empty planet (`market.empty[career.at] === 1`, not Capital / A.R.F.) with
     `career.ship.colonyKit === true`.
  2. When triggered (via pad action button `found colony · P#` or automatic
     prompt on landing):
     - Consumes `career.ship.colonyKit = false` (single-use; ship mass drops by
       `COLONY_KIT_MASS` via `refitMass`).
     - Calls `trade.seedColony(career.at)` (`activatePort` + `refreshPartners`
       in `orbit_sim.js`, already implemented and tested by `colony_bench`) so
       the new colony joins the trade network and `makeDelivery` draws it as an
       origin/destination from then on.
     - Clears `market.empty[career.at] = 0`, enables normal visit value accrual,
       and installs a **commercial EMC** (`setCommercial(galaxy, career.at, true)`)
       turning it into a typical settled planet with commercial EMC!
  3. Aim readout (`gui_predict.js`) & `careerDeparture` (`gravity_bot.js`):
     - Without `colonyKit`, `padOwner === OWNER_EMPTY` blocks rail departure
       (only impulse lift-off or `return-tow` applies), and the aim label warns
       `empty · no EMC (IMP / evac only)`.
     - With `colonyKit` aboard, `predictedCareerEconomics` and `careerDeparture`
       evaluate departure on the **post-founding** state (`empty = 0`,
       `arrivalMass = ship.baseMass`, commercial EMC-1 installed and buildable so
       building an `EMC-3+` pad or burning off `g >= 1.5` is priced honestly!),
       showing `founds colony` when exitable.
  4. Auto-aim helpers (`gui_helper.js`) and `GravityBot` skip landing on
     `market.empty` worlds whenever `!career.ship.colonyKit`.

### 4. Bot discipline (`gravity_bot.js`)

- **Before carrying a kit**: `careerPlan` treats `COLONY_EMPTY` worlds as pure
  gravity-assist bodies (`landingValue = -Infinity` — never land on a padless
  barren world!).
- **Buying & delivering a kit**: once a rival has surplus endgame capital
  (`purse >= COLONY_KIT_COST + postFoundExitReserve`, strong rail/engine capable
  of reaching and departing a `g >= 1.5` world) and an unsettled world exists,
  evaluate buying `colonyKit` and flying to the best reachable `COLONY_EMPTY` world.

### 5. Integration with fair starts and later plans

- Evaluate the composition gate and seating against settled worlds; empty
  worlds cannot serve as HQ, Capital, ARF or a bot's first landing. Keep the
  H composition checklist consistent with the actual gate.
- Test space and Capital starts, start gifts and radar with the new worlds.
  The kit is an endgame purchase, not a start gift. Human landings without a
  kit remain possible but can strand the ship, as described in §1 and §3.
- Keep gravity from empty worlds in every trajectory, including seat probes.
  Verify saved maps and replays preserve opening seats and colony state; do
  not assume existing seats remain valid when additional gravity is present.
- Fleet and FFT integration follow `0.7.45` and `0.8.0`; test founding and
  prediction under both once those plans are implemented.
