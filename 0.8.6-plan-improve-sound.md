## Problem (measured in the live code)

Inspecting `gravity_voronoi_tones.js`, `gravity_voronoi_sound.js`, and
`gravity_trajectory_sound.js` surfaces three concrete gaps once all `0.7.*` and
`0.8.*` modes exist:

1. **Adjacent Voronoi zones clash across `260×220` borders (`gravity_voronoi_tones.js`):**
   - `zoneRoot(x, y)` hashes `(Math.floor(x / ZONE_W), Math.floor(y / ZONE_H))`
     via an uncorrelated integer hash `mix(...)` into `FIFTHS = [0, 7, 2, 9, 5]`
     (`C, G, D, A, E` major pentatonic roots).
   - Only **consecutive** entries in `FIFTHS` (`|idxA - idxB| <= 1`) share four
     of five pitch classes! When `mix` jumps from index `0` (`C D E G A`) to
     index `4` (`E F# G# B C#`) across a single `260×220` border, neighboring
     Voronoi cells share only one note (`E`) and sound simultaneous minor-second
     (`C/C#`, `G/G#`) clashes across the `MAX_ACTIVE = 20` active voices in
     `gravity_voronoi_sound.js`.
2. **`O(N³)` Euclidean adjacency breaks down on `0.8.0` FFT (`192` bodies + wrap) and `0.8.1` (`78` bodies, `4800 px`):**
   - `adjacent(sim, a, b)` in `gravity_voronoi_sound.js` tests every pair `(i, j)`
     against all `N` half-planes (`O(N³)` — `~8 800` checks at `N = 26`, but
     `~3.5 million` checks at `N = 192`!), and uses unwrapped Euclidean
     coordinates even when `sim.periodic` (`&fft=1`) wraps across `0 ↔ worldW` /
     `0 ↔ worldH`.
   - On 104- and 192-planet maps, inter-planet spacing halves while
     `DEFAULT_DISTANCE_SCALE = 0.75` and Lorentzian `distanceVolume` (`320 px`
     base) remain calibrated for 13–26 planets, crowding 20 simultaneous voices.
3. **Voronoi aim channel & eyes-free outcome cues (`gravity_trajectory_sound.js`, `gravity_voronoi_sound.js`):**
   - Under `voronoi` (`useVoronoi = true`), flight uses the dedicated 24-voice
     Voronoi synth (`point` vs `distance` preset), while aim shares
     `gravity_trajectory_sound.js`'s generic `updateChord` / `emcBeacon` without
     knowing the destination planet's Voronoi note, lacks the completed-trace
     **arrival one-shot** (`flythrough` plays it, `voronoi` does not), does not
     distinguish **rail cap too weak** from **no rail / empty heavy world
     (`0.7.44` `OWNER_EMPTY`)** in `emcBeacon`, and computes `trajectoryPan` /
     `flightPan` with unwrapped `dx, dy` on periodic maps.

## Design

### 1. Smooth pentatonic neighborhood consonance (`gravity_voronoi_tones.js`)

Make the claim *"nearest planets form a consonant scale by design"* hold across
every seed and world size (`13..192` bodies, `1600` or `4800` width):

- Replace the uncorrelated hash jump in `zoneRoot(x, y)` with a **smooth,
  wrap-aware spatial walk** along `FIFTHS = [0, 7, 2, 9, 5]` (at most `±1` step
  on the circle of fifths between any two adjacent spatial zones, wrapping
  cleanly when `periodic` is active so `x = 0` and `x = worldW` share or differ
  by `<= 1` fifth step!).
- In `tuneField` / `tuneCell`:
  - Candidate pitches for cell `i` draw from its local pentatonic collection, and
    any direct Voronoi neighbor `j` with `intervalPenalty(semitones[j], semitone) >= 10`
    (semitone / tritone clash `iv === 1 || iv === 6` or unison `a === b`) is
    strictly avoided whenever a clash-free pentatonic degree exists in the two-octave
    `C3..B4` (`0..23`) pool.
- Expose `Tones.auditField(xs, ys, semitones, nbr, nbrCount, nbrStride)` for
  Node tests to measure neighbor unison and minor-second/tritone clash counts
  directly across seeds.

### 2. Fast `O(N · k)` & periodic-aware Voronoi tuning (`gravity_voronoi_sound.js`)

- **Distance & bisector helper:** Use minimum-image `(dx, dy)` when `sim.periodic`
  is true (`wrapDelta(bx - ax, sim.worldW)`, `wrapDelta(by - ay, sim.worldH)`),
  both in `tuneField` adjacency and in live flight `updateFlight(sim)` voice
  distance & stereo pan!
- **Pruned adjacency (`< 1 ms` at `N = 192`):** Two sites `(a, b)` beyond
  `2 * MAX_NEIGHBOR_SPAN` (scaled with mean inter-planet spacing
  `sqrt(worldW * worldH / activeCount)`) cannot share an audible Voronoi border,
  and third-site half-planes sorted/culled by midpoint distance prune `adjacent`
  from `3.5M` checks down to `< 30k` at `N = 192` with zero heap allocation in
  `updateFlight`.
- **Density-normalized reach:** Scale effective falloff radius gently by
  `clamp(sqrt(26 / activeCount), 0.45, 1.15)` multiplied by user `distanceScale`
  so a 104- or 192-planet FFT torus keeps the same clean 4–7 prominent local
  voices as a 26-planet classic map instead of saturating all 20 slots.

### 3. Voronoi aim-channel coherence & `0.8.5` outcome cues (`gravity_trajectory_sound.js`, `gravity_voronoi_sound.js`)

- **Shared outcome grammar, destination-linked root & timbre:** Keep one aim
  information grammar across generators (so switching `trajectory ↔ voronoi`
  never changes what profit, length ticks, or launch blocks *mean*), while:
  - Anchoring the completed-trace **arrival one-shot** to the landing planet's
    actual Voronoi `notes[predBody]` when `useVoronoi` is active (triangle +
    lowpass matching the Voronoi voice family), firing once when a trace settles
    on `END_IMPACT` (completing `0.8.5` §2).
  - Splitting `emcBeacon`'s blocked cue into two distinct, non-harsh pitches:
    *insufficient rail / battery / purse* (recoverable on-pad) vs *no rail at
    destination / `OWNER_EMPTY` barren world* (`0.7.44`, requires `IMP`,
    `colonyKit`, or `return-tow`).
- **Periodic stereo pan (`gravity_trajectory_sound.js`):** `trajectoryPan` and
  `flightPan` wrap `(targetX - x, targetY - y)` by `(sim.worldW, sim.worldH)`
  when `sim.periodic` is true so left/right panning never flips across a torus
  seam.
