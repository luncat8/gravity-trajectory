Gravity Map

Click on or above the line to add a planet. Drag to move. Arrow keys nudge the selected planet, Delete removes it, Escape deselects. Hover a planet and click its red cross to remove it. The shaded band is the gravity range (gravity ± margin).


Planet Number to generate Gravity Margin (±) Range Rolled preview

	map generator helper
	
	horizontal line from negative to positive gravity of planet.
	
	user can position planet ico (simple circle) on this line: GUI allow set, drag move, remove
	
	margin of gravity from values, default is +-0.5, to generate randomness.
	Configurable default gravity margin for new planets.
	
	Configurable gravity min/max , default is -1 .. +4
	
	click on or above line to add new, click below planet to remove (small red cross draw below)
	
	preview margin range on line
	
	color planet based on EMC-1 EMC-2 ability to typically launch from this planet (no hardcoded power, because EMC can be changed in new versions). typically mean on typical map, because it depends on near planet gravity. for my observation -0.2 ... 1.1 is green (EMC-1) and >2.5 || < -1 is red (cant on EMC-2). but this is subject to do some fast tests to find range.
