# 0.5.12 plan — corruption, bribes, arrest, rebellion and medical quest

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone.

## Goal

Add per-planet corruption (0..100) that reduces population growth, rises when
emissaries do not visit, falls by 0.2 per visit, rises when bribes are taken,
can lead to arrest, and at high levels can trigger a rebellion quelled by a
medical quest.

## Phase A — corruption core

### Data model

- `planet.corruption` (0..100), `planet.lastVisitStep`, `planet.bribedThisVisit`.
- Constants: `CORRUPTION_VISIT_DECAY = 0.2`, `CORRUPTION_PASSIVE_RATE`,
  `CORRUPTION_GRACE`, `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE = 1`,
  `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SCALE = 0.1`, `BRIBE_MIN`, `CORRUPTION_GROWTH_FACTOR = 100`.
- A.R.F. and capital have corruption fixed at 0.

### Rules

1. **Visit.** On landing on a planet (player, rival, or bot):
   `corruption = max(0, corruption - 0.2)`, `lastVisitStep = step`,
   `bribedThisVisit = false`.
2. **Passive rise.** Each world step, for planets not visited within
   `CORRUPTION_GRACE` seconds: `corruption += rate * dt`, clamped to 100.
3. **Population.** Colony growth multiplier
   `growth *= (1 - corruption / CORRUPTION_GROWTH_FACTOR)`; corruption ≥ 50
   halves growth.
4. **Bribe.** While on a planet (except capital/A.R.F.): `acceptBribe(career,
   planet)`:
   - Amount = `2 * launchFee(career, planet, SPEED_MIN)`.
   - Refused if `bribedThisVisit`, or amount < `BRIBE_MIN`, or the planet is the
     career's own EMC (the bot may still take one on a foreign EMC, per
     personality).
   - `corruption += CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE + CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SCALE * amount`;
     `purse += amount`; `bribedThisVisit = true`.
   - Arrest check: if `corruption > 50`, chance
     `(corruption - 50) / 100 * 0.5` of arrest → the career is sent to the
     capital via slow transport (forced, time penalty, no payment).
5. **Bot.** Bribes only on foreign EMCs when corruption allows and amount ≥ min;
   personality flag `bribeProne`. Never on own EMC. Taken before a rival's
   expected flyby when sit windows align.

## Phase B — rebellion and medical quest

### Rules

1. **Rebellion check.** On visit to, or passive tick on, a planet with
   corruption C ≥ 10, roll
   `P = 0.5 * (1 - exp(-0.04 * (C - 10)))`.
   On success: `rebel = true`, all its EMCs switch to commercial status (owner →
   commercial, upgrades retained), flyby-profit growth rate ÷4.
   No chance below 10; the formula is asymptotic and has no division by zero.
2. **Rebellious state.** `planet.rebel = true`, `planet.patients = 0` until the
   quest starts. Rebellious planets cannot be bribed.
3. **Medical quest.** Available when any rebellious planet exists:
   - At capital: "load medics" fills cargo with medics (half cargo hold).
   - Landing on a rebellious planet: medics auto-unload; patients auto-load
     (same count, up to half cargo hold).
   - Deliver patients to capital: reward granted; `planet.patients -= delivered`.
   - When `patients <= 0`: planet loses rebel status; EMCs stay commercial;
     normal profit growth resumes.
   - Medics auto-unload when the quest finishes for any reason (drop contract,
     evacuate, etc.).
4. **Task board.** Medical quest is a special task offered when rebels exist;
   accept/drop like deliveries.

## Validation

- Visit reduces corruption by 0.2; passive rise only after grace.
- Bribe increases corruption by formula; second bribe on same visit refused.
- Arrest probability 0 below 50% corruption; ≤ 50% at 100% corruption.
- Rebellion probability matches formula; no division by zero; stable at extremes.
- Medical quest reduces patients; planet depacifies at 0; EMCs remain commercial
  with upgrades retained.
- `dom_smoke`, all benches green.

## Scope boundary

Does not change normal delivery economy. Medical quest cargo uses existing cargo
hold; no new ship stats. Tuning rates is a balance pass.
