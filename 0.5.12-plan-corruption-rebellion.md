# 0.5.12 plan — corruption, bribes, arrest, rebellion and medical quest

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Depends on
0.5.11 for `planetKind`, `capitalPlanet` and the fixed Capital rail.

## Goal

Add shared per-planet political state. Corruption reduces colony population
growth, rises when emissaries stay away or rulers pay bribes, falls by `0.2`
per visit, can cause arrest and periodic rebellion. Rebellion permanently
seizes infrastructure as commercial rails, slowly raises the fly-visit payout
to `4×`, and is quelled by a medical mission that ferries patients to the
Capital.

## Pure formulas and state

Put the formulas in `orbit_econ.js` as exported pure functions; keep the shared
mutable arrays on `GravityGame.createMarket`, not on a career. Use typed arrays
of `OrbitWorld.PLANETS`:

- `corruption` (`0..100`), `bribeVisitStep`, `rebel`, `rebelSinceStep`,
  `patients`, `rebelCooldownStep`, `lastCheckEpoch`.
- `market.lastVisitStep` already records the last fly-visit; it is also the
  emissary timestamp and the identity of a visit for the one-bribe rule.
- `market.politicsSeed` and `market.politicsSerial` feed `hashBits`; no
  `Math.random()` in political rolls, so replays and benches are deterministic.
- Each `colony.corruption` is refreshed from the market before
  `OrbitEcon.advanceColony`; `orbit_sim.js` gets a `setCorruption` method.

Constants (starting values; a bench balance pass may tune the rates):

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `CORRUPTION_VISIT_DECAY` | `0.2` | per fly-visit |
| `CORRUPTION_GRACE` | `120` | world seconds before passive rise |
| `CORRUPTION_PASSIVE_RATE` | `0.1` | points per world second after grace |
| `CORRUPTION_GROWTH_FACTOR` | `100` | `growth *= max(0, 1 - C/100)` |
| `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE` | `1` | bribe corruption formula |
| `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SCALE` | `0.1` | per money unit |
| `BRIBE_MIN` | `1` | bot's small-bribe threshold |
| `ARREST_SECONDS` | `120` | slow transport to the Capital |
| `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` | `60` | one deterministic roll per planet per epoch |
| `REBEL_START` | `50` | zero rebellion chance at or below this |
| `REBEL_K` | `0.0322` | draft exponential rate |
| `REBEL_CAP` | `0.25` | draft asymptotic coefficient |
| `FLYBY_RAMP_SECONDS` | `240` | slow `1× → 4×` payout ramp |
| `MEDIC_PATIENTS` | `8` | two trips with the standard 8 t hold |
| `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` | `0.5` | total 4.00 per quelled planet |
| `PACIFY_CORRUPTION_MAX` | `40` | below `REBEL_START` after pacification |

Formulas:

```
growthFactor(C)       = max(0, 1 - C / 100)
bribeCorruption(money)= 1 + 0.1 * money
arrestChance(C)       = C <= 50 ? 0 : (C - 50) / 100      // 0.5 at C=100
rebellionChance(C)    = C <= 50 ? 0 : 0.25 * (1 - exp(-0.0322 * (C - 50)))
flybyMultiplier(age)  = 1 + 3 * (1 - exp(-age / FLYBY_RAMP_SECONDS))
```

Use `-Math.expm1(-x)` for numerical stability. The draft's rational
`1/(20-C)-0.5` formula and its "zero below 10" wording are superseded by the
draft's explicit asymptotic formula: rebellion has zero chance below 50 and
about `0.20` at `C=100`, with no division by zero. `growthFactor` multiplies
only positive `dPop`; famine losses remain losses.

## Visits, passive rise and population

- `harvestPlanet` is the single visit hook: subtract `0.2`, stamp
  `lastVisitStep`, clear `bribeVisitStep`, apply any active-contract/tourist
  landing logic as before. `HARVEST_TICK`'s per-step drip is not a new visit.
- `GravityBot.stepCareer` calls `GravityGame.advancePolitics(market, trade,
  runStep, rate)` once per world step, guarded by the step number; this is the
  one clock shared by the GUI and benches. It adds
  `CORRUPTION_PASSIVE_RATE * worldSeconds` after the grace, clamps to `0..100`,
  copies corruption into `trade` colonies, and performs one periodic rebellion
  roll per planet/epoch.
- A.R.F. and Capital corruption are fixed at `0`; neither bribes nor rebellion
  apply there.

## Bribes, arrest and bot personality

- `acceptBribe(career, planet)` requires the career to be landed on a normal,
  non-rebellious planet. `amount = 2 * planetProfitAt(market, planet, runStep)`
  — twice the growing fly-visit payout, **not** the rail's usage fee
  `launchFee(career, planet, SPEED_MIN)` — credited from local rulers to the
  career's purse and counted in new `bribes`/`bribeIncome` stats.
- Refuse when `bribeVisitStep[planet] === lastVisitStep[planet]` — one bribe per
  visit, so rulers do not complain about unfairness — or when the planet is
  Capital/A.R.F./rebellious. Add
  `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE + CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SCALE * amount` and mark the visit.
  A tiny bribe is legal but the bot refuses it below `BRIBE_MIN`; own EMC is a
  bot-policy refusal, not a global rule, because the human may choose.
- After adding corruption, roll arrest with `arrestChance(C_after)` using the
  deterministic political stream. At `C=50` the chance is `0`; at `C=100` it is
  `0.5`.
- Arrest relocates the career to `capitalPlanet` via slow transport: no fee, no
  reward, `arrestUntilStep = runStep + ARREST_SECONDS` in world-time steps,
  ready/planning blocked until then, medics/patients cargo cleared, and a GUI
  notice. Both rival `stepCareer` and the page's bot path respect the block.
- Bot policy: never bribe on its own EMC, reject `amount < BRIBE_MIN`, and use a
  seed-derived `bribeProne` personality on foreign EMCs. Prefer a bribe just
  before a rival's queued or expected fly-visit; otherwise leave it.

## Rebellion and seized infrastructure

- Once per `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` epoch, roll `rebellionChance(C)` per eligible
  planet with `hashBits(politicsSeed, planet, epoch)`. Do not roll every world
  step. On success set `rebel`, `rebelSinceStep`, `patients = MEDIC_PATIENTS`
  and `corruption` unchanged.
- Seize every career pad on that planet into one shared `REBEL_PAD`: keep
  catapult, panel, battery tiers and charge, but make ownership
  `OWNER_REBEL = -3`, charge the normal `CAREER.commercialFee`, and forbid
  claims/builds/upgrades permanently. Decrement each former owner's
  `padCount`; count the seized commercial rail as covered for completion.
- `planetProfitAt` multiplies a rebellious planet's ordinary payout by
  `flybyMultiplier(worldSeconds since rebelSinceStep)`, asymptotically `4×`.
  Live harvest and `estimateProfitLog` share the multiplier. Pacification
  restores `1×`, but the rails stay commercial.
- After pacification clamp corruption to `PACIFY_CORRUPTION_MAX` and start a
  rebellion cooldown so the same planet cannot re-rebel on the next epoch
  without corruption rising past `REBEL_START` again.

## Medical quest

Add `TASK_MEDIC = 3` and a dedicated `MEDIC_SLOT`; `BOARD_SIZE` grows by one.
A medical task is offered while any rebellious planet has patients. Task fields
hold `planet`, `medics`, `patients` and the existing `state`.

1. Accept only at the Capital with a free contract. Load
   `medics = min(floor(ship.hold.capacity / 2), market.patients[planet])` into
   `ship.cargoTons` — half the hold, no new ship stat.
2. Landing on the rebellious planet auto-unloads medics and loads the same
   number of patients, capped by the remaining patients. Set `TASK_LOADED` and
   keep `ship.cargoTons` equal to the patients aboard.
3. Landing at the Capital with patients pays
   `delivered * MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT`, subtracts `delivered` from
   `market.patients[planet]`, and clears the cargo. If patients remain,
   immediately reload the next half-hold of medics so a standard hold takes two
   trips. At zero patients, pacify the planet, drop the contract and clear all
   medical cargo.
4. `dropContract`, offer expiry, `evacuate`, arrest, career restart and career
   completion all call one `clearMedicalCargo` helper, so medics are unloaded
   automatically when the quest finishes for any reason.
5. `GravityBot.careerTarget` targets the Capital to load or deliver, then the
   rebellious planet to pick up; its score includes the per-patient reward and
   the temporary `4×` visit payout. It never bribes a rebel planet.

## Validation

- Visit subtracts `0.2`; passive rise starts only after grace; `C` stays `0..100`;
  `C=50` halves positive population growth and `C=100` stops it without
  reversing famine losses.
- Bribe formula, one-bribe-per-visit rule and arrest endpoints (`0` at 50,
  `0.5` at 100) hold; deterministic rolls replay identically.
- Rebellion probability matches the exponential formula, is `0` below 50, has
  no singularity at `100`, and is rolled once per epoch rather than per step.
- Seized pads retain upgrades, charge the commercial fee, stay commercial after
  pacification, and career completion remains possible.
- Standard hold quells `8` patients in two medical trips; upgraded hold does it
  in one; all quest-exit paths clear medical cargo and refit ship mass.
- `POLITICS=0` A/B leaves the 0.5.11 career numbers unchanged; `dom_smoke`,
  all benches, `node --check` and `git diff --check` green.

## Scope boundary

No normal delivery economy change, no new ship stats, no new planet geometry,
no change to burn physics or replay encoding. Tuning corruption, arrest,
rebellion and reward rates is a balance pass.
