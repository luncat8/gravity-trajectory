ERNIE-5.5f:

The useful lever is **not** gravity or distance — it is the **opening market's starting age** (`MONEY.initSec`). Opening fairness is a ratio of first-arrival pay, and the pay curve saturates with age, so a higher starting age compresses the ratio without touching the field.

## Why the physics proposals don't work

The oracle proves a fair HQ set already exists on all 12 seeds of 20/6; it ranks at #177/#172 on the two hard seeds. The planner fails because its exact check stops at 48 sets and the fair set costs ~22 s to reach.

| knob | 20/6 seeds 5 / 10 | full-cell effect |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | fair set #177 / #172, ~22 / ~17 s | 20/5 1/12, 20/6 2/12, 26/5 3/12, 26/6 3/12 |
| gravity ×1.2 less (`GS=0.833`) | none in 1000 / none in 250; best alt 0.50 / 0.54 | altFair 11/12, game fair 1/12, 114 s |
| distance +20% (`FS=1/1.44≈0.694` proxy) | none in 250; best alt 0.36 / 0.28 | worse; also less reach inside the 120 s horizon |
| EMC force ×1.2 (`FS=1.2`) | #24 / #26 | 20/6 3/12 but 26/5 1/12, 26/6 1/12, 13/4 8/12 — mixed |
| EMC speed ×1.2 (`FS=1.44`) | #95 / #238 | mixed, not robust |

EMC +20% does keep the heavy-world share inside the 2–4 band (2.9 of 10), so it is not a §2 violation; it just is not a global fairness fix.

## The option that works: `MONEY.initSec` 40 → 160–200

`INIT_SEC` only moves the market clock; gravity, distances, EMC and the heavy-world share are untouched.

| value | 20/6 seeds 5 / 10 | 20/4 | 26/6 sameHQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 (base) | #177 / #172 | 12/12 | 4/12 |
| 120 | #28 / #26 | 11/12 | 3/12 |
| 160 | — | 12/12 | 5/12 |
| 200 | #28 / #8 | 12/12 | 7/12 |

With the existing joint planner forced on, `INIT_SEC=120` makes both hard seeds fair — 64% and 56% in 2.9 s and 3.3 s; baseline is 31% / 48% in 4.1 / 4.5 s. That meets the 4 s budget on the exact seeds that were failing.

**Cost:** it breaks 0.7.7's "a quick hop pays what it always paid" — pay at t=0 rises from 0.24 to 0.68 (120 s) or 1.07 (200 s). The early economy needs rebalancing, and saved maps/benches re-baseline (already planned for +5). It is an economy decision, not a free knob.

## If the economy change is unacceptable

Keep the physics and make the exact check cheaper: coarse-to-fine fan, verify exit only for the chosen assignment, and pair-pinned ranking for >14 candidates. The fair set is in the top 250 on every 20/6 seed, so a 5–10× faster exact check brings it inside 4 s. Raising the pair-pinned threshold 14→20 alone improved the 0.7.42 chosen ratio 0.06→0.27 (seed 5) and no-site→0.38 (seed 10), but cost ~8 s and did not reach fair.
