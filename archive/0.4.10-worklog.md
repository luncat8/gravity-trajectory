# 0.4.10 — Per-Row copySpec Balance Button & Science Hull Research Panel

## Review of 0.4.9 (`f3aeaa0`)

The fly-time impulse engine, burn codec and in-flight GUI were solid and all
benches stayed green. Wiring the two remaining 0.4.0-plan items (§10 science,
§11 balance tooling) surfaced three real gaps in the foundations they reuse:

1. **`orbit_science.js` was a module-global singleton.** Its `families` map
   made the hull API fine for the early node benches but unusable as per-career
   state: careers restart in place and two careers must not share discoveries.
   Refactored to hull instances — `createHull()` plus hull-first
   `ensureHull`/`previewHull`/`acceptHull`/`statsHull`/`pointsHull` — with the
   old family-only functions kept as thin wrappers over a `defaultHull`, so
   every existing `career_bench.js` hull check is byte-identical.
2. **The distance solver mispriced an already-accepted vertex.** The projected
   gradient loop (200 fixed iterations, step `1/‖P‖_F²`) converges slowly on
   the real tech points: engine `(thrust, mass)` and catapult
   `(maxForce, efficiency)` tuples are nearly collinear and badly scaled, so
   re-previewing an accepted point reported a small nonzero distance (and a
   fee above the base) instead of `inside`. Added an exact-vertex short
   circuit before the iteration; the gradient path is unchanged for genuine
   interior/exterior targets.
3. **`orbit_science.js` was never loaded by the page** (it was node-only, as
   the old README line stated). Added the classic `<script>` tag after
   `orbit_tech.js`; the module already guards `module.exports`.

## Design & Implementation

### 1. §11 Per-row `copySpec` balance button (`gravity_gui.js`)

- `copyButtonHtml(row, item)` renders a compact `copy` mini button in every
  shop row: gear and part rows copy the tier on offer, the max-tier row copies
  the fitted record (`<i>max</i> copy`), and the pad-less panel/battery rows
  ("needs a catapult") stay item-less. The pad-less catapult row still copies
  `EMC-1`, so every tech record is reachable without widening the 390 px
  drawer — rows keep `flex-wrap`.
- `careerCopySpec(data)` reuses `OrbitTech.copySpec(kind, item)` and the
  existing `navigator.clipboard` pattern: clipboard present → write + "copied
  spec" notice; absent (`file://`) or rejected → the full spec line goes to
  the drawer notice so it stays selectable. The act is dispatched before the
  "career over" gate, so balance collection works on a closed career and with
  an empty purse (the button is never disabled).
- `career_bench.js` now prints all 27 `tech: KIND ID { … }` lines across the
  seven tables, giving the tweak-and-recheck balance dump §11 asks for.

### 2. §10 Science hull research panel (`orbit_science.js`, `gravity_game.js`, `gravity_gui.js`)

- **Per-career hull seeded from the tech tree.** `createCareer` owns a fresh
  `OrbitScience.createHull()`; `resetCareer` rebuilds it via `seedScience`,
  accepting the three engine points `(thrust, mass)` and five catapult points
  `(maxForce, efficiency)` labelled by their tech ids. The known tree is
  therefore discovered science; research invents points around it. Hull state
  is session-only, matching pads and contracts (career links intentionally
  carry no builds).
- **Game transaction.** `researchPreview`/`researchCost` (cheat-aware:
  communism → 0), `researchLabel` (`R<n>` per family), and
  `researchPoint(career, family, params, label)` — HQ-only, open-career-only,
  known-family, purse-gated; on success it deducts the preview fee, accepts
  the point into the hull and bumps `research`/`lastResearch`. The two
  researchable spaces are exported as `GravityGame.SCIENCE_KINDS`.
- **Panel.** A new `#careerScience` block under the shop: family switch
  (`engine`/`catapult`, active button marked `.on`), two range sliders per
  family with axis metadata in `SCIENCE_AXES`, a live readout
  (`[26.0, 1.8] · outside · dist 0.06 · fee 2.38`), an HQ/affordability-gated
  `research · fee` button, and per-family hull summaries including the custom
  `R#` labels. Slider events are delegated from the drawer (no per-slider
  listeners survive innerHTML re-renders); an input patches only the readout
  elements so the dragged range is never replaced mid-gesture, while family
  switches and career edits re-render the whole block from a retained draft.
- **Affordable defaults.** The initial sliders sit just outside the seeded
  hull (`engine (26, 1.8)` ≈ 2.38, `catapult (100, 0.74)` ≈ 3.04), so the
  starter purse of 6.00 funds the first invention and the panel teaches both
  pricing regimes; pushing a slider outward shows the `k·dist` growth
  (e.g. `(60, 0.5)` → 122.60).
- **CSS** (`.sciAxis`, `.sciVal`, `.sciHull`): sliders shrink
  (`min-width: 60px; max-width: 100%`) and wrap instead of overflowing the
  narrow drawer; long hull lines ellipsise like every other `.careerLine`.

   the knowledge hull only; the physics functions already accept generic
   records, so an 0.4.11 could surface an accepted engine/catapult point as a
   one-time purchasable prototype in the shop (custom `buildCost` from the
   research fee, install/build through the existing `installPart`/`buildGear`
   paths).

   launch, cargo delivery and sport-competition captions from the career
