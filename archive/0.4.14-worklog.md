# 0.4.14 worklog — fourth research family (battery) and one science-family table

## review of 0.4.13 (`f1e80ab`)

The HUD work holds up: the column scroll, the fold buttons and the `--hudMaxH`
reservation are consistent, and no wheel handler exists that `pointer-events:
auto` on `#leftHud` could swallow. The tank family was correct but it made the
cost of a family visible in three places at once, and it left the glue slightly
worse than before:

1. **A family was five edits.** `SCIENCE_KINDS`, `newPrototypes()`,
   `seedScience` (one hand-written loop per family, with a `tier > 0` filter
   only for tanks), the `unlockPrototype` nested ternary, and in the GUI
   `SCIENCE_AXES` + `SCIENCE_DEFAULTS` + a hand-copied `scienceValues` literal.
   A fourth family would have had to touch all of them, and a missing edit fails
   silently (an empty prototype list, an unseeded hull).
2. **`scienceMetricText` was a per-family prose ternary** that restated
   `METRIC_SCALE`. It cannot be right for a family that has none, and it drifts
   from the balance table as soon as a scale is retuned. `1 RU` is exactly
   `1 / METRIC_SCALE[family][axis]` in that axis' own slider unit, so the line is
   derivable data, not prose.
3. **Researched pad gear could not be applied network-wide.** `gearRowHtml`
   renders `all N` for a table tier, but `protoGearRowHtml` (researched
   catapults) only offered a single-planet `build`, so a paid breakthrough could
   not be rolled out to the pads already built — §4 of the plan's bulk rule
   applied to bought tiers only.

## implementation

### 1. One table per science family (`gravity_game.js`)

`SCIENCE_FAMILIES` holds `{ kind, table, params(record), record(...) }` for
`engine`, `catapult`, `tank` and `battery`; `SCIENCE_KINDS`, `newPrototypes()`
(one list per family), `seedScience` (every non-tier-0 ladder record, so the
tank `FT-0` special case is gone) and `unlockPrototype` (via `scienceFamilyOf`)
all derive from it. Exporting the table lets the bench assert that a new family
arrives with its balance data.

### 2. `battery (capacity, leak)` — the first pad-gear science family

- `OrbitScience.METRIC_SCALE.battery = [0.2, 1000]` (1 RU = 5 kWh = 0.001 leak
  per year) and `FRONTIER.battery = [capacity ↓×0.20 ↑×1.20, leak ↓×2.50 ↑×0.20]`:
  a bigger buffer is moderately hard, a leak-tight seal is the expensive
  direction, matching the existing "lighter is dear" axis of engine mass and
  tank empty mass.
- `OrbitTech.batteryRecord(id, capacity, leak)` places the invention on the
  `BATTERIES` ladder by capacity and interpolates `buildCost` like the other
  three families.
- Seeded from `BAT-1..BAT-5` (range `[1.5..16 kWh, 0.0003..0.001 leak]`), so the
  slider ranges `1..20 kWh` / `0.0002..0.0012` reach both directions.
- Accepted batteries are pad gear: the shop row offers `build` on the selected
  planet and `all N` for the network, `buildGear`/`bulkUpgrade` charge the new
  battery to its capacity, and ship mass never changes.

### 3. Review fixes in the panel and the shop (`gravity_gui.js`)

- `scienceMetricText` is now `1 RU = <1/scale> <axis label>` for every axis of
  `METRIC_SCALE`, so the readout follows the balance table and a family without
  a scale prints `?` instead of a wrong sentence. `scienceSpecText` no longer
  throws on a missing `METRIC_SCALE`.
- `scienceValues` is a copy of `SCIENCE_DEFAULTS`, not a third hand-kept table.
- `gearFits(pad, kind, item)` states the pad rule once (catapult may take an
  empty slot; panel/battery need a catapult), `bulkButtonHtml` is shared by table
  tiers and prototypes, `protoGearRowHtml` is family-generic (catapult and
  battery), and `protoRowsHtml` lists prototypes under their own ladder rows
  instead of three hand-written loops in `shopBlockHtml`.
