## what changed

**View bar (index.html, gravity_gui.js, gui_view.js, gui_hud_game.js)** — the radar checkbox is
replaced by two trajectory checkboxes that work in every mode:

- `trails` (`trailRivalsOn`, default on) — other players' past-flight trails: rival companies in
  career, rival ships in the lab. Own trail is *not* its job.
- `predicted` (`predRivalsOn`, default off) — other players' predicted trajectories. Career alone
  locks the box (`syncTrajectoryUi`: disabled while the selected company's HQ holds no radar);
  **building the radar also ticks the box** (`careerRadar`), so the reveal is on the moment the
  facility exists. Outside career there is no facility to own, the box just works.
- `clean render` is the own-layer switch now (visible in every mode): hides the own past-flight
  trail + thick orbit visit highlights, and nothing about other players.

**Radar overlay (gui_draw_world.js)** — `radarRecordShown` is one flat gate: parked `landed`
plates never hide (career), the selected ship always draws, and a rival draws when the predicted
box is on or the pointer hovers/presses it. Clean render is out of the business entirely;
hover/press stays as the per-ship peek with the box off, and the hovered ghost still rings its
landing planet. The reveal is keyed by `record.sim` (`radarHoverSim` / `radarPressSim`) instead
of career, so the same path serves every mode.

**Lab pool** — outside career the records come from `labRadarShips`: one record per flying
rival, predicted off the sim's own buffers under the same memo rule as the career's
`radarPredict` (a static sky predicts once, a step or a burn re-predicts; stamps per slot,
reset when the slot's sim is replaced). The selected slot is skipped — its buffers are the
aim/flight trace of that frame. Route mode is untouched: its plans are not flying ships, so the
pool stays empty there.

**Time plate** — `drawRadarLabels` puts a flying ship's `+N s` mark on the landing point its
time belongs to (the ghost chevron's position), not above the ship's current location; the
parked `landed` plate keeps floating above its planet.

**Copy** — view-bar titles, the radar build title and the built row, README view rows and
`drafts/new/radar.md` (human lines kept, `→` markers added) updated to the new controls.
