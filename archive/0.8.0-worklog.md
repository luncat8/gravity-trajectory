## step 1 — standalone solver (`gravity_fft.js` + `experiments/fft_test.js`)

Done, no game hook, no roster/page wiring, per plan `## order` 1.

- `gravity_fft.js`: radix-2 `fft1d`/`fft2d` (rows then columns, cached
  twiddle/reverse tables), `gridFor` (pow-2 grid, ≤10 px cells → 256×256 at
  1600×1000), `buildKernels` with the plan's tie convention (tied component
  zeroed, `(L/2)²` kept in the denominator, complete Nyquist row/column),
  geometry-keyed kernel spectra with the phi DC bin zeroed (mean-zero φ),
  signed CIC `deposit`/`gather` (wrap every neighbour, no cell-area factor,
  coordinate normalised into the world first so x and x+L deposit
  bit-identically), `solve` depositing `strength × PERIODIC_STRENGTH = 0.5`
  once, immutable snapshots whose `sample`/`samplePhi` apply `MU × scale` at
  sample time (the `g field` slider never re-solves).
- `experiments/fft_test.js` (`node experiments/fft_test.js`): round-trip on
  8×8/8×4/4×8/16×2, single-mode normalisation/axis/sign, impulse vs shifted
  kernels (φ compared mean-referenced, matching the DC convention), direct
  circular convolution vs `fft2d` product, signed/zero-sum CIC conservation
  incl. out-of-world deposits, kernel ties/oddness/DC, uniform background,
  self-force, seam + whole-cell translation + deposit-wrap identity, an
  independent wrapped-direct oracle (scattered/cell/seam/radial/cancellation
  samples) with `0.05|a|+0.01·A_REF` outside 2ε and `0.10|a|+0.01·A_REF`
  inside, two-cell half-world-cut strips (magnitude `MU·PS·Σ|s|/SOFT²`,
  mirror symmetry with the smooth background subtracted, bounded jump), and
  mean-zero φ / signed polarity / FD `−grad(φ)` vs acceleration.
  Result: **pass** (worst ordinary ratio 1.3 %, worst inside ratio 23 %,
  lattice strip samples 2055/2032 ≥ 192×8).

Fixes made while bringing the test up (test-oracle side, not solver
behaviour): φ impulse expectation is mean-referenced; kernel-vs-`pow`
comparisons take a few ULPs; origin zeros compare as `=== 0` (`-0`); exact
tie samples snap their mirror onto themselves (float `2b−p` lands 1 ulp off
the cut and the zero-at-tie convention would see the full jump); lattice
jitter is ±1 px around the cell centre (plan §5), not ±1 cell; dense-lattice
configs admit no outside-2ε point (spacing < 2ε), so they assert a healthy
`inside` count instead of `ordinary > 40`.
