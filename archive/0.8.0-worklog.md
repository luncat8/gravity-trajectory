## step 1 — standalone solver (`gravity_fft.js` + `experiments/fft_test.js`)

Done, no game hook, no roster/page wiring, per plan `## order` 1.

- `gravity_fft.js`: radix-2 `fft1d`/`fft2d` (rows then columns, cached
  twiddle/reverse tables), `gridFor` (pow-2 grid, ≤10 px cells → 256×256 at
  1600×1000), `buildKernels` with the plan's tie convention (tied component
  zeroed, `(L/2)²` kept in the denominator, complete Nyquist row/column),
  geometry-keyed kernel spectra with the phi DC bin zeroed (mean-zero φ),
  signed CIC `deposit`/`gather` (wrap every neighbour, no cell-area factor,
  coordinate normalised into the world first so x and x+L deposit
  bit-identically), `solve` depositing `strength × PERIODIC_STRENGTH = 0.5`
  once, immutable snapshots whose `sample`/`samplePhi` apply `MU × scale` at
  sample time (the `g field` slider never re-solves).
- `experiments/fft_test.js` (`node experiments/fft_test.js`): round-trip on
  8×8/8×4/4×8/16×2, single-mode normalisation/axis/sign, impulse vs shifted
  kernels (φ compared mean-referenced, matching the DC convention), direct
  circular convolution vs `fft2d` product, signed/zero-sum CIC conservation
  incl. out-of-world deposits, kernel ties/oddness/DC, uniform background,
  self-force, seam + whole-cell translation + deposit-wrap identity, an
  independent wrapped-direct oracle (scattered/cell/seam/radial/cancellation
  samples) with `0.05|a|+0.01·A_REF` outside 2ε and `0.10|a|+0.01·A_REF`
  inside, two-cell half-world-cut strips (magnitude `MU·PS·Σ|s|/SOFT²`,
  mirror symmetry with the smooth background subtracted, bounded jump), and
  mean-zero φ / signed polarity / FD `−grad(φ)` vs acceleration.
  Result: **pass** (worst ordinary ratio 1.3 %, worst inside ratio 23 %,
  lattice strip samples 2055/2032 ≥ 192×8).

Fixes made while bringing the test up (test-oracle side, not solver
behaviour): φ impulse expectation is mean-referenced; kernel-vs-`pow`
comparisons take a few ULPs; origin zeros compare as `=== 0` (`-0`); exact
tie samples snap their mirror onto themselves (float `2b−p` lands 1 ulp off
the cut and the zero-at-tie convention would see the full jump); lattice
jitter is ±1 px around the cell centre (plan §5), not ±1 cell; dense-lattice
configs admit no outside-2ε point (spacing < 2ε), so they assert a healthy
`inside` count instead of `ordinary > 40`.

## step 2 — hook `GravitySim` to the periodic FFT torus (DONE)

Implemented per plan `## order` step 2, no page wiring or roster expansion
(step 3). `classic_physics_test` fingerprints are bit-identical because every
periodic branch is gated behind `this.periodic` (default `false`).

- `gravity_sim.js`:
  - `require('./gravity_fft.js')` (Node) / `root.GravityFFT` (page) bridge.
  - Topology: `this.periodic` flag + `setPeriodic(periodic, gridSource)`
    (pad 0, no margin); `periodicGrid()` accessor returning the immutable
    snapshot `{…, revision}` or `null` for moving sources; `periodic` public
    boolean.
  - Lazy immutable snapshots: `ensurePeriodicGrid()` caches by `revision`,
    re-solves only when invalidated. Adoption: a probe passes `gridSource`
    (a sibling sim) and reuses its live snapshot (no re-solve, no mutation);
    any field edit clears the borrow and re-solves its own.
  - Invalidation hooks (each calls `invalidatePeriodic()` when `this.periodic`):
    `setField`, `setCount`, `setPlanetMask`, `setStrength` (and only when the
    value actually changed — `setStrength` returns `false` otherwise, so it
    does not re-solve), `resetField`. `refreshField`/`fillStep`/`setScales`
    never solve (the `g field` slider applies `MU * scale` at sample time).
    Direct array writers bypass `setStrength`; their hooks are step-5 touch points.
  - `advance` samples the grid when `periodic && fieldStatic`, else the direct
    minimum-image sum (moving periodic); wraps once after integration. Shared by
    live `stepOne`, sliced and full prediction.
  - `fieldAt` samples the grid (`periodic && fieldStatic`) else direct
    min-image; `MU`/`SOFT` sourced from one place.
  - Min-image everywhere periodic: `impact`, `markSystemsAt`, `stepInsideAt`,
    `fieldDrift` use `GravityFFT.minImage`; the `beyondPlanets` AABB early-out
    is bypassed when periodic. `impact` falls back to `stepX/bodyR` only when
    `planetCount === 0`. `outside()` returns `false` when periodic.
  - Seam-correct length: `routeStep`/`stepOne` use `hypot(vx,vy)*DT` (the
    pre-wrap step) only in periodic mode; classic keeps the live delta, so
    fingerprints are unchanged. `spawn` wraps its initial state.
  - Solve counters: `GravityFFT.solves`; `refreshField`/`spawn`/`predict`/
    `setScales` do not solve; strength/count/mask/seed/topology edits solve
    once before use; probes do not mutate a shared snapshot.

- `experiments/periodic_sim_test.js` (new headless test, added to fast tier):
  8 checks — wrap kinematics (cross-seam, no `END_EXIT`, path length does not
  jump by W), live/full/sliced prediction agreement across a seam (compared in
  float64 via spawn/advance wrappers, since `predX/predY` are `Float32Array`),
  spawn origin wrap to the far edge, impact/`stepInsideAt` span the seam,
  `fieldDrift` across the seam equals the min-image oracle (not ~W),
  solve-counter pins (no solve on refresh/slider; one re-solve on strength/
  count/field/reset), snapshot adoption (shared grid, no re-solve, immutable),
  and moving periodic source on the direct min-image sum with no grid solve.

## step 6 — strength calibration A/B, classic & periodic verification, smoke & archive (DONE)

### `PERIODIC_STRENGTH` calibration A/B (`0.40` vs `0.50` vs `0.60`)

Measured with `gravity_bench`-style trajectory sweeps (seed 7, 24 angles × 6
speeds `[40, 80, 120, 180, 240, 320]` across classic 26w and periodic 64w/104w)
and `career_gravity_bench.js` (`FFT=1 STATS=1 LOG=0 SEEDS=1-4 SECONDS=400`,
64-world periodic career with 2 normal rivals + fair seat search = 8 rival
runs per setting) against the classic 13-world baseline (`SEEDS=1-12`):

| configuration | starter escape | impact % | self-hit % | horizon/orbit % | avg flight | avg visits | mean deflection | career profit/s | property | flights | pads | dead pads | worn out | mean reach |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| classic 26w / 13w (`1.00`) | 100.0 % | 70.0 % | 16.9 % | 0.43 % | 8.89 s | 3.75 | 73.0° | 0.024/s | 11.41 | 177 (24 co) | 98 | 2 | 0 / 24 | 8.39 |
| periodic 104w / 64w (`0.40`) | 100.0 % | 99.9–100 % | 6.4 % / 7.3 % | 0.04 % | 4.22 s / 5.80 s | 5.59 / 5.34 | 37.4° / 43.2° | 0.011/s | 10.26 | 34 (8 co) | 35 | 0 | 1 / 8 | 13.24 |
| **periodic 104w / 64w (`0.50`)** | **100.0 %** | **100.0 %** | **7.3 % / 8.6 %** | **0.02 %** | **3.95 s / 5.25 s** | **5.44 / 5.20** | **40.8° / 47.8°** | **0.029/s** | **17.10** | **57 (8 co)** | **53** | **0** | **0 / 8** | **13.21** |
| periodic 104w / 64w (`0.60`) | 100.0 % | 100.0 % | 8.6 % / 9.4 % | 0.00 % | 3.68 s / 4.89 s | 5.28 / 5.05 | 44.3° / 49.8° | 0.012/s | 10.64 | 46 (8 co) | 36 | 0 | 0 / 8 | 11.74 |

Decision: **retain `PERIODIC_STRENGTH = 0.5`**. At `0.40`, weak wells let shots
drift longer (`5.80 s` avg on 64w), cutting flight cadence (`34` flights) and
causing `1 / 8` ships to wear out. At `0.60`, stronger local wells increase
self-hits (`9.4 %`), pinch starter pad reach (`13.21 → 11.74`), and halve net
profit (`0.012/s`). `0.50` peaks on career profit (`0.029/s`, closely matching
classic `0.024/s`), property (`17.10`), flights (`57`), and pads (`53`) with
zero dead pads, zero stuck companies, and zero worn-out ships.
