## 2. review of the previous task (multiplayer pause) — found and fixed

**The manual impulse loop always flew YOU's ship.** `canImpulse()`, the burn
aim, the grip, the trim branch, `shipAt` and the `coasting …` text read
`cargoSim` whichever player was selected. With a hand-flown R1 selected while
YOU's drive ship coasted:

- R1's launch row read `coasting · 51.0 px/s` and R1's launch click did nothing;
- R1, holding the clock, could arm and fire a burn on YOU's ship, priced from
  R1's career.

Fix: the loop follows the selected slot. `canImpulse()` is false outside career,
in replay and for a bot-flown player, otherwise `activeSim()` must be flying
with `burnLimit() > 0`; `executeBurn` burns `slotSim(slot)` with
`playerCareer()` and records into that slot's last shot. `careerBotBurn` and
`applyReplayBurns` stay on slot 0. Side effect: a selected bot-flown drive ship
shows the normal locked row instead of `coasting …` and a Space hint its flight
never honoured.

`experiments/multiplayer_impulse_test.js` (new) fails on the old code at
`the launch row answers for the selected player` and covers: R1 queues a shot
while YOU coast, R1's hold alone opens no burn, YOU can still pause into one, a
flying R1 burns its own fuel into its own shot at its own step, YOU's fuel and
shot untouched. `experiments/dom_stub.js`: `shipScreen` and `dragBurn` take an
optional sim.

Two smaller ones:

- a hand-flown rival's score meta showed bot scheduler text
  (`gap · retry in 6.0 s`); now only a bot row appends `actorStateShort`
  (`gui_hud_text.js`), asserted in the impulse test;
- the "another human shares the clock" loop was written twice in
  `gui_career.js`; now `otherHumanPlays()`.
