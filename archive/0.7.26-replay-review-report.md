#### Root causes in `gravity_bot.js`

1. **Unbuildable rival pads and Capital `P1` get the full "new network pad" bonus — scoring higher than real unclaimed planets and own pads (`careerScoreFacts`, `gravity_bot.js:2588–2598`).**
   - Any landing where `own.catapult === null` receives `CAREER_NETWORK_PAD_VALUE (4.0) + careerSiteValue (~1.4–2.3) - departure.cost - wear`, **without checking `GravityGame.planetBuildable(career, body)`**.
   - Because `departure.cost` on a rival pad is cheap usage tax (`~0.13–0.62`) and on Capital `P1` is `0.00`, whereas an unclaimed planet subtracts `EMC-1` build (`3.04`) or commercial exit (`2.27+`), foreign pads get a **`+2.4` to `+3.0` phantom score advantage** over unclaimed worlds — and even outscore the bot's own pads (`CAREER_BUILT_PAD_VALUE = 2.5`)!
   - *In your replay:* On turn 1, R2 already had an unclaimed-colony shot `P7→P5` in its 24-angle fan paying **`+3.74`** (enough to land with `9.74` and immediately build `EMC-1` on `P5`), scored `+5.36` — and passed it up for your HQ `P11` paying only `+1.10` because `P11` got `padVal = +5.11` (`score +6.10`). R1 similarly passed up unclaimed `P5` (`pay +3.19`, `score +4.73`) and `P0` (`score +3.08`) for your `P11` (`score +7.03`). At `t+817s`, R2 even flew `P5→P1` (Capital) at **`pay −0.71, fee 0.75`** (losing `1.46` cash) because Capital's fake `padVal +4.47` pushed its score to `+3.01`.
   - *Counterfactual check:* Patching only this unbuildable-planet bonus in memory on seed `93293` eliminated both rival bankruptcies and took R1+R2 from `1+1` pads to `3–4 + 2–3` pads (with one rival harvesting `53.84` in visit income — matching your `55.63`).

2. **Destination battery blindness → `4.54` commercial-rail trap and hair-trigger evacuations (`careerDeparture` `1791–1815`, `careerNextShot` `3846–3870`, `careerNoShot` `3891–3895`).**
   - `careerDeparture` computes exit capability from full `pad.battery.capacity` (`1.5 kWh`), ignoring live `chargeKwh - reservedKwh`. Both bots jumped onto your HQ `P11` expecting a `0.61` rival-tax exit right after you fired from `P11` and drained its battery.
   - Finding `P11` empty, `careerNextShot` immediately fell back to the commercial rail and paid **`4.54` each** (`P11→P6`) because `P6`'s fake `+4.23/+4.60` rival-pad bonus made a deeply cash-negative flight score `+2.31/+2.42`.
   - Later, when a rival pad was briefly drained below `30 px/s` (`P11` at `0.04 kWh` for R1 at `t+236.7s`; `P5` at `0.10 kWh` / `21 px/s` for R2 at `t+633.0s`), `careerNoShot` saw `career.stranded === true` (set for any non-own, non-Capital planet in `gravity_game.js:4491`) and evacuated after just **`idleTries >= 2` (8–11 s)** — wiping R1's `4.04` purse and R2's `2.98` purse plus `105–127 s` in transit, instead of waiting a short recharge for the solar panel (in R2's case, you upgraded `P5` to a full `BAT-2` literally `1.1 s` later).

3. **Zero multi-bot shot deconfliction (`careerLaunch`, `gravity_bot.js:3950–3995`).**
   - At `t+26.3s` (R2) and `t+28.3s` (R1) on `P11`, both bots independently selected the exact same commercial shot (`angle 1.92, v85.5, P11→P6`). Because both wait on `P11`'s shared rotating barrel, both launched on **the exact same step (`3608`, `t+30.1s`)**, paying `4.54` each.
   - `stepCareer` steps R1 before R2 every tick, so R1 harvested every flyby and landing report along the 77 s trajectory while R2 got **`+0.00`** (`purse 1.84 → 1.84`), landed at `0%` hull, couldn't pay the `2.00` mandatory repair, and went bankrupt at `t+118.1s`.

4. **Unconditional `HOLD-8` purchase + reachability-blind contracts + Capital `P1` deadlock (`careerBetweenFlights` `2236–2244`, `careerAccept` `1974–1996`, `careerNoShot` `3891–3897`).**
   - You stayed on `HOLD-0` (`10.0 t`, `91.7 px/s` on `EMC-1`), took 0 contracts, and snowballed observation income into pads. Both bots bought `HOLD-8` on landing 1 (`−0.76`, `+1.5 t` → `11.5 t`) yet delivered **0 contracts** all game; the extra `15%` mass permanently cut their `EMC-1` speed cap to `85.5 px/s` and raised launch energy, usage taxes, and commercial fees by `15%`.
   - `careerAccept` scores contracts by `reward - 0.02*tons` plus tiny pad bonuses without checking whether `from`/`to` are reachable, whether a Capital leg's `−3.00` fine exceeds the reward (R2 accepted `P1→P8` for `1.70`, losing money even before fees), or whether loaded mass can exit `to`.
   - Conversely, on Capital `P1`, `career.stranded` is `false`, so when R2 landed on `P1` at `t+836.2s` with `purse 0.54` and `cond 7%` (`0 payable · 276 nonviable exits`), it could neither launch nor trigger evacuation — spending the last **`168 s`** endlessly accepting and dropping unreachable contracts.

5. **Pad-building sequencing & overly high remote-expansion reserve (`careerBetweenFlights` `2212–2232`, `2275–2289`; `careerNextPad` `1955–1972`).**
   - `careerRepair` (`cond <= 0.70`) runs *before* on-site `EMC-1` claim (`3.04`), while `careerScoreFacts` only reserves repair cash when `arrivalCondition === 0` — so a bot can fly to claim a planet, spend up to `1.99` on non-mandatory repair, and drop below `3.04`.
   - Remote expansion (`careerNextPad`) requires `expansionReserve = 2.27 + 6.00 (evac) + 2.00 (repair) = 10.27`, meaning a bot needs **`purse >= 13.31`** to buy a `3.04` `EMC-1` (you routinely expanded at `4.44–6.63`), and ignores high-`g` worlds like `P4`/`P9` (`EMC-1 + EMC-2 = 10.87`, which you claimed at the end) because `careerSiteValue` only tests `EMC-1 + BAT-1`.

6. **Launch-book lock-in, missing `1.0×` top-speed rung in primary search, and no slingshot refinement (`careerPlan` `2843–2910`, `careerSit` `3914–3932`).**
   - With inflated rival `padVal`, stored shots `P11↔P6` keep clearing `CAREER_BOOK_ENOUGH = 6` on ~`+2` pay, so `careerPlan` skips the fan and shuttles between the same two planets while outer worlds (`P4, P8, P9, P10, P12`) ripen untouched for your `70–97 s` multi-flyby tours (`3.97` visit income & `3,246 px` per flight vs R1's `1.45` & `1,863 px`).
   - Primary `careerPlan` (`fallback = false`) sets `speedCount = 4` on `CAREER_SPEED_FRACTIONS = [0.38, 0.55, 0.72, 0.86, 1]` — never testing full `1.0×` rail speed — caps horizon at `45 s`, and performs no fine-angle refinement around grazing multi-flyby shots (unlike `ROUTE_REFINES` in Route mode).
   - When the shuttle dries up, bots sit `4 × 30–33 s` (`sit 1..4/4`) and then `forced` fires a negative-score self-bounce (`P5→P5`), draining the pad right before needing it.

#### Highest-impact improvements (if/when you want to tune `gravity_bot.js`)

1. **Separate pad valuation by ownership/buildability in `careerScoreFacts`:** only grant `CAREER_NETWORK_PAD_VALUE + site` to buildable planets (and weight `departure.canBuildStarter` positively because `3.04` buys a permanent asset rather than a sunk commercial fee); score rival pads below own pads (`< CAREER_BUILT_PAD_VALUE`) and Capital `P1` at `0` pad bonus so the `−3.00` fine is respected.
2. **Account for live pad charge & wait vs. commercial/evacuate:** check live `chargeKwh - reservedKwh` (plus solar recharge wait) in `careerDeparture`/`careerNextShot`; prefer waiting a short recharge over paying a `4.54` commercial fee on a net-losing hop or evacuating after 2 idle tries on a recharging pad; and allow evacuation from Capital `P1` when `idleTries` exceeds a threshold with `0` payable exits.
3. **Claim before optional repair & right-size `expansionReserve`:** if `career.at` is buildable and `cond > 0`, prioritize claiming `EMC-1` before optional (`cond <= 0.70`) repair (or include the `0.70` repair cost in `canBuildStarter` forecasting); trim the full `6.00` `evacFee` out of `expansionReserve` once the ship sits on a working own rail; and allow `EMC-1 + EMC-2` claims on high-`g` planets when cash covers both plus reserve.
4. **Delay `HOLD-8` and sanity-check contracts:** hold off buying `HOLD-8` until a small pad base (e.g. 2–3 pads) or a genuinely profitable local contract exists; in `careerAccept`, penalize Capital landing fines, check loaded exit/lift feasibility, and drop/avoid contracts after repeated unreachable scans instead of re-accepting another unreachable board entry immediately.
5. **Deconflict rival queues/flights:** discount expected `visitPay` (or skip exact `(origin, angleU, speedU)` duplicates) when another actor is already queued on the same pad/bearing or flying toward the same planet.
6. **Better harvest trajectories (especially for `normal`/`hard`):** include the `1.0×` speed fraction in the primary fan, add a small local angle refinement around the top 2–3 harvest candidates (like Route mode), and lower/gate `CAREER_BOOK_ENOUGH` on net cash profit (not inflated `padValue`) so bots explore fresh planets instead of repeating one depleted shuttle.

### 6. Is a separate bot log needed, or is `replay.txt` enough?

**`replay.txt` alone is enough — and actually better than `copy bot log` — whenever the codebase matches the replay.** Replaying `replay.txt` deterministically regenerates the exact `copy bot log` for `R1` and `R2`, *and* recovers early decisions that `copy bot log` drops once the ring buffer passes `TRACE_MAX = 240` lines (here, the critical `t+0..28.3s` opening was already evicted by the end of the run), while also letting us inspect rejected fan candidates, exact ledgers, and counterfactuals at any step.

A saved bot log is only needed if game/bot code has changed since the replay was recorded (causing `GRAVITY_ACTIONS:` to diverge) or when debugging a situation where you didn't export the replay.
