## 2. what the notes got right that the plan missed

- **0.7.1 said the wait is “30 s of flight”.** It is 60: 30 world minutes at
  `WORLD_SEC_PER_MIN = 60` is 1800 world seconds = 60 sim seconds
  (`steps = WAIT_MIN / (DT · rate)`, `steps · DT = 60`). The line was corrected
  in the problem table, the UI bullet and nothing else moved.
- **0.7.1 missed two readers of the panel clock.** `gui_career.waitSettlement`
  charges `solarCharge(..., minutes * 60)` and `gui_shop.panelWaitKwh` charges
  `CAREER_WAIT_MIN * 60`; both feed the wait preview and the `solar +X kWh/wait`
  row. The plan now routes them through `GravitySim.worldSeconds`, so the
  coming label rename is one constant instead of a grep.
- **0.7.1's `wait · 30 min` title promised “30 world minutes = 30 s of flight
  at ×1”** — same 30/60 slip, fixed. It also now says the label rename (drafts
  §2.9) is a separate pass and that `dom_smoke` pins `wait · 30 min`, so this
  plan leaves it alone.
- **0.7.2 depends on 0.7.1 for pace.** Since §2.9 answered *labels only*, the
  wear constant stays “per sim second” and `100 s` of flight still costs 1.0;
  the plan says so, so nobody re-derives wear for a pace that did not change.

## 3. the bribe: the arithmetic was fine, three GUI gates were not

Measured before touching anything (`node`, the real modules): the visit value
**does** grow while the ship sits — seed 7, rot 60: `0.574` on touchdown,
`0.648` after 5 flight s, `1.009` after 30, `2.818` after 180. So the note's
«2x less speed» and the device hypothesis are not what makes the button
useless. What does:

1. `pauseOnLanding` is on by default and `careerIdle` needs `!careerPaused`, so
   the clock stops on touchdown; sitting pays only after `wait` or the pause
   button.
2. The pad block is rebuilt by `refreshCareerPanel` on career *events*. A wait
   updates only `#careerPadCharge`, so the bribe button kept its touchdown price
   (`3.60` on the button, `3.75` owed) and read as stale/inactive.
3. `drawPadPlate` picks “below the planet” as its first slot — exactly where
   `drawPlanetProfits` draws the visit pill, so on the planet the player sits on
   the profit number was covered by the pad plate.

### fixed

- `gui_shop.js`: the button carries `id="careerBribe"`; `refreshFuelHud`
  (already called once per HUD tick while career runs) calls `syncBribeUi`, which
  re-prices it from `GravityGame.bribeAmount` and leaves any refusal to the
  event path. No rebuild, no allocation in the frame path.
- `gui_draw_econ.js`: one pill geometry (`PILL_W/H`, `pillTopY`) is shared by the
  painter and the plate; `overPill` rejects a plate slot that would cover the
  pill, so the plate moves above/right instead. `slot === 0` was the only
  collision, and the above-slot is clear by construction (asserted in the test).
