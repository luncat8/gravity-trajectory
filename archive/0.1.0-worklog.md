reference page produces.

## 2. gravity simulation (`gravity_sim.js`)

Fixed step 1/120 s, softened inverse-square sum over 26 planets, signed strength
`[-2, 2]` (default 1). The same `advance()` and `impact()` drive prediction and
live flight; `predict()` spawns from `planetR + 8` along the aim angle exactly
like `launch()`.

Tuning: a first pass with `SOFT = 26, MU = 6e5` made every slow shot fall back
into its own planet (24 of 26 planets are ~140-350 px apart, so 26 px of
softening inside a 10 px planet is effectively a point mass). Swept
`SOFT ∈ {26,45,60,80} × MU ∈ {4e5,5e5}` across 3120 shots and picked
`SOFT = 60, MU = 4e5`: shots around 60 px/s reach ~380 px, ~90 px/s cross the
field, the top of the power range still leaves the field. Recorded in the plan.

Prediction caps: 2450 steps (20 s horizon), one point per step into preallocated
`Float32Array` buffers, wall-clock check every 64 steps against 10 ms.

Validation (`experiments/gravity_bench.js`, log in `experiments/logs/`):

- 5616 predictions (26 planets × 36 angles × 6 speeds): avg 0.14 ms, worst
  3.8 ms, zero budget truncations; ends 2797 impact / 2170 leave field /
  649 horizon.
- parity: 216 shots replayed live against the predicted polyline — worst
  deviation 0.00007 px, and every impact ended on the predicted planet.
- repel sweep (all strengths `-1.2`): 156 paths, all finite, no runaway buffers.

## 3. interaction (`gravity_gui.js`)

- `pointerdown` on the trade canvas within 18 screen px of a planet selects and
  starts the aim; grip follows pointer deltas; direction = planet → grip, speed
  = linear map of grip length over 240 px to `30…320 px/s`. Shift multiplies the
  delta by 1/20; releasing shift never snaps the aim.
- release arms (dashed prediction redrawn), `Enter` or the launch button fires,
  click-only selects, `Esc` cancels. Grid pointer deltas are absolute, so the
  scaled grip clamps at the power circle instead of drifting.
- gravity slider writes `setStrength` and invalidates the prediction; the trim
  slider is spring-loaded (element value returns to 0 on `change`) and applies
  `value *= 1 ± n%` to the aim speed when armed, otherwise to the selected
  gravity.
- separate clock: `performance.now()` deltas only; the trade `sim.t` is never
  read. Verified by pausing trade and stepping 50 frames: trade clock frozen,
  experiment clock and cargo physics advancing.

## 4. rendering

Overlay canvas above the trade canvas with `pointer-events: none` so trade hover
keeps working. Gravity wells (ring radius by strength), dashed predicted path
with 1 s dots and an end marker, cyan live trail, gold cargo diamond with a
pre-rendered glow, grip arrow and a `px/s · degrees` plate. Labels are drawn in
screen space after the world transform so they stay legible at any scale.

Bug found while testing: the HUD was throttled to 10 Hz including the aim
readout, which made shift precision look like ~1/2.6 instead of 1/20 because the
readout lagged the pointer. The aim readout now updates per frame; the rest of
the HUD stays at 10 Hz.

Camera: fits the 1600×1000 world above the bottom control bar with a footer band
(`clamp(8%, 76, 120) px`). A first attempt reserved a fixed 76 px which, combined
with the narrow portrait width, double-counted the band and left the field in the
upper third on a phone.
