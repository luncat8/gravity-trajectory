# 0.7.16 Plan — Bribery Surcharge Feeds Back Into Corruption

Source notes: `drafts/politics.md` §1 (the human's explicit feedback note:
*"The bribed launch should increase planet corruption too — bribe is literally
corruption!"*). Continues `archive/0.7.1-plan-politics-economy.md`.

---

## Goal

Close the narrative and mechanical loop between bribery and planetary rot.
Paying a bribe on a corrupt planet (`padLift` surcharge when `!own` and
`market.corruption[planet] > 0`) must slightly worsen that planet's corruption
on departure. Using the commercial network in a rotten system props up the
local graft; building your own pad or funding a political cleanup (`quell`) are
the ways to break the cycle cleanly.

## What exists

- `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_MAX = 0.75`, `CORRUPTION_MAX = 1`, `CORRUPTION_SPREAD = 0.08`,
  `CORRUPTION_RELIEF = 0.25`.
- `padLift(galaxy, market, career, planet)` returns `{ fee, base, bribe, own }`
  where `bribe = own ? 0 : round(base * CORRUPTION_BRIBE_MAX * corruption[planet])`.
- `payLaunchFee(galaxy, market, career, planet, step)` deducts `lift.fee` from
  `career.ledger.purse`, pays `lift.base` to the EMC pad owner, and sinks
  `lift.bribe` out of the economy on departure (`careerBeginFlight`).
- `quellPlanet` already reduces `market.corruption[planet]` and refreshes quiet
  flags (`market.quiet[planet] = corruption >= CORRUPTION_QUIET_AT`).
- `career_bot` scores commercial departures through `exitCostAt` / `launchFee`
  and scores political cleanup through `quellGain`.

---

## Design

### 1. Bribe-to-corruption feedback law (`gravity_game.js`)

- Add a tunable constant beside `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_MAX`:
  - `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SURCHARGE = 0.06` (or `0.04 * lift.bribe` capped by
    `CORRUPTION_MAX`: proportional to actual bribe paid so a `0.10` surcharge on
    a cheap pad nudges rot less than a `1.50` surcharge on an expensive hub).
  - Concrete formula:
    `delta = CORRUPTION_BRIBE_FEEDBACK * lift.bribe` (with
    `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_FEEDBACK = 0.08`, so a `0.50` bribe adds `+0.04` rot, and
    a `0.00` bribe on an owned pad or clean world adds `0`).
- Apply inside `payLaunchFee(galaxy, market, career, planet, step)` whenever
  `lift.bribe > 0`:
  - `market.corruption[planet] = Math.min(CORRUPTION_MAX, market.corruption[planet] + delta)`.
  - Re-evaluate `market.quiet[planet]` if crossing `CORRUPTION_QUIET_AT` (`0.55`)
    so a planet tipped over the threshold by repeated bribery goes quiet
    immediately, matching `spreadCorruption`.
- Invariant: launching from your own pad (`lift.own === true`) or a clean world
  (`lift.bribe === 0`) never alters `market.corruption`.

### 2. Player visibility (`gui_career.js`, `gui_hud_text.js`, `gui_comms.js`)

- Launch fee readout when `lift.bribe > 0`: append a concise hint that paying
  the surcharge deepens local rot (e.g. `bribe +0.42 · worsens rot +4 %`), or
  surface it in the pad/politics tooltip so the player never wonders why rot
  ticked up between political events.
- When bribery tips a world across `CORRUPTION_QUIET_AT`, trigger the existing
  quiet-world notice (`P<n> went quiet`) and allow `H` (`gui_comms.js`) to
  explain why.

### 3. Bot awareness (`gravity_bot.js`)

- In candidate departure cost (`career_bot` commercial launch cost), add a small
  expected-rot penalty when `lift.bribe > 0` on a world the bot visits
  repeatedly or owns contracts around, OR let `quellGain` naturally value
  cleanup higher as rot rises. Keep it O(1) with zero allocations.

---

## Scope & non-goals

- Do **not** let bribery start corruption from `0` on a clean planet (`bribe` is
  `0` when `corruption === 0`, so `delta === 0` naturally).
- Do **not** change political event cadence or `quell` pricing unless `politics_test`
  shows runaway rot lock-in.

---

## Verification

- Extend `experiments/politics_test.js` (and `politics_gui_test.js` if HUD copy
  changes):
  1. Commercial launch on a corrupt world (`corruption = 0.4`) deducts
     `base + bribe` and increases `market.corruption[planet]` by the exact
     formula, clamped at `CORRUPTION_MAX`.
  2. Own-pad launch on the same corrupt world pays `0` bribe and leaves
     `market.corruption[planet]` unchanged.
  3. Crossing `CORRUPTION_QUIET_AT` via bribery sets `market.quiet[planet] = 1`.
  4. `node experiments/run_fast.js` stays green; 20-seed career check confirms
     rivals do not soft-lock on corrupt hubs.
