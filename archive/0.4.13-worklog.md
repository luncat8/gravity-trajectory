# 0.4.13 worklog — collapsible HUD panels, scrollable left column & tank research family

## review of 0.4.12 (`df5b896`) & low-res layout

1. **Left HUD column (`#leftHud`) & panel collapsibility**:
   - In the previous layout, `#careerHud` had its own `max-height: calc(100vh - 28px); overflow-y: auto;`, `#leftHud > .panel` lacked `flex: none`, and `#leftHud` itself had no `max-height`, no `overflow-y: auto`, and `pointer-events: none`. As a result, when `#scoreHud`, `#careerHud`, and `#tradeHud` were stacked in career mode, `#careerHud` scrolled internally while pushing `#tradeHud` off the bottom of the viewport with no way to scroll the column to reach it.
   - Only `#cargoHud` had a fold button (`#foldBtn`), so `#scoreHud`, `#careerHud`, and `#tradeHud` could not be collapsed on low-res screens; meanwhile `@media (max-width: 820px)` forcibly hid `#tradeHud .kicker, #tradeHud .stats, #tradeLegend`.
   - Real-browser `layout_check.js` at `800×560`, `520×760`, and `390×700` also caught a horizontal overflow (`163 > 136`) in `#careerBox` caused by `.careerCap` (`white-space: nowrap` without `max-width: 100%; min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`).
   - `hudPadPress` only checked `cargoHudEl.contains(node)`, not `leftHudEl.contains(node)`, and clicking `#foldBtn` did not call `measureHudRects()`.

2. **Prototype refit review (`0.4.11` / `0.4.12`)**:
   - In `gravity_gui.js`, `lighterTier(career, table, cur)` searched strictly for `table[i].tier < cur.tier`. When a researched prototype landed on an exact integer rung (e.g. `R1` at `tier === 2`), `lighterTier` skipped the owned standard part on that same rung (`IMP-2` or `FT-2`). Allowing `table[i].tier <= cur.tier` when `cur` is a prototype (`tierIndex(table, cur) < 0`) lets the player swap directly back to the standard part at that rung.

## implementation

1. **Top-to-down panel flow, collapsible headers, and whole-column scrolling (`index.html`, `style.css`, `gravity_gui.js`)**:
   - Added `.panelHead` / fold buttons (`−` / `+`) to all four HUD panels: `#scoreFoldBtn` (`#scoreHud`), `#careerFoldBtn` (`#careerHud`), `#tradeFoldBtn` (`#tradeHud`), and `#foldBtn` (`#cargoHud`), wired through a single `bindFold(btn, panel)` helper that toggles `.folded`, updates `textContent`, `title`, `aria-expanded`, and refreshes `measureHudRects()`.
   - Removed the inner `max-height` / `overflow-y` clipping from `#careerHud` and set `flex: none` on `#leftHud > .panel` so panels push each other from top to down at their full natural height.
   - Made `#leftHud` a scrollable column (`max-height: var(--hudMaxH, calc(100dvh - 88px)); overflow-y: auto; overscroll-behavior: contain; pointer-events: auto;`), with `reserveTradeFooter()` setting `--footReserve` and `--hudMaxH` on both `#cargoHud` and `#leftHud` so both columns stop cleanly above `#tradeControls`.
   - Restored `#tradeHud`'s stats and legend at `<= 820px` inside `#tradeBody` (collapsible via `#tradeFoldBtn`), widened `#leftHud` to `220px` on `600..820px` viewports (`158px` below `600px`), constrained `.careerCap` with `max-width: 100%; min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis;`, and extended `hudPadPress` to `#leftHud` (excluding `.scoreEntry`).

2. **Third research family: `tank` (`fuelCap`, `emptyMass`) (`orbit_science.js`, `orbit_tech.js`, `gravity_game.js`, `gravity_gui.js`)**:
   - Added `tank` to `OrbitScience.METRIC_SCALE` (`[0.02, 2.0]`, so `50 kg` fuel = `0.5 t` dry mass = `1 RU`) and `OrbitScience.FRONTIER` (`fuelCap`: `{ below: 0.20, above: 1.00 }`, `emptyMass`: `{ below: 3.00, above: 0.20 }`).
   - Added `OrbitTech.tankRecord(id, fuelCap, emptyMass)` placing custom tanks on the `TANKS` rung by `fuelCap` and interpolating `buildCost`.
   - Seeded `tank` in `GravityGame.seedScience` from the real tanks `FT-1..FT-3` (`t.tier > 0`, keeping the researched range at `[40..160 kg, 0.5..1.4 t]` so ultralight composite tanks below `0.5 t` pay the `below: 3.00` frontier surcharge) and unlocked `R<n>` tank prototypes in `unlockPrototype`.
   - Generalized `protoEngineRowHtml` into `protoPartRowHtml(career, row, proto)` in `gravity_gui.js` for both `tank` and `engine` prototypes, including ship mass and Δv in the refit tooltip, and fixed `lighterTier` so integer-rung prototypes can refit back to the owned ladder part on the same rung.
