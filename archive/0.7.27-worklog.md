## Decisions

- A battery purchase, including a bulk purchase, keeps that pad’s kWh, capped at the new capacity. It does not clear reservations. Only founding a pad supplies initial charge. Gear valuation may credit later reach, never a refill on purchase.
- Score and shop for the same affordable, exit-tested rail **and** battery at the ship’s relevant mass and field scale. A commercial ticket buys no property; neither rival ground nor the Capital can be claimed. On unclaimed ground, favour sunlight and reachable neighbours, discount deep wells and subtract the whole kit price. On landing: mandatory service, then claim, then optional service/refits. Remote expansion keeps the priced way out, one cheap launch and repair money, not a blanket evacuation fare.
- A queued bot destination remains visible through flight. Competing fans discount it, and remote shoppers leave it alone. Cancellation, retargeting, landing and restart release or replace the intent. A guest prices the host’s **unreserved** charge; an owned rail can recharge over time. No launch uses forecast capacity in place of available kWh.
- A losing opening shuttle gets one wider fan before the bot settles for it. Buying a hold must leave room for the next EMC or have a paying local job. Deliveries price the loaded pickup exit, Capital fines and the return to an empty ship. Dropping an unreachable board sets a game-step cooldown, not a count of shop visits. A projected Capital fine cannot rely on flybys a competitor could collect first.
