## changes

### `gravity_game.js`
- `CAREER.commercialFee` (flat `3`) deleted; new constants `COMMERCIAL_BASE 2.00`, `COMMERCIAL_KWH 1.50`, `DEEP_G 2.2`, `DEEP_TONS 16`, `DEEP_BONUS 0.6`.
- `commercialFee(career, planet, speed, mass?)` — `price(career, COMMERCIAL_BASE + COMMERCIAL_KWH × launchEnergy(mass ?? ship, padAt(planet).catapult, speed))`. `padAt` resolves the seized rebel rail correctly (seized rails keep their tier; they quote the ordinary commercial price modulo the rail-efficiency term, comment says so). `commercialMinFee()` — the dry 10 t starter ship at `SPEED_MIN` on the starter rail → `2.16`; undiscounted (callers apply the cheat wrapper).
- `launchFee`: own/Capital rail → 0; commercial or rebel → `commercialFee(...)`; else rival → `CAREER.taxPerKwh × launchEnergy × pad.catapult.tier`. An `EMC-1` host charges exactly today's price to the cent.
- `exitCostAt`: commercial at `SPEED_MIN` (Infinity when the rail cannot lift), rival tax at `SPEED_MIN` × tier, else min(commercial, foreign, first-EMC build cost, `evacFee 6`).
- Deep-origin branch in `makeDelivery` and `makeTouristDelivery`: `task.deep = tons ≥ DEEP_TONS && planetGravity ≥ DEEP_G` — pure, consumes no RNG draw; the note and the board row read one shared `deepText(task)` helper (`needs EMC-3+ at P#`), so board and bot agree. `reward = round2(min(pay, deliveryMax) × (1 + DEEP_BONUS))` — the bonus rides the **capped** ordinary reward, faithful to the plan's "on top of the ordinary reward"; a deep offer can reach `7.20`. Deliberately different from fragile (`FRAGILE_PAY` multiplies raw pay *before* the cap).
- `career_bench`'s "deliveries pay below the evacuation fee" invariant relaxed only for `t.deep` (`reward < evacFee || deep`); it still guards ordinary and fragile offers.
- Exports: `COMMERCIAL_BASE`, `COMMERCIAL_KWH`, `commercialFee`, `commercialMinFee`, `DEEP_G`, `DEEP_TONS`, `DEEP_BONUS`, `deepOrigin`, `deepText`.
