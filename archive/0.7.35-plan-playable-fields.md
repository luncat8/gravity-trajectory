# 0.7.35 Plan — Playable Designed Gravity Fields

Follow-on to `archive/0.7.35-plan-map-helper.md`.

`map-generator-helper.htm` designs a gravity roster, draws a concrete `gravity`
array on the game's 0.05 grid and saves it to `map_fields.js` (`kind: 'field'`,
with `seed`, `universe` and `gravity: [...]`). This plan wires `index.html`,
`planet_gravity.js`, `saved_map.js`, `gui_maps.js` and career replays so a
saved `field` record boots and plays a real career (or replay) on that designed
gravity draw while leaving seed-only runs untouched.

---

## Goal

Picking a `field` record in the saved-maps dropdown (`map_fields.js` or
`saved_maps.js`) — or booting `?map=<name>` / `?map=field:<index>` — starts a
career whose planets have the record's `gravity` values (except the HQs,
Capital and ARF, which `pinCareerStrengths` still pins to `1`, matching the
helper's note), and whose seat plan was proven on **that** field, not on the
seed's rolled draw.

---

## Design

### 1. One source for `planetGravity` during a run (`planet_gravity.js`, `gravity_game.js`)

Every consumer (`GravityGame.planetGravity` for `deep`/`fragile` contracts and
the seat search, `gui_world.js` `attachWorld` for `planetBaseStrength` and
`portPlanetStrength`) reads `PlanetGravity.gravity(seed, planet)`.

- `PlanetGravity.arm(values, seed)`, `PlanetGravity.disarm()`,
  `PlanetGravity.armed()` and `PlanetGravity.fieldTag(values)` live on
  `planet_gravity.js`.
- `arm(values, seed)` snaps a `Float64Array` copy of `values` onto the 0.05 grid
  and records `seed >>> 0` plus a 32-bit FNV-1a `fieldTag`.
- `gravity(seed, planet)` returns `armedField[planet]` when `(seed >>> 0)`
  matches the armed seed and `0 <= planet < armedField.length`, falling back to
  the seeded hash draw otherwise. Another seed (a probe or switched world)
  never reads the armed field.
- `seatPlanKey` in `gravity_game.js` appends `:<fieldTag>` when an armed field
  matches `world.seed`, so a cached seat plan for `seed=1` (seeded) and a
  cached seat plan for `seed=1` (designed field) never collide.

### 2. Planning seats for a field (`experiments/make_saved_maps.js`, `map_gen.js`, `gui_maps.js`)

- `MapGen.fieldRecord` / `MapGen.readFields` preserve proven `seats`,
  `targetPay`, start settings and `stats` on untouched field records while
  dropping stale `seats` if the helper edits or re-rolls a field's `values`.
- `MapGen.savedMapFields` reads `record.gravity` when present on a `field`
  record instead of redrawing `originalDraw(record.seed, universe)`.
- `FIELDS=1 node experiments/make_saved_maps.js` (or `FIELDS=<path>`) arms each
  field record in `map_fields.js`, plans its seats with `prepareCareerStarts`,
  refuses any field that fails the `accepted` fairness rule (`missing === 0`,
  `distinct`, `exitOk`, `ratio >= 0.55`), and writes `seats` + `targetPay` back.
  `--check` verifies seated field records alongside `saved_maps.js`.
- Unseated `field` records loaded directly on the page arm the field and run
  `planFieldOpening` (`prepareCareerStarts` + fairness check) before touching
  the live session: an unplayable field refuses cleanly (`no fair opening in
  this field`) and restores the previous field; a fair field caches its seat
  plan and starts with `· saved map planned its opening`.

### 3. Loading, saving, URLs and replays (`index.html`, `saved_map.js`, `gui_maps.js`, `gui_world.js`, `gui_share.js`, `gui_game.js`, `career_replay.js`, `gui_career_replay.js`)

- `index.html` loads `map_fields.js` right after `saved_maps.js`.
- `GravityMaps.applyField(record)` arms `record.gravity` on `record.seed` or
  disarms `PlanetGravity`; `applySeats` verifies `fieldTag` parity so seed
  seats are never injected into a field run or vice versa.
- `startRecord` / `runRecord` capture `gravity` when a field is armed for the
  active seed (`kind: 'field'` for start records, `kind: 'run'` with `gravity`
  for run snapshots).
- `gui_maps.js` lists `SAVED_MAPS` (`0..N-1`) and `MAP_FIELDS` (`field:0..M-1`,
  labelled `· field`) in `#mapPick`, supports both in `load map` and `?map=`,
  keeps `map=<name>` in `location.search` while a registry field is active, and
  disarms on `?seed=`, `new seed`, seed `#game=1` links, or seed switches.
- Both `CareerActions` (`GRAVITY_ACTIONS:...`) and `CareerReplay`
  (`#...&replay=...` / `gravity-career:...`) carry and range-validate optional
  `gravity`, arming the designed field when replayed on a seed-only page.

## Verification

- `experiments/map_gen_test.js`: `PlanetGravity.arm`/`disarm`/`fieldTag`, seed
  scoping, `readFields` preserving/invalidating `seats`, `savedMapFields` on
  `field` records, and `index.html` script wiring.
- `experiments/saved_map_test.js`: `FIELDS=` seat generation & `--check`,
  trapped-field refusal, seated `MAP_FIELDS` boot without search, HQ/Capital/ARF
  pinning vs designed planet strengths, unseated live planning, `map=` URL
  retention, and byte-identical seed run after disarming.
- `experiments/career_replay_test.js`: compact actions replay and full career
  movie recorded on a designed field and replayed on a fresh seed-only page,
  plus rejection of out-of-range `gravity`.
