## 1. Review of `0.7.40` & Design Improvements for `0.7.41`

Reviewed `archive/0.7.40-worklog.md` and `archive/0.7.41-plan-sport-competitions.md`
against `gravity_game.js`, `gravity_bot.js`, `gui_aim.js`, `gui_career.js`, and
`saved_map.js`. Tightened three areas while implementing:

1. **Mirror-synced `COMPETE_SLOT` + staleness check (`sportMirrorStale`):**
   `syncSportSlot` / `syncSportMirrors` mirror `galaxy.sport` (or `career.sport`
   on a solo headless career with `career.galaxy === null`) into every actor's
   `tasks[COMPETE_SLOT]` with `reward = sport.pool`, `startsAt`, and `deadline`,
   preserving per-company `state` and `capitalRefund`. Added `sportMirrorStale`
   to the 60-step board refresh gate in `gui_game.js` so another actor's entry
   stake or race win refreshes every live board view without waiting for a
   landing.
2. **Queued-barrel re-floor on late entry (`reFloorTurretForRace`):**
   If a player queues a turret shot on `sport.planet` before `startsAt` and
   *then* clicks `accept` on `COMPETE_SLOT`, `reFloorTurretForRace` in
   `gui_aim.js` re-floors the already-queued shot to the first barrel alignment
   at or after `sport.startsAt` so an entrant never fires before the starting
   gun regardless of whether they aimed or entered first.
3. **Inter-event cooldown (`nextAt`) & stake accounting (`stakeSpent`):**
   `closeSport` schedules `sport.nextAt = step + round(competeEvery * competeSec * (0.5 + draw) / DT)`
   so consecutive board refreshes do not chain back-to-back races without a breather,
   while `startCareer`'s step-`0` refresh announces the opening race (`startsAt = 45 s`,
   `deadline = 225 s`). Added `career.stakeSpent` into `careerStats.running` and
   the map-statistics profit tooltip (`stakes`) so purse accounting
   (`purse === base + income - running - assetSpend`) holds exact to `1e-9`
   across entries, wins, drops, and forfeits.

## 4. Post-0.7.41 Review Improvements

1. **Late race entry & drop while barrel is queued (`gui_career.js`, `gui_book.js`):**
   - Permitted `accept` and `drop` on `COMPETE_SLOT` in `careerAct` while
     `turret.pending` (since race entry/drop adds zero ship mass), re-flooring a
     queued barrel to `>= sport.startsAt` on accept (or canceling it if post-stake
     purse cannot cover `launchFee`), canceling a held race barrel on drop, and
     giving disabled `accept`/`drop` buttons accurate tooltips in `gui_book.js`.
2. **Pre-gun mid-flight forecast & dual-slot Capital escrow (`gravity_game.js`):**
   - Guarded `careerLandingFacts` and prospective `careerLegPlan` with
     `!(career.flying && career.flightStartStep < compete.startsAt)` and pre-gun
     `launchStep = Math.max(runStep, compete.startsAt)` so a flight launched
     before the gun never forecasts `sport.pool`.
   - Replaced all-or-nothing `completesEscrow` in single-leg `careerLegPlan` with
     exact per-slot `releasedEscrow` (`contractTask` vs `COMPETE_SLOT`) so
     completing a race never falsely releases an unrelated contract's `+3.00`
     Capital refund escrow.
3. **Bot race entry commit timing & solvency (`gravity_bot.js`):**
   - Moved `careerRaceEntry` out of `careerReadyShot` into `tickCareerPlayer` and
     `careerLaunch` after `careerSit` and fuel prep succeed, guarded pre-gun
     moving-port shots, verified barrel-alignment `fireAt + plan.steps <= sport.deadline`
     and post-stake solvency (`afterStake >= fee + escrow` and post-landing
     `exitCost`), and added `'sport'` decision-log tracing.
   - Added 4 regression checks to `experiments/competition_test.js` (15/15 green).
