## where the measurement moved the plan's estimates

- **Relocations** (`§4.2`, now the measured set): added `FRAGILE_PAY`, `DEEP_BONUS`,
  `COMPETE_ENTRY` (contracts), `ANNOUNCE_SEC` (sport), `TAU`, `STATS_SEC` (network),
  `OPENING_EXACT_*` (seat search), `WORN_OUT_BLOCK` (ship → network), `ENDGAME_LIMIT_STEPS`
  (career → flight); `fillLight` and `topTier` were unanchored singles and go to
  `game_career.js`. Stayed: `CHEAT_ENDGAME`, `EXPERIMENT`, `CAP_STEPS` (the core reads them),
  `SALT_TASK` (two readers). The bot moved 50 constants, not 59.
- **`pay` in the core**: the plan had it in `game_contracts.js` with a back reference from
  `game_sport.js` inside the settle loop. Four parts call it and it is the ledger primitive, so
  it sits beside `price`/`canPay` and the back reference is gone.
- **`careerAimList` in the bot core**: the constructor calls it; in `bot_career_plan.js` it was
  the only bot back reference (×2).
- **`keep`**: `releaseEnergy` (single reader `game_flight.js`) stays with `reserveEnergy`;
  `comms_copy.js` keeps its formatters, caps and `LAUNCH_GATE_ORDER` per §6 and the topic tables
  stay in `comms_topics.js`, although each has one reading part.
