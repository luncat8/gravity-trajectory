# 0.5.10 — finite fuel, priced burns, committed-launch top-ups

Implemented plan: `archive/0.5.10-plan-fuel-model.md`. The adjacent UI pass from
the same request is included: the desktop left HUD uses the free canvas margin,
and shop rows that cannot be used because of base ownership are hidden instead
of shown as disabled EMC upgrades.

The remaining open specs are `0.5.11-plan-special-planets.md` and
`0.5.12-plan-corruption-rebellion.md`.

## What changed

### Fuel economy

- `gravity_game.js` removes `refuelShip` and every automatic fill. A new or
  restarted career starts dry; only `CHEAT_ENDGAME` explicitly fills the tank.
  Launch, landing, evacuation, pad building and refits no longer create fuel.
- Constants: `FUEL_KG_PER_KWH = 20`, `FUEL_PRICE = 0.08`, `FUEL_BUY_STEP = 10`.
  A full FT-1 (40 kg) costs `3.20`, above the `3.00` commercial EMC fee.
- `produceFuel(career, planet, kg)` converts whole kilograms from the landed
  owner's pad battery, using only `chargeKwh - reservedKwh`. It cannot drain a
  commercial or rival rail, and it cannot eat a queued launch reservation.
- `buyFuel(career, kg)` works on any landed pad, clamps to tank room and purse,
  never makes the purse negative, and is free under communism. Both functions
  return kilograms actually added and update `fuelProducedKg`, `fuelBoughtKg`
  and `fuelSpent`.
- `installPart` preserves fuel and clamps it to the new tank cap. Refit previews
  in `orbit_tech.js` now use the actual tank fuel by default, with an optional
  override for prospective-fuel calculations.

### Bot pricing and top-up

- `CAREER_BURN_FUEL_COST` is gone. Burn scores charge `fuelKg * fuelPrice`.
- Plans carry `fuelKg`, `fuelProducedKg` and `fuelBoughtKg`; actor state carries
  a reusable fuel quote and a top-up counter.
- `careerPrepareFuel` runs after the sit decision, so the bot does not buy fuel
  merely to idle. It forecasts one scratch shortlist with the affordable maximum
  fuel, then buys only the whole kilograms the winning burn needs.
- `careerFuelOffer` converts own unreserved charge first and purchases second.
  Purchase keeps the launch fee plus `CAREER_PURSE_RESERVE`; conversion also
  keeps a full-cap departure energy reserve so the priced shot remains firable.
  That reserve was necessary: without it, conversion could lower the live pad
  cap below the already-planned launch speed.
- `careerCommitFuel` performs the transaction and traces `fuel +N kg`.
  Player-bot and rival launch paths both commit a pending request, or call
  `careerPrepareFuel` when the plan has no request yet. A failed commit defers
  the launch instead of flying with an unfunded burn.
- `careerRescueBurn` searches with prospective fuel and carries the request
  until the rescue launch is accepted. `careerPriceBurn` rejects stale shortlist
  speeds above the live cap.

### UI and ownership hiding

- The pad block now has a fuel gauge, a 10 kg price line, `charge → fuel` and
  `buy fuel` buttons. Buttons explain why they are disabled: no engine/tank,
  full tank, no unreserved own charge, or no purchasing power.
- Predicted net, aim text, launch text and live burn text subtract the burn's
  replacement fuel cost. The pad impulse note says `tank empty` when relevant.
- Foreign-owned planets keep the rail/ownership explanation but hide unavailable
  pad upgrade rows. Catapult-less planets hide panel/battery rows. Ship refit
  rows appear only at HQ; away from HQ the drawer says to land at HQ to refit.
- Desktop left HUD width is `clamp(280px, 24vw, 350px)`: about 346 px at
  1440×900 and 307 px at 1280×800. The ≤820 px width is 248 px. The ≤599 px
  width stays 158 px because widening it overlapped the right cargo HUD and made
  science controls unclickable at 390 px. Fuel buttons wrap/shrink so the mobile
  drawer does not overflow horizontally.

Unfitted baseline:

- 167 flights, 31.20 earned, 82 pads, 0 stuck.

`ENGINE=1` (fitted drive, finite fuel, top-ups and rescue enabled):

- 115 flights, 13.00 earned, 76 pads, 1 dead pad, mean reach 3.91, 0 stuck.
- 57 burns, 13 priced plans, 7 rescue plans.
- 95.0 kg bought, 9.0 kg produced, 7.60 spent, 2 committed top-ups.

`ENGINE=1 NOFUEL=1` (fitted drive, dry tank, bot top-ups disabled):

- 157 flights, 18.81 earned, 67 pads, 0 stuck, 0 burns.

The finite-fuel bot flies fewer, more deliberate shots and claims more pads than
the no-fuel control. Fuel is not a free speed-up; it is priced into the plan.

`ENGINE=1 DEADEND=1`:

- 30 flights, 7.08 earned, 34 pads, 16 stuck.
- 11 burns, 2 priced plans, 3 rescue plans, 1017 no-shot turns, 15 sits,
  162 contract drops.

`ENGINE=1 DEADEND=1 NOFUEL=1`:

- 22 flights, 1.00 earned, 26 pads, 18 stuck, 0 burns, 1076 no-shot turns.

The seeded dead-end fixture therefore still has profitable fuel rescues:
+8 flights, +6.08 earned, +8 pads and two fewer stuck companies with fuel.

## Test and browser notes

- `career_bot_bench` needed explicit fuel funding because the old fixtures
  assumed a free full tank. `fundFuel` tops up the purse by the purchase cost and
  buys through the real transaction, preserving legacy purse assertions.
- The flat-world fitted-drive pricing fixture forces `careerBurnPriceMargin`
  negative to test plumbing; with replacement-cost fuel, that particular burn is
  not economically profitable by default. The economic gate is tested separately
  by the marginal-burn check and the seeded A/B.
- `runCareer` now resets the burn shortlist before direct `careerPlan` calls;
  otherwise `careerPrepareFuel` could see stale shortlist speeds from a previous
  origin/cap.
- `layout_check` had two stale expectations. The folded-stack assertion now
  includes `gameHud` height, and the seeded reload check compares the restored
  HQ and seeded HQ gravity to the pre-reload world instead of expecting gravity
  exactly 1. The dim-planet panel test builds an owned base first and expects the
  next panel tier, matching the new ownership-hiding rule.
- Chromium was run through `/tmp/chromium` with the local NSS/NSPR stubs recorded
  in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`. A 1440×900 career screenshot confirmed the
  wider left drawer and visible fuel controls without page errors.
