## 3. the real find: the gravity bench did not start careers the way the page does

Seed 2 R1 "sits on HQ P7 at g 2.1 and never flies" is not a game bug — the page
cannot produce it. `startCareerRun` does two things the bench never did:

1. `prepareCareerStarts(galaxy, world, trade, mask, count, 1 + bot.count, cargoSim)`
   before anybody joins → one jointly planned fair HQ set (`galaxy.mapSeats`).
   Without it each company calls `pickHq` alone, so the bench played different
   homes than the page for the same seed.
2. every starting HQ is pinned at `planetBaseStrength[hq] = 1` (special worlds too).
   `openingTraceHome` already assumes exactly that: it saves the candidate's seeded
   strength, sets it to **1**, traces the fan, restores. The picker therefore scores
   a home it will never see, and a fixture that leaves the well in place measures a
   world the page cannot create.

On the old fixture 10 of 60 rival-games opened inside wells the page never allows
(seed 2 R1 on P7 g 2.1, seed 19 *both* rivals on g 2.8 and g 3.1) and never flew —
dead weight that inflated the standard error of every A/B and mis-attributed the
loss. `career_gravity_bench.js` now does both (`SEATS=0 HQWELL=0` reproduces the
pre-0.5.22 numbers bit for bit: the shipped tree gives the 0.5.21 varA 400 s line
exactly), `stuck` is 0 on the page fixture, and the same two steps went into
`medic_offer_probe.js`. Written up in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.

**Reproduced on the page** (`experiments/career_start_seat_test.js`, new: the page
side of the same contract, on the vm stub). For seeds 1, 2, 3, 19 and 24 the page
seats every company on `galaxy.mapSeats` in join order and every HQ reads exactly
g 1.00 — and the bench now agrees seat for seat:

| seed | page seats | new bench HQs | old bench HQs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3, 9, 11 | P3 P9 P11 g1.0 | P2 P9 P5 (g 1.4 / −1.0 / −0.4) |
| 2 | 3, 9, 6 | P3 P9 P6 g1.0 | P9 P7 P4 — **R1 stuck on P7 g 2.1** |
| 19 | 11, 8, 12 | P11 P8 P12 g1.0 | P4 P12 P1 — **both rivals stuck, g 2.8 / g 3.1** |

So a rival cannot start on a g > 1 world: the reported "stuck HQ" was the bench's
own fixture. Mutation check: with the `planetBaseStrength[planet] = 1` pin removed
the new test fails with `seed 1 YOU opens in a g 2.90 well`.

The bench also prints the purse total in its `TOTAL` line now (it was the score the
A/B is judged on and had to be added up by hand) and one `PURSE seed n Rn x` line
per rival-game under `PAIRED=1`, so two runs can be diffed game by game.

## 4. why the medical quest is dead — and why naming the Capital does not fix it

`medic_offer_probe` grew three counters (`at-capital` seconds and visits, and
`offer and free at capital`). With 0.5.16 merged the hold gate is gone (hold 8 on
24 of 24 rival-games, "demand without a hold" 6060 s → 0, offer-seconds 3621 →
8478) and the mission is still taken **once** in 24 games.

The offer is not the gate. `canAccept` wants `career.at === career.capitalPlanet`
— the only task in the game that must be accepted on a particular planet — while
`careerGoalBodies` names a goal only for an *accepted* contract plus the
competition planet. Nothing sends a rival to the Capital: it lands there 20 times
in 24 games (767 s of 28 800 rival-seconds, 2.7 %), always by accident.

**varD (measured, rejected, not merged)**: adding the Capital to `careerGoalBodies`
while an offer stands and letting `careerTarget` return it although nobody can pad
the Capital changes **nothing** — the probe output is byte-identical to varA, and
the 30-seed purse is 1566.06 vs 1575.06. A goal only biases the fan
(`targetAngle` + `CAREER_TARGET_OFFSETS` extra aims); the shot is chosen by money
and a Capital landing pays `CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY`. The mission's reward — patients ×
the per-patient pay, two legs away — belongs to no shot score. That is the fix to
measure next, and it is written up as `0.5.23-plan-medic-capital.md`.
