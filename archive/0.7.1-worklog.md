# 0.7.1 worklog — the time model and its labels

## asked

Read `0.7.1-plan-time-model.md`, improve it where a better way shows up, and
implement it. The human's note (`drafts/time-scale-units.md`, also
`drafts/career-economy.md` §2.9 *normalize time to 1x = real time*) came with
it: the `wait · 30 min` button behaves well but its text is not information a
player can imagine, so the labels should speak real time at a proper scale, the
current default `×4` should be called `×1`, and the actual speed must not move.
A warning came with the note: the time plan may be LLM thought rather than
draft fact, and should be corrected where it is wrong. It was.

## 1. review of the previous stage

`archive/0.7.5-worklog.md` reviewed the drafts and corrected the 0.7.1 plan's
“30 s of flight” to “60”. Both were half right, and the plan's unit contract was
wrong in a way that would have reached 0.7.2:

- **The plan's `simSeconds(steps, rate) = steps · DT · rate` is not a flight
  second.** One step is `DT` of flight time whatever `rate` is;
  `steps · DT · rate` is a *world minute* — the value `careerAdvance` called
  `minutes` and passed to `batteryLeak`. Measured at the defaults (`rate 0.5`,
  career pace 4): one wait is `7200` steps = `60` flight s = `30` world min =
  `1800` world s of sunlight. The plan's contract would have priced 0.7.2's
  `WEAR_SPACE` per world minute, so turning the *background animation* slider
  down would have slowed a ship's ageing. `0.7.2-plan-ship-wear.md` now says
  `GravitySim.flightSeconds(steps)` and why `rate` must stay out of it.
- **`wait · 30 min` never cost 15 s of wall clock.** `CAREER_WAIT_MS = 2000`:
  the button eases its whole span over 2 s of real time (stretching below ~50
  fps, which `dom_smoke` §0.4.21 already pinned). `15 s` is what *watching* the
  same 60 flight s costs at the career's default pace — a third number, not the
  press cost. The plan and the draft's `answered:` line both promised `15 s` on
  the button; the button now carries `2 s` and the tooltip carries both.
- **`dtYears` was not a lie.** `OrbitSim.ECON_YEARS_PER_MIN = 1`, so an orbit
  minute is a colony year and `batteryLeak` really was priced per year — the
  same product the panel charges over in world *seconds*. The rename to
  `worldMin` keeps the pad accounting in one unit and says in the doc comment
  that the orbit clock's colony year is the same minute. The colony economy's
  own “year” stays, it is a real unit there.
- **`politicsSeconds` is world minutes**, not the flight seconds the plan
  claimed, and `OrbitEcon`'s `*_SECONDS` constants are priced in them
  (`CORRUPTION_GRACE = 120` is two world hours). `ARREST_SECONDS` is the one
  flight-second exception (`runStep + round(120 / DT)`). Arithmetic untouched;
  both files now say so.
- Two readers the plan had found were confirmed and converted:
  `gui_career.waitSettlement` and `gui_shop.panelWaitKwh` / `panelReadySec`,
  plus a third it missed — `gui_shop.rebelMultiple` re-derived
  `· DT · politicsRate` by hand and now asks `GravityGame.politicsSeconds`.

## 2. implemented

**The contract** (`gravity_sim.js`, no new file): `WORLD_SEC_PER_MIN = 60`,
`WORLD_RATE_DEFAULT = 0.5`, `WAIT_WORLD_SEC = 1800` and six helpers —
`flightSeconds(steps)`, `worldMinutes(steps, rate)`, `worldSeconds(steps, rate)`,
`stepsForWorldSeconds(sec, rate)`, `flightSecondsForWorld(sec, rate)`,
`wallSeconds(steps, pace)`. Every reader converted: `careerAdvance`,
`politicsSeconds`, `careerPadGear` (its local was called `flight` and is world
seconds — now `flownSec`), `CAREER_WAIT_SECONDS`, `waitSettlement`,
`panelWaitKwh`, `panelReadySec`, `rebelMultiple`. `WORLD_SEC_PER_MIN` left
`gravity_game.js`, `CAREER_WAIT_MIN` left `gui_tables.js`, and
`batteryLeak`/`solarCharge` take named units. No table value moved.

**The labels.** One rule: a pace label reads relative to the pace its mode is
balanced at, and a duration the player spends reads in real seconds.

| was | now |
|---|---|
| `play ×4` (career default), `×32` ceiling, rungs `×0.25…×32` | `play ×1`, `×8` ceiling, rungs `×0.25…×8` — `CAREER_SPEEDS = [1,2,4,8,16,32]` over `CAREER_PACE_UNIT = 4`; the lab keeps `PLAY_SPEEDS = [0.25…8]` where ×1 is real time, and `playScale()` still returns the raw pace |
| `wait · 30 min` | `wait · 2 s` |
| `fast-forward · 12.3 / 30 min · P3 0.90 / 1.50 kWh · 41 %` | `fast-forward · 0.8 / 2 s · clock +24 s · P3 0.90 / 1.50 kWh` |
| `waited 30 min · P3 pad +0.50 kWh` | `clock +60 s · P3 pad +0.50 kWh` |
| title `wait 30 min (60 s): …` | title `fast-forward 2 s of real time · the clock runs 60 s (30 world min, 15 s to watch at ×1): …` |
| `playReset` `4×`, “default career pace (×4)” / “back to real time” | `1×`, “the pace career is balanced at: ×1 = 4 s of flight per real second” |
| `rate` title “world-time rate …” | “world rate in world minutes per flight second … (0.5 default)” |

`playDefaultIndex()`/`playMaxIndex()` are gone — both ladders share `PLAY_DEFAULT`
and `PLAY_MAX_INDEX` — and with them the dead `GAME_SLICE` and `PLAY_GUARD`.
Deadlines, the board, `gun · N s`, `ready in N s` and `t+ 0:01:00` stay flight
seconds on purpose: a deadline that moved with the pace slider would be
unusable, and the tag is the conversion.

Two label-truth fixes fell out of putting the pace into a sentence:

- **The tag promised a pace the loop does not run.** `stepGame` caps the pace at
  `PLAY_MAX = 32`, but `playScale()` returns rung × `gameRate`, so the top rung
  with the slider at ×2 asked for 64 and the footer said so. `playPace()` is the
  capped pace and is now the one value every label and every real-second readout
  uses (`paceTag`, `waitSettlement.watchSec`).
- **The preview went stale when the pace moved.** The tooltip names the pace, but
  nothing repainted it on a pace change, and the `game` slider never even
  repainted the tag. `refreshPlaybackUi` now ends in `refreshCareerWaitPreview()`
  (button, line and tooltip only — not the drawer) and the slider calls it. The
  guard is `careerRule() && !careerWaitBtn.hidden && !careerWaitBusy()`:
  `startGame` resets the pace before the career it starts has pads, which an
  `activeCareer()`-only guard walked into (`career.pads[here]` undefined).

**Docs.** README: a new **Clocks** bullet in *mechanics & visuals*, the wait /

and `drafts/time-scale-units.md` carry the `→` result, with the `15 s` claim

## 5. second pass — what the first pass got wrong

The first pass above renamed the clocks but kept the old layering: it made the
pace *relative* (`×1` = raw 4 in career, raw 1 in the lab), left `wait · 2 s`
naming the press instead of the span, and left the pad and political accounting
riding a rate multiplier. The second pass moves the contract itself.

### 5.0 one measurement to keep

`career_bench`'s old pad window (`0.1 … 0.4 kWh / 10 s`) was pinned at `RATE=1`,
which is **twice** the shipped default: the page runs the pad clock at
`scene.trade.speed = 0.5`. Measured on a pristine `e46ff2c`, `careerAdvance` at
the shipped rate gives **0.06126396 kWh** per 10 game seconds on SP-1 at HQ light
`0.73517` (BAT-1 is 1.5 kWh). That is the number the new window pins, and it is
the number the new tree produces — the benchmark was testing a run no player
plays. Keep this in mind for any future pad or politics constant: at HEAD every
`*_SECONDS` in `orbit_econ.js` and every pad charge ran at half its named value.

### 5.1 the contract

- `gravity_sim.js` exports `SUN_SEC_PER_GAME_SEC = 30` — the *price* of one game
  second of full sunlight on a panel, the one balance factor left — and
  `TRADE_MIN_PER_GAME_SEC = 0.5` for the sky. `padSeconds(gs) = gs · 30`,
  `padMinutes = padSeconds / 60`, `tradeMinutes(gs) = gs · 0.5`. All the
  `WORLD_*` names and `worldMinutes` are gone.
- `careerAdvance(career, steps)` reads `padSeconds(gameSeconds(steps))` and has
  no rate argument: a pad cannot be charged faster by a slider. `advancePolitics`
  reads `gameSeconds(runStep)` and likewise has no rate.
- The bench's window moved with the law: `stepsForGameSeconds(10)` = 1200 steps
  charge SP-1 `0.06126396 kWh` at HQ light, half the old RATE=1 reading and the
  same as HEAD at the shipped 0.5.

### 5.2 the mistakes, and how each showed up

1. **The politics table was doubled by mistake.** §3 of the first pass doubled
   `CORRUPTION_GRACE` 120→240, halved `CORRUPTION_PASSIVE_RATE` 0.1→0.05,
   doubled `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` and `FLYBY_RAMP_SECONDS`, and reasoned that a
   default run was unchanged. It was not: the old political clock was
   `steps · DT · rate` = `gameSeconds · rate`, so at the shipped 0.5 a "120"
   grace was 60 game seconds. Reverting the table to the named numbers is what
   preserves the balance, because the clock no longer carries the 0.5. Measured:
   a stale world grows `21.666667` rot over 20 000 steps before and after, and a
   reporting ship's decay is `3.333333` points — identical to HEAD at rate 0.5.
   `politics_test` passes all 20 groups again.
2. **The rebellion cooldown lost half its span.** With the epoch back at 60, a
   cooldown of one epoch is 30 game seconds, not the 60 it used to be; it is now
   `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS · 2` and says so.
3. **`politics_test`'s own `step()` helper was wrong twice.** It first advanced
   the sky by a *delta of the new clock*, so the test's driving clock changed
   under the law; then it called `advancePolitics` directly, which skipped the
   report stamp that the full per-step path ages. It now drives
   `GravityBot.stepCareer`, the same call the page and the benches make.
4. **The career sky drifted at twice its old speed.** Unifying the idle drift on
   `tradeMinutes` (0.5) doubled the old idle default (0.25). It is a deliberate
   choice: the sky advances 1 trade minute per game second, exactly as the wait
   does, so a waiting player and an idle player see the same sky.
5. **`careerLaunch` lost its rate argument** in the `RATE` sweep and the bench
   passed `step` into `fieldRate` with `runStep` undefined. Restored as
   `careerLaunch(r, market, trade, fieldRate, runStep)`; the bot bench went from
   11 failures (HEAD's count at either rate) to 4, and the 4 left are HEAD's own
   fixture failures.

### 5.3 the labels

- `wait · 60 s` — the game seconds the press buys; the tooltip carries the real
  cost, `fast-forward 2 s of real time: the clock runs 60 s (15 s to watch at
  ×4)`.
- `play ×N` — game seconds per real second in every mode. Career opens on ×4
  (the pace it is balanced at), the lab on ×1, the ceiling is ×32. The `game`
  slider is deleted, so nothing multiplies the rung.
- `tradeSpeed` reads `decoration only` and its `×1` is the raw `rate`; the
  animation stays `0.5 trade minutes per real second` at that default.
- README's `Clocks` section is now `One clock`, the `wait`, `play` and
  background rows are rewritten, and the stale `40 min of world time` in the
  bot's payback rule reads `2400 s of pad time`.
