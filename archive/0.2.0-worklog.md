## 0.2.0 step 1 — 2026-10-21 — split the world and the economy out of the sim

Behaviour-preserving refactor ahead of the colony economy, plus the natural
traits the new economy reads. No trade rule changed; the world fingerprint is
identical (see `../logs/0.2.0-bench.txt`).

Changes:

- `orbit_world.js` (new): planet placement, the station ellipses and their
  closed-form `planetXAt/planetYAt`, and the traits the economy needs — type,
  metal, light, habitat, and the system's Z / luminosity / belt count. Placement
  consumes the sim's own random stream in the historical order, traits use a
  separate seed-derived stream, so adding traits cannot move a planet.
- `orbit_econ.js` (new): the ten goods (3 intra + 7 inter), RATE/NEED/DRIVER
  tables, `systemFractions`, `resourceMapFor`, the colony record, the growth law
  (§4), warehouse cap, stages, market supply/tags and the export/import picks.
  No DOM, no canvas; `advanceColony` runs on module scratch and allocates
  nothing.
- `orbit_sim.js`: geometry deleted, delegates to the world; gains the colony
  scaffolding (`colonies`, `colonyOn`, a shared map scratch) used by the next
  step.
- `orbit_view.js`: the hover card names the world's type — the only visible
  difference.
- `experiments/colony_bench.js` (new): 1589 checks on determinism, the jitter
  bound, the site ordering and the map's cost.

Two design corrections made while implementing the map, both recorded in the
plan's §3:

- the plan's star-sized normalisers (`terran/2`, `hz/2`) saturate on a 26-planet
  world, so counts normalise against 4 terran worlds and the light scale against
  1.5: `sqrt(lum)`;
- **food and colonists had to become per-planet**: with a star-level food ease
  every world in a system scored the same and a rock out-scored a terran world.
  Geology reads the star, agriculture reads the world — that is what makes a
  barren rock a camp and a terran world a farm.

Bug found in the first cut, now covered by the bench: the map jitter fed a float
back into the integer hash (`hash01` chained into `hash01`), so every planet got
the same ±0.06 draw and every seed produced the same map. `World.hashBits`
chains integers; `hash01` divides once at the end.

`node --check` on every classic script.

Next step: wire the colonies into the sim — founding per planet, the 3-good

## 0.2.0 step 2 — 2026-10-21 — wire colonies into the sim & growth law tests

Replaced the single-good `portProd/portCons/portStock` model in `orbit_sim.js`
with per-planet colonies driven by `OrbitEcon.advanceColony` on every `TICK`
event (`3 min = 3 y`), wired the 3-good intra-planet trade (`bulk`, `goods`,
`migrants`), and added the growth and sim law suite to `experiments/colony_bench.js`.

Changes:

- `orbit_econ.js`:
  - `carryingCapacity`, `isAbandoned`, `goodShortfall`, and step-level event
    callbacks (`EVENT_STAGE`, `EVENT_FAMINE`, `EVENT_RECOVERY`) with a `0.45`
    recovery hysteresis band so comfort near `0.40` does not chatter.
  - Four law corrections uncovered while writing the growth bench (and updated
    in `0.2.0-plan-colony-economy.md` §3–§4):
    1. `NEED[bulk]=12, NEED[goods]=6` diluted `NEED[food]=20` to `50 %` of
       total need, so even `ease[food]=0` gave `comfort = 0.50 > 0.40` and
       could never trigger famine. Scaling intra `RATE` and `NEED` by `1/4`
       (`RATE: 300, 225`, `NEED: 3, 1.5`) keeps the exact same per-capita
       self-sufficiency thresholds while restoring `food` to `75.6 %` of total
       need.
    2. `ease[food]` now gates the star's light and biosphere bonus by the
       planet's own `habitat` (`0.05 + habitat·(0.60 + 0.20·lumNorm + 0.15·terranFrac)`),
       so a `habitat = 0.03` super-Earth does not get `+0.27` free food ease
       from sister worlds.
    3. `K` clamps `capital/25` to `[0, 1]` matching the production drivers, and
       `dCap` removes a duplicate `* dt` (`surplusSum` was already scaled by
       `dt`), keeping `pop <= K` and coarse steps consistent.
    4. `starveFor` counts consecutive years at `POP_FLOOR` with `comfort < 0.4`,
       and stages only advance when not starving, so doomed outposts do not
       promote on day 1 and are abandoned at ~150 y (`< 200 y`).
- `orbit_sim.js`: one colony per colonised planet (`colonies`, `colonyOn`,
  `colonyAt`, `seedColony`), `TICK` advancing all colonies with zero per-frame
  economy work, slot-compacting `removePort` on abandonment, `bestExport` load
  with the two-year guard, `pickExportDest` by `shortfall / (1 + dist/220)`,
  `migrants` flow (`comfort >= 0.6`, `pop < 0.8·K`, `habitat·(1 − fill)`),
  expansion gated on `town`/`city` with habitat-weighted `scoreTarget`, and the
  40-entry event log ring buffer.
- `experiments/colony_bench.js`: 1622 checks covering needs order (plus the
  transposed-table regression check), logistic convergence to `K`, capital
  surplus/decay, warehouse cap, chunking determinism (`1×100 y === 400×0.25 y === 10×10 y`),
  coarse flag, ascending stage events, alternating famine/recovery transitions,
  seeding and abandonment within 200 y, two-year export guard, shortfall/distance
  routing, migrants people-not-tons invariant, heap route re-arm survival, and
  5 000 y NaN-free sweep (`0.27 µs/colony-y`, `21.4 µs/tick` for 26 colonies).
- `experiments/dom_smoke.js`: asserts active colony records, stages and log at
  `t+07:00`.
