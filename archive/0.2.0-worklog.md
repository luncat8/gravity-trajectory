## 0.2.0 step 1 — 2026-10-21 — split the world and the economy out of the sim

Behaviour-preserving refactor ahead of the colony economy, plus the natural
traits the new economy reads. No trade rule changed; the world fingerprint is
identical (see `../logs/0.2.0-bench.txt`).

Changes:

- `orbit_world.js` (new): planet placement, the station ellipses and their
  closed-form `planetXAt/planetYAt`, and the traits the economy needs — type,
  metal, light, habitat, and the system's Z / luminosity / belt count. Placement
  consumes the sim's own random stream in the historical order, traits use a
  separate seed-derived stream, so adding traits cannot move a planet.
- `orbit_econ.js` (new): the ten goods (3 intra + 7 inter), RATE/NEED/DRIVER
  tables, `systemFractions`, `resourceMapFor`, the colony record, the growth law
  (§4), warehouse cap, stages, market supply/tags and the export/import picks.
  No DOM, no canvas; `advanceColony` runs on module scratch and allocates
  nothing.
- `orbit_sim.js`: geometry deleted, delegates to the world; gains the colony
  scaffolding (`colonies`, `colonyOn`, a shared map scratch) used by the next
  step.
- `orbit_view.js`: the hover card names the world's type — the only visible
  difference.
- `experiments/colony_bench.js` (new): 1589 checks on determinism, the jitter
  bound, the site ordering and the map's cost.

Two design corrections made while implementing the map, both recorded in the
plan's §3:

- the plan's star-sized normalisers (`terran/2`, `hz/2`) saturate on a 26-planet
  world, so counts normalise against 4 terran worlds and the light scale against
  1.5: `sqrt(lum)`;
- **food and colonists had to become per-planet**: with a star-level food ease
  every world in a system scored the same and a rock out-scored a terran world.
  Geology reads the star, agriculture reads the world — that is what makes a
  barren rock a camp and a terran world a farm.

Bug found in the first cut, now covered by the bench: the map jitter fed a float
back into the integer hash (`hash01` chained into `hash01`), so every planet got
the same ±0.06 draw and every seed produced the same map. `World.hashBits`
chains integers; `hash01` divides once at the end.

Validation: `colony_bench` 1589/1589 · `gravity_bench` BENCH OK (parity
0.000068 px, worst 2.66 ms of the 10 ms budget) · `dom_smoke` SMOKE OK ·
`node --check` on every classic script.
