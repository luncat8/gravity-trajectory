# 0.5.9 — four LLM plans reviewed, dead-end launches priced with one burn

Implemented plan: `archive/0.5.9-plan-burn-rescue.md`. The other three reviewed
plans remain open at the repo root: `0.5.10-plan-fuel-model.md`,
`0.5.11-plan-special-planets.md`, `0.5.12-plan-corruption-rebellion.md`.

## Review of the previous state (0.5.8)

0.5.8 prices the best three *finite ballistic* shots with one early nudge. That
pass cannot help a turn whose fan never lands: the shortlist is empty, so
`careerNextShot` falls through to `careerNoShot`, sits, drops its contract and
may eventually strand the company. The previous worklog's next step asked for a
bounded rescue ring exactly there, with a fitted drive and a dead-end A/B.

The 0.5.8 `ENGINE=1` fixture is also the right one: stock 12-seed rivals never
buy a drive (0 burns), while `career_bot_bench`'s research careers have one but
call `careerPlan` directly. Neither stock bench observes launch pricing without
the explicit `ENGINE=1` fit.

A first-flight inconsistency surfaced during the rescue probes: actor state and
`career_bot_bench`'s state fixtures initialized `burnNext = 0`, while 0.5.8's
pricing and production `careerFlightBurn` use `CAREER_BURN_AT = 24`. A benchmark
that flies the priced shot directly burned at step 0 and reached a different
body from the queued plan. All actor paths now initialize and reset the live
burn state at step 24; `GravityBot.CAREER_BURN_AT` is exported for tests.

## Changes

- **`careerNextShot`** (`gravity_bot.js`): after the normal, long-horizon and
  commercial ballistic passes all return `null`, try one bounded rescue from the
  rail the commercial pass selected, then the own rail, then fall through to
  `careerNoShot`. A rescued plan skips 0.5.8 shortlist re-pricing; the
  commercial fallback is preserved until a rescue wins or both rescues fail, so
  a drained own EMC can still pay and fire the 3.00 commercial fee.
- **`careerRescueBurn`**: preallocated 16 ring bearings plus the contract bearing
  and its opposite, two speed rungs (`capU`, `round(0.6 × capU)`), deduplicated
  by `(angleU, speedU)` to at most `CAREER_RESCUE_SHOTS = 36` launches. Each
  launch is stepped to `CAREER_BURN_AT` and priced with the existing
  `careerBurnPlan(..., rescueOnly = true, fee)`, using the same
  `CAREER_BURN_MAX_TRIES`, full-tank start and `careerBurnGain` coast margin as
  the live flight. The winning finite landing must score above the 0.5 sit
  margin; fuel cost is folded into `taskPay`, `fromBook = false`, no burn is
  pre-applied and no book/replay/human-aim path changes.
- **Shared scratch helpers**: `careerBindBurnSim`, `careerBurnLaunch`,
  `careerRestoreBurnProbe` and `careerReadyShot` now serve both 0.5.8 shortlist
  pricing and 0.5.9 rescue. Rescue ring/rung/snapshot arrays are preallocated;
  the bound scratch sim's `field`, `visited`, `planetBody`, `steps` and body
  count are restored. `careerScan.rescue` counts priced launches,
  `careerBurnRescues` counts accepted rescues, and `careerBurnPrices` remains
  the 0.5.8 shortlist counter. `CAREER_RESCUE_SHOTS`,
  `CAREER_BURN_PLAN_TRACES` and `CAREER_BURN_AT` are exported for tests.
- **Burn schedule**: constructor rival/player state, `resetActor`,
  `startCareers` and `attachCareerPlayer` now set `burn = 0`, `burnTries = 0`,
  `burnNext = CAREER_BURN_AT`, `burnStep = 0`. `career_bot_bench`'s hand-built
  state fixture uses the exported constant instead of 0.
- **Benches**: `career_bot_bench` adds four checks — no drive stays a no-shot
  turn; fitted drive rescues a stubbed dead end within the fan bound, keeps the
  plan's money identity and firable commercial rail, logs the decision and
  leaves shortlist pricing at zero; the rescued shot flies, burns and lands on
  the priced body; a viable ballistic plan skips rescue. `career_gravity_bench`
  adds `DEADEND=1` (dry HQ, `g = 2` well, fitted drive), `NORESCUE=1`, counters
  for no-shot/sit/drop events and totals for rescues, evacuations and priced
  plans. `DEADEND_WELL` defaults to 2 because `g >= 3` traps the bounded
  one-burn ring in every direction and measures nothing.
- **Docs**: `README.md` rivals row and bot file description; one pitfall note in
  `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`; implemented plan archived with `git mv`. The
  three remaining root plans are standalone specs, not implementation logs.
