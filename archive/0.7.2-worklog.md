## 2. where the plan was improved

| plan said | built | why |
|---|---|---|
| the caller applies the weather multiplier | `careerAdvance` charges the base space wear, `careerWeatherWear(career, sim)` the `(multiplier − 1)` share | `careerAdvance` alone must satisfy “100 s costs 1.0”; two writers of the same wear would double it |
| at 0 the company “evacuates or is restarted by the broke path” | `checkEnded`: landed at 0 with purse < repair → `CAREER_BROKE` | evacuation does not mend the ship; without this a worn-out company on its own pad is neither stranded nor broke and sits forever |
| `careerDeparture` returns `ship worn out` at 0 | only for the planet the ship stands on | the same function prices the exit of an aimed destination; gating it would paint every aim of a `repair & launch` red |
| total = repair + `launchFee` or own pad energy | one credit sum on a fee rail; `repair · cost, N kWh` on an own pad | credits and kWh do not add |
| at 0 “`#launch` is disabled” | `#launchOnly` is disabled; `#launch` stays the repair | the main button named an action it refused |
| `careerStats.maintenance` | maintenance inside `careerStats.running` | §4 of the plan already said so; a new field would touch every caller's scratch row |
| reserve “the repair cost” | reserve a full `WEAR_REPAIR_COST` | the cost that matters is the one after the next flight |
| a `STATS=1` line in `career_bench` | `WEAR` lines in `career_gravity_bench` | that is the bench that owns `STATS=1` and the rival economy |
| a 200 px/s rescue burn | reference only | the thrust cap holds a real burn near 34 px/s (≈ 0.017 wear) |

New in the rule beyond the plan: `repairLaunchBlock` (the launch gate as it
will be after a repair, purse counted for both), so the label and the click
cannot disagree, and `WEAR_EMC_MAX` as a named constant.

## 5. what the numbers do

`career_gravity_bench STATS=1`, same code with and without wear
(`experiments/logs/0.7.2-ship-wear-ab.txt`, per seed in `0.7.2-ship-wear.txt`):

| 24 rival companies, 12 seeds | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| profit per company | 0.024 /s | 0.009 /s |
| property per company | 7.68 | 4.52 |
| pads | 67 | 50 |
| purse total | 196.38 | 132.52 |
| flights | 137 | 135 |
| repairs per company | — | 4.3 |
| maintenance per company | — | 5.46 = 48 % of income |
| worn out at the end | — | 0 / 24 |

The draft's numbers are a heavy sink: an average rival flight costs about 0.64
condition (1.27 cr), and maintenance takes half of what flying brings in. No
company was left worn out, so the service rule works; the price level is the
balance question the draft left open. `0.6.10-map-statistics.txt` was
regenerated by the same run and now holds the with-wear table.
