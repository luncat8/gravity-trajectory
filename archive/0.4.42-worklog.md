# 0.4.42 worklog — build cost from research

## Review

The 0.4.41 ladder (3/8/12/16/20) priced every gear record by its rung. Rung
interpolation also priced prototypes, so an invented catapult was a rung guess,
not a cost of its own spec, and the research fee and the build cost could drift
apart. The commercial 3.00 is a separate flat operating fee and is not a build
price.

## Implementation

- `orbit_science.js`: `buildCostOf(family, spec)` = `BUILD_SHARE` (0.10) × the
  preview fee of the spec against an origin-only hull, to cents. Added the
  panel and hold families (merit, scale, frontier) so SP and HOLD follow the
  same rule. Hold scale 1 RU = 8 t, the starter hold size: at 0.25 RU/t HOLD-24
  cost 7.62, out of reach of the starter purse.
- `orbit_tech.js`: stock records take their `buildCost` from the spec
  (`priceStock`), tier-0 "none" records stay free, and the prototype builders
  (`engineRecord`, `catapultRecord`, `tankRecord`, `batteryRecord`) price from
  their spec. The rung cost interpolation and its floors are gone; `tier` stays
  as the shop ordinal. `orbit_tech.js` now requires `orbit_science.js`, and
  `index.html` loads science first.
- Pins that encoded the rung table now assert the spec rule: prototype engine,
  catapult, tank and battery costs equal `buildCostOf`, a heavier tank of the
  same fuel is cheaper, the tiny-prototype floors are replaced by equality.
  The merit "no rows" check uses `helper` (no research family), not `panel`.
- README: EMC-1 buy price 3.80, EMC-2 shop price 9.79, the commercial fee
  wording, the architecture lines for `orbit_tech`/`orbit_science`.
- `experiments/build_cost_probe.js` prints every record's spec, research work,
  build cost and the 0.4.41 price (`experiments/logs/0.4.42-build-cost.txt`).

## Next step

The user accepted EMC-1 3 and EMC-2 8, and this rule gives 3.80 and 9.79. Two
options, both small: lower `BUILD_SHARE` toward 0.08 (EMC-2 then 7.83, barely
above the 7.74 floor, and the 10–20 % range is left), or give the catapult
research a lower `TIER_SCALE`. Batteries and engines are cheap relative to the
catapults in the research scales themselves; decide whether that is intended
before retuning. Then remove the rung ordinal from the shop order and upgrade
rule, and play one career past the first research.
