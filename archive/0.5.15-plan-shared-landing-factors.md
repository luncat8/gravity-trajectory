# 0.5.15 plan — one landing model for the aim line, the launch list, the helper scans and the bots

## goal

A landing has to be worth the same thing wherever it is priced. Today four
places price one: the aim readout (`refreshPredGain`), the launch list and the
helper scans (`moneyFacts`), the quest score (`helperQuestScore`) and the rival
bots (`careerScoreFacts`). Each has its own copy of “delivery reward, medical
payout, competition, fee, exit”, each grew a medic branch of its own, and the
copies already disagree (the aim readout did not price the medical payout at
all; the quest score paid a delivery load leg 0.5 and a medic pickup leg 0.25
where the bot paid both 0.35).

The fix is a **factor table in the rules layer**, not another helper. A helper
then only chooses what the factors are worth against each other.

## the model — `gravity_game.js`

One pure function fills a caller-owned scratch object, so nothing is allocated
per candidate and the page, the bots and the benches all read the same law:

	careerLandingFacts(out, career, origin, body, flownLen, length, steps, runStep, visitPay)
		out.cash    money that reaches the purse at the landing
		out.credit  value of a leg that only advances a contract
		out.leg     which leg it completes

| leg | condition | cash | credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LEG_DELIVER` | delivery `LOADED`, landing on `task.to` | `task.reward` | — |
| `LEG_LOAD` | delivery `ACCEPTED`, landing on `task.from` | — | `task.reward × LEG_CREDIT` |
| `LEG_PATIENTS` | medical quest, patients aboard, landing on the Capital | `aboard × MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` | — |
| `LEG_MEDICS` | medical quest, no patients aboard, landing on the rebellion | — | `task.reward × LEG_CREDIT` |
| `LEG_COMPETE` | launched from the competition planet, flight long enough, before the deadline | `task.reward` | — |

Every leg is also gated on the contract deadline (`runStep + steps <=
task.deadline`): the board drops an expired contract before the ship arrives, so
a leg that cannot be completed pays nothing. `LEG_CREDIT = 0.35` is the share of
a reward that is worth counting while the payout is still unrealized (cargo
aboard or medics boarded is not yet money).

The same file names the open work, so neither the scans nor the bots need to
know what kind of contract is running:

	careerGoalBodies(career) -> [next contract stop, competition planet]

One scratch list, read before the next call. Bots route to the first entry,
scans aim at all of them.

## consumers

| consumer | reads | ranks by |
|---|---|---|
| `gui_predict.js` `refreshPredGain` | `cash` | the shown gain (predicted net adds fee/exit/fuel) |
| `gui_book.js` `moneyFacts` | `cash`, `credit`, `leg` | net money for the launch list and the aim economics |
| `gui_helper.js` scans | `money.net`, `money.credit`, `money.builds` | the goal row below |
| `gravity_bot.js` `careerScoreFacts` | `cash` + `credit` | `taskPay = cash + credit` for the plan score |
| `gravity_bot.js` `careerTarget` | `careerGoalBodies` | first entry |

### helper goals — a table instead of branches

	const HELPER_QUEST_CREDIT = 10;
	const HELPER_GOALS = {
		score:     { credit: 0,  builds: false, label: '',                empty: '' },
		profit:    { credit: 0,  builds: false, label: 'best money shot', empty: 'no paying shot found' },
		profitEmc: { credit: 0,  builds: true,  label: 'best money shot', empty: 'no EMC-buildable landing' },
		quest:     { credit: 10, builds: false, label: 'quest shot',      empty: 'no quest shot found' }
	};

`best profit` and `profit + EMC` rank `net`; `quest scan` ranks `net + 10 ×
credit` (contract progress is worth ten times its realized share, because a leg
gates the whole payout while the harvest it forgoes is small) and aims half its
candidates at `careerGoalBodies`. The launch-book seed is priced on the same
goal, so a press keeps a saved shot only when no fresh aim is better *for that
goal*. The notice prints purse money and the leg it completes — `quest shot ·
P7 · +2.00 · patients` — never the ranking score.

One `helperStartCareer(goal, mode, build)` guards the landed ship, builds the
candidates and seeds them; the three buttons are thin rows over it.

## tests — `experiments/career_guidance_test.js`

- every leg's cash/credit/leg identity, including an expired contract paying
  nothing and a too-short flight not winning the competition,
- the bot prices a medical Capital landing to the same cash and leg as the rule
  (one table, two consumers),
- `careerGoalBodies` for accepted delivery, loaded delivery, medics out,
  patients home, empty board,
- the aim readout counts the patients landing home,
- the quest button is wired, its scan finishes inside the frame guard and its
  notice prints cash plus the leg,
- an unflyable world reports the goal's own empty line (`no quest shot found`).
