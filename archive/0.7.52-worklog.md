# 0.7.52 — radar + OBS review fixes & stacking `+N s` plates by landing planet

## review of radar and OBS (0.7.49–0.7.51)

Reviewed the full radar (`0.7.49`, `0.7.51`) and observation-device (`0.6.1`, `0.7.50`) paths across
`gravity_game.js`, `gravity_bot.js`, `gui_draw_world.js`, `gui_tables.js`, `gui_career.js`,
`gui_shop.js`, `gui_game.js`, `gui_maps.js`, `dom_stub.js`, and their tests:

1. **OBS uninstall gate bypassed `partRefusal` / `installPart` (`gravity_game.js`, `gui_career.js`,
   `gui_shop.js`)** — `partRefusal` documents itself as the single refit gate ("never from a second
   copy of these gates"), yet `0.7.50` wired `uninstallRefusal` into `gui_career.js` and
   `gui_shop.js` separately while leaving `GravityGame.installPart(career, KIND_OBS, OBS[0])`
   ungated in the rule layer. Wired `uninstallRefusal` directly into `partRefusal` when a career
   has a live political market and dropped the duplicate GUI gate checks.
2. **`radarPredict` falsely stamped `radarStamp` when `keep === sim` (`gravity_game.js`)** — when
   `keep === sim` (`activeSim()`), `radarPredict` skipped `predictFlight` as intended, but still
   updated `stamp.steps / vx / vy`. Switching the selected slot while paused or mid-burn-aim could
   then see a clean stamp over stale or burn-preview buffers. Returned early before touching
   `stamp` when `keep === sim`, and added `t0` (`flightFieldT`) to `radarStamp` and
   `labRadarStamps` so distinct flights at step 0 never alias.
3. **Radar bots missed `careerPlayer`'s live flight headless and read GUI buffers on the page
   (`gravity_bot.js`)** — `careerRadarHot` passed `keep = this.careerPlayer.sim` to `radarPredict`,
   so in headless runs (`career_bot_bench`, replays without frame renders) `careerPlayer.sim` was
   never predicted, while on the page `careerPlayer.sim` could hold an uncommitted `predictBurn` or
   a frame-throttled trace. `careerRadarHot` now traces `careerPlayer.sim` on `this.exitProbe`
   (`storePath = false`), keeping `cargoSim` untouched and making bot radar deterministic.
4. **Ghost chevron drawn on partial/non-impact traces & oriented by current velocity instead of
   arrival tangent (`gui_draw_world.js`)** — `drawRadar` drew a landing chevron even when
   `record.body < 0` (exit / time cap / budget-truncated coast, contradicting the `RADAR_BUDGET_MS`
   spec) and pointed the chevron along `cargo.vx/vy` instead of the trajectory's final segment at
   the landing point; also connected the final point when `stride > 1`. Fixed all three.
5. **Per-frame string concat in `drawRadarLabels` & gifted/restored radar view (`gui_tables.js`,
   `gui_draw_world.js`, `gui_game.js`, `gui_maps.js`)** — cached `+N s` strings in
   `RADAR_TIME_STRINGS` (`gui_tables.js`) so `drawRadarLabels` is 0-alloc per frame, and ticked
   `predRivalsOn` when a career starts with a gifted radar or restores a saved run owning radar,
   matching `careerRadar`.
6. **`dom_stub.js` canvas text capture** — indexed `textColors`/`textXY` by `texts.length` and
   raised the per-frame capture cap from 64 to 256 so 26-planet maps never truncate radar plates or
   drift when a test clears `texts` alone.

## stacking overlapping `+N s` plates by landing planet

- **`gui_draw_world.js` (`drawRadarLabels`)** — visible flying records with a landing
  (`record.body >= 0 && record.tLand > 0 && radarRecordShown(record)`) are insertion-sorted in
  preallocated scratch arrays (`radarFlyOrder`, `radarFlyX`, `radarFlyY` — 0 allocations per frame)
  by `(body, tLand, index)`. Each landing planet forms a contiguous slice:
  - the earliest arrival on a planet keeps its exact landing-point slot `(x, y)`;
  - later arrivals on that **same** planet check `radarPlateOverlaps` (`|dx| < 46 && |dy| < 16` for
    `44×14` plates with a `2 px` gap) against already-placed plates in that planet's slice and step
    upward (`y -= 16`) only when they actually overlap;
  - if upward stacking crosses the top viewport margin (`y < 7`), `stackRadarPlanetGroup` falls
    back to stepping downward (`y += 16`) so top-edge landings never collapse onto the clamp;
  - hidden rivals (`predicted` off and not hovered/pressed) and ships aimed at other planets never
    displace a plate; parked `landed` plates stay unchanged.
- **Copy (`README.md`, `drafts/new/radar.md`, `drafts/new/observation-device-OBS-1.md`)** —
  documented per-planet `+N s` stacking and gifted-radar auto-tick in `README.md`, and added `→`
  markers to the landed notes in `drafts/new/radar.md` and `drafts/new/observation-device-OBS-1.md`.
