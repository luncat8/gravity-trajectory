# 0.5.16 plan — a rival kits itself out before its first launch

## problem

The rivals never buy their first cargo hold, so most of them cannot use the
contract board at all: a delivery needs a hold of at least 4 t (the smallest
cargo is 4 t), a medical mission at least 2 (`medicLoad` is half the hold), and
`makeMedic` refuses to even offer a mission below that. Measured in
`experiments/logs/0.5.15-helper-factors.txt`: 4 of 6 seeds at 1200 s end with
both rivals still on HOLD-0, 5 of 12 rival-games ever buy it, and one seed calls
`careerBetweenFlights` 91 times without once standing on its own HQ.

The between-flight shop (`GravityBot.prototype.careerBetweenFlights`) runs on a
landing, on a sit and on a no-shot. A rival stands on its HQ only before the
first launch — queued, not landed — so the opening purchase never happens, and
whether a company ever gets a hold depends on it happening to land back at home
later. That is why the mid run shows so few medical missions even though the
rebellions are there.

## change

Ship parts are fitted wherever the vessel stands, so the shop does not need a new
call site — it needs to stop asking for home. Drop `career.at === career.hq` from
the first-hold buy in `careerBetweenFlights` and keep only `career.at >= 0`: a
rival that lands anywhere with the purse buys HOLD-8 (0.76) on that landing,
through the existing debits, reserves and one-pad-per-call rule, with no
rival-specific special case.

If the opening still comes down to luck — a rival that spends its purse before it
ever lands — add the missing first pass in `startCareers`, right after
`startCareer` resets the company onto its HQ:

	this.careerBetweenFlights(r.career, r.sim, 0);

Measure the deleted condition on its own first, then this against it; the trace
line should name what an opening shop bought, so the decision log stays readable
either way.

## why this order

The dropped condition is the smaller change, reaches the same measured cause (the
shop never fires where the ship is) and invents nothing. `careerResearch` keeps
its own HQ test, which is pacing rather than a rule — leave it for the A/B to
judge, since a part it fits after a research point now works on any planet too. A
second `MEDIC_SLOT` does not address the gap at all: the offer already survives an
active contract and is rarely reachable, so more offers would not be accepted.
## decided (0.5.22 session — plan step 1 merged)

Numbers: `experiments/logs/0.5.16-rival-first-shop-ab.txt` (rewritten in 0.5.22).
`gravity_bot.js` now carries step 1 — `career.at === career.hq` dropped from the
first HOLD-8 buy in `careerBetweenFlights`, and the buy names itself in the
decision log (`gear · P7 HOLD-8 · 8 t`) — plus nothing from the "if the opening
still comes down to luck" branch.

- **Why nothing decided before: the bench fixture, not the change.** The gravity
  bench seated every company with the standalone HQ picker and left the world
  seed's gravity on its HQ, while the page plans one fair HQ set
  (`prepareCareerStarts`) and pins every starting HQ at g 1 (`startCareerRun`) —
  the same pin `openingTraceHome` applies while it scores a candidate home. 10 of
  60 rival-games therefore opened inside a well the page never allows (seed 2 R1
  on P7 g 2.1, seed 19 *both* rivals on g 2.8 / g 3.1) and never flew at all.
  `career_gravity_bench` now does both (`SEATS=0 HQWELL=0` restores the old
  fixture); on the faithful fixture `stuck` is 0 at 1200 s.
- **On that fixture step 1 wins.** 30 seeds · 1200 s · 60 rival-games, purse = the
  career score: 1390.77 → 1575.06 (+13 %), paired **+3.07 ± 2.02** per game ·
  33 up / 27 down; earned +38 %, pads +16 %, mean reach 4.31 → 4.82, medic trips
  2 → 8, patients home 8 → 20, flights −5 %, dead pads 2 → 8. A positive delta
  clears the decision rule without a bigger sample.
- **varB (the opening pass in `startCareers`) is worse**: +0.79 ± 2.28 on purse
  while it earns the most of the four (250.71) — the pass runs the whole

- **varC (buy only where the fitted ship still leaves) is varA on this fixture**:
  identical totals, the `careerDeparture` gate never refuses a fit once the HQ
  well is 1. It costs two extra calls per shopping pass for nothing. Not merged.
- **The medical quest is only half revived.** The hold gap is gone (hold 8 on
  24 of 24 rival-games, "demand without a hold" 6060 s → 0, offer-seconds 3621 →
  8478) but accepts are still 1 in 24 games: a rival lands on the Capital 20 times
  in 24 games (2.7 % of its time) and nothing sends it there. See
  `0.5.23-plan-medic-capital.md`.

## watch out

- The between-flight shop also runs the expansion claim, which spends purse on a
  pad. If letting it buy the hold anywhere snowballs the opening, split the shop
  into "fit the ship" and "claim a pad" and call only the first one at start;
  measure before choosing.
- `installPart` refuses a ship in flight, so a shop pass that runs while a career
  is still queued (`career.at < 0`) must stay a no-op rather than a wasted
  action: count the action only on success, as the shop already does.
- `careerSit`/`careerNoShot` already call the same function, so an opening call
  must not double-count `this.careerBuilds`/`careerUpgrades` in a way a bench
  reads.
- A career that starts broke (cheat runs) must be unaffected: the shop's own
  purse checks already guard it.
