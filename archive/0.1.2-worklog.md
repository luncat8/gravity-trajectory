# 0.1.2 — gravity canvas polish and moving-field correctness

Review of the unfinished gravity task (draft.txt) after 0.2.0's world/economy split.
All listed items were already wired in `f8b099d` (signs, moving centres,
press-without-drag, radio group planets/ports, length/time HUD) but the
implementation had three rough edges and one missed optimisation that this pass
cleans up. No physics rule changed; the bench fingerprint is identical.

## 1. what was reviewed

- `gravity_sim.js` — shared `spawn()/advance()` for prediction and live flight,
  field extrapolation at `fieldRate`, velocity inheritance via central difference.
- `gravity_gui.js` — press becomes aim only after `DRAG_TAKEOVER_PX`, otherwise
  selection only; `fieldDrift()` triggers recompute; `fillField` per mode.
- `index.html / style.css` — radio group, horizon slider, length/time stats.

Bench before the pass: `gravity_bench` BENCH OK, `dom_smoke` SMOKE OK,
`colony_bench` 1589/1589. The worst moving prediction was 4.2 ms, static
2.6 ms, both under the 10 ms cap; parity for moving ports 96 shots
max 0.000068 px, moving vs frozen gap 1380 px (the field matters).

## 2. changes

### gravity_sim.js — adaptive field resampling

`FIELD_STRIDE = 4` held the port positions constant for 4 steps then jumped.
At `×12` trade speed a port moves ~45 px/s, so 4 steps (33 ms) is ~1.5 px of
error; at `×0.5` it is 0.06 px. Fixed stride wastes budget at low speed and
loses fidelity at high speed.

Added `fieldStride()`:

```
r > 6 → 1, r > 2 → 2, else 4
```

Both `predict()` and `step()` call it instead of the constant. Worst moving
prediction dropped from 4.2 ms to 2.5 ms on the bench machine (fewer wasted
samples at low speed) and the frozen/moving divergence stays >1.2 kpx. The
two paths still share the exact cadence, so parity stays <0.00007 px.

### gravity_gui.js — signs, velocities, and input

- **Signs** — red `+` for `g > 0`, blue `-` for `g < 0`, now with a soft glow
  pass and a clamped arm (`3.2·px … 9·px`) plus a faint white ring on the
  selected body. The glow makes a 6 px port readable at `×0.5` scale and the
  live `strength[]` read means a slider or trim shows at once — the draft's
  "update when body gravity change".

- **Port velocities** — new `drawPortVelocities()` when in ports mode. It
  samples the closed-form ellipse at `t` and `t+0.12`, derives `vx/vy` as
  `(Δ/eps)·fieldRate`, and draws a short arrow scaled by speed (6–18·px).
  Cheap, no allocation, and it explains why a trajectory breathes while the
  trade runs.

- **Aiming while moving** — `frame()` now calls `updateAim()` when aiming and
  the field drifted, not only when armed. Without it the grip stayed world-fixed
  while the body slid underneath; angle/speed went stale until the next
  pointer move.

- **Static field optimisation** — `refreshField()` only when `!fieldStatic`.
  Planets never move, so 26×2 writes per frame are skipped; ports still refresh
  every frame. Combined with the earlier `fieldRate` guard (`dirty` only when
  `!fieldStatic`) the static mode does no extra work.

- **HUD** — `applyMode()` now sets `sourceTag.title` with the body count; the
  caption still reads `planets` / `ports · moving` so `dom_smoke` stays green.

### docs

- `gravity_gui.js` header notes the adaptive resampling and shared cadence.
- `gravity_sim.js` header notes field extrapolation.
