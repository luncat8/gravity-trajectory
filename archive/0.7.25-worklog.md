# 0.7.25 — View line over the canvas · pad block for the landing settings

Three placements asked for by the operator, plus the red fixture 0.7.24 left open.

## 1 · view modes on one line above the canvas

The `view` group left the left HUD column. `#viewBar` sits over the canvas
(`position: fixed`, top centre, `translateX(-50%)`): the three backdrop radios
(`space` / `gravity field` / `potential`), the `full view` checkbox, the `clean
render` checkbox and `#viewTag`, one row, the potential legend under it.

- `main` / `full area` was a two-radio group; it is now one `#viewFull`
  checkbox ("full view"), off = the main frame. `refreshViewUi` writes it from
  `scene.view.frame`, the change listener calls `setViewFrame(FRAME_FULL)` or
  `setViewFrame(FRAME_MAIN)`, and `z` still toggles.
- `#viewGroup` is gone from `index.html`; the left column keeps
  `gravity positions`, `body gravity`, `relative trim`, the horizon, the sound
  and background drawers and the experiments. `.viewCheck` (the shared
  radio/checkbox pill styling) is unchanged, so `clean render` looks the same in
  its new home.
- Width: the bar may only use the strip between the two HUD columns, so
  `max-width` is `min(760px, 100vw - 700px)`, with `max(190px, 100vw - 620px)`
  under 1100px (where `#viewTag` hides first) and a wrap as the last resort.
  Under 599px there is no strip at all: the bar docks bottom-left above the game
  bar at `calc(var(--footReserve) + 8px)`, which the left column never reaches.
- The legend keeps its place in the reading order of the picture and only shows
  while `potential` is the backdrop (`potentialLegendEl.hidden`).

## 2 · auto-repair in the place the commercial EMC held

`#careerAutoRepairLabel` moved from the game footer into `#planetActions` — the
pad block that sits above the action row inside the flight panel, the block the
commercial button used to share. It is the landing setting that belongs to the
ship, so the footer keeps only the clock controls (`wait`, `❚❚ time`,
`pause on landing`) and playback.

- The pad block now holds `buy EMC-1` and the setting: `setPlanetActions` owns
  both visibilities, `planetActionsEl.hidden = !build && !autoRepair`, where
  `autoRepairShown()` = the career clock is allowed to pause and YOU is not a bot
  (`gui_predict.js`). `refreshCareerClockUi` no longer touches the label — it
  only mirrors the value onto the checkbox.
- The block shows whenever a hand-flown career ship is landed, whether or not
  the planet offers a build, so the checkbox has one home instead of jumping
  between the panel and the footer. The two early `setLaunchState` branches
  (armed impulse, queued barrel) hide the block through `hidePlanetActions()`,
  which hides the label with it.

## 3 · commercial EMC in the launch dropdown

`#launchCommercial` moved into `#launchMenu`, next to `repair`, `launch` and
`evacuate`, and `setPlanetActions` split: the new `setCommercialOption` prices
it (fee, `costly`, `on`, the fire/standing-rail label) and returns
`{ ready, shown }`.

- The caret shows while the ship is worn, an evacuation is on offer, or the
  planet offers a commercial rail, so the entry is reachable on any landed ship.
  The commercial rail is the last fallback of `careerDeparture`, and the menu is
  where it belongs; the pad block keeps the offer that is about the planet
  itself (`buy EMC-1`).
- `evacPrimary` now also waits for `!commercial.ready`, so a planet whose only
  way out is the priced commercial rail keeps a live launch control instead of
  promoting the tow — the old behaviour, where a ready commercial button held
  the main action down, is preserved.
- A new ship on its own pad therefore has a caret with only the commercial entry
  visible (repair and launch stay hidden until the ship is worn).

## 4 · `wear_flight_test.js` is green again

The 0.7.24 note: the test hand-built rival slots without `shots`, and
`stepCareer` attaches a burn to `r.shots[r.shots.length - 1]`, so it threw. The
three hand-built slots now carry `shots: []`, matching what `resetCareerFlight`
builds for a real rival. No assertion changed, and `run_fast.js`'s KNOWN_RED
list never named the file, so the tier is simply green again.
