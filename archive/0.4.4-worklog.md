# 0.4.4 Worklog — weak starter catapult and career-aware headless bot

## Review of 0.4.3

0.4.3 shipped the career drawer and explicitly left bot career awareness as the
next step. The starter EMC-1 was also too strong for the intended early game:
its 45 kN rail was not the dry-ship constraint (the full BAT-1 allowed about
111 px/s). The existing solo rule and deterministic rival searches were otherwise
sound, so this slice keeps the career controller separate from the shared-market
rival loop rather than making career a multiplayer mode.

## Implementation

### 1. Weak initial catapult

`orbit_tech.js` changes EMC-1 `maxForce` from 45 to 15 kN. With the starter
10 t ship and full BAT-1, the rail is now the binding constraint at 91.65 px/s;
a 38 t loaded ship is capped at 47.02 px/s. EMC-2–5 and the energy model are
unchanged. The launch-model and DOM expectations now use the 85–95 px/s starter
band and 90 px/s smoke shots.

### 2. Synchronous career controller in `gravity_bot.js`

The new headless API is intentionally independent of the GUI's hand-flown solo
career:

- `careerPlan(career, sim, market, runStep, fallback)` plans only from
  `career.at` after `sim.canLaunchFrom` succeeds. Every snapped candidate speed
  is bounded by the live `sim.speedLimit(origin)`, including a partially drained
  battery. It uses `predictFast`, uniform angles, target-directed angles, and a
  wider/longer fallback search.
- `careerScoreProbe` combines visit/task pay (including delivery and sport
  rewards), landing-pad value, and launch energy's recharge opportunity cost.
  Its exact score is `taskPay + padValue - energy * wait * 0.04`, where `wait`
  is the number of 30-minute solar waits needed to replace that shot's energy.
  Missing-pad landings are rejected when the predicted cash cannot build the
  required exit pad; the final pad also preserves the purse reserve.
- Active delivery pickup/dropoff and an affordable HQ hold refit are explicit
  landing objectives. This prevents high visit income from making the bot
  ignore the cargo game indefinitely.
- `careerBetweenFlights` performs idle rescue/evacuation, HQ HOLD-8 refit,
  contract acceptance, loaded-pad catapult/battery upgrades, and new-pad
  expansion through `GravityGame.buildGear` / `installPart`. Optional shopping
  preserves a 6.00 purse baseline, plus the hold cost until the first refit.
  The controller reuses its probe, best-plan record, and evaluation record;
  candidate evaluation adds no per-candidate objects.

### 3. Full-career bench and purse baseline

`experiments/career_bot_bench.js` runs the actual `GravitySim` fixed-step flight,
market harvest, pad charging, task board, landing settlement, shopping, recharge
waits, and evacuation rules. It does not mock rewards or purchases. Focused
checks cover the weak rail, occupied-pad-only planning, full and low-charge
speed caps, score-term accounting, and idle buying/upgrading.

The full-run gate is 26/26 catapults, `CAREER_COMPLETE`, and final purse at
least the 6.00 starting baseline on seeds 7, 31, 42, and 913. `SEEDS=42
TRACE=1 node experiments/career_bot_bench.js` prints individual flight plans.

## Next-step suggestion

Add an opt-in GUI career autopilot only if desired, using sliced candidate
evaluation so the synchronous headless planner cannot block a frame. Keep the
6.00 full-career purse baseline as its regression gate. The remaining draft
features are the science-hull UI and the impulse-engine flight control.
