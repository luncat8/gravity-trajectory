# Playable fields: gravity and cache foundation

## Review

The map helper's original-seed panel calls the game's gravity function. Once a
field is armed, that would show the override instead of the original draw. Its
roster can also exceed the world's planet limit: saving that design currently
produces a record too large to play. The plan now specifies an independent seeded
draw and requires the future loader and helper to reject invalid sizes.

The plan originally named only the seat-plan cache. The opening-table and
pair-trace caches also depend on gravity and need field identities; otherwise a
second field on the same seed can reuse the first field's traces.

## Changes

- `planet_gravity.js` can arm a seed-keyed copy of a finite, grid-snapped field,
  disarm it, and return a snapshot. Planets beyond its length use the original
  draw. The exact encoded grid sequence identifies the field without hash
  collisions; a failed arm leaves the previous field intact.
- The seat-plan, opening-table and opening-trace cache keys include that field
  identity. The helper's original draw and baseline bypass the override.
- `planet_gravity_field_test.js` checks isolation, normalisation, immutable
  snapshots, seeded fallback and all three caches; it is in the fast tier.

This is infrastructure only: the game does not yet load or play field records.
No registry, page boot, link or replay behavior has changed.

## Next step

Validate and arm field records at the page's map-start boundary, disarm on every
seed-only transition including the same seed, and reject oversized helper saves.
Then implement field-aware addresses, replay safety and the `FIELDS=` seat
planner before presenting designed fields as playable.
