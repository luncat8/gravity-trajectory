# 0.5.23 plan — Capital task refund, medical payout, board affordability, and Capital leg valuation

Standalone spec (implemented & archived). Supersedes the pre-0.7.32 draft of `0.5.23`.

## context and post-0.5.22 shifts

Between `0.5.22` and `c93f91f`, ship wear (`0.7.2`), Capital recovery (`0.7.32`),
career endgame tuning (`0.7.34`), and unified helper/bot scoring (`0.7.35`)
reshaped the career baseline (`c93f91f` on `SEEDS=1-30 SECONDS=1200`):
- `career_gravity_bench.js`: `purse 511.52`, `earned 91.78`, `pads 246`,
  `medic trips 0`, `patients home 0`.
- `medic_offer_probe.js` (`SEEDS=1-12`): `accepts 1`, `boardings 0`,
  `patients home 0`, `offer and free at capital 0s`.
- At `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT = 1`, one `HOLD-8` medical cycle paid `+4.00`
  (`4` patients) against `−3.00` to enter the Capital, `~2.27…4.54` for the
  seized commercial rebel rail, and `−3.00` to re-enter the Capital (`−4.3…−6.5`
  net cash before wear). No rational helper or bot would fly it.

## design decisions (confirmed)

1. **Medical payout (`orbit_econ.js`)**:
   - `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT = 2` (`+8.00` per 4-patient `HOLD-8` trip, `+16.00`
     across a two-trip `MEDIC_PATIENTS = 8` quelling).
2. **Universal Capital accept refund & clawback (`gravity_game.js`)**:
   - `CAPITAL_REFUND = 3` (`-CAPITAL_VISIT_PAY`).
   - Accepting **any** quest (`TASK_DELIVERY`, `TASK_MEDIC`) or sport
     (`TASK_COMPETE` on `COMPETE_SLOT`) while landed on `career.capitalPlanet`
     immediately credits `+3.00` to `ledger.purse` and `career.earned`, recorded on
     `task.capitalRefund = 3` (leaving `career.lastReward` untouched).
   - `TASK_COMPETE` gains explicit `accept` / `drop` (`state: TASK_ACCEPTED`,
     alongside an open `career.contract` since sport uses no hold); unaccepted
     sport still settles on a qualifying landing.
   - Completing the task clears `task.capitalRefund = 0` so the `+3.00` stays
     with the company.
   - Removing or failing an accepted task (`dropTask` / `dropContract`, deadline
     expiry in `refreshBoard`, `careerBeginFlight` fragile break, `arrestCareer`,
     or `evacuate` medical abort) claws back `-task.capitalRefund` from
     `ledger.purse` and `career.earned` and runs `checkEnded(career)`.
   - Bot spending reserves (`gravity_bot.js` `careerRefundEscrow`) hold active
     `capitalRefund` (`contract` + `COMPETE_SLOT`) in escrow until a completing
     shot fires, preserving `ledger.purse >= 0` if a task is dropped or expires.
3. **Board-level clearing of unaffordable medical offers (`gravity_game.js`, `gui_game.js`)**:
   - Never mutate task boards per-frame or while `career.flying` is true.
   - `medicAffordable(career, market, planet)` checks whether `ledger.purse` covers
     either the rebel commercial rail after `+3.00` Capital accept refund (when
     already at `capitalPlanet`) or `3.00 + commercialMinFee()` to reach the
     Capital, accept (`+3.00`), and pay the rebel rail back.
   - `refreshMedicSlot(career, market, step)` is PRNG-free (`boardStale` stays
     expiry-only so `SALT_TASK` rolls stay deterministic) and runs on
     `refreshBoard`, landed shop/fuel/bribe mutations, and landed 1 Hz political
     ticks.
   - `acceptTask` on `TASK_MEDIC` refreshes `task.deadline` to at least
     `nowStep + deliverySec * 2` (`480 s`) so accepting late in an offer window
     never starves the two-leg flight clock.
4. **Shared helper & rival Capital valuation (`gravity_game.js`, `gravity_bot.js`)**:
   - When `career.contract < 0` and an affordable `TASK_MEDIC` offer stands on
     `MEDIC_SLOT` (away from the Capital), `careerGoalBodies(career)` includes
     `career.capitalPlanet` so human `quest scan` and rival `careerTarget` aim at
     the Capital (`[]` is preserved for an empty board).
   - `careerLandingFacts` keeps `out.cash = visitPay` honest (so `predGain` and
     `best profit` never pretend landing on the Capital pays immediate cash) and
     sets `out.leg = LEG_MEDICS` with
     `out.credit = (CAPITAL_REFUND + medics * MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT) * LEG_CREDIT`.
   - Rival `careerScoreFacts` values that `LEG_MEDICS` Capital arrival (`+
     CAREER_MEDIC_VALUE`), `careerMedicNet` prices the full two-leg cycle
     including Capital refund and rebel commercial return, and `careerAccept`
     away from the Capital reserves `career.contract` when the standing medical
     mission outscores weak local deliveries.

## validation

- `SEEDS=1-12 SECONDS=1200 node experiments/medic_offer_probe.js`:
  `accepts 1 → 5`, `boardings 0 → 3`, `patients home 0 → 12`, `at-capital 230s/7 → 511s/16`.
  On `SEEDS=1-30`: `accepts 16 · boardings 4 · patients home 32`.
- `SEEDS=1-30 SECONDS=1200 PAIRED=1 STATS=1 LOG=0 node experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`:
  `purse 511.52 → 669.21 (+30.8%)`, `earned 91.78 → 218.69`, `pads 246 → 254`,
  `dead pads 5 → 4`, `medic trips 0 → 13`, `patients home 0 → 44`,
  `rebel 64/64 → 58/63` (`5` rebellious planets fully quelled), `0` invariant
  failures.
- Fast unit & GUI suites: `politics_test.js`, `politics_gui_test.js`,
  `career_guidance_test.js`, `bot_gravity_test.js`, `career_stats_test.js`,
  `comms_facts_test.js`, `comms_topics_test.js`, `obs_fragile_gui_test.js`,
  `career_bench.js`.
