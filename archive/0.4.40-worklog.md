# 0.4.40 worklog — both Voronoi presets

## Review

The prior work correctly identified the experiment's overall default as its
point-frequency preset but only implemented the separate distance-frequency
preset. Scaling its distance could not reproduce the missing fixed notes or the
first preset's different volume and envelope steepness.

## Implementation

- Added the original experiment's MIT-licensed point tuning, including its
  initial note selection and two Voronoi-neighbor refinement passes. Field
  coordinates use a fixed ×0.75 conversion; notes remain stable as the ship
  moves or the sound distance slider changes. Active roster/field changes cause
  retuning. The game's unbounded cells differ from the experiment's bounded map.
- Voronoi defaults to point-frequency with volume steepness 39 and envelope 77;
  frequency steepness 15 is inactive in point mode. The 54/53/6 distance-pitch
  preset remains selectable. `n=36` is the experiment's site count, not a sound
  parameter, so selecting a preset does not alter the game's planet roster.
- Added a persistent preset selector, live switching and preset-specific hints.
  Both presets retain the distance-scale adjustment and ship-relative pan. Old
  Voronoi settings without a preset receive the point default; explicitly saved
  other generators and mute preferences remain unchanged.

## Validation

  switching, stable notes, scale, geometry, storage and settings.
- `node experiments/trajectory_sound_test.js`, `node experiments/sound_test.js`,
