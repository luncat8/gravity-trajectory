# 0.4.23 plan — launch book, patient career bot, profit helpers

## Goal

Career flights are searched over and over and the winners are thrown away.
This step keeps them:

1. **Launch book** — every finished career trace the player or a rival company
   found is stored with its visit log, so it can be re-priced and re-flown
   without searching again.
2. **Patient bot** — a rival never flies a losing trajectory that cannot claim a
   pad; it sits out the gap and lets pads charge, the board rotate and visit
   profits grow.
3. **Auto helpers** — `best profit` and `profit + EMC` buttons scan from the
   landed planet and arm the best money shot.
4. Launch button energy unit is `kWh` (a `kW` label was a typo).

## Launch book

`GravityGame.createLaunchBook()` lives on every career (`career.launchBook`), so
the player and each rival company keep their own memory. Reset on
`resetCareer`.

Entry fields, all plain numbers plus two typed-array copies:

| field | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `origin` | start planet |
| `angleU`, `speedU` | aim in engine units (exact re-fire) |
| `body` | predicted landing planet |
| `steps`, `time` | arrival step and seconds |
| `length` | traced path length (px) |
| `mask` | bit mask of the planets the trace entered |
| `logPlanet`, `logStep`, `logCount` | visit log, copied out of the probe |
| `score` | planner score when it was saved (list eviction only) |

The visit log is what makes the book cheap: `estimateProfitLog()` re-prices it
against the live market, so a stored shot scores exactly like a fresh trace
without running one.

Rules:

- **dedupe** — same `origin`, `body` and `mask` are the same harvest, so only
  the faster arrival (`steps`) is kept;
- **capacity** — `BOOK_MAX = 10`, the weakest `score` leaves first;
- **tag** — `launchBookTag(fieldStatic, gravityScale, forceScale, strength)`
  fingerprints the world a trace was made in. A different tag means the paths
  are stale: the book is skipped (and dropped on the next save). A moving field
  (ports) has its own tag, which is also why the list is off while the gravity
  bodies move.
- **live checks** — pad charge (`speed <= speedLimit`), purse (`launchFee`),
  contract and competition gates are re-read on every use; the stored trace
  only supplies geometry and harvest.

## Bot

- `careerScoreFacts()` holds the money terms for a trace given facts
  (`body`, harvest, length, steps); `careerScoreFlight()` feeds it a probe and
  `careerScoreStored()` feeds it a book entry.
- `careerPlan()` scores the book for the pad the ship stands on first. A stored
  shot at or above `CAREER_BOOK_ENOUGH` ends the search with no trace at all;
  otherwise the usual fan runs, seeded with the book score so a new winner must
  actually beat the known one. The fan winner is saved back into the book.
- `careerShouldWait(plan, tries)` — a plan that scores below
  `CAREER_MIN_SCORE` and lands where no EMC can be built is a donation: the
  company sits out `CAREER_SIT_GAP` (60 s of world time) and shops instead.
  After `CAREER_SIT_TRIES` skips it takes the least bad shot, so a company can
  never deadlock. A padless landing that can pay for its own EMC always counts
  as progress.

## Page

- career drawer gets a `launch list` block: one row per saved trajectory from
  the planet under the ship, best live money first, each row arms its shot
  (`data-act="book"`); the block states why it is off (moving gravity, no
  ship, empty book).
- helper row gets `best profit` and `profit + EMC` (career only). They scan
  from the landed planet with the same sliced pipeline as the other buttons,
  score pure money (harvest − EMC fee − exit cost, plus contract rewards) and
  arm the winner. `profit + EMC` only accepts landings where a starter EMC is
  affordable.
- every finished career trace is saved: the player's armed preview when it
  ends, and each candidate a helper scan finishes.
- the armed launch button labels pad energy in `kWh`.

## Validation

- `career_bench`: book dedupe keeps the faster arrival, capacity eviction,
  tag staleness, stored score equals the traced score.
- `career_bot_bench`: book reuse skips the fan (trace counter unchanged),
  stored plan re-fires and scores like the fan plan, patient policy waits on a
  losing plan, anti-deadlock fallback, careers still complete.
- `dom_smoke`: `kWh` label, list rows arm the aim, list off in ports mode,
  helper buttons run a career scan and leave an armed profitable shot.
