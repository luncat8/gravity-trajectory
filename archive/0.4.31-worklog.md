# 0.4.31 worklog

## Review of 0.4.30

1. **`onExperimentInput` cleared `harvestTick` (`gravity_gui.js`)**:
   `setExperimentScales(nextGravity, nextForce, nextHarvest)` coerced `harvestTick = nextHarvest === true`, while `onExperimentInput()` called `setExperimentScales(nextGravity, nextForce)` with two arguments. Dragging `g field` or `catapult` after ticking `stay-pay · per tick` silently reset `harvestTick` to `false`. Fixed by passing `harvestTick` in `onExperimentInput()`, with a regression check in `experiments/dom_smoke.js`.
2. **Visit highlight mismatch between profit pills and `drawSystemVisits` (`gravity_gui.js`)**:
   `0.4.30` switched `drawPlanetProfits()` to `liveHarvestMask` + `predHarvestMask` (`predLogPlanet`, the path ahead of the ship), while `drawSystemVisits()` still drew thick dashed green orbit ellipses on `sim.predVisit` and permanent dim rings on `sim.visited`. Extracted `updateHarvestMasks()` ahead of `drawSystemVisits()` so both planet outlines and profit pills share the same 0-alloc `Uint8Array(PLANETS)` masks.
3. **Checkbox styling in `.ctl` (`style.css`) + unguarded `exitCost` in `gravity_bot.js`**:
   Added `margin: 0; accent-color: var(--accent)` for `.ctl input[type="checkbox"]`, and guarded `(plan.exitCost || 0).toFixed(2)` / `(plan.exitSource || 'own')` in `GravityBot.prototype.careerLaunch` so minimal plan fixtures in `career_bot_bench.js` pass cleanly.

## Changes in 0.4.31

### 1. Career clean canvas render (enabled by default) & planet-outline visit highlights
- **`index.html` / `style.css` / `gravity_gui.js`**: Added `#careerClean` (`checked` by default) inside `#viewGroup` (`#careerCleanLabel.viewCheck`, visible only when `careerRule()` is active, leaving `#viewTag` untouched).
- **No thick orbit visit ellipses & no past trajectory path**: When `careerRule() && cleanCareerView` is active:
  - `drawSystemVisits()` omits all `ctx.ellipse(...)` orbit strokes and permanent `sim.visited` rings, leaving the thin background orbits from `orbit_view.js` clean.
  - `drawTrail()` (the blue past-trajectory path `rgba(103, 224, 255, 0.9)`) and rival past-flight trails in `drawRivalsWorld()` are skipped, while `drawPrediction()` (upcoming dashed trajectory) stays visible.
- **Planet outline + outer outline highlight**: Added 0-alloc `drawPlanetVisitOutline(x, y, r, px, pulse, inside)` to highlight a visited/upcoming planet's own rim (`r + 0.5 * px`) plus a tight outer ring (`r + 3.4..5.0 * px`), clear of the EMC ring (`r + 6.5 * px`) and profit pill below the planet. Unchecking `clean render` restores classic orbit highlight ellipses and past-flight trails.

### 2. Science research always available when affordable
- **`gravity_game.js`**: Removed `career.at !== career.hq` from `GravityGame.researchPoint` so inventions succeed on any planet or in open space (`career.at = -1`) whenever `career.ended === CAREER_OPEN` and `purse` covers the fee (ship part refits via `installPart` remain at HQ; pad prototypes build on any planet).
- **`gravity_gui.js`**:
  - Removed the `'research is done back at HQ ...'` lock from `scienceAcceptButton`, `syncSciencePurse` / `refreshScienceReadout`, and `careerResearch`; updated header `#sciHead` to `'research lab · purse ...'`.
  - Handled `sciFamily` and `sciAccept` in `careerAct` ahead of the `careerWaitBusy() || turret.pending || gravity.status === STATUS_FLYING` guard so the player can switch families and accept research during flight, wait, or turret wind-up. If a queued commercial/guest launch (`turret.pending`) becomes unaffordable after spending on research, `cancelTurret()` releases it cleanly.
  - Hooked `syncSciencePurse(game.career)` into `updateGameHud()` whenever `purseText !== hudPurseText` so `#sciAccept` enables live on the exact frame harvest income or rival pad tax brings the purse above the fee.
- **`gravity_bot.js`**: Allowed `GravityBot.prototype.careerResearch` to unlock its catapult bridge-rail prototype away from HQ while keeping tank/engine prototype + immediate `installPart` refits at HQ.

### 3. Science research 1.5x cheaper
- **`orbit_science.js`**: Scaled `K_DIST` (`6.0 -> 4.0`), every `BASE_PRICE` entry (`engine: 2/1.5`, `catapult: 2`, `panel: 1`, `battery/hold/tank: 1/1.5`), and `DEFAULT_BASE` (`1/1.5`) by `PRICE_SCALE = 1 / 1.5`. Because `priceOf` computes `base + K_DIST * TIER_SCALE * (dist + frontier)`, recombination fees, hull-distance fees, and directional frontier surcharges are all uniformly `1.5x` cheaper.
