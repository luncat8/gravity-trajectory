# 0.4.26 worklog — seeded gravity and simplified HUD

## Review of 0.4.25

The launch-book smoke coverage still exercises the shared rail gates, stale
books and book-seeded helpers. The real-browser layout check had a stale

## Changes

- Each planet gets a deterministic, independently hashed gravity from −1 to
  +5 in 0.05 steps; only the player's initial career HQ is forced to 1. Separate
  base and masked strengths preserve edits and genuine zeroes across roster
  changes, and feed the field, moving ports and bot simulations. The lab keeps
  its existing −2 minimum for manual edits and extends the maximum to +5.
- Raised the global gravity multiplier and replay codec limit to ×9.
- Replaced the seed/rate HUD row with a seed and mode in the address. Reloading
  regenerates the seeded world; unrelated query parameters are retained, replay
  hashes take priority, and starting a new run drops an old replay hash.
- Removed the flight abort button while retaining lab clear, route clear plan and
  the decoder for previously recorded abort steps. Updated smoke fixtures to
  wait for natural flight endings.
- Moved aim/launch guidance into one shared help section and kept goal, score and
  specific controls in a separate selected-mode section. Removed duplicate HUD
  hints and updated the README, including replay and URL limitations.
