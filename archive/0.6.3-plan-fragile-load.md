# 0.6.3 plan — fragile load: the quest that wants a gentle launch

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/career-economy.md` §2.4 (`add quest` — «fragile load - cant survive
>EMC1. available on light planets»). Depends on the delivery task and on
`careerLandingFacts`.

One reading of the note, confirmed by the author: «cant survive >EMC1» is *the
load dies on launch acceleration*. The launch is lost when the ship is fired
harder than the load survives, whatever the rail. An `EMC-1` at full force on a
loaded ship sits below the limit, so `EMC-1` always carries it; a stronger rail
breaks it at full force, and the pilot can always aim a gentle shot on any rail.
(The other reading — any rail above `EMC-1` breaks it at any speed — would
punish the pad tier instead of the force and forbid a slow `EMC-2` shot that
carries the load fine.)

## problem

The draft asks for one more delivery kind: cargo that cannot survive a strong
launch, offered on light planets. It is the one quest that makes a pad upgrade
a thing to handle with care — `0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md` §3 prices the opposite
pressure, freight that only a big rail lifts — and it makes the light worlds a
career map already prefers into the right place to be.

## the model

A fragile delivery is a `TASK_DELIVERY` with one extra field, `task.fragile`; no
new kind and no new board slot, so every walker of the board (the row, the bot's
score, the aim readout, the launch book) keeps working with one `if`.

- **What breaks it.** A *loaded* fragile contract is lost when the launch
  acceleration exceeds `FRAGILE_ACCEL` (`2.0 m/s²`):
  `speed² / (2 · RAIL_M) > FRAGILE_ACCEL`, i.e. a launch above
  `FRAGILE_SPEED = sqrt(2 · FRAGILE_ACCEL · RAIL_M) ≈ 105.8 px/s` — from your
  own rail, a rival's, a rebel's, the commercial one or the Capital's. The rail
  still fires, the contract is dropped with its cargo, nothing is paid. Before
  the pickup nothing is aboard, so the flight to the load may be as hard as the
  pilot likes; every launch after it must be gentle, a relay hop included.
- **Why acceleration and not energy or tier.** `launchEnergy = k · m · v² /
  efficiency` is *lower* on a better rail for the same shot, so an energy line
  would let the strong rail through and stop the weak one; a tier line would
  stop a slow `EMC-2` shot. On a loaded `HOLD-8` ship (21 t) the `EMC-1` force
  cap is `63 px/s` (`0.71 m/s²`), the `EMC-2` force cap `155 px/s`
  (`4.29 m/s²`): `EMC-1` cannot break it, a stronger rail can — the note's
  «cant survive >EMC1», priced by force.
- **Offered only where a gentle shot can leave.** A fragile offer is made only
  when its origin has `g ≤ FRAGILE_G` — the note's «available on light planets»
  — and its load is at most `FRAGILE_TONS`. The line is measured, not chosen: an
  `EMC-1 + BAT-1` lifts a loaded `HOLD-8` ship (19.5 t, cap 65.6 px/s) off 99 %
  of planets at `g 0.75`, 87 % at `g 1.0` and 44 % at `g 1.5`
  (`RAIL=1 MASS=19.5 SEEDS=12 node experiments/hq_gravity_probe.js`), and that
  cap is well under `FRAGILE_SPEED`. On an eligible offer one more draw makes it
  fragile with `FRAGILE_CHANCE`; the draw is spent only there, so every other
  board keeps its stream. Tourists are never fragile.
- **Where it is checked.** One predicate in `gravity_game.js`,
  `fragileBreaks(task, speed)`. `careerBeginFlight` reads it (the void sets
  `career.fragileLost` for that launch and drops the contract), and
  `careerLandingFacts` reads it with the shot's launch speed: a shot that would
  break the load carries no delivery reward and no leg, so the aim line, the
  launch book, the helper scans and the bots all see the void before the click.
  A flight already under way passes no speed — it survived its launch.
  `canAccept` needs nothing new: the slowest shot is always gentle, so no rail
  can make the job impossible.

## numbers (starting points, a bench tunes them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FRAGILE_ACCEL` | `2.0` | the launch acceleration m/s² the load survives (`FRAGILE_SPEED ≈ 105.8 px/s`) |
| `FRAGILE_G` | `0.75` | offered only from a planet this light (measured above) |
| `FRAGILE_TONS` | `8` | the heaviest fragile load: a full `HOLD-8` |
| `FRAGILE_CHANCE` | `0.25` | share of eligible offers that are fragile (≈ 5 % of all delivery offers, seeds 1–12) |
| `FRAGILE_PAY` | `×1.4` | a fragile reward is `1.4 ×` an ordinary delivery of the same tons and distance |

`FRAGILE_PAY` is the only value tuned for feel. An 8 t delivery pays
`0.98…2.42`, so a fragile one pays up to `3.39`, under the ordinary
`CAREER.deliveryMax = 4.50`; the cap is not raised.

## GUI

- **Board row**: the kind cell says `fragile` (beside `delivery`, `tourists`,
  `medical`, `sport`) and the state line ends `· launch ≤ 105 px/s`; the accept
  title says a harder launch voids the contract once loaded.
- **Pad block**, with the load aboard: `fragile load aboard · launch ≤ 105 px/s
  · this rail reaches N px/s`, then `safe at any aim`, or in the warn colour
  `aim softer or it breaks`.
- **Aim line**: a shot above the limit drops the delivery reward from the
  predicted profit, appends `· breaks the fragile load · launch ≤ 105 px/s` and
  turns the aim readout to the warn colour. The launch stays legal: it is an
  unprofitable shot, not a forbidden one.
- **Launch**: firing it anyway prints `fragile load lost · fired at N px/s · …
  contract void`.
- **Flight HUD**: the work caption reads `fragile · launch ≤ 105 px/s` while a
  fragile contract is open.
- **Help drawer**: the career `detail` sentence gains the fragile clause.

## Validation

- `experiments/fragile_test.js`: the limit as one acceleration and one speed;
  `EMC-1` at full force is gentle on a loaded ship and `EMC-2` is not; a gentle
  launch keeps the contract on an own `EMC-2`; a hard one voids it and the slot
  forgets the flag; a hard flight to the pickup breaks nothing; the landing
  table carries no reward for a breaking shot; over seeds 1–12 every fragile
  offer comes from `g ≤ FRAGILE_G` with `≤ FRAGILE_TONS`, is never a tourist
  job, stays under the cap, and is the rare job.
- `experiments/obs_fragile_gui_test.js`: the board row, the pad line on a weak
  and a strong rail, the aim note and warn state, the lost load on launch.
- `career_bench` keeps its three known failures; `game_bench` is untouched (the
  rule does not exist outside career).

## open

- A bot prices the void through `careerLandingFacts`, so a hard shot is worth
  less to it, but nothing stops it firing one for another profit and losing the
  load; no bench counts `fragile taken / lost` yet.

## Scope boundary

No new task kind, no change to `CARGO_TONS`, no damage model beyond the one
launch test (an impulse burn never breaks the load), no effect on the trade
economy or the trade backdrop.
