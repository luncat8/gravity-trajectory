  task touches.

  `stepHarvest`, `harvestPlanet`, `harvestStay` and `planetProfitAt` — all

## 2. Implementation

- `gravity_sim.js`: `PRED_LOG_MAX` 64 → 96 (with `BOOK_LOG_MAX` derived from
  it); preallocated `predLogDwell` (per row) and `predLogOpen` (per planet,
  −1 outside); `routeBeginAt` opens a `step 0` row for every spawn-inside
  system; `routeStep` drips one dwell into kept rows after `stepInsideAt`,
  closes left rows, then opens entry rows (cap at `PRED_LOG_MAX`), and the
  `END_IMPACT` append carries `dwell 0`. No heap allocation in either path.
- `gravity_game.js`: `stayPayLog(market, planet, baseStep, from, to,
  reportUntil, total)` mirrors `harvestStay` step by step and threads the
  caller's `total` so every value joins the accumulator in the live
  `stepHarvest` order — the sums match bit for bit (entry rule) or to
  summation order (tick rule, ≤ 2.4e-13 measured). `estimateProfitLog` gained
  the trailing optional `dwells` parameter: `HARVEST_ENTRY` (or a missing
  trail) skips `step 0` rows and prices entries exactly as before; under
  `HARVEST_TICK` a `step 0` row pays the lump at step 1 then drips
  `2 .. dwell`, an entry row pays its entry then `s0+1 .. s0+dwell`, both
  gated by `reportUntil` and both skipping Capital/`−3`, A.R.F./`0` and quiet
  systems; `landing.value` still feeds only entry rows. `estimateProfit`
  passes `sim.predLogDwell`. `BOOK_LOG_MAX = GravitySim.PRED_LOG_MAX`;
  `recordLaunch` allocates `logDwell` per entry, copies `trace.logDwell` or
  fills 0 (older callers), and back-fills a missing trail on a pre-0.7.39
  entry.
- `gravity_bot.js`: `careerLogDwell` alongside the log store, copied by
  `careerLogStore` and passed by `careerSaveBest`; `careerScoreStored` passes
  `entry.logDwell`; `careerBurnPrefix` gained `dwell` and `open` (mirroring
  the sim) plus `joinedDwell`; `careerBurnLaunch` opens spawn rows and drips
  during the 24-step prefix; `careerBurnVisitPay` joins prefix rows first and
  merges probe `step 0` rows into the prefix's still-open row (dwell only, no
  phantom entry — a probe entry would price a second `−3` on Capital and break
  entry-rule parity), then appends probe entries at `offset + step`;
  `prepareLookahead` skips `step 0` rows before stamping `lookMarket`
  (lab lookahead is entry-only); the `careerFanShot` `END_TIME` seed gate
  counts entry rows only (spawn rows are contiguous at the front).
- `gui_book.js`: `storedMoney` passes `entry.logDwell`; `saveTrace` records
  `probe.predLogDwell` and appends element 12 to the `bookTrace` tuple (13
  elements); `applyCareerBookTrace` accepts 12- and 13-element tuples (the
  plan's one legacy exception, zero-filling 12) and rejects a mismatched
  dwell trail; `refreshLaunchList`'s cache key gained `harvestTick`; the
  `· ≥ticks` suffix in `launchRowsHtml` is gone.
- `gui_predict.js` / `gui_hud_text.js`: `predTickNote()` and both consumers
  removed — the aim label and HUD profit line no longer print `· ≥ticks`
  (the `expTag` `· ticks` marker stays, per plan).
- Test-side intended-behavior updates: `wear_flight_test` burn-prefix
  assertions now expect the spawn row (`count 2`, `{planet 0, step 0}`) plus
  the prefix entry (`{2, prefixSteps}`); `dom_smoke`'s aim loop requires an
  entry row (`predLogStep > 0`) to mean "enters a system", and its vacuous
  `cSim.harvestMode === GravitySim.HARVEST_TICK` (both `undefined`) now reads
  `GravityGame.harvestMode()`; `wear_forecast_gui_test` pins
  `predLogDwell[0] = 0` on its hand-built logs and asserts `≥ticks` is absent
  from the aim label and launch list while the stay-pay is on.
