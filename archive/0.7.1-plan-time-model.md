# 0.7.1 plan — game seconds and the shared orbit clock

Standalone contract for the time model. The economy tables use game seconds;
the orbit clock belongs to both the trade sky and moving-port gravity. A
background control that moves those ports is a gameplay control and must say so.

## 1. units and clocks

One physics step is `GravitySim.DT = 1/120` of a **game second**. Flights,
board deadlines, contracts, barrels, arrests, pad accounting and politics use
this step. No reader invents a second conversion from steps.

| quantity | conversion | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| game seconds | `gameSeconds(steps) = steps · DT` | the economy and flight unit |
| real seconds | `wallSeconds(steps, pace) = gameSeconds(steps) / pace` | watching cost; zero pace returns zero |
| pad seconds | `padSeconds(gameSec) = gameSec · SUN_SEC_PER_GAME_SEC` | sunlight price, with factor 30 |
| pad minutes | `padMinutes(gameSec) = padSeconds(gameSec) / 60` | battery-leak unit |
| orbit minutes | `tradeMinutes(gameSec, rate) = gameSec · rate` | trade sky and moving gravity together |
| steps for a span | `stepsForGameSeconds(sec) = max(1, round(sec / DT))` | integer fixed-step boundary |

`SUN_SEC_PER_GAME_SEC = 30` is a balance price, not an animation multiplier.
The colony economy keeps its own year; it is not a unit on the flight HUD.

**Playback** is `play ×N`, game seconds per real second in every mode. The
ladder is `0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32`; career opens at ×4, the lab at ×1.
Playback scales the wall-clock slice fed to the fixed-step loop. It does not
change the step sequence, a deadline, a price or a recorded shot. There is no
second `game` multiplier.

**Orbit rate** is raw trade minutes per game second in a flight, or per real
second in the idle lab. Its default is 0.5, displayed as ×1; the slider spans
×1…×24 (raw 0.5…12). Moving ports are gravity sources: changing their orbit
rate changes the gravity encountered over a flight and its prediction. It does
not change body strength. Static planets, pads, politics and visit-profit age
do not read orbit rate.

The rate is locked in career, route contests, pinned links, replays and runs
with saved shots. Career flights, idle time and waits all advance orbit time by
`gameSec · lockedRate`. The default career rate therefore advances 30 orbit
minutes per wait; a linked raw rate of 1 advances 60. Pad sunlight remains
1800 pad seconds and politics remains 60 game seconds in either case.

## 2. shared orbit/gravity control

The control lives under `orbital trade · background`, titled `orbits · gravity`.
It is not described as decoration-only.

- Rate tooltip: moving ports are gravity sources; rate changes trajectories,
  not gravity strength. State the default, range and independent economy clocks.
- Pause tooltip: pausing orbits also pauses moving-port gravity; an airborne
  ship continues on its flight clock. In a game, orbit motion follows the game
  clock and its time controls instead.
- A visible hint repeats the gameplay effect for touch and keyboard users. The
  range input references it with `aria-describedby`.
- A locked slider keeps the explanation on its wrapping label, because a
  disabled input may not display its own native tooltip. The visible hint names
  the lock reason: career, replay, route contest, shared link or saved shots.
- Orbit, moon, link and route checkboxes only toggle visual layers.

Do not split the port animation from its gravity field: that would draw bodies
in positions different from the ones pulling the ship. A purely cosmetic clock
is only appropriate for effects that are not gravity sources.

## 3. URL contract

`&rate=` and `&speed=` both carry **raw** orbit speed. The displayed multiple is
`raw / 0.5`, never the value written back into the URL.

- raw 0.5 → ×1, default parameter omitted;
- raw 1 → ×2, `speed=1` or a pinned `rate=1`;
- raw 2 → ×4, `speed=2`;
- reopening any of these must preserve raw speed, the label and moving gravity.

A pinned `rate=` fixes the run. An ordinary `speed=` may be edited in the lab
until a shot or shared-run lock applies. The seed URL and replay codec must not
convert through the display multiple.

## 4. wait and playback labels

`WAIT_SEC = 60`; `WAIT_PAD_SEC = 1800`; `WAIT_PAD_MIN = 30`.
One press eases the span over `CAREER_WAIT_MS = 2000`, crediting at most 20 ms
per frame so a slow machine stretches the press instead of skipping settlement.

- button: `wait · 60 s`;
- tooltip: `fast-forward 2 s of real time: the clock runs 60 s (15 s to watch at
  ×4)`, followed by pad, board and visit gains;
- progress: `fast-forward · 0.8 / 2 s · clock +24 s · P3 0.90 / 1.50 kWh`;
- completion: `clock +60 s · P3 pad +0.50 kWh`;
- restored button: `wait · 60 s`.

The preview follows the live playback pace. Deadlines, `gun · N s`,
`ready in N s` and `t+ 0:01:00` remain game seconds. Battery leak is a fraction
per pad minute; BAT-1 loses `1 − exp(-0.001 · 30)`, about 3%, per wait.

## 6. political units

`politicsSeconds(market, step)` returns game seconds. The political table is:

| constant | span or rate |
|---|---|
| `CORRUPTION_PROFIT_START` | `+1.00` visit profit (passive rot starts above this) |
| `CORRUPTION_PROFIT_FULL` | `+3.00` visit profit (passive rot reaches max rate) |
| `CORRUPTION_PASSIVE_RATE` | `0.1` max points per game second at `+3.00` (linear ramp from `+1.00`) |
| `CORRUPTION_REPORT_RATE` | `0.02` points per game second while reporting on pad |
| `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` | `60` game seconds per epoch |
| `FLYBY_RAMP_SECONDS` | `240` game seconds |
| `ARREST_SECONDS` | `120` game seconds |
| pacify cooldown | two epochs = `120` game seconds |

Passive corruption uses the visible uncollected visit profit (`1.00 → 3.00`)
rather than a hidden grace timer, so the planet card's profit readout and its
colour stages (mint `≤ 1.00`, amber `1.01…1.99`, coral `≥ 2.00`) directly show
neglect and rot speed. Separate balance multipliers belong to
`0.7.6-plan-balance-knobs.md`.

## 7. validation

- `orbit_clock_test`: moving-port predictions and flights differ by orbit rate;
  static planets do not; pause freezes orbit/gravity time but not flight steps;
  tooltips and locks explain the effect; URLs round-trip without doubling;
  career idle/wait orbit time matches the locked field rate while politics does not.
- `career_bench`: conversion identities and pinned wait energy. Known fixture
  failures must be compared with the base checkout, not called passes.
- `politics_test`, career pause/statistics/departure/expansion and multiplayer
  career tests: game-clock rules remain intact.
- `dom_smoke`: the entire suite, including the stretched wait restoring
  `wait · 60 s` after exactly 7200 steps.
- Real-browser layout check: the expanded background hint and controls fit.
  Compare any unrelated HUD failures with the base checkout.
- `node --check` on touched scripts and `git diff --check`.

## 8. outside this contract

No wear, repair prices, fleet or new economy multiplier. Separate pad, rot,
pay and wear knobs belong to `0.7.6-plan-balance-knobs.md`. Re-anchoring panel
tables to remove the sunlight factor is a balance migration, not a label fix.
