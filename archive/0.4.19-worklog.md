# 0.4.19 worklog — career EMC economy: claims, usage tax, turning barrels, flying rival companies

## what changed in 0.4.19

### 1. Prices (`orbit_tech.js`, `gravity_game.js`)

- EMC ladder is `9 / 18 / 30 / 45 / 60` (was `3 / 6 / 10 / 15 / 20`), so the stated first rung stays
  the cheapest one.
- `CAREER.evacFee` 6.00, `CAREER.commercialFee` 3.00, `CAREER.taxPerKwh` 1, `CAREER.turretMinSec` 2,
  `CAREER.turretSpanSec` 3.

### 2. EMC network (`gravity_game.js`)

- `createGalaxy` / `resetGalaxy` seed each planet's EMC turn period (`2 + 3·hash01` s) and phase.
- `turretAngle(galaxy, planet, seconds)` is the barrel direction: a launch fires along it, so a shot
  waits at most one turn for the barrel to reach the aim angle.
- `padAt` / `padOwner` / `claimOwner`: own EMC first, then the best rival EMC on the planet, else the
  commercial tier-1 EMC that stands on every planet. `OWNER_COMMERCIAL` is `-1`.
- `launchFee`: free on your own EMC, a flat 3.00 on the commercial rail, `taxPerKwh × launch energy` on
  a rival's rail; the fee is credited to that company's purse and `taxEarned`.
- `exitCostAt` = cheapest way off a planet (commercial rail when it lifts the ship, a first EMC, an
  evacuation), `padLift` = the rail's full-battery cap with the ship as loaded, `rivalSeed`, `pickHq`.
- `refreshNetwork` writes every actor's `launchPads`; `syncCareerNetwork` keeps a galaxy-less career
  (the benches) mirroring `padAt` too, so a solo pad is what its sim gates on.

### 3. Career state & GUI (`gravity_gui.js`, `index.html`)

- Queued launch: a click arms `queueTurretShot` — the button reads `gun · N s`, the world clock stays
  frozen, `click` / `Esc` cancels, and `tickTurret` fires the exact armed shot when the barrel lines up.
- `drawCareerEmc` draws each planet's claim: an owner-coloured ring plus the turning barrel (the line
  from the draft); the drawer's pad block, planet picker and launch note carry the owner tag
  (`YOU` / `R1` / `R2` / `commercial`).
- Pad block states `no EMC here · commercial rail · fee 3.00` and the lift preview on a padless planet;
  the purse toast shows `EMC fee −x` before the pad kWh.
- `career.forceScale` now follows the experimental force slider, so every cap readout (picker, plate,
  drag range) uses the same multiplier as the sim.

### 4. Flying rival careers (`gravity_bot.js`)

- `startCareers` / `stepCareer` / `careerLaunch` / `careerLand` / `careerRestart`: each rival runs a
  whole career on the shared map — own HQ, purse, ship, task board, pads and claims — and pays tax when
  it fires from somebody else's EMC.
- Rivals harvest the market on every flight step (they used to earn nothing but contracts), so their
  purses actually track the shared market.
- Controller fixes: an optional HQ refit no longer forces every plan to land on the HQ (that deadlocked
  a ship parked where the HQ was unreachable); a park with no reachable trajectory evacuates home after
  two tries; a comatose company is refinanced at its old HQ after 30 s instead of leaving an empty map;
  a destination without your EMC now keeps a margin over the way off it.
- `bot.reset` drops a leftover launch network, so a career's pads never leak into a money/route run.

### 5. Benches

- `career_bench.js`: new prices, `humanOnly` career, commercial launch off a padless planet, bulk
  upgrade totals, endgame cap.
- `career_bot_bench.js`: the 6.00 start is now a grind guard (survive + claim a second planet), while
  the 26-pad completion runs start from a funded mid-game purse; score terms include the launch fee.
- `dom_smoke.js`: career flies rivals, the queued launch (clock frozen while the barrel turns), the new
  prices and copy, claim tags and the commercial picker label.
