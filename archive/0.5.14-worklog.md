# 0.5.14 worklog — helpers: taken quests as profit, and the quest scan

Follows `archive/0.5.13-worklog.md`. Two helper items from its aftermath: the
career auto-aim helpers priced only visit money plus the delivery/competition
payout, so a taken medical quest paid nothing in a `best profit` scan; and
there was no helper aimed at solving quests at all.

## Review of 0.5.13 (what this stage changed and why)

- **Taken quests counted as profit.** `moneyFacts` (gui_book.js) now adds
  `aboard × MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` when the taken contract is the medical
  quest with patients aboard and the shot lands on the Capital — the exact
  payout `settleMedic` makes. Since `traceMoney`/`storedMoney` both feed
  `moneyFacts`, the profit helpers (`best profit`, `profit + EMC`) and the
  launch list all price the medical leg now, and the aim readouts agree with
  the money the landing actually pays.
- **The quest scan.** New career-only `quest scan` button next to the profit
  helpers. It runs the same flyability gate (affordable fee, physically
  sampled departure) but ranks candidates by `helperQuestScore` first —
  delivery completion pays the full reward, the cargo-load leg half, the
  medical pickup leg a quarter, the Capital delivery per patient, the
  competition its reward — with net money ×1 breaking ties. Half of its 34
  aims are random from the landed pad, the rest are aimed straight at the
  quest planets (contract from/to, Capital, medic planet). The launch-book
  seed is re-priced on the quest scale too, so a press keeps a saved shot
  only when no fresh aim is a better quest shot. Notices read
  `quest shot · P7 · +2.00` (cash, not the quest-weighted score) and
  `no quest shot found` when nothing flyable exists.
