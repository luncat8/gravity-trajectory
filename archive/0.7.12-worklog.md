# 0.7.12 — make the two timing budgets useful without moving them — 2026-11-16

## review and decision

0.7.11 fixed two stale `gravity_view_test` expectations for stated reasons: HQ
pinning changes seed 61's blue pixels, and cap 4 makes the visit card `+0.47`.
No new issue there. Its remaining next step was the machine-speed failures in
`route_bench` and `gravity_bench`. A clean base already failed these on this box
(0.7.10 measured 97.9 vs 60 ms for the wall trace, 21.62 vs 16 ms for the field
raster); this session's first route run was 69.8 vs 60 ms, while its first field
run happened to be just under the line at 15.62 ms. The machine varies.

**Keep the limits**: 60 ms of work for the 1,800 s pathological route trace,
16 ms for a 1/4-resolution 2D field frame. They describe real interaction
latency, not just a score relative to a synthetic CPU loop. Normalizing them
could make a real frame miss appear green. Instead, remove redundant work and
compare old/new implementations on the *same* box, in one warmed process.

The other next-step question was whether to omit the ~2 s pair ranker on a
refused career opening. **Keep it for now.** The 0.7.10 measurements found that
single-home rankings accepted none of eight worlds, while fair worlds sometimes
needed the second or third exact batch (up to index 34). A refusal is not known
until the exact final-field checks run. The current 48-set best-of-head bound
already avoids walking all 220 sets when none is fair; skipping the pair-pinned
ranking would also change the order that *finds* fair sets. No opening-search
law or seat order changed in this stage.

## changes

- `gravity_sim.js`: `setPlanets` caches the conservative union box of all
  static planet impact discs and system ellipses. `impact`, `markSystemsAt` and
  `stepInsideAt` skip their planet loops when a ship is outside it. The
  step-inside state is cleared there, so a returning ship can enter and earn
  a new visit again. The box includes masked planets (conservative) and is
  recalculated on world replacement; the empty-planet fallback is unchanged.
- `gravity_field.js`: a row wholly within the kernel LUT uses a branch-free
  per-pixel loop; only a row beyond the table takes the analytic fallback.
  Field scratch arrays are Float64, avoiding Float32 rounding on every body
  contribution. The fallback tests squared distance before `>> 6`, which
  also fixes false black pixels when a far zoom overflowed the signed index.
  Arrays are still made once per live renderer, not in its frame loop.
- `route_bench`: asserts impact beyond the ellipse but inside the physical
  disc, leave/re-enter of a system, the active mask and replacement geometry.
  Its header now states the budget the assertion actually used (60, not 45 ms).
  `gravity_bench`: compares the CPU raster with the exact field at normal and
  two zoomed-out scales, including distances above signed 32-bit range. The
  old renderer fails the new zoom check (max pixel drift 191); the new one
  passes. The original parity, pixel-error and **absolute timing** bars remain.
- Test routing in `AGENTS.md` and `experiments/README.md` now mentions both
  the long-route bench and the field/view checks; `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`
  records the A/B technique and the two boundary traps.

## measurements

Same-process A/B on the same seeded geometry after three warmups; five
alternating passes (`/tmp/budget_ab.js`, scratch only). Old files loaded with
`git show HEAD:<file>`; neither threshold was used to choose a faster host.

| work | before | after | invariant |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 216,000-step empty-space route, 1,800 s wall | 61.7–77.8 ms | **15.8–19.7 ms** | same 216,000 steps, 27,001 points, 54,000 px |
| 320×200 fallback field, 48 bodies | 12.61–15.46 ms | **8.42–10.69 ms** | same-world pixel delta max 1 |

In the full benches after the change: route wall 19.3 ms, field raster 9.78 ms,
zone raster 8.19 ms. A slower CPU can still fail an absolute timing check;
that is a reported budget miss, not an excuse to weaken parity or change the
limits. The common field view and empty-space route now have headroom on this
sandbox where they did not before.
