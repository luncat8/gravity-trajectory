# 0.4.41 worklog — balance leftovers: EMC cost floor, starter tech, shop ladder

## Review

The EMC cost rebalance landed with the whole ladder cheap (3/6/10/15/20) but
three career suites were red and never re-run: `dom_smoke` died early on the
panel row, `career_departure_test` and `career_guidance_test` on the starter
purse. Two real bugs hid behind the visible cost question:

- EMC-2 at exactly 6.00 equaled the starter purse, and the guidance fixture
  carries 6.00 + 1.74 arrival profit = 7.74 to its landing. Every planet the
  calibration calls starter-blocked became a free rescue: buy EMC-2 on arrival
  and fly on. The tests' invariant ("a blocked destination must stay
  unavailable at the starter purse") was gone.
- `seedScience` seeded `knownGear` from the science families only. Panels and
  holds are not research families, so SP-2..SP-4 and HOLD-8/24 knew nothing and
  got no shop buttons in any normal career — the panel row read `max` forever.
  The communism shop checks had masked it.

Verified as already implemented and kept: separate `gameHud`/`tradeHud` panels
with their own clocks and the game-speed slider; the planet-ownership rule
(`planetBuildable` gates `buildGear`, the shop and the launch panel; a planet
holds one player EMC plus the commercial rail and evacuate); rival EMC use with
the tax paid into the owner's purse and the commercial one-attempt fallback
when the standing rail finds no shot.

## Implementation

- Catapult ladder is now 3/8/12/16/20. The floor matters more than the shape:
  EMC-2 must cost above 7.74 (starter purse + first-shot arrival profit) or the
  blocked calibration loses its teeth, so 6 → 8, and the rungs above re-spaced
  to even +4 steps, still far under the old 40-era top prices. The reason sits
  on the cost table comment.
- `seedScience` seeds starter knowledge per gear kind from `OrbitTech.TABLES`
  (STARTING_RESEARCH_TIER applies to every kind; panels and holds default to
  fully known) and seeds the science hull from that same knowledge, so the hull
  keeps its "only EMC-1/2 ship with starter tech" two-point shape that
  `career_bench` pins.
- `unlockPrototype` now proves stock rungs: a researched prototype at or above
  a stock tier brings that tier into the shop beside the invention, so "next
  needs to be researched in sci" literally delivers the next stock level.
- Shop rows: `gearNext` picks the row's buildable offer — next known stock
  tier, else the lowest researched prototype that still upgrades the pad — so
  `max` prints only when nothing is buildable (and names the science-locked
  tier next to it: `EMC-3 · needs science`). A promoted prototype takes the
  row's build button and drops its duplicate grouped row; other prototypes keep
  their grouped `↳` rows and disabled/built markers exactly as 0.4.11/0.4.14
  contract them. Promoted prototype buttons carry the stat note in their title.
- Recalibrated the stale expectations the red run had hidden: dom_smoke's
  catapult hull summary (2 seeded points, per career_bench) and its
  away-from-HQ accept check (fund the fee it claims to cover), career_bench's
  rung-cost pin for a tier-2.22 catapult record (6.88 → 8.88 on the new
  ladder), and the README/career_bot_bench price notes (EMC-1 4.50/9.00 → 3.00,
  EMC-2 18.00 → 8.00).
- `career_departure_test` now asserts per blocked planet (not just the fixture)
  that the starter purse alone cannot leave; `experiments/debug_career_costs.js`
  keeps the kit/cash probe and the 100-seed sweep for future balance passes.
