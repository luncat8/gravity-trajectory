# 0.4.42 plan — build cost from research, not from rungs

## Goal

Gear and parts stop having a price ladder. Each record's build cost is a
continuous function of its spec: a fixed share of the research work it takes to
invent that spec from the initial point. A catapult, a battery or an engine
priced by the same rule as the research fee that unlocks it.

## Model

- **Initial point.** The origin of each research family: nothing known. The
  build basis is the fee of inventing the spec against an origin-only hull.
  Measured from there, every axis is a climb, so the frontier term (the
  surcharge beyond the researched range) is charged as well as the distance.
  That is what makes a from-scratch climb several times one recombination hop.
- **Share.** `BUILD_SHARE` = 10 % of that work, rounded to cents. The share is
  one constant, so the families differ only through their research scales
  (`METRIC_SCALE`, `FRONTIER`, `MERIT`, `TIER_SCALE`), which are the existing
  research balance.
- **Waste is cheap to build.** A waste axis (mass, leak, efficiency loss) adds
  no distance from the origin, and its merit falls as the waste rises, so a
  heavier or leakier spec of the same capability costs less.
- **Tier is an ordinal.** `tier` still sorts the shop, gates upgrades and sets
  the isp interpolation. It sets no price.
- **Families.** Catapult, battery, engine and tank keep their science families.
  Panel (kW) and hold (t) gain merit and frontier rows so every gear and part
  follows the rule. Helpers (candidate counts) have no research family and keep
  their fixed prices.
- **Commercial rail.** The flat `commercialFee` (3.00 a launch) is an operating
  fee, not a build cost, and is unchanged.

## Acceptance

- Every stock record with a research family has a build cost equal to
  `BUILD_SHARE` × its origin-based research work, and a prototype's cost comes
  from its spec with no rung interpolation.
- Starter-blocked planets stay blocked at the 6.00 starter purse (302 of 302 on
  seeds 1..100 in `debug_career_costs.js`), and EMC-2 stays above 7.74.
- Holds stay affordable from the starter purse (HOLD-24 under 6.00).
- Existing bench, DOM, departure, guidance, bot and game suites pass after the
  pins move to the spec rule.

## Scope boundary

Balancing the families against one another (for example EMC against batteries
and engines) is a research-scale question and is reported, not tuned here.
Removing `tier` from the shop order and upgrade rule is a later step.
