# 0.1.3 — grand tour, coplanar moons & belts, adaptive 60 fps trajectory

Standalone specification of the grand-tour gameplay loop, visual Keplerian moon
and ring/belt systems, continuous in-flight prediction, and help drawer UI.

## 1. static planet launch base across all modes

- Planets (`0 … PLANETS - 1`) are always the physical launch bases and solid
  landing targets, regardless of whether gravity comes from `planets` (static)
  or `ports · moving` (orbiting trade stations).
- `GravitySim.setPlanets(count, px, py, pr, orbitRX, orbitRY, tiltCos, tiltSin)`
  stores the planet cores and precomputes rotated ellipse bounds and inverse
  semi-axes (`1 / rx`, `1 / ry`, `max(rx, ry)²`).
- `spawn()` launches from `(planetX[origin], planetY[origin])` at distance
  `planetR[origin] + SPAWN_GAP` along the aim vector with speed `speed`, never
  inheriting moving station velocity.
- `impact()` tests collisions against all static planet cores.
- Per-port gravity strengths in `ports · moving` mode are keyed by parent
  `portPlanet[p]` so colony founding or `removePort` compaction preserves every
  port's edited strength.

## 2. grand tour goal, visited systems & staged hops

- **Planet system region**: a point `(x, y)` lies inside planet `p`'s system iff
  it lies inside `p`'s rotated port-orbit ellipse:

  ```
  u = (dx · cos θ + dy · sin θ) / orbitRX[p]
  v = (-dx · sin θ + dy · cos θ) / orbitRY[p]
  u² + v² <= 1
  ```

- **Visited tracking**: `visited[p]` (`Uint8Array`) marks every system entered
  during live flight (`launch()` and every fixed `DT` step in `step()`). Marks
  persist across launches until `clear` resets the run.
- **Predicted visits**: `traceProbe()` runs the identical `markSystemsAt()` test
  into `predVisit[]` and reports both total systems along the predicted path
  (`predVisits`) and newly visited systems (`predNewVisits`). Both the grip
  badge and HUD update immediately whenever the trajectory changes.
- **Hop rule**: initially `landedBody = -1` (any planet may launch). Landing on
  planet `b` (`END_IMPACT`) sets `landedBody = b`. Thereafter only `b` can arm a
  launch (`canLaunchFrom(planet)`).
- **Visual markers**:
  - visited systems show a persistent mint ring on the planet and port ellipse;
  - newly predicted systems highlight their port ellipse in dashed gold;
  - `landedBody` shows a pulsing launch-pad beacon and cardinal ticks;
  - hovering or attempting to drag any non-landed planet renders a lock tooltip
    with an inline directional arrow plus a dashed world-space arrow vector
    pointing straight to `(planetX[landedBody], planetY[landedBody])`.

## 3. continuous trajectory while flying & adaptive 60 fps cadence

- Pre-launch `predict()` respects the user `horizon` slider (`5 … 60 s`).
- Once launched (`STATUS_FLYING`), `predictFlight(budgetMs)` traces ahead from
  `(cargo.x, cargo.y, cargo.vx, cargo.vy)` on the exact `liveSteps % stride`
  field cadence up to `FLY_HORIZON = 240 s`, saving and restoring `stepX/stepY`
  in preallocated buffers, and `step()` continues until `END_IMPACT` or
  `END_EXIT` without a time cap.
- `trailX/trailY` compact `2:1` in place when full (`TRAIL_MAX = 8192`), doubling
  `trailStride` so arbitrarily long flights keep an unbroken trail with zero
  heap allocation.
- Frame intervals smooth into `emaFrameMs` (ignoring tab-switch gaps `> 120 ms`).
  While `emaFrameMs <= 17.5 ms` (~60 fps), moving fields and in-flight
  predictions update every animation frame; if frame time exceeds `17.5 ms`,
  auto-recompute steps down (`interval = 2` or `4`) and tightens `budgetMs` so
  prediction never pulls frame rate below 60 fps.

## 4. visual secondary moons & belts

- Purely visual background bodies in `orbit_view.js` keyed from an independent
  seed stream (`sim.seed ^ 0x51a7`), with zero effect on trade or cargo physics.
- All moons and ring/belt particles of planet `p` share `p`'s orbital plane:
  major-axis orientation `Ω_p`, inclination `cos i_p = B_p / A_p`, prograde
  spin `sign(orbitOmega[p])`, and aligned apocenter (`ω_p = 0`).
- Mean anomaly advances incrementally per frame by Kepler's third law scaled by
  planet gravity:

  ```
  M += dir · K_vis · sqrt(|g_p|) · a^(-3/2) · dt
  ```

  and solves `E - e · sin E = M` so particles move slower at apocenter and faster
  at pericenter.
- Rendered in two passes (`z < 0` before `drawPlanets()`, `z >= 0` after
  `drawPlanets()`) for natural 3D limb occlusion on Saturn-like double rings and
  transiting moons.

## 5. help drawer GUI & passenger role

- Slide-out `#helpDrawer` toggled via `? help`, `close ×`, `h`/`?`, or `Escape`.
- Startup randomly assigns the passenger role between `tourist` and
  `emperor’s emissary` (with a `reroll` button in the drawer) and explains the
  grand-tour objective, hop rules, and controls.
