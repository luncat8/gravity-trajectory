# 0.4.2 Worklog — Career rule: purse, task board, evacuation, cheats, launch gate

## Review of 0.4.1

0.4.1 shipped the pure modules (`orbit_tech.js`, `orbit_science.js`,
`career_bench.js`) and suggested exactly this slice: the `career` rule in
`gravity_game.js` plus launch gating in `gravity_sim.js`. Issues found while
reading it before starting:

- both IIFEs were invoked with `this`, which is `undefined` in strict mode
  inside a classic script → the browser would never have seen `root.OrbitTech`
  (the node path masked it). Now `globalThis`.
- `orbit_tech.js` was `var` style with string ids everywhere (`'EMC-1'`),
  forcing a lookup per call and a `gearBuildCost`/`applyBulkUpgrade` pair that
  duplicated what the career state has to do anyway; `maxForce` was a dead
  field; `impulseDeltaV` divided fuel in kg by a mass in t.
- the probe behind the launch band: at seed 42 / t=420 the share of shots that
  land on *another* planet peaks at 90–93 % for 90–110 px/s (exit < 5 %) and
  falls to 51 % at 160 and 23 % at 200 px/s. The catapult numbers are tuned to
  that band, not to `SPEED_MAX`.

## Implementation

### 1. `orbit_tech.js` (rewritten to records)

Tables are arrays of records `{ id, tier, …, buildCost }` (`CATAPULTS` EMC-1…5,
`PANELS` SP-1…4, `BATTERIES` BAT-1…5, `HOLDS`, `ENGINES`, `TANKS`, `HELPERS`);
callers hold the record, no id lookups in hot paths. Launch model:

- `launchEnergy(massT, catapult, speed) = KWH_PER_T_V2 · m · v² / efficiency`
- `maxSpeed(massT, catapult) = √(2 · maxForce · RAIL_M / m)` (rail force cap;
  `RAIL_M = 2800` so EMC-5 reaches the sim's top speed dry while EMC-1 binds on
  loaded ships)
- `speedLimit(chargeKwh, massT, catapult)` = min of the energy and force caps;
  `canLaunch(...)`. EMC-1 + BAT-1 lifts the 10 t dry ship to ~111 px/s and
  stalls a 38 t load below 60 px/s.
- `solarCharge(panel, light, dtSec)`, `batteryLeak(battery, charge, dtYears)`,
  `upgrades(pad, kind, item)` (planet gear only goes up a tier),
  `bulkUpgradeCost(pads, kind, item)`, `starterGrant()`, `copySpec()`.

### 2. `gravity_sim.js` — launch gate

`setLaunchNetwork(pads, ship)` attaches the career's pad array and ship record
(`null` = lab/other rules, nothing changes). With a network:

- `canLaunchFrom(planet)` requires the vessel to sit on that planet
  (`landedBody === planet`) **and** a built catapult;
- `launchEnergy / speedLimit / canFire(planet, speed)` expose the pad's
  energy and force caps (`SPEED_MAX` / `true` without a network);
- `relocate(planet)` puts the idle vessel on a planet (career start,
  evacuation) keeping visits.

Because the GUI's `canAimFrom` already asked `canLaunchFrom`, a drag from a
planet without a catapult is refused by the existing hop-lock visuals.

### 3. `gravity_game.js` — the `career` rule

- `RULES.career` (`solo: true`; every rule carries `solo` now), `CHEATS`
  (`communism`, `endgame`); `encodeLink`/`decodeLink` carry `&cheat=` only for
  career, reject it elsewhere, and reject shots on a career link (careers are
  not replays).
- ledger: `fee` field, `openFlight(ledger, sim, fee)`; `beginFlight` wraps it
  with `MONEY.cost`, career launches with fee 0 (the pad battery pays).
- career record: `ledger` (shared purse), `hq`, `at`, `stranded`, `pads[26]`
  (`{catapult, panel, battery, chargeKwh}`), `padCount`, per-planet `light`
  (floor 0.25 · star luminosity), `ship {hold, engine, tank, cargoTons, mass}`,
  `owned`, `tasks[5]` (4 delivery slots + 1 competition slot), `contract`,
  counters, `lastEnergy`, `lastReward`, `ended`.
- `startCareer(career, sim, seed, trade, cheat)`: purse 6 (endgame 99), HQ =
  seeded pick among planets with `planetLight ≥ 0.5`, starter pad
  (EMC-1/SP-1/BAT-1 charged), ship on HQ via `sim.relocate`; board filled.
- building: `buildGear(career, planet, kind, item)` (first catapult brings a
  charged SP-1/BAT-1; panel/battery need a pad; upgrades only; unstrands when
  built under the ship), `bulkUpgradeCost/bulkUpgrade` (all built facilities
  at once, total shown first), `installPart` (HQ only, paid once, hold must
  hold the cargo).
- task board (`refreshBoard`): deliveries pair a colony with a real export
  (`OrbitEcon.bestExport`) with the colony of greatest `goodShortfall` (else
  lowest `marketSupply`); reward `0.5 + tons · 0.06 · (1 + scarcity) ·
  (1 + dist/900)` capped at 4.5 < evacuation fee 5, 240 s deadline;
  competitions (1/3 chance per refresh) pay 2–4 for one landing flight of
  ≥ 1500–3000 px from a given planet within 180 s. Draws come from
  `hashBits(seed, serial, salt)` → the board is a pure function of
  (seed, world, clock).
- flights: `careerBeginFlight` (pad charge −= energy), `careerAdvance(career,
  rate, steps)` (solar minus leak on the world clock for every built pad),
  `careerEndFlight` (settle landing: cargo pickup / delivery pay / competition
  pay, strand without catapult, **lost in space** on exit or time cap, board
  refresh, end check).
- `evacuate` (fee 5, ship back on HQ, cargo rides along). `checkEnded`:
  broke when the purse is negative or the stranded ship cannot pay any way out
  (cheapest catapult under it, or the evacuation; only the evacuation when
  lost); complete when all 26 pads exist and no cheat is on.
- `compareScores('career')` orders by purse like `money`.

### 4. `gravity_bot.js`

`reset` honours `RULES[rule].solo`: level forced `off`, YOU stays a user.

### 5. GUI glue (`gravity_gui.js`, `index.html`)

`orbit_tech.js` loads before `gravity_sim.js`. No career radio yet: the rule is
reachable through `?rule=career[&cheat=…]` and `#game=…&rule=career`. Wired:
`RULE_HELP.career`; `game.cheat`/`game.career`; `startGame` attaches the
network (and forces bot off / user for solo rules) or detaches it; `stopGame`
detaches; `fire` → `careerBeginFlight`; clock step → `careerAdvance`; flight
end → `careerEndFlight`; `launch()` asks `sim.canFire`; the launch button says
`pad charge … kWh · max … px/s`, `stranded · build a catapult or evacuate`,
`lost in space · evacuate`, `broke · career over`; status/rule tag
(`career · 1 / 26 pads`); purse toast shows `pad −x.xx kWh` and `task +r`
instead of `cost −1.00`; replay links carry `cheat`; `GravityScene.career`
exported for the smoke test and console use.

### 6. Solo clock stall (pre-existing, found by `game_bench`)

`game_bench` never finished: its scripted solo tour enters the 26th system
mid-flight, and `clockDriverFlying()` then stopped counting the human's own
flight as a clock driver (`hasLiveUser()` false, slot 0 not a bot, no bot
rivals flying) — the vessel hung "in flight · clock runs" forever. The same
happens in a solo `money` run the moment the launch fee takes the purse below
1.00 (reproduced on clean `main`, probe: 1.27 → 0.27, `liveSteps 0`). With
rivals on, their flights masked it. `clockDriverFlying()` is now: a human
flight (slot 0 or a user rival) always drives the clock; bot flights drive it
only once no human can still act. `dom_smoke` gained a solo money run whose
broke flight must land.

## Balance notes

- rail length raised from 1800 m to 2800 m: with 1800 m the fully fitted
  endgame ship (17.9 t) capped at 190 px/s on EMC-5; now EMC-5 lifts it to
  ~237 px/s and the 24 t delivery (41.9 t) to ~155 px/s, while EMC-1/BAT-1
  stays in the 80–120 px/s landing band dry and stalls loaded.
- the evacuation fee (5) exceeds every delivery reward (≤ 4.5) and the
  cheapest catapult (3) — a catapult-less delivery is never profitable; the
  bench checks it across 5 seeds × 4 offers.
