## The report: "collection happens on the last encounter"

It does not. A headless career flight (seed 42, 12 248 steps, 43 payouts) pays
at the moment of each **entry**: step 519 `+0.2646` on P1, step 713 `+0.2738`
on P5, … and a later pass through a system pays only what regrew since the
previous one (P24 `+0.3162` then `+0.0320`). `stepHarvest` runs every step of
every flight, for the player and for every rival, so a long flight already
collects from the same system several times. Nothing to move — the defect was
in the drawing.

## What was wrong with the render

`drawPlanetProfits` lit a pill from `sim.predLog*` only: the traced path
*ahead*. The trace starts at the ship, so a system being harvested has already
left that log, and the pill went dark exactly when the money arrived. The only
thing that could clear a highlight was the next re-trace — every frame at
60 fps, every 2nd–4th frame when slower, and truncated mid-path on a slow
frame, which is the "unhighlight does not happen" the report describes.

The highlight now has two sources: the traced path ahead (unchanged) and, while
a ship flies, the ledger's live `inside` flags — set by the step that flies the
ship in, cleared by the step that flies it out, so the drop is on exit and
never waits for a trace. A system the ship is inside gets a brighter plate and
white text; a system the path will reach keeps the old green. The mask is
exposed as `harvestMarks()` for the smoke test.

## The stay-pay experiment

`experimental · stay-pay · per tick`, career only (the box is dark in every
other mode and springs back if anything ticks it). With it on,
`GravityGame.stepHarvest` also calls `harvestStay`: every step inside a system
pays what regrew since the last stamp, on top of the entry lump. It is world
state like the scales, so a career link carries `&ht=1` and a reload restores
it. Career only means every ledger harvesting in career — yours and the rival
companies', which share the market.

Two deliberate omissions in `harvestStay`: no `market.stamp` bump (that one
invalidates a rival's sliced plan, and a drip on every step would leave no plan
ever finishing) and no `shotVisits` (lingering in a system is not another
encounter). The stamp question is deferred, not solved.

The systems a ship launches from are inside already, so their stored value —
pad time included — is collected by the first flight step. The alternative,
stamping them at launch, was implemented first and reverted: it deletes that
value from the shared market, which showed up as the mode paying *less* than
the entry rule on three of the bench flights (`−0.25` on the worst). The
numbers and the open alternative are in `0.4.30-plan-harvest-ticks.md`.

Because the traced estimate still prices the entry rule, the HUD profit line
and both aim lines say `≥ticks` while the box is on: the shot pays at least
what they show.
