### 3. Is `archive/temp/saved_maps.js` enough to recreate the map?

**Yes — bit-for-bit for this run.** Entry `10` (`seed 93293 · 13 planets · 3 seats`) stores `seed`, `universe: 13`, `seats: [11, 2, 7]`, `targetPay: 1.175128135558583`, `rule: "career"`, `bot: "normal"`, `player: "user"`, `cheat: null`, `rate: 0.5`, `gs: 1, fs: 1, ws: 1, ht: false`, and boots to the exact same step-0 snapshot as `replay.txt` and `?rule=career&seed=93293&rate=0.5`.

Minor format differences worth knowing in general:
- A `kind: "start"` record stores `universe` count (prefix mask) rather than an arbitrary `mask`, and omits `source` (defaults to planets `0`), `corruption` toggle (defaults to `false`), and per-rival human/bot overrides — all of which matched defaults in your game.
- The in-game **Save map** button saves your live orbit clock (`rate: 0.5`) and omits `note`/`stats`, whereas `experiments/make_saved_maps.js` writes `rate: 1` plus `note` and `stats` (`rate` doesn't affect opening positions, gravities, colonies, or seat selection — only how fast planets orbit during flights).
