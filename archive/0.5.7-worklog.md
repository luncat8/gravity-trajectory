# 0.5.7 — play area 1.5×, zoom frames, bot impulse, heavy-spread potential

## Review of the previous change (0.5.6)

The pad-gear change left two loose ends that this round had to touch anyway:

- every sim ended a flight 12 px outside the 1600×1000 planet field, so the
  framed canvas *was* the game: a shot that missed everything was lost at the
  edge of the screen, and no flight could ever be watched outside the frame;
- `careerBurnPlan` existed but nothing called it. Rivals and the page's own bot
  flew every shot ballistically and the page never fitted an engine, so the
  impulse drive researched in the science tab was dead weight.

## Changes

- **One play area** (`orbit_world.js`, `orbit_sim.js`, `gravity_sim.js`):
  `AREA_SCALE = 1.5` derives `AREA_W/H = 2400×1500` and `AREA_PAD_X/Y = 400/250`.
  `GravitySim` derives its bounds from that ratio (`padX/padY` per instance,
  `outside()`, `burnLimit()` gating) and `orbit_sim.js` re-exports the same
  numbers as `Sim.*`, so the live flight, every prediction, the bot probes and
  the benches read one setting. Planets keep their positions: seeds and orbits
  are unchanged.
- **Zoom frames** (`orbit_view.js`, `gui_view.js`, `gravity_gui.js`,
  `index.html`, `gui_tables.js`): `FRAME_MAIN` frames the planet field,
  `FRAME_FULL` the whole play area; `z` toggles them, a radio row in the `view`
  group selects them, and `resize()` picks `FIELD_BOX` or `AREA_BOX`. A frame
  change invalidates the field renderer and the HUD.
- **Bot impulse** (`gravity_bot.js`, `gui_game.js`, `experiments/career_bot_bench.js`):
  `careerFlightBurn` gives each flying actor one early nudge (0.2 s into the
  flight) and rescues afterwards, at most two burns per flight, never in the
  last 0.1 s. The search is a 12-angle ring at two Δv rungs plus the contract
  bearing and its opposite, then a fine fan around the winner — ≈40 traces
  instead of the old ≈100-angle fan. `careerBurnGain = 2` is the nudge margin:
  the landing score is partly an estimate of a pad's future earnings, so a
  small modeled gain is noise. The page's own bot company only *plans*
  (`careerBotBurn` in `gui_game.js` applies the burn, records it into the live
  shot at its step and says so in the drawer); rivals apply their own burns in
  `stepCareer`. Burns are once again a real choice: measured on the two
  research worlds, 14 and 15 burns per career.
- **Heavy-spread potential** (`gravity_field.js`): the potential view keeps its
  palette (blue negative, green positive) and now paints the red wash the
  legend always promised: `smoothstep(4, 64, g²)` mixed at 0.65 over the
  potential colour, i.e. red from the legend's `g ≥ 2` line, solid at
  `g ≥ 8` — the halo's size *is* how far a heavy planet reaches. The shader
  pays one `dot` and one mix; the 1/4-res fallback accumulates the pull beside
  `φ` (two extra multiply-adds per body per pixel) and `samplePotential` takes
  the squared pull, so shader, raster and tests agree.
- **Tests**: `career_bot_bench` gains three checks (a flight burns once, spends
  tank fuel, logs the burn; `rescueOnly` costs one coast trace and leaves a
  healthy flight alone; a relaunch resets the burn budget), `dom_smoke` flies a
  bot-owned career company until the page records its burn and checks the
  notice, `gravity_view_test` pins the red spread, `gravity_bench` times the
  potential fallback raster, `career_bench` reads the pad draw at launch (a long
  coast recharges the battery to full), `game_bench` re-pins the 60 s money tour
  for the wider area. `dom_smoke`'s career-wait aim check now measures the live
  pad limit: a rival guest queued on your rail claims charge, so the range can
  shrink as well as grow.

## Validation

- `career_bot_bench`, 33 checks OK. Research careers (`BURN_GAIN=2`, the
  default): hostile world `g1.4 f0.6` purse 18.11 · earned 1.80 · 14 flights ·
  1 delivery · 14 burns; friendly world `g0.8 f1.5` purse 70.73 · earned 14.02 ·
  17 flights · 8 deliveries · 15 burns.
- Burn A/B on the same seeds (`career_bot_bench`, purse / earned / flights /
  burns): `NOBURN` 18.94 / 2.98 / 38 / 0 · 21.09 / 11.81 / 27 / 0;
  rescue-only (`BURN_GAIN=1e9`) 18.73 / 2.98 / 38 / 1 · 21.09 / 11.81 / 27 / 0;
  `BURN_GAIN=0.75` 10.83 / 1.80 / 26 / 25 · 65.35 / 12.88 / 19 / 17; `BURN_GAIN=2`
  18.11 / 1.80 / 14 / 14 · 70.73 / 14.02 / 17 / 15. Coasting is only marginally
  cheaper than the nudge on the hostile world and much worse on the friendly
  one; a 0.75 bar nudges every flight and loses money there.
- `gravity_bench` OK: play area `1600×1000 ×1.5 = 2400×1500 (pad 400/250)`, a
  coasting ship exits at x −403.3; prediction sweep worst 8.137 ms (10 ms cap);
  `ends: open 0, impact 3696, exit 1841, horizon 79`; field raster 1/4 11.57 ms;
  potential raster 1/4 17.68 ms (24 ms budget) — the potential fallback is the
  dearer of the two because it accumulates the pull as well.
- `gravity_view_test` OK, potential parity max diff 2/255, and a well under a
  `g = 10` pull is red-dominant while a shallow pull keeps its blue/green sign.
- `dom_smoke` OK; the new career block records a bot burn at step 120
  (Δv 9.1 px/s, fuel 44.9 kg) and the drawer notice `bot burn Δv … → P8`.
- `game_bench` OK (60 s money tour re-pinned to 7 shots / 9.0622 — the wider
  area lets a greedy tour reach planets the old edge cut off), `career_bench`
  OK, `career_gravity_bench` OK (24 rival careers, 167 flights, 82 pads, 0 dead
  pads, mean reach 4.04), `bot_bench`, `colony_bench`, `route_bench`,
  `gravity_seed_test`, `career_departure_test`, `career_guidance_test`,
  `balance_lab_test` (3601), `balance_lab_smoke` (31) OK.
- `node --check` on every touched `.js`.
