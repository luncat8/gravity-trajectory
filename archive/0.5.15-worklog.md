# 0.5.15 worklog — one landing model for aim line, launch list, helpers and bots

Follows `archive/0.5.14-worklog.md` (helpers count taken quests as profit, plus
a quest scan). The user's review of that stage: the quest ranking is still
oriented on the medic quest and lives beside the money ranking, while the bots
price the same landing on their own. So the ask is architecture, not a third
helper — one set of factors, every consumer.

## Review of 0.5.14 (what was wrong, and what this stage fixed)

- **The quest button was never wired.** `gravity_gui.js` declared
  `helperQuestBtn` and `gui_helper.js` styled it, but no click listener ever
  called `helperStartQuest`, so the new helper could not be started from the
  page at all. The stage's own test passed because it only asserted that the
  scan *finished*: a dead button finishes instantly. Fixed (one listener) and
  the test now requires the scan to be active right after the click.
- **The finish notice printed the ranking score.** `helperTick` read
  `best.money`, which nothing ever set, so the `quest shot · P7 · +2.00` line
  the worklog advertised came out as the ×100 quest score (`+1280.00`);
  `helperLastMoney` was written and never read. The best candidate (and the
  launch-book seed) now carry `money` and `leg`, and the notice prints purse
  money plus the leg (`· patients`).
- **The aim readout ignored the medical quest.** `refreshPredGain` had its own
  copy that added a loaded delivery's reward and the competition but not the
  per-patient payout, so aiming patients home showed no money while the landing
  paid it. It reads the rule's table now.
- **Four pricers, three answers.** Delivery, medical, competition, fee and exit
  were copied into `gui_predict`, `moneyFacts`, `helperQuestScore` and the bot's
  `careerScoreFacts`. A delivery load leg was worth `0.5` of its reward to the
  quest scan and `0.35` to the bot; a medic pickup leg `0.25` vs `0.35`; the
  quest scan had no delivery deadline. This stage removes three of the four
  copies (see the plan artifact).

## Steps

1. **`gravity_game.js` — the landing table.** `careerLandingFacts(out, career,
   origin, body, flownLen, length, steps, runStep, visitPay)` fills caller
   scratch with `cash` / `credit` / `leg`; `LEG_LOAD`, `LEG_DELIVER`,
   `LEG_MEDICS`, `LEG_PATIENTS`, `LEG_COMPETE`, `LEG_NONE` and
   `LEG_CREDIT = 0.35` are exported. Every contract leg is gated on the
   contract deadline — an expired contract is dropped by the board before the
   ship arrives, so it pays nothing (the aim readout already checked this for
   deliveries; the bot, the launch list and the quest scan did not, and now all
   four agree).
2. **`gravity_game.js` — the goal list.** `careerGoalBodies(career)` returns the
   next stop of whatever contract is open (delivery: load at `from`, deliver at
   `to`; medical: out to the rebellion, patients home to the Capital) plus the
   competition planet, on one scratch list. The scans aim at all of it, the bots
   route to the first entry; neither has a contract-kind branch any more.
3. **`gravity_bot.js`** — `careerScoreFacts` calls the table (`taskPay = cash +
   credit`, `cashPay = cash`), `careerTarget` reads `careerGoalBodies`, and
   `CAREER_PICKUP_VALUE` disappears (it was the rule's `LEG_CREDIT`).
4. **`gui_book.js`** — `moneyFacts` calls the table and exposes `credit`/`leg`
   on the shared `shotMoney` scratch, so `traceMoney`, `storedMoney`, the launch
   list and the helper scans all price from it.
5. **`gui_predict.js`** — `refreshPredGain` calls the table with the aim's origin
   and flown length, which brings the medical payout into the aim readout and
   keeps the launch-list net untouched (fee, fuel and exit are still added on
   top by the economics paths).
6. **`gui_helper.js`** — `helperQuestScore` is gone. `HELPER_GOALS` is the whole
   ranking law (`credit` weight, `builds` gate, notice label, empty line);
   `helperScore` returns `net + credit × goal.credit`; `helperStartCareer` is the
   single guard/build/seed path behind `best profit`, `profit + EMC` and
   `quest scan`; `helperMakeQuest` aims at `careerGoalBodies`; the launch-book
   seed is priced on the button's own goal (`helperBookSeed(..., creditWeight)`).
   The untargeted `quick scan`/`scan here` in career mode weigh the same cash and
   credit instead of their own task-kind bonuses, so a taken leg is no longer
   invisible without a goal.
7. **Wiring and copy** — `helperQuest` gets its click listener; `index.html`,
   the tooltips and the README helper row describe cash + credit instead of
   "quest value first".
8. **Tests** — `experiments/career_guidance_test.js` rebuilds the quest section
   around the table: every leg's cash/credit/name, the deadline gate, the
   bot-vs-rule agreement on one landing, the goal list for five board states,
   the aim readout pricing patients home, the quest button running a real scan
   inside the frame guard, its notice printing cash, and the empty line.
9. **Measurement** — `experiments/medic_offer_probe.js` replaces the guess from
   the 0.5.13 worklog; results in `experiments/logs/0.5.15-helper-factors.txt`.

## Measured finding — the medic bottleneck is not the slot

The 0.5.13 worklog blamed the single `MEDIC_SLOT` offer for "2 medic trips for
23 rebellions" at 1200 s. Measured on the same worlds (6 seeds, 12 rival-games,
`experiments/medic_offer_probe.js`):

- 24 rebellions, 1334 offer-seconds, but only 70 s standing on the Capital with
  no contract — the offer is *already* a standing offer while another contract
  runs, so a second slot would add offers the rivals cannot accept either.
- the offer does not exist at all for a rival without a cargo hold: `makeMedic`
  refuses while `medicLoad` is 0, and 4 of the 6 seeds end with both rivals still
  on HOLD-0. Deliveries (4 t+) are closed to them as well.
- the reason is a *first shop* gap: `careerBetweenFlights` runs on landing, on a
  sit and on a no-shot only. A seed-3 run called it 91 times and never once with
  the ship on its own HQ — rivals stand on the HQ only before their first
  launch, and nothing kits the company out at start. Across the sample they
  spend 422 s of 1200 s on their own HQ with no hold and enough purse for
  HOLD-8 (0.76) + the 3.00 reserve.

## Next step suggestion

**Let a company kit itself out at start** (`0.5.16-plan-rival-first-shop.md`):
one between-flight shop in `startCareers`, before the first launch, buys the
8 t hold and the opening claims; then measure the same three horizons against
0.5.13's numbers (2400 s earned 63.03, 1200 s 36.19, 400 s 15.96) because every
rival's opening changes. The rebellion shape gap (13 vs 24 pads) is still open
and still needs the reclaim rules change; it is easier to judge once rivals
actually carry cargo.
