## Economic model — revised (per user)

The old "cash-out only at HQ" idea was **rejected**. New model:

- HQ is just the starting planet; **no special HQ cash-out**. Money earned
  directly from completed tasks.
- A planet can only be **launched from** if it has a built EM catapult.
- Catapult, solar panel, battery are **planet equipment**: built once per
  planet, shared by every visitor, persisting between visits. They do **not**
  add to ship mass.
- Landing on a planet **without** a catapult → must call an **evacuation**,
  an automatic expensive return to HQ; the fee is high enough that any
  delivery to a catapult-less planet is unprofitable. Core progression gate:
  earn money → build catapult on target planet → land and launch with profit
  (if catapult power is enough to continue/return).
- Player can build catapult/panel/battery on any planet anytime if they have
  the money.
- **GUI bulk upgrade**: applying a selected upgrade tier upgrades **all**
  already-built facilities of that kind across planets at once; GUI shows the
  total cost before confirming.

Only ship parts add to ship mass: dry mass, cargo hold (empty + cargo),
impulse engine, fuel tank.

## Implementation

### 1. `orbit_tech.js` (rewritten)

Static tables + pure math, node-safe, module.exports guard.

- Planet gear tables: `CATAPULTS EMC-1..5` (maxForce 45→180 kN, efficiency
  0.62→0.88, buildCost 3→20), `PANELS SP-1..4` (power 60→420 W),
  `BATTERIES BAT-1..5` (capacity 1.5→16 kWh, leak).
- Ship parts tables: `HOLDS HOLD-0/8/24`, `ENGINES IMP-1..3`, `TANKS FT-0..3`,
  `HELPERS HELP-0..2`.
- `shipMass(parts, cargoTons)` = dry 10 t + hold.emptyMass + engine.mass +
  tank.emptyMass + cargo. Planet gear excluded.
- `launchEnergy(mass, catapult, speed) = k*m*v^2/eff`, k = 7.5e-6: 10 t dry
  at 100 m/s ≈ 0.75 kWh (inside BAT-1); loaded 38 t ≈ 2.85 kWh (blocked on
  EMC-1/BAT-1, OK on EMC-5/BAT-5).
- `canLaunch(chargeKwh, ...)`, `solarCharge(panel, light, dt)` (W*s -> kWh,
  planetLight × star lum), `batteryLeak`, `impulseDeltaV` (rocket equation).
- Planet-gear economics: `gearBuildCost(kind, id)`,
  `bulkUpgradeCost(planets, kind, toId)` (sum to upgrade all already-built
  facilities of a kind), `applyBulkUpgrade(planets, kind, toId)` → `{planets,
  cost}` (pure, non-mutating).
- `copySpec(kind, id)` → `tech: KIND ID { … }` clipboard line for the LLM
  balance loop.
- `starterGrant()` (purse 3.00, ship hold HOLD-0/engine null/tank FT-0,
  start planet EMC-1/SP-1/BAT-1 with 1.5 kWh charge).

### 2. `orbit_science.js` (unchanged from first phase)

Knowledge volume: N-D convex hull of discovered tech points per family.
`preview(family, params)` projects onto the hull via projected gradient with
simplex projection; inside = cheap base price, outside = base + `K_DIST*dist`.
`accept` adds a vertex, expanding the hull. Reproduces the brief's triangle
example.

### 3. `experiments/career_bench.js` (rewritten)

14 checks, all OK: dry-ship launchable; 24 t + HOLD-24 blocked on EMC-1,
launchable on EMC-5; efficiency cuts energy; impulse Δv needs engine+fuel;

solar W*s→kWh, dim world charges slowly; battery leak; evacuation fee makes
catapult-less delivery unprofitable; bulk upgrade cost sums and applies
correctly (pure); copySpec format; starter grant.
