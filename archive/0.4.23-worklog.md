# 0.4.23 worklog — launch book, patient career bot, profit helpers (review)

Plan: `0.4.23-plan-launch-book.md`. This file records the review of that stage,
which arrived as a single commit with no worklog.

## Review of the delivered implementation

- `gravity_game.js`: `createLaunchBook` / `recordLaunch` / `bookReady` /
  `launchBookTag` with `BOOK_MAX = 10`, `BOOK_LOG_MAX = 64`. A book is per
  career, tagged by the field (static/moving, strengths, gravity/force scales),
  dedupes by origin + landing + visited-planet mask, keeps the faster arrival,
  and stores the visit log so `estimateProfitLog` re-prices it at the live
  market. Restart clears the book.
- `gravity_bot.js`: `careerPlan` tries stored entries before the fan, reuses a
  shot scoring `CAREER_BOOK_ENOUGH` without tracing, and saves a fan winner.
  `careerShouldWait` sits out a losing plan that cannot claim a pad.
- `gravity_gui.js`: launch list in the career drawer (loop / EMC / pad rows,
  "use" arms a saved aim, off when gravity bodies move), two profit helper
  scans (`best profit`, `profit + EMC`), kWh launch label.

Validated as delivered: `career_bench` (book dedupe, prune, tag, restart),
`career_bot_bench` (stored score == fresh score, book answers without a trace,
stale speed falls back to the fan, patience expires), `dom_smoke` (list,
helpers, moving-field switch, kWh label) — all pass on the reviewed commit.

## Issues found by the review, fixed in 0.4.24

- `careerPrice(career, CAREER.evacFee)` priced a number as if it were a record:
  `undefined` made every rival evacuation look unaffordable. A rival that left
  the map (`at = -1`) or sat on an unliftable rail could stay grounded forever
  while `checkEnded` still saw a solvent purse.
- A rival task board aged only on landing. An accepted contract whose target
  was unreachable rejected every fan candidate and never expired.
- A queued turret reservation survived a finished career (all 26 pads), so that
  pad stayed short of charge for the player and other companies.
- A commercial fallback kept `commercialPlanet` set across a deliberate sit, so
  the next attempt paid the fee again even after the company's own pad charged.
- `profit + EMC` scanned 120 s but the armed preview used a 60 s slider; a
  third of runs armed a shot whose canvas preview reported "time" instead of a
  landing. The 0.4.23 smoke assertion re-predicted with the short horizon and
  was therefore flaky.

validation numbers.
