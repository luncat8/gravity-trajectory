## changes

`gravity_game.js`:

- `createCareer` gains `visitIncome`, `flownPx`, `feesPaid`, `buildSpent`,
  `sciSpent`; `resetCareer` zeroes them.
- `careerEndFlight`: `visitIncome += endFlight(ledger)` (the flight's visit
  income) and `flownPx += sim.flightLength` on every finished flight.
- `careerBeginFlight`: `feesPaid += fee` (commercial rail fee or rival guest
  tax; own and Capital pads stay free).
- `buildGear` / `bulkUpgrade`: `buildSpent +=` the purse debit; `installPart`
  and `researchPoint` add `buildSpent` / `sciSpent` beside their debits, so
  every purse movement is mirrored by exactly one statistic field.
- New exported getters:
  - `careerStats(out, career)` — `income` (visits + contracts + bribes +
    guest tax earned), `running` (fees + fuel + evacuations at `CAREER.evacFee`),
    `profit = income − running`, `property = buildSpent`, `science = sciSpent`,
    `flown = flownPx`; the purse identity `purse = starter + profit − property −
    science` holds by construction.
  - `mapStats(out, market, step)` — `rot = Σ corruption` and `standing =
    Σ max(0, planetProfit)` over the in-play planets (`market.planetMask`).

`experiments/career_stats_test.js` (new):

- a scripted two-company career on a seeded 13-planet universe flies three
  legs (own pad, own pad, commercial launch into the rival's HQ planet), fits
  a hold, researches a hull point, bribes and evacuates;
- `checkMoves` asserts each transaction moves exactly its own statistic
  fields and the purse by exactly the debit/credit;
- `checkIdentity` asserts `purse = starter + profit − property − science` to
  `1e-9` after every transaction;
- `checkMapStats` asserts `rot` and `standing` equal their direct sums over
  the in-play planets, on a fresh career and at the end.
