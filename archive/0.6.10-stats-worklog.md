## changes

### `gravity_game.js` (rule layer)
- Module constants `STATS_SAMPLES = 256`, `STATS_SEC = 2`, `STATS_EVERY = round(STATS_SEC / DT)` steps.
- `createCareer` gains `visitIncome`, `flownPx`, `feesPaid`, `buildSpent`, `sciSpent`; `resetCareer` zeroes them.
- `careerEndFlight`: `visitIncome += endFlight(ledger)`, `flownPx += sim.flightLength`.
- `careerBeginFlight`: `feesPaid += fee` (commercial rail fee / rival guest tax; own & Capital pads free).
- `buildGear` / `bulkUpgrade` / `installPart`: `buildSpent +=` purse debit. `researchPoint`: `sciSpent +=` cost.
- `careerStats(out, career)` — income (visits + contracts + bribes + tax earned), running (fees + fuel + evacuations × evacFee), profit, property, science, flown; purse identity `starter + profit − property − science` holds by construction.
- `mapStats(out, market, step)` — rot = Σ corruption, standing = Σ max(0, planetProfit) over in-play planets.
- `sampleStats(galaxy, market, step)` — writes one sample every `stats.every` steps (starts at STATS_SEC game seconds); block of 256 samples per series thins itself (keep every second sample, double interval) when full; returns at once on non-sample steps. Series layout: `[map rot, map standing, then 4 per company: profit, property, science, flown]`.
- `resetGalaxy` allocates `galaxy.stats`; `joinGalaxy` appends 4 series per joining company.
- Exports `careerStats`, `mapStats`, `sampleStats`.

### `gravity_bot.js`
- `stepCareer` calls `GravityGame.sampleStats(market.galaxy, market, runStep)` right after `advancePolitics` (the one place the career clock ticks for page and benches).
