# 0.6.1 plan — the observation device: a visit that reports, and a planet that can be bought quiet

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/politics-quests.md` §3.4. Depends on 0.5.12 (`corruption`, bribe,
arrest) and on the ship parts in `orbit_tech.js`.

One reading of the note, confirmed by the author: «possibility to uninstall this
device on a planet with high corruption level» is *the ship's device, removed
while the ship stands on that planet* — not a device that each planet owns. The
device stays owned and refits anywhere; the planet itself keeps nothing but a
flag.

## problem

The draft's politics has a missing hinge. Every fly-visit pays the planet's
flyby value and decays its rot by `0.2`, whether or not anyone carries a report
back to the Capital. The note asks for a *device* that makes the visit a report:
fitted, it pays and decays rot; pulled off on a rotten world, the value stops
being cleared, so the lord's bribe — that same value × rot × 4 — is the only way
to cash it.

That turns corruption from weather into a choice, and it gives the `1.00` bribe
floor and the `rot 50` arrest line a player-facing reason.

## the model

The observation device is **one ship part**, not a pad gear: the report travels
with the emissary, so it is a thing the ship carries.

- **Every ship starts with it.** `OBS` is a two-record table in `orbit_tech.js`
  (`KIND_OBS`, last of `PART_KINDS`): `OBS-0` (`fitted: false`, mass 0) and
  `OBS-1` (`fitted: true`, mass `0.4 t`). The starter grant fits `OBS-1` and the
  career owns both records from the start, so the refit is free both ways,
  wherever the ship stands, like every owned part. The draft's start — «only
  visit-observation and competitions» — needs a ship that reports from its
  first launch, so the device is never something to buy.
- **The starter mass does not move.** `HULL_MASS = 9.6 t` is the bare hull and
  `SHIP_DRY_MASS = HULL_MASS + OBS-1 = 10 t` stays the starter ship, so every
  bench and every calibrated opening keeps its numbers; taking the device off
  makes the ship `0.4 t` *lighter*. `refitMass` sums the obs slot first, which
  keeps the starter mass bit-exact.
- **No research family and no build cost.** A part nobody ever buys needs no
  price. (A lighter researched device would be one `MERIT` row and one frontier
  row; it is not built.)
- **The visit law gets one test, in one place:**

	visitReports(ledger, market, planet) =
		planet is the Capital or A.R.F.  → true   (their flat entry is not a report)
		otherwise                        → !market.quiet[planet] && ledger.reports

  `ledger.reports` mirrors `career.ship.obs.fitted` (written by `refitShip`, the
  one place a fit changes); a ledger with no career — the money rule, a rival
  outside career — always reports. `market.quiet` is one
  `Uint8Array(OrbitWorld.PLANETS)` on the shared market (like `seized`): the
  quiet state belongs to the *planet*, for every company.
- **A visit that does not report** pays nothing and **clears nothing**: no
  `lastVisitStep` stamp, so the value keeps growing; no rot decay; no
  `market.stamp` bump (nothing a rival planned on has changed). It still counts
  as an encounter, writes `visitPay = 0`, and opens the once-per-visit bribe
  window. The next reporting ship collects everything that was left.
- **The bribe is how an unreported value is cashed.** `bribeAmount` is unchanged
  — `(visitPay + grown) × rot × 4` — so on a non-reporting visit it is the whole
  value accrued since the last report, the note's «more easy to take bribe». A
  bribe taken on such a visit stamps `lastVisitStep`: the value is spent once
  and regrows from zero, so landing again at once finds nothing — a quiet world
  is not a money printer. A bribe on a reporting visit is exactly as before.
- **The estimate is the same law.** `estimateProfitLog` / `estimateProfit` take
  the flying company's ledger and skip every visit `visitReports` refuses, so
  the aim line, the launch book, the helpers, the pill and the bots price a
  quiet planet, or a ship without its device, at exactly what the entry pays.
  The experimental stay-pay goes through the same test.
- **Go quiet.** `quietRefusal(career, market, planet)` → `''` or the reason;
  `goQuiet` acts only on `''`. Allowed while the ship stands on an ordinary
  planet whose `corruption > QUIET_MIN` (75, the note's number), the device is
  fitted, the planet is not already quiet and not in rebellion (rebels take no
  bribes, so silence buys nothing there). It sets `quiet[planet] = 1` and moves
  the device to the shelf (`ship.obs = OBS-0`): the ship is `0.4 t` lighter and
  reports nowhere until the player refits it. Refitting does **not** un-silence
  the planet; only quelling it does (`pacifyPlanet` clears `quiet`).

What going quiet buys: rivals' fly-bys no longer drain the planet's value or
cool its rot, so the value sits near the cap for whoever lands and takes the
bribe. What it costs: no visit income there for anyone, the arrest roll at
`rot > 75` (≥ 25 % per bribe), a rot that only climbs, and a rebellion that ends
the bribes for good.

## numbers (starting points, a bench may tune them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `OBS-1` mass | `0.4 t` | what the ship sheds without the device |
| `HULL_MASS` | `9.6 t` | the bare hull; `+ OBS-1 = SHIP_DRY_MASS 10 t` |
| `QUIET_MIN` | `75` | the draft's «high corruption» line for go quiet |

## GUI

- **Flight HUD, ship group**: one `report` row — `OBS-1`, or `none · visits pay
  0` — so the player can see why a visit stopped paying before hunting a bug.
- **Ship refit**: an `observer` row like hold/tank/engine (`visits report` /
  `no reports`, the pad cap the lighter ship reaches, `back OBS-0` / `fit
  OBS-1 · free`). Unfitting prints `no observation device: visits pay nothing`.
- **Pad block**: `corruption N% · quiet · no reports until quelled` on a quiet
  planet, and the action row `go quiet · P#` — shown only while the law allows
  it, with the whole consequence in its title.
- **Help drawer**: the bribe row and the go-quiet row carry `data-help=
  "politics"`; while the pointer is on them the existing help drawer previews
  `POLITICS_HELP` (`gui_tables.js`, same shape as a rule's entry: reports, the
  bribe formula, arrest, the device and go quiet) and falls back to the rule's
  text, or shuts, when the pointer is anywhere else in the career box.
- **Profit pill**: shows what *this ship's* visit pays — `+0.00` on a quiet
  planet or without the device — and a quiet planet gets its own border colour
  and the tag `quiet · rot N%` over the planet (the seized pill's treatment).

## Validation

- `experiments/obs_test.js`: the starter fit and mass; a reporting visit; a
  visit without the device (pays 0, clears nothing, a rival then collects it);
  a quiet planet and its neighbours; the Capital still charges; estimate/entry
  parity; the quiet bribe is the whole unreported value and cashes it once;
  every `quietRefusal` reason; refit does not un-silence, pacification does.
- `experiments/obs_fragile_gui_test.js`: the report row, the observer refit, the
  go-quiet row only above 75 % with the device fitted, the help topic on the
  row and its fallback, the quiet line, the pill tag.
- `politics_test`, `politics_gui_test`, `dom_smoke`, `career_bench` (its three
  known failures) unchanged.

## open

- Bots never go quiet and never unfit; they price a quiet planet at 0 and take
  its bribe through the bribe rule they already have. A bot that *chooses* to go
  quiet is `0.6.6-plan-rival-characters.md` material.
- No A/B bench has measured a quiet world's income yet
  (`career_gravity_bench`, quiet planets / bribes / arrests / purse per run).

## Scope boundary

No new corruption formula, no change to the arrest roll or the rebellion chance,
no player-set tax (that is `0.6.2`), and nothing about the Capital or A.R.F.,
which never report and never go quiet.
