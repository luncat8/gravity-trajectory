# 0.5.23 worklog — Capital accept refund, medical mission economics, board affordability, and Capital leg valuation

## asked

Revise and implement `0.5.23-plan-medic-capital.md` on top of post-`0.5.22` career
changes (`0.7.2` ship wear, `0.7.32` Capital recovery, `0.7.34` career endgame,
`0.7.35` shared helper/bot valuation), resolving three design questions confirmed
with the user:
1. Gameplay-first scope across shared valuation (`gravity_game.js`), human helper
   (`gui_helper.js`), and rival bot (`gravity_bot.js`).
2. Clear unaffordable `MEDIC_SLOT` offers at the board level (when offers are
   generated/refreshed or landed cash changes — never per-frame or in flight).
3. Raise `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` from `1` to `2` (`+8.00` per 4-patient
   `HOLD-8` trip) and introduce a universal Capital accept refund: accepting
   **any** quest (`TASK_DELIVERY`, `TASK_MEDIC`) or sport (`TASK_COMPETE`) while
   landed on the Capital immediately refunds `+3.00`, while dropping, expiring,
   breaking fragile cargo, arrest, or aborting deducts `−3.00` back.

## 1. why rivals and helpers ignored `TASK_MEDIC` at `c93f91f`

Measuring clean `c93f91f` showed zero completed medical trips across 30 seeds:
- `SEEDS=1-12 SECONDS=1200 node experiments/medic_offer_probe.js`:
  `rebellions 21 · offer-seconds 3605 · at-capital 230s (7 visits) · offer and free at capital 0s · accepts 1 · boardings 0 · patients home 0`.
- `SEEDS=1-30 SECONDS=1200 STATS=1 LOG=0 node experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`:
  `flights 1124 · earned 91.78 · purse 511.52 · pads 246 · rebel 64 / 64 seized · medic trips 0 · patients home 0`.

Four compounding gates caused this:
1. **Negative cycle cash**: at `1.00`/patient, a 4-patient `HOLD-8` cycle paid
   `+4.00` against `−3.00` Capital entry + `2.27…4.54` rebel commercial rail +
   `−3.00` Capital return (`−4.27…−6.54` net before wear).
2. **Zero pre-accept Capital valuation**: `careerLandingFacts` and
   `careerGoalBodies` gave `0` credit and named no goal for an untaken
   `MEDIC_SLOT`, so flying to the Capital looked like a pure `−3.00` loss.
3. **Contract-slot preemption away from the Capital**: `careerBetweenFlights`
   ran `careerAccept` before `careerPlan`, locking `career.contract` into small
   local deliveries before the ship could fly to the Capital to board medics.
4. **Stale rolling offer deadline on accept**: `makeMedic` stamped `deadline`
   when the rebellion offer first spawned (`240 s` rolling board refresh); a ship
   arriving near the end of that window inherited only seconds to complete a
   two-leg rescue.

## 2. changes shipped

- **Economy (`orbit_econ.js`, `gravity_game.js`)**:
  - `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT = 2` (`8` patients per rebellion = `16.00` across
    two `HOLD-8` trips or one `HOLD-24` trip).
  - `CAPITAL_REFUND = 3`: `acceptTask` on `career.capitalPlanet` credits `+3.00`
    (`ledger.purse` + `earned`, `task.capitalRefund = 3`, `lastReward` unchanged)
    for `TASK_DELIVERY`, `TASK_MEDIC`, and `TASK_COMPETE` (`COMPETE_SLOT` can be
    explicitly accepted alongside an open contract, while unaccepted sport still
    settles on landing).
  - `clawbackTaskRefund` deducts `−3.00` (`ledger.purse` + `earned` +
    `checkEnded`) on `dropTask` / `dropContract`, `refreshBoard` expiry,
    `careerBeginFlight` fragile break, `arrestCareer`, and `evacuate`
    (`clearMedicalCargo`), preserving the `career_stats_test.js` purse identity.
  - `acceptTask` on `TASK_MEDIC` refreshes `task.deadline` to
    `Math.max(task.deadline, nowStep + deliverySec * 2)` (`480 s`).
- **PRNG-free board affordability (`gravity_game.js`, `gui_game.js`)**:
  - `medicAffordable` checks whether `ledger.purse` covers the rebel commercial
    rail (after `+3.00` at the Capital, or `3.00 + commercialMinFee()` away from
    the Capital) and a fitted hold (`>= 2 t`).
  - `refreshMedicSlot` updates `MEDIC_SLOT` without touching `SALT_TASK` PRNG
    streams (`boardStale` stays expiry-only), gated on `!career.flying &&
    !career.arrested`, and runs on `refreshBoard`, landed shop/fuel/bribe
    spending, and 1 Hz political ticks in `advanceCareerOneStep`.
- **Shared Capital valuation & rival bot (`gravity_game.js`, `gravity_bot.js`)**:
  - `careerGoalBodies` includes `career.capitalPlanet` when `contract < 0` and an
    affordable `MEDIC_SLOT` offer stands away from the Capital (`[]` preserved
    for an empty board).
  - `careerLandingFacts` tags non-Capital → Capital flights under an untaken
    affordable `MEDIC_SLOT` with `LEG_MEDICS` and
    `credit = (CAPITAL_REFUND + medics * MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT) * LEG_CREDIT`,
    keeping `out.cash = visitPay` honest so `predGain` and `best profit` never
    claim immediate cash on touchdown.
  - `gravity_bot.js` prices the two-leg cycle in `careerMedicNet` (fixing a
    `this.careerExit` scratch-object aliasing trap across consecutive
    `careerDeparture` calls), reserves `career.contract` away from the Capital
    when the medical offer beats local deliveries, includes `CAPITAL_REFUND` in
    `careerNetReward` at the Capital, and escrows active `capitalRefund` via
    `careerRefundEscrow(career)` across optional gear/research/hold/repair/fuel
    and non-completing paid launches (fixing a Seed 14 `R2` negative-purse bug
    caught by `career_gravity_bench.js`).
- **GUI (`gui_book.js`, `gui_career.js`, `gui_shop.js`)**:
  - Added `accept` / `drop` buttons on `COMPETE_SLOT` (`sport`), Capital
    `+3.00` / `−3.00` tooltips and notices, replay `targetSlot` routing, and
    updated the Capital shop banner (`taking work refunds +3.00`).
- **Docs & tests (`drafts/*`, `experiments/*`)**:
  - Updated `drafts/politics-quests.md`, `drafts/career-economy.md`, and
    `drafts/README.md`; archived `archive/0.5.23-plan-medic-capital.md`.
  - Extended `politics_test.js`, `politics_gui_test.js`, and
    `career_guidance_test.js` for Capital refund/clawback across delivery, medic,
    and sport, purse identity, landed-only `MEDIC_SLOT` clearing, and untaken
    Capital `LEG_MEDICS` valuation.
