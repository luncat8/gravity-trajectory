## Review of the previous state (0.5.7)

The last stage gave rivals and the page's bot company a working impulse drive,
but left their **launch planning** ballistic:

- `careerNextShot` fans ~96 traces, queues the best ballistic shot, and only
  then does `careerFlightBurn` nudge that shot at step 24. The queue's score,
  landing body and EMC-build decision describe a flight nobody flies.
- The suggested validation — "re-run `career_gravity_bench` to see whether
  rival pads and reach improve" — cannot answer that question as written. On
  12 seeds / 400 s the 24 rival companies never buy an engine (instrumented:
  0 flights with a drive, 0 burns), and `career_bot_bench`'s research careers
  *do* have one but call `careerPlan` directly, bypassing `careerNextShot`.
  So both benches are blind to launch pricing. Noted in
  `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.
- Two smaller inconsistencies made a naive re-price wrong: a burn search
  scores with `fee = 0` (right mid-flight, wrong before the launch fee is
  paid), and `careerBeginFlight` refills the tank, so the flight has more fuel
  than the ship had while it was parked.

## Changes

- **Shortlist** (`gravity_bot.js`): `careerPlan` records the best three finite
  candidates of its fan (angle/speed units, score, book flag) in preallocated
  typed arrays. It spans every fan pass of one `careerNextShot` (normal,
  fallback, commercial) and is reset by the caller, so two rivals planning on
  the same world step cannot mix shots. No extra traces.
- **`careerPriceBurn`**: a dedicated `GravitySim` (bound by `attachWorld`,
  `setScales`, `setPlanetMask`, `useField`, allocated once) mirrors the live
  field, launches each shortlisted shot, steps it to `CAREER_BURN_AT` and runs
  the *existing* `careerBurnPlan` with `rescueOnly = false`. A shot that lands
  before the nudge window is skipped. The winning shot's price is
  `burn.score − fee − energy·wait·cost` (`burn.score` already nets the tank
  fuel) and must beat the ballistic winner by 0.5.
- **Accurate gates**: `careerBurnPlan`/`careerBurnScore` take an optional `fee`
  that reaches `careerScoreFacts`' solvency check; mid-flight calls still pass
  nothing. The search runs with a full tank (restored afterwards), matching the
  refuel at launch. `careerBurnBest` now keeps the winning landing's
  `taskPay`/`padValue`/`builds`/`exitCost`/`exitSource`.
- **Take it**: the plan's angle, speed, body, score, money terms and
  `fromBook` are rebuilt from that burn landing; the fuel cost is folded into
  `taskPay`, so `score = taskPay + padValue − fee − energy·wait·cost` still
  holds. `burnGain`/`burnBody` feed the decision log (`burn price` line, and
  `· burn +X.XX → P#` on the plan/queue lines). No burn is pre-applied: the
  flight still finds and records its own, so shots, replays, fuel and the launch
  book are unchanged.
- **Benches**: `career_bot_bench` gets four checks on the shared entry point
  (no drive → no pricing; priced plan's identity, shortlist and bounded fan;
  the priced shot flies and burns; no burn pays → ballistic score unchanged).
  `career_gravity_bench` gets `ENGINE=1` (fit the drive explicitly) and
  `NOBURNPRICE=1` A/B, and prints burns and priced plans.
- **Docs**: `README.md` rivals row and `AGENTS.md` files section updated.

  from 0.5.7: hostile `g1.4 f0.6` purse 18.11 · earned 1.80 · 14 flights ·
  14 burns; friendly `g0.8 f1.5` purse 70.73 · earned 14.02 · 17 flights ·

  31.20 · 82 pads · 0 dead · mean reach 4.04 · 0 stuck**, bit-identical to the
  0.5.7 baseline (no company owns a drive, so pricing is a no-op).
- `ENGINE=1` A/B on the same 12 seeds, 400 s (24 rivals):

  | | flights | earned | pads | dead | mean reach | burns | priced plans |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | burn pricing **on** | 107 | 15.83 | 85 | 0 | 4.00 | 104 | 60 |

  +6.16 earned, +1 pad, 0 dead pads either way, and mean reach within 0.05.

## Next step

Only the fan's best three shots are re-priced, so a launch whose *ballistic*
trace never lands — the ones a burn could turn into a first visit to a new
system — is still invisible to `careerNextShot`. Add one bounded rescue pass:
when the ballistic fan finds no viable plan and the ship has a drive, price a
small ring of launch angles by their one-burn landing and take the best that
clears the fee and fuel. Then extend the `ENGINE=1` bench with a dead-end
fixture (a drained HQ pad on a deep well) and check that rivals stop sitting
out those turns without regressing the 12-seed A/B above.
