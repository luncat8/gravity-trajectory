# 0.7.14 — photo prompt variants in the caption pack — 2026-11-16

## review of 0.7.13

The fairness probe now measures the game's actual seat choice against an
independent exit-safe matching; no game code changed. Nothing in that work
touches the photo queue. The remaining gap here was in the caption pack: one
paragraph per caption, and `--variants N` only changed the seed, so two
compositions of one scene could not be queued.

## change

- `photos/generator/comfy_queue.py`: `read_prompts` collects `>` paragraphs and
  splits them on a blank quoted line (`>`). A caption now carries `texts`;
  `make_jobs` writes one file per paragraph for a career caption
  (`circus.jpg`, `circus-2.jpg`) and cycles the paragraphs for a planet caption
  under `--variants`. `print_table` shows the text count, and the help names
  `circus`.
- `photos/prompts.md`: §18 `circus` — two MOSS captions (signal panels in both
  hands, then the matte-anodised aluminium spyglass at one eye), both with the
  era anchor first, the catapult as materials and geometry, and the acrobat
  small against the machine. The section also lists the era negative for the
  separate negative field. Eighteen captions total.
- `photos/README.md`: the variant syntax, the extra card and the checklist row.
- `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`: era-loaded nouns and the small-figure/large-
  machine composition rule, under the existing optional-art section.
