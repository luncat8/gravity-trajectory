# 0.4.27 worklog — career departure viability and seeded guidance

## Review of 0.4.26

The prior worklog left two follow-ups: tune the seeded gravity range against the
career free-planet target, and investigate the 2/4 hard-over-easy result on
seeded money runs. Kept the bot search policy unchanged and made the balance
investigation a separate plan so the old uniform-field control stays distinct.

## Changes

- Set seeded planet gravity to a deterministic uniform `−1…+3.5` draw in
  `0.05` steps. The career still has 13 active planets and three actor HQs, so
  calibration counts the other ten as free.
- Added a cached departure probe for career scoring. It tests a sampled fan of
  eight bearings and four snapped speeds over the 120-second career horizon,
  including force/energy caps, the ship's arrival mass, available gear,
  affordability, launch fees and exit costs. Bots reject landings without a
  viable departure; between-flight repairs require a sampled exit; fresh and
  saved career-helper shots use the same net-outcome gate. This is a bounded
  trajectory sample, not an exhaustive proof over every possible bearing and
  speed.
- Career aim, predicted visits and predicted profit now show the net of the
  launch fee and the sampled departure cost. Positive net is green, a loss is
  neutral, and no affordable departure is red; the canvas aim label follows
  the same state.
- The Help title now follows the selected or previewed mode. Updated the
  guidance copy, README, DOM probe and regression coverage. Fixed the contract
  branch in the general career helper so its task scoring uses the predicted
  landing body.
- Added `0.4.27-plan-bot-balancing.md`. It calls for measuring startup and
  shared search-budget costs before deciding whether hard should receive more
  time; no hard-bot budget change was made here.
