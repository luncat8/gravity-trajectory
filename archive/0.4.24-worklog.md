# 0.4.24 worklog — copyable bot decisions and career dead-end recovery

Plan: `0.4.24-plan-bot-decision-log.md`. User brief: the profit helper buttons
work visually; bots sometimes wait or get into a state where they cannot move;
add a button that copies a log of bot decisions showing what each bot is doing
now and why it cannot fly.

## Implementation

### Decision trace (`gravity_bot.js`)
- `TRACE_MAX = 240` ring buffer of formatted lines; `traceAdd` is called only at
  decision sites (start, plan, commercial, no shot, sit, forced, queue, fire,
  land, board, evac/contract recovery, restart, queue dropped, launch, route
  ready). `lastDecision[index]` gives each actor's most recent line.
- `careerScan` counts the current attempt's fan traces, impacts, payable shots,
  bounce/insolvency/no-landing rejections and book entries; the fan only bumps
  integers. `careerNoPlanWhy()` turns those counters plus the live gate state
  into the reason on a `no shot` line.
- `careerRailText()` / `actorStatus()` / `actorStateShort()` compute the live
  state on demand: planet, purse, pads, flights, rail owner, charge → speed
  cap, fee, exit cost, saved shots from here, contract target, sit/idle tries,
  retry time, last decision. `rivalDoneWhy()` names a classic rival's stop
  reason (purse below the launch fee, shot cap, tour complete, plan ready).
- `decisionLog(runStep, header)` = header + live state for every bot actor +
  the newest decisions, newest last.

### Dead-end recovery (`gravity_bot.js`)
- `careerFee(career, value)` prices flat fees; `careerPrice` stays for records.
  Both evacuation checks now work.
- `stepCareer` ages a grounded company's task board once a world second when a
  deadline has passed, and releases a queued turret reservation when a career
  ends. `reset()` releases queues left by the previous run; `resetActor` resets
  `waitTries`; each actor carries its own `index` for the trace.
- `careerLaunch` on a planless attempt: evacuates a stranded ship after two
  tries, drops an unreachable contract after three and retries next step,
  otherwise logs the blocker and retries after the rival gap. The commercial
  fallback fee is logged while the rail is still selected, and the choice is
  dropped when the company sits the shot out.
- Patience tightened to `CAREER_SIT_GAP = 30 s`, `CAREER_SIT_TRIES = 4`: still
  patient, but a company cannot look frozen for six minutes.

### Page (`index.html`, `gravity_gui.js`, `style.css`)
- `copy bot log` in the run actions; clicking refreshes a read-only
  `bot decisions` panel and copies when `navigator.clipboard` exists. Without a
  clipboard (`file://`) the textarea is shown, focused and selected. Close
  clears it; `startGame`/`stopGame` hide it.
- Header carries rule, level, rival count, seed, world time/step, rate, gravity
  and force scales, static/moving field (lists on/off) and a paused-clock note.
- Rival scoreboard meta appends the short state; the row's `title` carries the
  full live state.
- Helper fix: a career helper now arms with one `predictFull` at the scan
  horizon and `dirty = false`, so the canvas preview, `recordArmedTrace` and the
  actual 120 s flight agree. The 0.4.23 smoke check verifies the armed preview
  instead of re-predicting with a shorter horizon.

### Diagnostics
- `experiments/career_rival_probe.js`: headless rival careers on the shared
  clock; reports the longest grounded streak per rival with purse, pad charge,
  cap, fee, exit, contract, book and queue state, and can print the decision
  log (`LOG=1`).

## Decisions / open points
- The log is a snapshot at click time; clicking again refreshes it. The
  scoreboard carries the live one-word state between clicks.
- Waiting is still deliberate when a losing shot cannot claim a pad — the log
  now says which plan was sat out, with its score and the retry time.
- `predictFull` on helper completion is one full trace per button press, outside
  the sliced helper budget; a full 120 s trace measured ~5.9 ms in the headless
  page, inside one 16.7 ms frame.
