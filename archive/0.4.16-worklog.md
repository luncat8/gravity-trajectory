# 0.4.16 worklog — red cost gates, bottom trade bar, 2 s eased wait

## user feedback

1. Shop/research rows that are unavailable **by cost** were dimmed with the
   same 0.35 opacity as an undiscovered or state-gated item; they should stay
   readable in reddish text and differ from those gates.
2. `wait · 30 min` and the flight `playback` controls belong at the bottom of
   the canvas, next to the trade-time controls.
3. Waiting should not teleport the clock: fast-forward it over 2 s with an
   easy in-out pacing.

## implementation

### 1. `costly` price class (`gravity_gui.js`, `style.css`)

- Added `purseTooSmall(career, price)` and an optional `costly` argument on
  `shopButton` / `shopButtonRef`, emitted only when the purse is the blocker
  (`disabled && costly`). `button.costly:disabled` keeps `opacity: 1`,
  `color: var(--warn)` and a faint red border, while state gates (HQ only,
  cargo too big, no catapult, max tier) stay at the default dim.
- Applied to the first-catapult build, gear tiers, `all N` bulk rows, ship
  refits, prototype gear builds, the research accept button (also patched live
  in `refreshScienceReadout` while sliders drag) and the launch button when
  only the fee blocks it (career pad notes stay a state gate).

### 2. wait + playback in the trade bar (`index.html`, `style.css`, `gravity_gui.js`)

- `#careerWait` and `#careerWaitInfo` moved from `#careerOperations` into
  `<footer id="tradeControls">`; the drawer keeps only `evacuate`.
- The `playback` group left the Cargo HUD: `#playbackGroup` is now a compact
  footer cluster `play ×N  −  reset  +` next to the trade slider, which is
  relabelled `time`. Its ids are unchanged, so `refreshPlaybackUi`, the replay
  reset and the benches keep working; `route` still hides the cluster.
- `refreshCareerPanel` shows the wait button only in career; the settlement
  line is a full-width centred second row of the footer. The footer already
  feeds `reserveTradeFooter()`, so both HUD columns shrink for the taller bar.
- Narrow screens drop the playback divider and the options row, and the bar
  wraps with `space-between`; the settlement line wraps instead of clipping at
  <= 820 px and the button keeps a min width while its label runs
  `wait · 30 min -> waiting · 100 %`.
- `reserveTradeFooter()` also publishes the measured reserve as
  `--footReserve` on `documentElement`, and `.photoCard` uses it for `bottom`
  (fallback 64/60 px), so the taller career bar no longer slides under the
  postcard.

### 3. 2 s ease-in-out wait (`gravity_gui.js`, `experiments/dom_smoke.js`)

- `CAREER_WAIT_MS = 2000`; `careerWaitAnim` holds the click-time snapshot,
  rate, total steps and trade start. `careerWaitTick(now)` runs from `frame()`
  and, for `p = (now − start) / 2000`, advances:
  - `tradeSim.advanceTo(startT + 30 · easeInOut(p))` — the planet orbits race
    visibly and the trade clock follows;
  - `game.clockSteps = startClock + round(easeInOut(p) · steps)`;
  - the pads are restored from the `Float64Array` snapshot and recharged with
    one cumulative `careerAdvance(career, rate, steps)`.
  Cubic ease-in-out: `4p³` then `1 − (2 − 2p)³/2`.
- The snapshot is what keeps the end state exact: battery leak is exponential
  and solar charge linear, so per-frame deltas would drift from the instant
  wait (and from `waitSettlement`). One cumulative call from the snapshot is
  bit-identical to the old wait.
- The board ages and contracts settle once at `p = 1` (`refreshBoard`,
  `checkEnded`, the same `careerMsg` note), then `careerChanged()` refreshes
  the shop and the settlement line.
- The button reads `waiting · N %` and `on`; the footer line reads
  `fast-forward · X / 30 min · N %`. `gameLocked()` and `careerAct` block
  aiming, launching and shopping during the 2 s, a second click is ignored,
  and `startGame` / `stopGame` cancel the run.
