## what changed in 0.4.17

### 1. Separate bottom panel for `auto helpers` and `launch` (`index.html`, `style.css`, `gravity_gui.js`)

- Moved `#helperGroup` (`quick scan`, `scan here`, `tweak best`, `#helperProgress`) and `#cargoFoot` out of `#cargoHud` into a standalone bottom-right `<aside id="cargoFoot" class="panel" aria-label="launch controls">` anchored above `#tradeControls` (`bottom: var(--footReserve, 64px)`).
- Updated `reserveTradeFooter()` and the `ResizeObserver` in `gravity_gui.js` so `#leftHud` reserves `--footReserve` above `#tradeControls` while `#cargoHud` reserves space above `#cargoFoot` (`footRect.top - 8`), preventing any vertical overlap across all 8 tested viewports (`1440×900` down to `390×700` and `800×560`).
- Added `#cargoFoot` to `hudRectEls` (`hudRects` size `12`) and `hudPadPress` so canvas planet plates and `drawGripLabel()` avoid overlapping `#cargoFoot`, while pads underneath the panel padding remain draggable.

### 2. Inline `buy` and `evacuate` buttons in place of disabled stranded `launch` (`index.html`, `style.css`, `gravity_gui.js`)

- Added `#strandedActions` (`#launchBuild` + `#launchEvac`) inside `.cargoActions` on the same grid row as `#launch`.
- In `setLaunchState()`, when `careerRule()` is active and the ship is stranded in an open career (`career.stranded && gravity.status !== STATUS_FLYING`):
  - `#launch` is hidden and `#strandedActions` is shown in place.
  - When stranded on a padless planet (`career.at >= 0`), `#launchBuild` (`buy EMC-1 · 3.00`, with `.costly` red price gating when `purse < 3`) and `#launchEvac` (`evacuate · 5.00`) sit side by side; clicking `#launchBuild` builds `EMC-1` on `career.at`, unstrands the ship, and immediately restores `#launch`.
  - When stranded in deep space (`career.at < 0`), `#launchBuild` hides and `#launchEvac` spans the full row.

### 3. 4× career playback fly speed default and range max (`gravity_gui.js`)

- Extended `PLAY_SPEEDS` to `[0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32]` with `PLAY_DEFAULT = 2` (`×1`), `PLAY_MAX_INDEX = 5` (`×8`), `PLAY_CAREER_DEFAULT = 4` (`×4`), and `PLAY_CAREER_MAX_INDEX = 7` (`×32`).
- Entering career mode (`startGame`) or pressing `#playReset` (`4×`) in career sets playback to `×4` with ceiling `×32`; leaving career mode (`stopGame` or switching to `tour`/`route`/`money`) resets playback to `×1` (`1×`) with ceiling `×8`.

### 4. Predicted route profit (`3.25-3`), profit per time of flight, and time of flight (`index.html`, `style.css`, `gravity_gui.js`)

- Updated `refreshPredGain()` to include completed career delivery or sport task rewards when a predicted trajectory lands on the target planet before the deadline.
- Added `predBuildCost(sim)`, `predProfitText(sim)`, and `predRateText(sim)`:
  - When a career trajectory lands on a planet without a catapult (`pads[sim.predBody].catapult === null`), `predProfitText(sim)` appends the separate catapult build cost subtraction (e.g. `3.25-3`); when landing on an existing pad or outside career, it formats as `3.25`.
  - `predRateText(sim)` formats profit per time of flight (`+X.XX/s`).
- Added `<div id="predProfitRow" class="stat goalStat">` (`pred profit` / `#predProfitReadout`) to `#cargoHud .stats` displaying `+<profit[-3]> · +<rate>/s · <time> s`, updated `#predVisitReadout` with `+<profit[-3]>`, and updated `refreshAimLabel()` (canvas grip label + `#aimReadout`) to include `+<profit[-3]> · +<rate>/s · <time> s`.
- Tightened `.stats` (`grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`) and `.stat` (`min-width: 0; white-space: nowrap`) in `style.css` so long readout strings never widen `#cargoBody` or wrap mid-token, and clamped `drawGripLabel()` to the left edge of `#cargoHud`/`#cargoFoot` when flipping left.
