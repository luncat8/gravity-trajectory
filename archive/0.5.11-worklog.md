## Player and planet capacity follow-up

The scoreboard supports 16 rivals and the world supports 48 planets. A player
increase activates one previously inactive, seeded random-position planet and
creates the rival on it without restarting the run; removing a rival leaves the
planet in play. The roster, route scheduler, bot trace, colours and scoreboard
cover all slots. Rivals are allocated on demand; above two rivals the career search narrows its
angular fan as the roster grows. Player HQs are unique after a
restart, including when only dark free planets remain; broke rivals restart at
their existing HQ. Queues using a removed company's rails are cancelled.

The original 26-world geometry, traits and RNG draws are unchanged; extra
worlds use a separate seed stream. Trade starts with its original 26-world
range and can expand when extra worlds become active. The physics and field
buffers cover 48, while their simulation loops stop at the highest active
planet. Launch-book masks use exact 48-bit integer values rather than 32-bit
bitwise shifts.
