## 4. what the numbers do

`career_gravity_bench STATS=1`, 12 seeds × 2 rivals, 400 s, `WEAR=1`, one law
changed per row (`LOG=0`, so the historical logs stay as they were; the full
run is `experiments/logs/0.7.7-visit-balance.txt`):

| variant | gravity | cap | bribe | bribe priced | profit/company | property | pads | maintenance | share of income | mean rot | standing | bribes taken |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.7.2, logged | uniform | 3 | rot × 4 | no | 0.009/s | 4.52 | 50 | 5.46 | 48 % | 134 | 16.4 | 0 (0 %) |
| cap 3 | smooth | 3 | 2× | no | 0.011/s | 5.29 | 53 | 5.53 | 45 % | 127 | 15.0 | 2 (2 %) |
| cap 4 (previous stage) | smooth | 4 | 2× | no | 0.011/s | 5.11 | 52 | 5.29 | 43 % | 147 | 18.0 | 3 (3 %) |
| cap 4, old gravity | uniform | 4 | 2× | no | 0.014/s | 5.64 | 58 | 4.58 | 37 % | 148 | 18.7 | 2 (2 %) |
| **cap 4 + this pass** | smooth | 4 | 2× | **yes** | **0.012/s** | **6.05** | 52 | 5.28 | 40 % | 149 | 18.2 | **7 (7 %)** |

So, against the state the previous stage inherited (`0.7.2`, profit `0.009/s`,
maintenance `48 %` of income): **profit per company +33 % (0.009 → 0.012), the
maintenance share 48 % → 40 %, property 4.52 → 6.05, bribes 0 → 7 % of income**.
That is the number the task asked for; wear is still a real sink, but it no
longer eats half of what flying brings in.

Two readings for the next balance pass:

- **the cap alone raises the ceiling, not the income.** The growth slope is
  unchanged (`k = 0.00625/s`) and a rival is bound by flights, pads and energy,
  not by a planet's asymptote: `3 → 4` left profit at `0.011/s` and mostly moved
  rot (`127 → 147`, standing `15.0 → 18.0`), because the passive-rot ramp is
  written in absolute money (`1.00…3.00`) and a `4.00` world now sits above its
  full rate for longer.
- **the lighter middle is a map change, not an income change.** On the same seeds
  the smoothed map gave fewer pads and less income than the uniform one
  (`58 → 52` pads, `0.014 → 0.011`/s): the bot's site pricing reads a well's
  depth, so the band it prefers moved with the bias. It is kept for playability —
  more usable `0.9…1.5` worlds (band share 14.3 % → 19.2 % of draws, heavy middle
  16.5 % → 11.9 %) — and should not be sold as a profit lever.

Bribe pricing moved the rivals from 3 bribes to 7 and their share from 3 % to
7 % of income; the persona gate means half the companies still refuse, which is
the intended spread.
