# 0.5.12 worklog — corruption, bribes, arrest, rebellion, medical quest

Plan: `archive/0.5.12-plan-corruption-rebellion.md` (moved here with 0.5.11's plan).

## Review of 0.5.11 (special planets, player/planet capacity)

Read before touching code; four things mattered for this stage:

- **The market never reached the page.** `applyMarketSpecials` filled the special
  planet pointers, but nothing set `market.galaxy`, so every rule that has to read
  the shared map (claim owner, pads, and now rot/rebellion) was inert in the GUI
  while the benches bound it by hand. Fixed once with `bindRun(market, galaxy,
  world)` and called from `gui_world.applyPlanetUniverse` on the career rule.
- **`careerBetweenFlights` was called without its `runStep`** on the rival landing
  path (`careerLand`), so the between-flight policy priced pad gear and bribes
  against `undefined`. Every money-per-second comparison in `careerPadGear` was
  therefore NaN-driven. The step is now passed, which changes rival behaviour
  measurably (see the A/B log: `SPECIAL=0 POLITICS=0` no longer reproduces the
  0.5.10 baseline bit for bit — 161 flights vs 167, reach 4.72 vs 4.04).
- **`placeShip` was not exported**, so no bench or page fixture could move a
  career without flying. Exported; the arrest transport and the GUI bench use it.
- Fixture traps kept for the next stage: the launch gates read `sim.bodyCount`
  (not the planet mask), `planetMask` needs `planetCapacity` set before
  `setPlanetMask`, and a rival's rail may only exist on a planet where the player
  did not build first (one player EMC per planet).

## Steps

1. **Laws in `orbit_econ.js`** — §8 block: `CORRUPTION_*`, `ARREST_SECONDS`,
   `REBEL_*`, `FLYBY_*`, `MEDIC_*`, `PACIFY_CORRUPTION_MAX`, and the pure
   `growthFactor` / `clampCorruption` / `bribeCorruption` / `arrestChance` /
   `rebellionChance` / `flybyMultiplier`; `advanceColony` multiplies positive
   `dPop` by the growth factor only, so famine still starves.
2. **`orbit_sim.js`** — `corruption` column plus `setCorruption`; the economy tick
   copies the market array into the colonies before `advanceColony`.
3. **Market state in `gravity_game.js`** — typed arrays for corruption, bribe
   stamps, rebel/seized, patients, cooldown and epoch, `politicsSeed/Serial/Step`,
   `visitPay`, and `bindRun`. One political clock (`advancePolitics`, idempotent
   per world step, inert without a bound run) does the passive rise, the visit
   decay bookkeeping, the colony copy and one rebellion roll per planet epoch.
   The epoch stamp is only consumed when the planet is actually rolled, so a
   planet that rots later still gets its roll in that epoch.
4. **Bribes and arrest** — `bribeAmount = 2 * (visitPay + planetProfit)`,
   `bribeRefusal`, `acceptBribe`, one bribe per `lastVisitStep` stamp, arrest roll
   from the deterministic stream after the rot is added, and a slow transport to
   the Capital that blocks planning, drops medical cargo and shows a countdown.
5. **Rebellion and seizure** — `seizePlanet` keeps the best former rail with a
   full battery, clears both owners' pads, sets `OWNER_REBEL = -3`, charges the
   commercial fee permanently (`seized`) and seeds the patients; `pacify` clears
   the live rebellion, halves the rot above 40 and starts a cooldown while the
   ground stays seized. `coveredPlanets` counts a seized rail, so the catapult
   win is still reachable.
6. **Medical quest** — `TASK_MEDIC = 3` plus `MEDIC_SLOT` (board 5 → 6),
   half-hold medics, patients home at 0.5 each, pacification on the last patient,
   one `clearMedicalCargo` on every exit path, and the offer leaves the board as
   soon as its planet is quelled (a hold that cannot lift a medic offers nothing).
7. **Bot** — `careerBribeProne` persona, `careerBribe` policy, arrest gates in
   `careerStep`, medic routing in `careerTarget`/`careerAccept`, and the rebel
   rail priced as the commercial fee (`source: 'rebel'`) with no `setCommercial`
   fallback on seized ground (that used to invent an EMC-1 the planet lacks).
8. **GUI** — `claimTag` rebel branch, `REBELLION` and `rot N%` pills in
   `drawPlanetProfits` (cached strings, no per-frame allocation), the pad block's
   rot/bribe/arrest/medical lines with a live bribe button, the medical row in
   the task board, the arrest countdown in the clock, and the run binding in
   `applyPlanetUniverse`.

## Balance readings

- Short runs are untouched: 400 s × 12 seeds is identical with politics on and
  off (`157 flights · 15.96 · 67 pads · reach 4.69`), rot stays at 13.2 and
  nothing rebels.
- Long runs lose buildable ground: at 1200 s the rebellion leaves 24 seized
  planets and costs 10 pads (30 vs 40), at 2400 s it seizes 21 of 39 and a third
  of the earnings (43.84 vs 64.50). The rivals quell only 4 of 19 rebellions
  (10 medical trips, 32 patients home at 2400 s; 2 trips, 8 patients at 1200 s),
  so the 4× fly-by payout and the medical reward do not yet pay the loss back.
- Bribes and arrests now occur (6 bribes at 1200 s, 12 bribes and 1 arrest at
  2400 s). The first attempt produced none at all: `careerBetweenFlights` had no
  `runStep`, so `bribeAmount` was NaN and every bribe was refused. The bot takes
  a bribe when it is worth 2.00, when the resident rot is 25 or more, or when a
  rival is queued on the same planet.

## Next step suggestion

Make the rebellion a contest instead of a tax: teach the rivals (and the page's
profit helper) to chase the 4× fly-by payout and the medical quest — a queued
medical trip scores the reward plus the ramp, and a rebel planet with patients
outranks a plain delivery — then re-run the A/B. If the earnings gap survives
that, the epoch is too fast: `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` 60 with 0.2/epoch at C=100
reliably consumes the frontier inside five minutes, so a 120 s epoch (or a
rebellion that clamps the rot after the seizure) is the lever to test next.
