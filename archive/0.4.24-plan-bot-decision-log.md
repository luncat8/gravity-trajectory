# 0.4.24 plan — bot decision log, career dead-end recovery

## Goal

Rival companies can sit for minutes or stop flying entirely with no visible
reason. Make every bot decision inspectable and remove the states that are
genuinely stuck, while keeping the deliberate "sit out a losing shot" policy.

1. **Decision trace** — a bounded, timestamped log of what each bot decided,
   with the numbers behind the decision (score, pad charge, fee, purse,
   contract, book, next retry).
2. **Live state** — the scoreboard shows each rival's current state in one word
   and the full reason on hover; the copied log opens with the same state for
   every active actor.
3. **Copy bot log** — one button copies the trace and the live state, and also
   opens a read-only panel with the text. `file://` has no clipboard
   permission, so the panel is the primary path, not an afterthought.
4. **Dead-end recovery** — a rival must never be parked forever by a stale
   contract, a ship lost in space, an unaffordable evacuation price, a reserved
   pad charge left behind by a finished company, or a commercial rail chosen
   for one attempt and then forgotten.

## Decision trace (`gravity_bot.js`)

`TRACE_MAX = 240` lines, one string per decision. Decisions are rare (one per
attempt, flight or landing), so formatting them at the decision site is cheap;
nothing is added to the per-frame hot loop.

Line format: `[t+ 123.4] R1 sit · score -1.35 · no EMC build · sit 3/4 · retry in 30.0 s`.

Career decisions recorded: `start`, `plan` (book reuse or fan, with score
terms), `commercial` (fallback rail tried, with why), `no shot` (with the
blocker), `sit` / `forced` (patience spent), `queue` (barrel wind-up), `fire`,
`land`, `evac`, `drop contract`, `restart`, `queue dropped`. Classic rivals
record `plan`, `launch`, `land`, `done` and `route ready`.

`careerScan` counts the last attempt's traces, impacts, bounce/exit/solvency
rejections and book hits. `careerNoPlanWhy()` turns those counts plus the live
rail, purse and contract into the reason on a `no shot` line. The fan only
increments counters — no allocations.

`actorStatus(index, runStep)` returns the multi-line live state: short state,
planet, purse, pads, flights, the rail (owner, charge, speed cap, fee, exit),
the saved shots from here, the active contract, and the last decision.
`actorStateShort(index, runStep)` is its one-word form for the HUD. A classic
rival's short state names its stop reason (`purse 0.90 < launch fee 1.00`,
`shot cap`, `tour complete`) instead of just `done`.

`decisionLog(runStep, header)` joins a header, every active actor's live state
and the trace. The page supplies the header (rule, level, seed, world time,
step, scales, field, pause state).

## Dead-end fixes (`gravity_bot.js`)

- **Fees are not items.** `careerPrice(career, item)` reads `item.buildCost`;
  passing `CAREER.evacFee` (a number) yields `undefined`, so every evacuation
  purse check was false. Add `careerFee(career, value)` for flat fees and use
  it for the evacuation in both `careerBetweenFlights` and `careerLaunch`.
- **Boards age on the world clock.** A rival's task board was refreshed only on
  landing, so an accepted contract with an unreachable target never expired and
  every fan shot was rejected by its `requiredBody` gate. `stepCareer` calls
  `refreshBoard` once a world second for grounded companies when a deadline has
  passed; `careerLaunch` drops an active contract after three planless tries
  and retries at once.
- **A lost ship comes home.** With the fee fixed, `careerBetweenFlights`
  evacuates `at < 0` on the next idle step; `careerLaunch` evacuates a stranded
  ship after two planless tries.
- **No reservation leaks.** `stepCareer` releases a queued shot when its career
  ends (complete or broke), and `reset()` releases any queue left over from the
  previous run.
- **One-attempt commercial rail.** A commercial fallback that finds a plan is
  kept for that queued shot, but is dropped when the company sits the shot out,
  so its own recharged pad is used at the next attempt.
- **Bounded patience.** `CAREER_SIT_GAP = 30 s`, `CAREER_SIT_TRIES = 4`: a
  company can still wait for pads and visit profits instead of donating a
  launch, but never looks frozen for more than two minutes of world time.

## Page (`index.html`, `gravity_gui.js`, `style.css`)

- `copy bot log` in the run actions, visible whenever a game has bots; `bot
  decisions` panel below it with a close button and a read-only textarea.
- Clicking refreshes the snapshot, shows the panel and copies when a clipboard
  is available; without one the textarea is focused and selected for manual
  copy. `startGame`/`stopGame` hide the panel and clear it.
- Rival scoreboard meta appends the short state; the row's `title` carries the
  full live state.

## Validation

- `career_rival_probe`: long grounded streaks over several seeds; the log shows
  the exact decision sequence that produced them.
- `career_bot_bench`: a stranded rival with cash evacuates; a lost ship comes
  home; a stale contract is dropped; ended careers release reservations; the
  fee helper prices flat fees; the trace is bounded and readable.
- `dom_smoke`: button visibility per mode, clipboard copy contents, `file://`
  fallback panel, short state in the rival meta, close button.
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`, `game_bench`, `bot_bench`, `route_bench`,
  `gravity_bench`, `colony_bench` — no regressions.
