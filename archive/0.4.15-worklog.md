## user feedback & root cause on the science cost plot

1. **GUI layout (`0.4.14`):** confirmed OK by the user; verified across all 8
   viewports down to `390x700` in headless Chromium (`experiments/layout_check.js`),
   catching and fixing one remaining `<= 599px` `.careerTab` width overflow inside
   `#leftHud` (`169px -> 158px`).
2. **Why directional parameter difficulty was invisible — and inverted — on the
   science plot:**
   - All four seeded ladders (`IMP-1..3`, `EMC-1..5`, `FT-1..3`, `BAT-1..5`) lie
     along diagonal performance chords (`IMP-1 = [12, 1.0] → IMP-3 = [40, 2.5]`,
     `EMC-1 = [15, 0.62] → EMC-5 = [180, 0.88]`).
   - `OrbitScience.distToHull` previously used symmetric Euclidean distance in
     both directions before adding `frontierWork`. Inside the researched
     bounding box (`frontier = 0`), a weak heavy engine `[12, 2.5]` paid the
     exact same `$11.00` Euclidean distance to the diagonal chord as a strong
     lightweight engine `[40, 1.0]`.
   - Worse, in the off-diagonal corners, symmetric Euclidean distance made the
     **easy** corner look more expensive than the **hard** corner: weak & heavy
     engine `[5, 3.5]` cost `$21.70` vs weak & light `[5, 0.6]` `$14.60`, and
     strong & sloppy catapult `[210, 0.52]` cost `$40.90` vs strong &
     super-efficient `[210, 0.95]` `$31.10`.
   - `METRIC_SCALE.battery[1] = 1000` gave the entire `0.0002..0.0012` leak axis
     only `1.0 RU` of span (vs `3.8 RU` on capacity), muting the vertical leak
     gradient, and `scienceCostColor` blended unknown cells from the exact same
     teal RGB (`#153d3b`) as `SCI_PLOT_KNOWN`, hiding the hull boundary on the
     easy side.

## implementation

### 1. Directional hull distance (`orbit_science.js`)

- Added `directionalHullDist(points, target, dirs)` alongside the unchanged
  3-arg Euclidean `nearestHullDist`. Given `dirs[d] = { below, above }`, moving
  from any hull combination `y` to `target` scales easy steps (`weight < 1`) by
  `min(1, weight)` and keeps `1.0` on hard steps (`weight >= 1`).
- Formulated as a lifted positive-combination Wolfe minimum-norm polytope
  `z = Σ w_i x_i + u^+ - u^-` (`Σ w_i = 1, w_i >= 0, u^± >= 0`), solved in `O(n)`
  active-set iterations with zero hot-loop allocations while keeping exact
  Euclidean `plainDist <= INSIDE_EPS * (1 + normTarget)` interior classification.
- Retuned `FRONTIER` easy weights to `0.10` and `METRIC_SCALE.battery` to
  `[0.2, 2500.0]` (`1 RU = 5 kWh = 0.0004 leak/y`):
  - `engine [12, 2.5]` `$2.90` vs `[40, 1.0]` `$11.00`; `[26, 3.0]` `$2.96` vs
    `[26, 0.5]` `$17.44` (`5.9×`);
  - `catapult [100, 0.57]` `$4.50` vs `[100, 0.93]` `$16.08`;
  - `tank [90, 1.65]` `$2.10` vs `[90, 0.25]` `$15.40`;
  - `battery [10, 0.0011]` `$1.31` vs `[10, 0.0002]` `$5.44`.
