# 0.7.13 — measure the career opening that the game actually seats — 2026-11-16

## decision

Fix the fairness **probe**, not the opening law. The default seed search was
already choosing distinct, buildable, exit-safe first sites whose payouts meet
the 0.55 band. The old probe's 52% and 25% reports on seeds 4 and 6 were not
valid counterexamples. No `gravity_game.js` code, seat order, saved map or
fairness threshold changed in this stage.

## diagnosis

The earlier 0.7.10 diagnosis that the probe traced an unpinned field was wrong:
it already passed the planned HQs as forced homes. Its `assignDistinct` instead
maximized the *minimum* pay without constraining the *maximum*. Seed 4's pinned
field lets HQ P10 land on P5 for 10.89 or P9 for 6.20; with the other seats at
5.62 and 6.89, the former reports 52% while the latter yields 82%. Seed 6 has
an analogous 6.03 vs 1.92 choice, making a false 25% instead of 79%. Checking
exits only **after** picking sites could also reject a world with an alternative
exit-safe assignment.

| seed | planned HQs | game's first sites | chosen pay range | weakest / best |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| 4 | 12, 10, 8 | 6, 9, 11 | 5.62–6.89 | 82% |
| 6 | 10, 1, 9 | 11, 6, 0 | 1.52–1.92 | 79% |

## probe and routing

- Build the independent assignment from `careerOpenings` **after** the HQ set
  is pinned. `targetPay` plus the light-site and Capital restrictions measures
  distinct *buildable* landings; `sitePay` and `exitOk` measure distinct,
  exit-safe landings. This keeps a dead end separate from a simple shortage.
- An independent augmenting-path matcher looks for distinct sites inside a
  *positive-pay window*. For each possible lower bound, binary-search the
  smallest upper bound that admits an exit-safe matching; compare the best
  weakest/best ratio. A fixture with a rich but lopsided assignment fails if
  the upper bound is removed. Other fixtures catch a reused site, an ignored
  exit, an inactive site and a zero-pay opening.
- Check the **game's chosen** site IDs, eligibility, exit flags and exact
  min/max/sum against those final-field rows. Both the independent best band
  and the actual game choice must meet the requested ratio. Print the game's
  sites/pay, and disclose the oracle's ratio when it is better: for 26 planets,
  three seats, seed 4 the game chooses 89% while the oracle can reach 95%.
- Add the default probe to `run_fast.js`'s slow tier and list it under career
  opening checks in `AGENTS.md`, the README files and the findings. Correct
  the erroneous explanation in the 0.7.10/0.7.12 historical worklogs and
  update the still-open 0.6.8 roster plan's measured table.

## validation

- `node experiments/career_start_fairness.js`: all eight default seeds have
  distinct buildable, exit-safe sites and game choices within the 0.55 band;
  lowest chosen ratio **65%**. Seeds 4 and 6 report 82% and 79%.
- `REFUSED_SEED=10 node experiments/career_start_fairness.js`: the eight
  defaults still pass, and the refused seed has no distinct buildable site;
  the bounded walk took about 3.4–4.0 s against its 12 s ceiling.
- `COUNT=26 SEATS=3 SEEDS=1-4`: 4/4 distinct, exitable and fair, lowest chosen
  ratio 82% (the old 2/4 result was also a probe error).
- `COUNT=13 SEATS=5 SEEDS=1-4`: intentionally **fails** at 0/4 distinct
  buildable sites for the *chosen* HQs. The field has 7–10 eligible light
  planets before selecting HQs, so this alone does not prove that no better
  HQ set exists. Matrix mode and a larger-roster contract remain open in the
  0.6.8 plan; the default gate must not silently loosen for this roster.
- `SEEDS=1-4 node experiments/career_start_affordability.js`: twelve actual
  first-flight EMC-1 builds and starter exits passed (separate affordability
  check; it does not claim that bots choose the fair matching).
- `node experiments/run_fast.js --slow`: fast and slow tiers passed, including
  `career_start_seat_test`, the new fairness check and saved-map regeneration
  check. `node --check` on both changed scripts and `git diff --check` passed.

No game-search performance claim follows from fixing the measurement. Test a
cheaper pair-ranker alternative only after the open roster matrix identifies
which selected HQ sets, if any, actually miss attainable fair openings.
