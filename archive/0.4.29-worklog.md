  staying silent; and the smoke test re-presses the EMC helper (bounded, six
  presses) while the notice reports the empty find.
  Pinning also exposed a latent fixture fragility: `recordLaunch` dedups by
  rewriting the existing entry object in place, so the smoke test's rigged
