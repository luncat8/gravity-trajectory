# 0.7.42 Worklog — Experimental Balance Multipliers (`pads ×`, `rot ×`, `pay ×`)

Implemented `archive/0.7.42-plan-balance-knobs.md` (`drafts/career-economy.md`
§2.9 & §2.11, `drafts/time-scale-units.md`): added the three career-only
experimental multipliers `pads ×` (`#expPads`, `padScale`, `&ps=`, `0.25..4`),
`rot ×` (`#expRot`, `rotScale`, `&rs=`, `0..3`), and `pay ×` (`#expPay`,
`payScale`, `&ys=`, `0.25..3`) at default `×1.00` with zero baseline drift.

## 1. Engine, Bot & Forecast Wiring

- `gravity_game.js`:
  - Extended `EXPERIMENT` with `padMin: 0.25, padMax: 4`, `rotMin: 0, rotMax: 3`,
    and `payMin: 0.25, payMax: 3`.
  - Added module state and accessors `setPadScale` / `padScaleOf`,
    `setRotScale` / `rotScaleOf`, `setPayScale` / `payScaleOf`, and pad-clock
    helpers `careerPadSeconds`, `careerPadMinutes`, `careerWaitPadSeconds`.
  - Wired `padScale` into `careerAdvance`, `careerArrivalCharge`, and
    `postLandingRailCap`.
  - Wired `rotScale` into `stepPolitics` (`CORRUPTION_REPORT_RATE * rotScale` and
    `passiveCorruptionRate(...) * rotScale`), keeping `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS = 60`,
    `CORRUPTION_VISIT_DECAY`, and one-time `bribeCorruption` unscaled so `rot ×0`
    freezes passive rot and reporting decay while bribes and 60 s rebellion rolls
    remain live.
  - Added `planetProfitUnscaledAt` so `planetProfitAt` scales non-Capital /
    non-A.R.F. normal and rebel fly-by payouts by `payScale` while `stepPolitics`
    continues to read the unscaled neglect value (`pay ×` orthogonal to `rot ×`).
  - Included `padScale` and `payScale` in `seatPlanKey` so `prepareCareerStarts`
    does not reuse a `×1` cached seat plan under non-`1` `pads ×` or `pay ×`.
- `gravity_bot.js`:
  - Wired `careerPadSeconds` / `careerPadMinutes` and `CAREER_PAD_HORIZON * padScale`
    into `careerPadGear`, `careerGearOffer`, `careerEnergyWait`, and
    `careerChargeAfter`.
- `gui_tables.js`, `gui_draw_econ.js`, `gui_career.js`, `gui_shop.js`, `comms_copy.js`:
  - Scaled `PROFIT_MAX_CENTS` by `EXPERIMENT.payMax` (`+48.00`), read
    `planetProfitUnscaledAt` (gated by `rotScaleOf() > 0`) for career neglect
    pill border colors in `gui_draw_econ.js`, and wired `careerPadSeconds` /
    `careerPadMinutes` / `careerWaitPadSeconds` into `waitSettlement`,
    `panelWaitKwh`, and `CommsCopy.buildSnapshot`.
