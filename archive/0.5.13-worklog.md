# 0.5.13 worklog — the bribe reprice and the rebellion contest

Follows `archive/0.5.12-worklog.md` (corruption, bribes, arrest, rebellion,
medical quest) and closes the two questions it left open: what a bribe should
be worth, and whether the rebellion can be a contest instead of a tax.

## Review of 0.5.12 (what this stage changed and why)

- **The bribe ignored corruption.** `bribeAmount = 2 × (visitPay + regrown)`
  paid the same on a clean world as on a rotten one, so a bribe was free
  pocket change everywhere and the arrest roll was the only price. The design
  intent (player review): the visit value is the base — a planet whose visit
  profit is low has already been reported to the Capital, so its lord has
  nothing to buy — and the lord's appetite is the rot. A bribe is therefore a
  *parking income*: it matters when the company sits on a rotten world with no
  better task, and it is priced by the arrest roll exactly where that roll is
  live.
- **The rebellion was a tax, not a contest.** The 0.5.12 A/B left the long run
  a third below the politics-off baseline (43.84 vs 64.50 earned at 2400 s):
  rivals quelled only 4 of 19 rebellions, and neither the 4× fly-by nor the
  medical quest paid the lost buildable ground back. The worklog's own next
  step: score the medical quest higher, re-run the A/B, and only then touch
  `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS` or the rot clamp.
- **The rot clamp on seizure is a dead lever** (analysis, not measured):
  rebel planets are skipped by the passive-rise loop, and pacification clamps
  rot to 40 regardless of what it was at the seizure, so clamping at the
  seizure changes no reachable state. It was dropped without spending a bench
  run on it.

## Steps

1. **Bribe law in `orbit_econ.js`** — `BRIBE_VALUE_COEF = 4` and the pure
   `bribeValue(value, c) = value × (clampCorruption(c)/100) × 4`, exported.
   Coefficient 4 pins the curve to the old behaviour at rot 50 (2× the visit)
   and doubles it at rot 100 (4×) — the maximum payout sits exactly where
   `arrestChance` is 0.5. A clean lord pays 0 and `acceptBribe` refuses with
   the existing 'the rulers owe nothing yet'.
2. **`bribeAmount` in `gravity_game.js`** — same visit value as before
   (`visitPay` collected at the landing plus `planetProfit` regrown while the
   ship sits), now scaled by `OrbitEcon.bribeValue`. Comments in the game, the
   bot's bribe block and the `gui_shop` button tooltip restate the law.
3. **Contest, lever 1: the bot wants the mission** — `CAREER_MEDIC_VALUE`
   0.8 → 2.5 in `careerAccept`, so a queued medical trip (reward 4.0 at 8
   patients, a 4× fly-by landing on the medic leg, pacification afterwards)
   outranks a plain delivery.
4. **Contest, lever 2 (probe, rejected): the epoch** — `REBEL_CHECK_SECONDS`
   60 → 120 measured *worse* at 2400 s (earned 35.77 vs 47.03, medic trips
   10 → 2, seized unchanged at 21) and was reverted: while rot pins near the
   cap, halving the rolls barely changes the seizure probability inside a
   long run, it only reshuffles the chaotic timeline.
5. **Contest, lever 3: the Empire pays for its frontier** —
   `MEDIC_REWARD_PER_PATIENT` 0.5 → 1.0, so a full two-trip pacification is
   worth 8.0, about two plain deliveries. Every consumer (task offer,
   `settleMedic`, the bot's patient-leg `cashPay`) reads the constant.
6. **Tests** — `politics_test` gains the `bribeValue` formula checks (rot 0
   pays 0, rot 50 pays 2×, rot 100 pays 4×, clamped, no value no bribe) and a
   clean-lord refusal inside the bribe group (fixture rot 10 → 25, amount 4 →
   2); `politics_gui_test` prices the pad-block button at rot 25 → +2.00.
7. **Docs** — README politics paragraph: the bribe law restated, patients home
   at `1.0` each.

## Balance readings

- **The contest closes the gap.** 2400 s × 3 seeds: earned 63.03 with
  politics on vs 64.50 off (97%, was 68% at 0.5.12 and 73% after levers 1-2).
  The map still reshapes — 21 seized, pads 13 vs 24 — but 32 patients home at
  1.0 put 32.0 of the 63.03 into the rivals' purses: the medical quest, not
  the fly-by ramp, is what pays the rebellion back.
- **1200 s is now above the off baseline**: 36.19 vs 35.61 earned (was
  33.92), 8 patients home.
- **400 s stays effectively unchanged**: 15.96 earned, 157 flights, pads 66
  vs 67 — the bribe reprice removed two of the three short-run bribes and one
  pad decision rode along.
- **Bribes behave like the design**: 12 → 5 at 2400 s, 6 → 1 at 1200 s, 3 → 1
  at 400 s. The clean-world free money is gone; what remains is taken on
  rotten worlds (bot gate rot ≥ 25 or amount ≥ 2, and the rot-scaled amount
  only reaches 2 on a world that is already rotten). Arrests 0 in this sample
  — at rot ~25-43 the arrest chance is 0 and the bot's greed is gated below
  the danger line; the arrest path stays covered by the tests.

## Next step suggestion

The earnings gap is closed but the *shape* gap is not: at 2400 s the rivals
end with 13 pads vs 24 in the off run — a third of the frontier is permanently
commercial, and the contest pays cash, not ground. Two ways to read that:
either accept it (seized ground is the rebellion's teeth, and the purse is
whole) or let a pacified planet's rail be claimable again after a cooldown —
that is a rules change (the `seized` flag, `planetBuildable`, the completion
count and the README all say "never buildable again"), so it needs its own
plan file and a test that a reclaimed rail cannot be re-seized into a loop.
Smaller items: the 1200 s run shows only 2 medic trips for 23 rebellions —
the single `MEDIC_SLOT` offer is the bottleneck, and a second offer slot (or
an offer that survives an active contract) would let the mid run contest more
of its frontier; and `dom_smoke` still stops at its pre-existing
commercial-departure fixture, untouched by this stage.
