# 0.4.38 worklog — Voronoi distance sound

## Review

The prior flight synth panned the nearest planet correctly relative to the ship,
including as the ship turned. Missing flight or aim telemetry, however, did not
explicitly fade the corresponding voice until the surrounding phase changed.
Those voices now fade when telemetry disappears, including during replay changes.

## Implementation

- Adapted the triangle/filter/unipolar-LFO voice and distance curves from the
  MIT-licensed experiment-sound-voronoi distance preset (54/53/6). The nearest
  planet center and its Voronoi neighbors sound simultaneously, with bounded,
  reusable, cell-pinned voices and no allocations in telemetry updates.
- Recompute unbounded cell adjacency using live active planet centers; cap the
  neighborhood at 20, keeping its closest members. Unlike the experiment, pan
  follows ship-relative bearing. Distance is center-to-center as in the preset.
- Added Voronoi distance as the fresh generator default, sharing the existing
  aim cues, launch, volume and gesture/lifecycle handling. Existing selections
  and explicit mute remain untouched; the trajectory synth and its flight modes
  remain selectable. A backend switch suspends the discarded graph.
- Updated settings, documentation, browser test and added geometry, mapping,
  polyphony, movement, masks, lifecycle and preference tests.
