# Map generator helper

`map-generator-helper.htm` is the design tool for the planet gravity field: a
roster of groups (gravity, ± margin, how many worlds) that the generator is
supposed to produce. This pass reviewed the page against the shipped rules and
then did its integration list.

## review: what the page got wrong

- **`OrbitTech.maxSpeed` instead of `speedLimit`.** `maxSpeed` is the rail's
  force cap only; a launch is also capped by the battery's energy. `EMC-1` hides
  the difference (force 91.7 vs energy 111), `EMC-2` does not (force 224 vs
  energy 168), so every EMC-2 world was one tier too optimistic.
- **Escaping to infinity.** The colour asked whether the rail beats the well's
  escape speed, but a launch only has to reach the next system. On the softened
  Plummer well that is the potential difference between the surface and a
  typical neighbour, and it is what the shipped observations show: 100 % exits
  at g 1.0, 88 % at 1.5, 18 % at 2.5 (`experiments/hq_gravity_probe.js`). The
  old model painted a world at g 1.0 as "needs EMC-2" when the starter rail
  leaves it.
- **Rolls off the grid.** A previewed value was a raw float; the game only holds
  0.05 steps in −1 … 3.5.
- **The axis could divide by zero.** `gravMin >= gravMax` gave `NaN`
  coordinates and a blank map; a range change left planets outside the axis
  (drawn off the line, never generated); nothing clamped the range to the field.
- **Editing fought the table.** Every `change` rebuilt the table (caret and
  focus lost), a drag rebuilt it twice, a typed gravity was stored unsnapped and
  re-displayed unrounded, `-0.00` showed for a rounded negative, and Backspace
  removed a row while a `<select>` had focus.
- **No way in or out.** No saved-map distribution, no concrete field, no save.

## the integration list

- **saved maps → distribution**: the page loads `saved_maps.js` and reads each
  record through `GravityGame.planetGravity` (never a copy), drawn as an orange
  line over the design; `all saved maps` aggregates every record.
- **generate**: the roster is drawn in order onto the 0.05 grid (seeded per
  draw, so a design repeats), and the result is the blue histogram with the
  generator's own share (grey, sampled over 120 seeds) behind it, the rail-tier
  zones shaded, and the counts per rail plus the light-world count for a first
  landing. Fewer than three worlds at ≤ 1.5 warns: the default career cannot
  open there.
- **save**: the design is written to `map_fields.js` beside the page, replacing
  a record of the same name, and loads back from the picker. A page without
  object URLs (or a `file://` page that asks where to put the file) says so
  instead of throwing.
- **game dropdown**: done — the picker beside the load buttons selects one
  record instead of a random one.
- **playable fields**: not done. The game's gravity is a function of the seed,
  so a designed field has no path into a run; the override, the record kind and
  the address rule are specified in `0.7.35-plan-playable-fields.md`.

## validation

- `node experiments/map_gen_test.js` — pass (0.2 s): the draw is the historical
  one on every seed, the game re-exports the same function, rosters and draws
  stay on the grid and inside their margins, the tier limits are where the rails
  stall and a loaded ship is never easier, the saved maps match
  `planetGravity` planet for planet, the registry round-trips through node and
  through a page context, the committed `map_fields.js` is exactly an empty
  registry, and every `MapGen`/`PlanetGravity` property the page calls and every
  id it looks up exists.
- `node experiments/saved_map_test.js` — pass (31 s, including the new picker
  block: a picked record seats its own run and the notice names it).
- `node experiments/gravity_seed_test.js` — pass (the draw is unchanged).
- `node experiments/dom_smoke.js` — pass (the picker element, the page boot).
- The page itself, booted in jsdom (not committed — the harness is not a repo
  dependency): 29 checks over boot, add/remove/edit rows, drag targets, the
  seeded redraw, the mass change, the saved-map overlay, a collapsed range, the
  save path and reloading the saved design. Two real defects came out of it
  (`URL.createObjectURL` absent; the draw needed 7 planets, not 4) and are fixed.
