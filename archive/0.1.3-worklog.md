# 0.1.3 — worklog: grand tour, Kepler moons & belts, 60 fps live prediction

## 1. review of previous stage (0.1.2 / 0.2.0 integration)

Reviewed `gravity_sim.js`, `gravity_gui.js`, `orbit_view.js` and `orbit_sim.js`
after the `0.2.0` colony economy integration and found four concrete issues:

1. **Port slot compaction vs gravity strengths**: `0.2.0` added `removePort()`,
   which compacts the last port into an abandoned slot and shrinks `portCount`,
   while `gravity_gui.js` stored `portStrength[]` by raw slot index and assumed
   ports never moved slots or shrank. Keyed `portPlanetStrength[]` by parent
   planet (`0…25`) and added `syncPortRoster()` so port strengths survive
   founding and abandonment and invalid selections clear cleanly.
2. **Jerky moving-field updates (`FIELD_DRIFT_PX = 1.5`)**: at `×0.5` trade speed
   ports move `~0.4–1.1 px/s`, freezing the dashed trajectory for `1.5–3 s` at a
   time even though `predict()` costs `< 0.3 ms`.
3. **Trail truncation & live flight time cap**: `recordTrail()` stopped writing
   after `TRAIL_MAX = 4096` points (`68 s`), leaving a gap behind long flights,
   and `LIVE_LIMIT = 240` terminated flights before landing.
4. **HUD pointer occlusion**: `#tradeHud` and `#cargoHud` intercepted pointer
   events across their full bounding boxes, blocking planets `P0`, `P1`, `P15`
   and `P21` at `1280×800` / `1440×900` in a real browser.

## 2. changes implemented

- **`orbit_view.js` — coplanar Keplerian secondary moons & belts**:
  - Added flat SoA buffers (`MOON_CAP = 512`, `TRACK_CAP = 128`, zero per-frame
    allocations) seeded independently from `sim.seed ^ 0x51a7`.
  - Derived each planet's 3D orbital basis (`sysPXX/PXY/QXX/QXY`, `sysCosI`,
    `sysSinI`) from its port ellipse so all double rings (gas/ice giants),
    shepherd rings, outer rubble belts and moons share the exact same inclination,
    node angle, prograde direction and apocenter axis.
  - Integrated mean anomaly by `dir · K · sqrt(|g|) · a^(-3/2) · dt` reading live
    `OrbitScene.planetG`, solved `E - e sin E = M`, and split drawing around
    `drawPlanets()` (`z < 0` back pass, `z >= 0` front pass) plus batched moon
    tracks and `#showMoons` toggle.

- **`gravity_sim.js` — planet bases, system visits & continuing flight path**:
  - Separated static planet systems (`setPlanets()`) from gravity centres
    (`setField()`): `spawn()` and `impact()` always use static planet cores in
    both `planets` and `ports · moving` modes.
  - Added exact rotated-ellipse `markSystemsAt()` shared by `traceProbe()`,
    `launch()` and `step()`, tracking persistent `visited[]`/`visitedCount`,
    `predVisit[]`/`predVisits`/`predNewVisits`, `landedBody` and
    `canLaunchFrom()`.
  - Added `predictFlight(budgetMs)` to continue the trajectory ahead of the live
    flying cargo up to `FLY_HORIZON = 240 s` on the exact `liveSteps % stride`
    cadence until landing, removed the `LIVE_LIMIT` cutoff, and added in-place
    `2:1` trail decimation in `recordTrail()`.

- **`gravity_gui.js`, `index.html`, `style.css` — adaptive 60 fps, markers & drawer**:
  - Replaced `FIELD_DRIFT_PX` with an EMA frame-time guard (`FPS_DROP_MS = 17.5`):
    recomputes every frame at `>= 60 fps` when centres move, change or fly, and
    steps down cadence + budget if frame time rises above `17.5 ms`.
  - Added persistent mint system/planet visit rings, dashed gold predicted-visit
    highlights, landed launch-pad beacon, and blocked-launch tooltip with both an
    inline directional arrow and an animated world-space vector arrow pointing
    to `landedBody`.
  - Added `#helpDrawer` GUI (`? help`, `close ×`, `reroll`, `h`/`?`, `Esc`),
    panel fold toggle (`−`/`+`), click-through HUD panels, and random startup
    goal `random('tourist', 'emperor’s emissary')`.
