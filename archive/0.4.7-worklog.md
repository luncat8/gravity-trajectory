# 0.4.7 — gravity field view, solar panel shop readout & pre-wait settlement

## Review of 0.4.6

- **Pad cap above `SPEED_MAX` under `expForce > 1`**: `GravitySim.prototype.speedLimit`
	and `padCap` returned `OrbitTech.speedLimit(...)` unclamped, while `speedCeiling`
	and `SPEED_UNITS_MAX` cap at `320 px/s`. At `forceScale = 3` on `EMC-5` + `BAT-5`
	that produced `433 px/s` in `careerPlan`, `careerPad`, `careerShop` and inflated
	`shot` kWh. Clamped both `GravitySim.prototype.speedLimit` and `padCap` at
	`SPEED_MAX` (`320 px/s`), simplified `canFire` to delegate to `speedLimit`, and
	reused `padCap` for the unbuilt-pad preview in `padBlockHtml`.
- **Stale armed grip after career edits**: `clampArmedToPad()` ran only from
	`onExperimentInput()`, leaving `aimCeiling`, `aimSpeed` and `gripLen` stale when
	`wait · 30 min`, pad builds/upgrades, HQ ship refits or cargo load/drop changed
	the pad ceiling while a shot was armed. Wired `clampArmedToPad()` into
	`careerChanged()`, and refreshed the career panel in `syncField()` when
	`scene.trade.speed` moves.

## Changes

- **`gravity_sim.js`**: added `GravitySim.prototype.fieldAt(x, y, out)` (exact
	multi-body Plummer acceleration `MU * gravityScale * s / (r^2 + 3600)^1.5`,
	zero allocation) and exported `SOFT` / `SOFT2`.
- **`gravity_field.js`**, **`orbit_view.js`**, **`index.html`**, **`style.css`**,
	**`gravity_gui.js`**: added `view` (`space` / `gravity field`, `f`, `?view=field`)
	with `<canvas id="f">` layered beneath `#c` and `#g` (`OrbitScene.setBackdrop(false)`
	clears `#c` transparent while keeping orbits, moons, links, routes, planets,
	ships and pointer hit-testing on `#c`, and `drawWells` hides idle single-body
	well rings in field view). Hue encodes pull direction (`fract(atan2(ay, ax) / 2pi)`)
	and lightness encodes potential gradient magnitude (`0.56 * g^1.5 / (g^1.5 + 40^1.5)`
	fading into `#02060c`). Uses a WebGL 1 full-screen triangle shader when available
	(auto-dropping 4x in resolution if `emaFrameMs > 17.5 ms`, falling back to 2D on
	`webglcontextlost`) or a zero-alloc LUT 1/4-resolution (`ceil(w/4) x ceil(h/4)`)
	2D `ImageData` rasterizer (`~8.4 ms` dynamic on 26 bodies, `0.00 ms` on static
	frames via dirty-state caching).
- **Career panel shop row & `wait · 30 min` settlement (`gravity_gui.js`)**:
	- Extended `waitGain(career, planet, panel)` via `panelWaitKwh` and added
		`panelReadySec(career, planet, pad, panel)` so the `panel` shop row prints
		`+N kWh · ready in N s` from `career.light[planet] * panel.power` and the
		pad's full-cap `launchEnergy`, with `cur -> next` in the button `title` and a
		live `solar +N kWh/wait · ready in N s` countdown on drained pads.
	- Added `waitSettlement(career)` and `#careerWaitInfo` + `careerWait.title` so
		`wait · 30 min` states beforehand the exact net pad kWh (after `batteryLeak`
		+ `solarCharge` capped at capacity), board ageing / rotating offers (turning
		`.warn` with `drops contract!` when waiting would expire the active contract)
		and galaxy visit-profit growth.
- **Tests (`experiments/dom_stub.js`, `gravity_bench.js`, `career_bench.js`,
	`dom_smoke.js`)**: added `createImageData` / `putImageData` to `dom_stub.js`;
	verified `fieldAt === advance/dt`, cardinal/repel hue & gradient lightness
	invariants, 1/4-res LUT accuracy (`max diff 3 / 255`, `mean 0.516`), WebGL
	top-to-bottom `uStep` + slow-frame 1/4 drop + context-loss fallback, panel shop
	readouts across bright/dim worlds, exact agreement between `waitSettlement()`
	and post-click settled kWh/steps/profit, contract-expiry warning, `SPEED_MAX`
	clamping under `fs=3`, and armed grip re-clamping after `careerWait`.
