# 0.3.4 — rule-aware score panel and rival comparison

A compact in-game scoreboard compares the player's run with active rivals without
mixing score details into the gravity controls. The rival bots remain deterministic
and follow the selected game rule: `tour`, `route`, or `money`.

## 1. Layout

- Stack a game-only score panel above the orbital-trade background panel in the
  upper-left HUD column.
- Keep the score panel hidden in lab mode; the trade panel stays in its original
  position when no game is active.
- Keep game setup, replay controls, seed and purse in the right cargo panel.
- On narrow screens, compact the left HUD without hiding the score-bearing fields.

## 2. Comparable score rows

Show one row for the player and one for every active rival (`R1`, optionally `R2`).
Use the selected rule's primary score in the bold field and supporting tie-breakers
in the detail field:

- `tour`: systems visited; shots, flight time and purse as details.
- `route`: systems visited and total route distance; shots, flight time and purse
  as details.
- `money`: purse; systems, shots and flight time as details.

The route distance shown for a rival includes its current flight as well as
completed flights. The player and rivals have stable, distinct colors (mint, violet,
rose), with a compact live standing / final result indicator.

## 3. Rule-aware bots

- `off`, `easy`, `normal` and `hard` remain available for each game rule.
- `easy` and `normal` show one rival; `hard` shows two.
- Keep the validated rule-specific bot scoring and difficulty tuning; score rows
  must reflect the same `compareScores` order used to decide the winner.
- Preserve fixed-step lockstep and replay determinism.

## 4. Validation

- Headless UI checks verify the panel's lab/game visibility, per-rule labels and
  score fields, rival count, and solo state.
- Bot checks cover `tour`, `route` and `money`, in-flight route summaries, the
  difficulty ladder, sliced decision parity and contested replay invariance.
- Run gravity prediction, game replay, long-route and colony economy benches.
