# 0.8.8 worklog — space weather visual prototypes (`drafts/prototype/`)

Implements and fixes the standalone visual prototypes requested in
`drafts/prototype/space-weather.md` (stage §1 of `0.8.8-plan-space-weather.md`;
career gameplay rules remain untouched).

## `space-weather-GLM5.3.html` — stationary trails and wind-turn border cracks

- **Un-cleared view canvas:** `render()` composited a translucent LUT image plus
  border highlights and wind arrows onto `#view` without clearing, leaving
  non-moving ghost trails until brighter clouds painted over them (`533,428`
  stale pixels in clear-vs-composite diff). Filling `#070910` at the top of
  `render()` eliminates all ghost trails (`stale: 0`).
- **Border birth-darkness crack on wind turn:** `texShift` zeros exposed border
  strips while interior field texels sit near fade equilibrium
  `EQ = FIELD_RATE / FADE` (`τ = 25 s`), so any wind rotation swept a ~40-texel
  dark front inward from the newly exposed edge. `paintField` now seeds texels
  inside `feedRects` (`inFedBorder`) directly at local equilibrium `a * a * EQ`
  (post-turn inflow strip mean `0.26–0.37` vs `0.02–0.06` before).
- **Integer-shift band frame:** band spawn, border feed and HUD readout use
  exact accumulated integer shifts `(shiftIX, shiftIY)` matching `texShift`, and
  `bandTick(dt)` retires bands exiting either side or stalling >8 s when wind
  turns parallel.

## `space-weather-a.html` — wind direction change without redraw

- `updateWind()` previously called `reset()`, wiping all three panels whenever
  the direction slider moved, and `startBand`/`stepField` assumed constant wind
  via `WIND_SPEED * time` and `windX * time`.
- Replaced time-multiplied wind offsets with integrated `(driftX, driftY)` and
  an immutable per-band normal frame `(bandNx, bandNy, bandC, bandMin, bandMax,
  bandBest, bandStall)` captured at band birth. Panel 02 (`field.index === 1`)
  also seeds uncovered inflow weight `(1 - coverage)` near equilibrium so wind
  turns introduce no dark inflow wedge. Panel energy ratio across an instant
  wind turn is `1.000` on all three panels.

## `space-weather-2clouds-c55h.html` — +50% wind at 0, speed slider, two bar options

- Added `windMul = 1 + 0.5 * (1 - mix)` (`1.5×` wind speed at slider `0`, `1.0×`
  at `1`, verified `12.000 / 8.000 = 1.500`), integrated through `driftX`,
  `driftY` so moving the slider never jumps existing clouds.
- Added `speed` slider (`0..4×`, scaling motion, evolution and generation),
  `bar 1` / `bar 2` mode selector, and `band` trigger; `resize()` now preserves
  existing `W` and `B` history instead of clearing on window resize.
- `bar 1` shifts band layer `B` in-place by integer grid steps `(ix, iy)` from
  sub-cell accumulators `(accX, accY)`, writing FBM-modulated wispy borders
  strictly on exposed windward edges (`sx >= GW || sy >= GH`) with queued
  re-spawn while straddling the border.
- `bar 2` (ported from `7812a90f1a80`) samples a drift-frame stripe in
  `(x + driftX, y + driftY)` with value-noise-modulated width and ragged centerline
  and adds `+ bandValue2(x, y) * 0.7` directly onto `baseField`, amplifying live
  underlying cloud cores along the bar.

## `space-weather-shape.html` — smooth polar Perlin rounds, metaball round-band, tuning & copy

- Replaced `MODE_K = [2, 3, 4, 5, 7]` angular Fourier harmonics (whose `k²`
  curvature terms created regular sinusoidal flower/gear ripples) with circular
  sampling of 3D quintic Perlin gradient noise (`(nx0 + rho cos θ, ny0 + rho sin θ, nz)`)
  plus 2-pass cyclic binomial smoothing for organic, non-periodic polar rounds.
- Evaluates clouds, curved ribbon bands (`KIND_RIBBON`), and the new 20–40
  smaller noise-round bands (`KIND_ROUNDS`, default `30` rounds wrapped
  periodically across the cross-wind span) into a preallocated `L4/L2/L1`
  metaball field (`~3.9 ms/frame` at `1920×1080` in V8, zero steady-state heap
  allocations), extracting closed Marching Squares loops with uniform arc-length
  resampling (eliminating grid-vertex double-knot kinks) and `quadraticCurveTo`
  + `ctx.fill('evenodd')` across `2..8` dynamic contour bands.
- Added live `adjust` panel (`weather bands`, `contour bands`, `band rounds`,
  `round size`, `band scatter`, `metaball`, `polar noise`, `polar detail`,
  `clouds`, `cloud size`, `morph`, `band type: both / rounds / lines`, `reset`)
  and `copy settings` button exporting `DEFAULTS`-compatible JSON to clipboard
  and `#cfgOut`.
