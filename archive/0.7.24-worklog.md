# 0.7.24 — Helper profit prices only the shot's own wear · auto-repair on landing

- The helper's profit scans (`best profit`, `profit + EMC`, `quest scan`, untargeted scores, book seeds and the launch-list rows) price through one table: `moneyFacts` in `gui_book.js`. It subtracted the pre-flight repair — the repair the launch button pays first when a combined `repair & launch` is affordable — from every shot's `net`, so a scan of a worn ship ranked and printed shots as if each of them had to buy the player's pre-existing wear. `net` is now `cash − fee − exit − wear`: only the repair cost the shot itself adds (launch + space + landing wear, `forecastWearCost`, plus the mandatory full repair the shot makes unavoidable stays in the exit budget, not in `net`). The pre-flight repair still gates `launchable` and still reduces the purse handed to the exit pricer; it is the launch control's action, not the route's cost.
- Verified numerically in a stub page: at condition 0.5 with the combined action affordable, `traceMoney(...).net` equals `cash − fee − wear` exactly (repair-now 1.00 excluded, route wear 0.36 kept). The aim preview pill (`predictedCareerEconomics`) still itemizes and nets the repair — it is the launch button's own breakdown, not a ranking, and was left alone.
- New footer checkbox `auto-repair` (default checked, sits under `pause on landing`, career-only, hidden in lab/route/during playback, `scene.autoRepair()` on the test contract). On your landing (`endGameFlight`, slot 0 only) with the career open, not arrested and the ship worn, it buys the same full repair the repair button buys — recorded as the same `act: repair` action so saved runs replay identically — while the purse covers it and keeps one cheap way off any planet (`commercialMinFee()`, the reserve the bot keeps). At 0 % condition the whole purse may serve, mirroring the bot. When it cannot be afforded the ship is left alone and the notice says why. Disabled in both replay modes (the recorded repair lands through the action log) and for a bot-controlled player, like `pause on landing`.
- `experiments/auto_repair_test.js` covers the checkbox default, the purse gate, the reserve floor, the whole-purse case at 0 %, the toggle, and a real launch→land that auto-repairs. `dom_smoke` fixtures that expect a worn landing (careerEnv, buildEnv, 0.4.17 commercial fixture, 0.7.2 launch-control fixture) now fly with the setting off; the careerEnv fixture asserts it defaults checked and visible.
- The scan tooltips and the README helpers table say what the scans price now.
