## WS1 — exit margin (in progress)

- Opening and bot exit probes now try 75% of the cap and then the cap, over
  16 bearings. New-starter eligibility requires the 75% margin; existing
  rails can still depart at full power. The starter-build path also checks the
  margin, rather than just reporting `starterCanExit`.
- First-site validation uses the same probe in the field with planned HQs
  pinned to 1g. This matters for seed 2: the drawn-field-only check wrongly
  rejected a site that is safe in its actual first-flight field.
- `career_start_fairness` default: 8/8 buildable and exitable, 7/8 within
  the 0.55 band. The alternative-HQ run for seed 7 at 13 planets / 3 seats
  reported best ratio 0.49 (candidate space 220 HQ sets). The oracle prunes
  using drawn-field light flags, so verify its pruning is sound under HQ
  pinning before treating that result as a proof. The original all-seeds
  fair assertion remains and fails. Drawn blocked share: 24/100 (2.4 per 10).
- The 13/3 and 13/4 matrices over seeds 1–8 measured 4.6 and 6.1 seconds
  with the stricter probe, above the existing 4-second mean-time limit. The
  limit and fairness assertions remain unchanged; search performance needs
  attention.
- Pinned the comms bribe fixture to a non-A.R.F. HQ: seed 7's selected HQ
  moved to the A.R.F., whose refusal intentionally hides that chip. Re-pinned
  the 600-step balance-knob hash for the changed bot exit probe; the test
  still compares omitted and explicit ×1 settings bit-for-bit.
- `career_exit_margin_test.js` covers full-power-only starter rejection,
  margin-safe building, and a full-power departure from an existing rail.
  `career_departure_test.js` measures physical blocking, not the new stricter
  build margin; a commercial full-power exit can still be affordable.
