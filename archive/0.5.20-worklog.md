# 0.5.20 worklog — helpers on more than one player, a skill switch that keeps the run

Four notes from play, two of them marked "probably solved" by the player. The
review found the two open ones, and the two "solved" ones had one defect each
left: a difficulty radio that *started* a run from the lab and a career restart
that wiped the map to change a bot's skill.

## What the notes asked for

1. "Changing player causes bugs with trajectory and auto helpers — probably not
   properly supporting multiple players or lists. With the 2nd player: two
   trajectories at once after switching to another player, returning and
   pressing helper again. Also unchecking the bot box does not allow the auto
   helpers until the player is switched."
2. "(probably solved) changing bot difficulty is slow — it looks like it
   recalculates something."
3. "(probably solved) pressing best profit lowers the profit in some cases; the
   game is paused. I expect it to compare new random searches with the existing
   routes and only improve when nothing changes."

## Changes

1. **A bot box flip refreshes the page, not only the row** (`gui_game.js`).
   `setSlotType` handed a career player over with `applySlotTypeNow` and then
   refreshed only `syncScoreControls`; the helper buttons, the launch gate and
   the clear button read *who flies the selected slot*, so unchecking the box on
   the selected rival left `auto scan`/`best profit` disabled until another row
   was clicked. The career branch now runs the flat `refreshGameUi` (which calls
   `syncScoreControls` itself) and `applySlotTypeNow` no longer refreshes a
   second time.
2. **One human trajectory at a time** (`gui_game.js`). `selectSlot` saved the
   leaving player's aim but left that player's trace in the sim, and the route
   layer draws every non-selected player's *plan* — so after a round trip the
   canvas showed the other human player's line beside the selected one ("two
   trajectories after pressing helper again"). The leaving human slot's
   `predCount` is cleared now: its plan, best and armed aim survive in
   `slotPlans`/`slotAim` and are re-traced on return, while bot rivals' plans
   stay visible as opponents. Two players can no longer share one canvas trace.
3. **`tweak best` works wherever a shot is armed** (`gui_helper.js`). Its gate
   read `activeRouteBest()`, which only the route rule ever fills, so in career,
   tour and money the button was permanently disabled while its own handler
   (`helperStartTweak`) already supported an armed aim. One `helperTweakBase()`
   now answers for the button, its label, and the press: the route best in
   `route`, the selected player's armed shot everywhere else (a career or lab
   aim), and the group tag prints the shot the nudge starts from.
4. **The launch list follows the company** (`gui_book.js`). The row cache key
   held the book version, the field tag, the landing, the rail charge and the
   purse — all of which two companies can share (two empty books, both landed,
   both purses equal), so a switch could keep the previous company's saved shots
   on screen. The career object is part of the key now.
5. **Difficulty is a skill switch, not a world rebuild** (`gui_game.js`,
   `gravity_bot.js`). `setBot` always called `startGame` — in a live run that
   rebuilt the seeded world and every career (the "recalculates something"
   pause), and in the lab it *started* a run from a difficulty radio. Now:
   - the lab only records the level for the next run;
   - a live switch calls the new `GravityBot.setLevel`, which re-skills the
     controllers and drops the cached/sliced decisions (`forgetPlan`) made at the
     old level — the seed, clock, purses, pads and companies stay;
   - outside a career the level also fields its rival count on the spot
     (`setRivals`, a seeded free start planet, and the active slot falls back to
     YOU when its rival is dropped); a career keeps its companies and only their
     skill changes, because a company has a purse, pads and a history;
   - `off` is still a roster reset (its level fields no rivals), so it restarts;
   - a roster pinned by the scoreboard's `+`/`-` is never overridden by a level.

## Validation

- `node experiments/helper_multiplayer_test.js` — new: a bot box flip enables
  the helpers on the spot (both rows), the tweak runs on an armed career shot,
  one human trajectory survives a three-way player round trip (route), a
  difficulty switch keeps seed/clock/purse and fields hard's two rivals in under
  a millisecond, a career keeps its companies across easy/normal/hard, and six
  paused `best profit` presses on seed 520383 never lower the predicted net.
- `node experiments/multiplayer_career_test.js` — still `MULTIPLAYER CAREER OK`
  (R1 fires with its own purse/pads, helpers scan from its planet, a switch
  cancels a running scan).
- `node experiments/dom_smoke.js` — full suite green, including the route
  multi-user plans/bests, `YOU=bot` route planning, "money & rivals UI:
  hard R1/R2 + solo verified", the challenge/replay links and the career gates.
- `node experiments/route_bench.js` — the one failure (`wall trace: whole trace
  92.8 ms exceeds 60 ms`) reproduces on the pre-change tree: a machine-speed
  timing assertion, not a regression.
- Best profit was also re-checked by hand on the seed from the previous session
  (six presses, the launch-book seed wins four of them, net never falls).
- `node experiments/career_level_probe.js` — new: with a rival company already
  in the air, career normal→hard costs 34 ms and keeps the seed, the selected
  player, the roster, both HQs, every purse and pad charge; the flying ship keeps
  its trail (119 points and counting), the clock never walks back, the bot log
  records `level · hard`, and hard→easy back costs 25 ms with the scoreboard
  radios following on their own.
- `node experiments/helper_multiplayer_test.js` (with the level probe added to
  the README `experiments/` list) is the pair a play pass should tap first.
