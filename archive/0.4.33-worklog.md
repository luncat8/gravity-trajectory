# Mass-dependent research fees

- Reviewed the previous science change: zero easy-direction weights made heavier designs no more expensive, but also left their fees flat; the monotonicity test did not seed tank and battery hulls. Seeded those families in the test.
- Applied a stable power-law mass factor to the whole engine and tank research fee, with 1 t and 0.5 t reference masses. Doubling mass divides the fee by 1.5 when the underlying research work is unchanged; decreased difficulty can make the saving greater. Nonpositive mass cannot be researched.
- Showed the factor and its rule in the science panel. The cost plot now colors all cells by actual fee, including recombinations, with the hull outline retained.
- Tested the 2× and 4× mass ratios, research stability, invalid mass, all-family monotonicity, fee payment and UI.

Validation: career bench, DOM smoke, game bench, career departure and guidance tests pass; `git diff --check` passes.
