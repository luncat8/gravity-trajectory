# Multiplayer pause and career preview

Reviewed the existing rule table and GUI wiring. The table already specified career's full-flight horizon and pause support, but the GUI only used sliced full-horizon prediction in route mode and still stored one global pause flag.

## Changes

- Career aimed previews use resumable, full-rule-horizon trace jobs, including after selecting another player and returning. Moving fields do not restart unfinished snapshot jobs.
- Human slots independently hold/release time. The shared pause is their aggregate; launch, resume and bot handover cannot release another player's hold. Controls reflect the selected slot; bots cannot press pause.
- Initial and end-of-flight auto-pause apply to all human slots. The fixed-step batch stops at a human landing rather than advancing the rest of the frame first.
- Turret queues and reserved energy belong to slots. Queued shots fire for their owner even when another slot is selected; reset/removal/handover release reservations.
- Multiplayer wait releases the selected player's hold and uses ordinary shared-clock stepping, rather than fast-forwarding past other human ships/holds. The button explains this behavior. Single-human eased wait observes pause and restores landing pause when complete.
- Cleared transient burn aiming on selection changes.
