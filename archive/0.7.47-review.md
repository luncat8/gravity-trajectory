## Archived simulation patch

`archive/temp/1.patch` proposes three independent hot-path changes:

- fuse ellipse entry and planet collision scans, caching squared collision radii;
- update dwell counters through a preallocated list of open planets rather
  than scanning every planet on every physics step;
- omit repeated wall-clock reads for the effectively unbudgeted full trace.

Reproduced the archived patch's `two_leg_test` failure in an isolated copy.
Adding only `legProbe.impactR2.set(sourceSim.impactR2)` to `prepareLegProbe`
makes all 35 checks pass. The earlier worklog's attribution to the inline
inside check was incomplete: the planner copies geometry manually, bypasses
`setPlanets`, and leaves the new collision cache zeroed. Its second-leg
traces therefore miss collisions.

Keep the open-row list and full-trace clock change. Skip the fused geometry
scan/cache: it duplicates `stepInsideAt`/`impact` and adds a cache invalidation
obligation to every copied probe. The smaller change keeps geometry shared
with existing callers and creates no per-step allocations. Finite-budget
traces retain their clock checks.

`route_log_test` independently full-scans scripted ellipse membership and
checks logs after every slice: overlapping systems, exits, re-entry, masks,
full log buffer, restart, and body-only collisions. Existing harvest and
quest tests cover economic and copied-probe integration.

  its budget. H calls this from report rendering as if it were read-only;
  merely reading H spent physics work outside the frame budget. A fresh
  search's progress became 0.0041667 on the first report. Zero/negative

  has already drained planning; they do not validate that decision lifecycle.
- The search has atomic units larger than a frame: eight light probes, a
  complete home fan or HQ proposal, and set construction/ranking chunks.
  An 8 ms argument is a cooperative target, not a hard responsiveness bound.

  The EMC-3 branch also accepts planets with a full-power EMC-2 exit, and
  accepts an EMC-3 full-power-only exit as that class. Recalibrate the intended
  heavy classes before changing roster exclusions: these determine which
  fairness failures tests are allowed to skip.
