  than scanning every planet on every physics step;

Adding only `legProbe.impactR2.set(sourceSim.impactR2)` to `prepareLegProbe`
makes all 35 checks pass. The earlier worklog's attribution to the inline
inside check was incomplete: the planner copies geometry manually, bypasses
`setPlanets`, and leaves the new collision cache zeroed. Its second-leg
traces therefore miss collisions.

Keep the open-row list and full-trace clock change. Skip the fused geometry
scan/cache: it duplicates `stepInsideAt`/`impact` and adds a cache invalidation
obligation to every copied probe. The smaller change keeps geometry shared
with existing callers and creates no per-step allocations. Finite-budget
traces retain their clock checks.

checks logs after every slice: overlapping systems, exits, re-entry, masks,
full log buffer, restart, and body-only collisions. Existing harvest and
quest tests cover economic and copied-probe integration.
