# 0.4.20 worklog — commercial EMC button, asymptotic visit profit, continuous career clock

## review of 0.4.19 (before touching anything)

- 0.4.19 shipped the EMC economy (claims, usage tax, turning barrels, flying
  rivals) but left two holes the user hit immediately:
  - The commercial rail was only an *implicit* fallback in `padAt`: no
    button, and `careerOverText`/`#launch` hid behind the stranded state, so
    after landing on a padless planet the 3.00 launch looked unavailable.
    On a planet with your own (or a rival's) EMC the commercial rail could
    never be chosen at all.
  - The visit market was `min(age·k, 0.75)`: a hard cap of 0.75 reached
    after 120 s.
- The queued turret shot (barrel wind-up with the clock frozen) also fought
  the goal of a continuously living world: rivals froze between flights.
- Improvement kept from the review: instead of replacing `launch` with
  buy/evac on stranded planets, one always-visible `#planetActions` row
  (buy / commercial / evacuate) now sits beside it whenever the ship is
  down — one visibility rule, no mode swaps.

## what changed in 0.4.20

### 1. Commercial EMC-1 as an explicit choice (`gravity_game.js`, GUI)

- `career.commercialPlanet` + `setCommercial(career, planet)` mark the one
  planet whose commercial rail the ship stands on. `padAt`/`padOwner` check
  the choice first, so the 3.00 rail is selectable on **every** planet —
  even above your own or a rival's EMC — and selecting it never claims the
  planet and never blocks building (`buildGear` clears the choice when your
  first own catapult appears on the selected planet; `placeShip` and
  `careerEndFlight` clear it on arrival).
- New `#planetActions` row in `index.html`: `#launchBuild` (`buy EMC-1 ·
  9.00`), `#launchCommercial` (`commercial EMC-1 · 3.00`, an on/off toggle
  with the `.on` state), `#launchEvac`. Switching the rail re-clamps an
  armed drag through `placeGripFromSpeed()`, relocates a `.on` sit-out, and
  the aim readout / launch button print `commercial launch · 3.00` /
  `launch · commercial 3.00`. The launch gate now only blocks when the
  career is lost or the standing rail cannot lift the ship at all
  (`padLift < SPEED_MIN`), which fixes the "no 3 cr launch" bug for every
  stranded state.
- `exitCostAt` now also considers the usage tax of a `SPEED_MIN` shot off a
  rival's rail, so the predicted-profit subtract (`predExitCost`) is the
  true cheapest exit (3 commercial / tax / 9 build / 6 evac).

### 2. Visit profit: max 3.00, asymptotic (`gravity_game.js`)

- `MONEY.cap` 0.75 → 3 and `planetProfitAtStep` is now
  `-cap·expm1(-age·k/cap)` with `k = 0.00625` kept, so the early slope is
  unchanged and the cap is reached only at infinity: 8 s ≈ 0.0496, 80 s ≈
  0.461, 200 s ≈ 1.022, 480 s ≈ 1.896, 2000 s ≈ 2.953; the seeded opening
  print is `+0.24`. Profit pill thresholds retuned (gold ≥ 100¢, mint ≥
  50¢, pale ≥ 14¢, full scale 300¢); help text says so.

### 3. Bot companies (`gravity_bot.js`)

- Fixed a real solvency bug: the padless-landing gate in
  `careerScoreFlight` ignored *this flight's* launch fee and exempted
  same-planet bounces (`body !== origin`), so bots paid 3.00 commercial
  fees to bounce in place and went broke (trace: P2→P2 at purse 5.00 →
  3.43 → 1.52). The gate is now `purse − fee + cash >= exit + reserve +
  exit·0.5`, bounces included; a first attempt that reserved exit cash
  when *building* on the planet under the ship was reverted — after a build
  the next exit is free, and the reserve only bled fees (recorded in
  `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`).
- Rival careers plan with the commercial fallback too (switch via
  `setCommercial`, then re-check `padLift`/fee, retry both plan passes,
  clear the choice when nothing plans), battery energy-waits only apply on
  own pads, and the live rivals are staggered 3 s apart
  (`CAREER_RIVAL_PHASE`, `gapPhase`) so their plan searches do not spike on
  the same frame.
- Result: with the harder 9.00 pads even the 6.00 grind now completes all
  26 pads on every bench seed (0.4.19 only guaranteed a second pad).

### 4. Time: free launch at any moment, pause on landing (GUI)

- The turret queue is deleted (no `turret*` state anywhere): `launch()`
  fires immediately for everyone. The career career clock now runs
  continuously while landed — rivals fly, pans charge, the trade sim
  advances at `stepsDone·DT·trade.speed` — and the 30-minute wait steps
  the rivals too (settled visit gain can therefore fall short of the gross
  `waitSettlement` preview; kWh and step counts still settle exactly).
- `careerPaused` freezes the whole career world (rivals included). A
  landing pauses it iff `pauseOnLanding` (default **on**; footer checkbox
  `#careerPauseLand`, toggling while landed applies at once). The footer
  `#careerClock` button shows `⏸ clock`/`▶ clock` and toggles manually; a
  launch or wait always unpauses. The end-of-flight line reads `impact
  P<n> · clock paused` so the frozen world is never a surprise.
- EMC barrels no longer rotate: `galaxy.barrel` rests along the last shot
  fired from that planet (`careerBeginFlight` records `sim.angle`), with a
  seeded rest angle before the first shot; while aiming, your standing
  barrel follows the aim. `drawCareerEmc` uses `barrelAngle()`.
