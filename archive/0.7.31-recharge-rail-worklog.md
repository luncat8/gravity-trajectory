# 0.7.31 — Blocked-rail panel shop (review §5 topic 2, last piece)

## What changed

All in `gravity_bot.js`, and all inside the "no payable plan" recharge path:

1. **Reservation blindness removed from the panel question.** `careerChargeAfter`
   and `careerRechargeExit` take `ignoreReserve`. The *sit* forecast still
   subtracts `pad.reservedKwh` (kWh promised to a planned launch is not
   available to leave on); the *panel shop* asks what the pad would fly after a
   refit, so it reads the pad's own charge. Without this, a pad whose whole
   charge was promised could never be refitted — the state where a refit matters
   most.

2. **Exit-cache ordering.** The panel shop is now asked *before* the resident
   forecast. `careerExitSpeed` caches by pad + speed cap, so asking the resident
   panel first cached a `0` against caps the stronger panel shares, and the
   panel forecast then reused that `0` (panel true in isolation, false in
   sequence). `careerRechargeExit` also builds caps from the probe's
   `forceScale` (`this.departureSourceSim || this.probe`) rather than the live
   sim's, so a forecast is not sampled on a different gravity than the launch it
   predicts.

3. **Panel horizon is the long one.** The shop forecasts
   `CAREER_RECHARGE_TRIES` (8) sits, not the remaining ordinary sits. A starter
   panel cannot open an interplanetary shot inside 4 sits — map 10's P4 needs
   SP-2 — and that is exactly the pad the shop exists for. This is what the
   original map-10 replay showed: `P11 SP-2 · recharge a blocked exit` at
   t+81215 after fifteen no-shots at 0.08–0.30 kWh.

4. **One shop per patience cycle.** `r.panelTried` marks the cycle spent once the
   shop has *engaged* (panel priced and affordable, then bought or refused by
   the forecast). A shop refused only for *price* has not engaged: the same wait
   that charges the pad is what makes the panel affordable, so the next sit may
   try again. A commercial-fare wait never shops at all — the money it protects
   is the money a panel would spend.

5. **Dead-pad honesty.** A pad whose rail cannot leave even at a full battery is
   not "blocked patience", it is finished; the tow is the answer. A rail that
   can leave but only slowly is the refit case. The long-wait sit
   (`careerRechargeWaiting`, `rechargeTries`, `careerRailLive`, `careerRailWait`)
   was implemented, measured, and **removed**: the bench priced those long waits
   below the marginal self-bounce departures they enable (seeds 7/10/12 lost
   0.010–0.016/s each, mean 0.0213 → 0.0204), so only the refit is kept.

## Root causes this closes (review §5)

- **Topic 2 — destination battery blindness / commercial trap**: the "no payable
  plan" state no longer spends the ordinary sit budget four times and then pays a
  tow or a losing ticket. On map 10 the company's own pad now gets the panel it
  needs; sits fall 17 → 5 and no-shots 30 → 3 on the same 120 473 steps.
- **Topic 4 — reachability-blind contracts / Capital P1 (partial)**: the
  dead-pad/refit split is now explicit, but the Capital `P1` no-exit case is
  still unfixed.
