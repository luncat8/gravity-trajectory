# 0.4.36 worklog — trajectory sonification listening pass

## Review of the previous state

0.4.35 added the trajectory synth backend: profit chord, length ticks, EMC
beacon, visit fly-through and two flight mappings. The worklog's next step was
a headphone listening pass over the default mix. This iteration instead makes
the sonification usable without a screen: the aim becomes audible as an
orientation cue, flight becomes true left/right volume by distance and
direction, and the fly-through phrase gains a launch note and a closing fifth.

## Implementation

- `gravity_trajectory_sound.js`:
  - Flight now splits the tremoloed signal into explicit left and right gains
    summed through a channel merger: `level = 0.02 + proximity * 0.46` (near
    planets are loud, far planets are quiet) times a constant-power left/right
    balance from the planet's screen-side offset. Distance and direction are
    heard as left/right volume, with low distance louder.
  - New aim orientation voice: a quiet sine drone on the aim bus. Pan is the
    aim angle (`cos(aimAngle)`), pitch spans one octave above the launch
    planet's pentatonic note by pull strength (`aimSpeed / aimCeiling`), and
    loudness grows with pull. It plays only while the grip is pulled, stays far
    below the profit chord and EMC beacon, and fades when there is no aim data.
  - Fly-through opens on the launch planet note and closes the destination on a
    destination note plus a bright fifth; visit notes are slightly longer.
  - Blocked EMC beacons are a touch quieter; the destination event is louder.
- `gravity_gui.js`: `updateSoundTelemetry` now passes `aimBody`, `aimAngle`,
  `aimSpeed` and `aimCeiling` to the aim telemetry. The outcome aim hint
  mentions the orientation drone.
- `experiments/trajectory_sound_test.js`: covers the aim orientation drone
  (pan direction, pull pitch, silence without aim data), explicit left/right
  flight gains (near louder, side balance, distant planet still audible on its
  side), instrument mode raising pitch and lowering volume with distance, and
  fly-through launch/destination/closing-fifth cues. The fake context now
  initializes `suspends = 0` and supports `createChannelMerger`.
- Docs: `0.4.35-plan-trajectory-sound.md` documents the orientation layer, the
  left/right flight gains, the launch/closing fly-through notes and a "Playing
  by ear" section describing how the cues combine for a player who cannot see
  the screen.

## Notes

The orientation drone is deliberately quiet: it orients without masking the
profit chord, length ticks or EMC beacon. A full per-point trajectory scan
(pitch = local gravity or speed along the predicted path) remains a future
variant; nothing here changes physics, economics, aim inputs, replays or bots.
