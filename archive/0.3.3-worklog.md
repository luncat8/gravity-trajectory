# 0.3.3 worklog — per-planet money rebalance & bot competitor

Started by reviewing the previous `0.3.2` money implementation against `draft.txt`:

1. **Previous `0.3.2` gaps found**:
   - Money was never truly scarce (`P0 = 12.00` + `+0.25` landing subsidy + `+3.00`
     tour bonus meant almost any chain of hops stayed above `10.00`).
   - Payouts were tied to run-wide first-entry intervals rather than **time since
     each individual planet was last visited**, so revisiting or routing toward
     long-neglected regions of the map carried no strategic signal.
   - Planet-side profit labels on the canvas (`draft.txt`: *"show visit profit
     near planets in this mode"*) and a standalone `money` rule were missing.

## step 1 — per-planet growing profit market (`gravity_game.js`, `gravity_sim.js`)

- Replaced flat landing/tour subsidies with a shared 26-planet market
  (`createMarket`, `lastVisitStep[0..25]` initialized to `-4800` steps = `-40 s`,
  `MONEY = { cost: 1, k: 0.00625, initSec: 40, cap: 0.75, purse0: 3 }`).
  Every planet opens at `+0.25` (4 planets break even on a `1.00` launch), drops
  to `+0.00` when harvested, regrows `+0.05` after `8 s`, `+0.50` after `80 s`,
  and caps at `+0.75` after `120 s`.
- Added `stepInsideAt` and per-pass `predLogPlanet` / `predLogStep` /
  `predLogCount` tracking in `gravity_sim.js` (`routeBeginAt` / `routeStep`), plus
  `predictFast` (skips path-buffer writes for headless bot sweeps) and fixed-step
  `stepOne()`.
- Caught a subtle 1-step off-by-one in `stepOne()` via `game_bench.js`: checking
  `liveSteps >= stepLimit` only at the top of `stepOne()` caused replayed aborts
  to run an extra no-op `(N + 1)`-th loop iteration in `advanceGameOneStep`,
  nudging `game.clockSteps` by `+1` step (`0.0003` purse drift on subsequent
  visits). Finishing immediately when `liveSteps >= stepLimit` at the end of step
  `N` (with a `0`-step guard at the top) made live aborts and all four replay
  cadences match bit for bit.
- Added `RULE_MONEY` (`10` launches, ranked by final purse) alongside `tour` and
  `route`, plus `&bot=easy|normal|hard` link codec (omitted when `off`).

## step 2 — deterministic shared-market bot competitor (`gravity_bot.js`)

- Built `GravityBot` (`off`, `easy`, `normal`, `hard` with 2 rivals + top-4 1-ply
  lookahead) using preallocated typed arrays (`probe`, `rivals[0..1]`,
  `lookMarket`, `savedVisited`, `top*` — zero allocation across an entire race).
- Player and rivals step in exact integer `DT` lockstep on `game.clockSteps` and
  harvest from the **same** `game.market`: when a rival sweeps a cluster first,
  those planets reset to `+0.00` on the canvas and the player must steer toward
  long-unvisited planets to win.
- Capped `stepJob` slices by **both** `BOT_STEP_MS = 3 ms` and
  `SLICE_CANDIDATES = 64` (**worst slice 3.98 ms**). Without the candidate-count
  bound, `dom_stub`'s frozen per-frame `performance.now()` would drain an entire
  1,440-candidate `hard` search in one frame; with both bounds, `60fps 30/30`
  holds in live play AND multi-frame `thinking N %` works in headless tests.
- Added `bot.settle` in `endGameFlight` when the player stalls broke before the
  rule's goal or finishes the 10th shot in `rule=money`, so final `WIN` / `LOSS` /
  `TIE` verdicts compare completed runs deterministically across all cadences.

## step 3 — canvas & HUD (`gravity_gui.js`, `index.html`, `style.css`)

- `drawPlanetProfits`: zero-allocation pills beneath all 26 planets using
  preallocated `PROFIT_STRINGS` (`+0.00` .. `+2.00`) and `predHarvestMask`
  (dim when freshly visited, mint/gold as unvisited age grows, bright green on
  predicted route harvests).
- `drawRivalsWorld` & `drawRivalTags`: rival base diamonds, trails, ship
  chevrons, and `R1` / `R2` badges.
- Added `money` rule radio, `rivals` radios (`off`, `easy`, `normal`, `hard`),
  `#rivalRow` / `#rivalTag` (`thinking N %`, live rival status, `WIN` / `LOSS` /
  `TIE`), predicted `+X.XX` profit in aim & HUD readouts, updated `moneyToast`,
  and `copy rival` (`#botCopy`).
