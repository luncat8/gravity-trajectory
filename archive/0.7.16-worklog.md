## the reported defect

«Ensure total corruption increment from bribe is more than same by not visit.
Because while visit profit < 1 corruption not growth, and bribe cleans visit
profit (and adds a one-time corruption increment). By logic a bribe should sum
up to more corruption than not visiting the planet at all.»

Both clauses were true and together they made the button a trap:

- the passive rot read the **value** clock (`planetProfit` →
  `lastVisitStep`), which every landing restarts, so the bribe — always taken on
  a visit, because the window only opens there — was paid for a world whose rot
  clock had just been reset to `0`;
- the same landing also calms the world by `CORRUPTION_VISIT_DECAY = 0.2`;
- the bribe's own rot was `1 + 0.1 · money`, one-time, and could not outrun the
  three effects above.

Measured at `c09b2be` (`26` planets, one normal planet aged `240 s`, read
`300 s` later, mean of seeds `1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11`): never visited `35.86`,
visit only `14.77`, **visit + bribe `16.20`** — a bribe left the world cleaner
than staying away by `19.67`. At a `120 s` age and a `900 s` horizon the gap
was `21.39`; at `600 s`/`900 s` it closed to `+1.01`, still less than the
increment the bribe is meant to add.

## change

1. **A bribe buys a report's silence, so it takes the report back.**
   `market.reportStep`/`market.reportPrev` are the rot clock and one landing of
   history; `advancePolitics` raises rot from `reportStep`, never from the value
   clock, and `acceptBribe` restores `reportStep = reportPrev`. The world then
   rots from the step standing before the landing — exactly as if the ship had
   never come — while the value clock keeps the market moving (what the next
   visitor collects, what the bribe paid for). An unreported visit stamps no
   report, so the restore is a no-op there. A ship sitting on the world ages
   both clocks at half speed, as it aged the one.
2. **The rulers add their own one-time increment.**
   `CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SURCHARGE = 1`, so `bribeCorruption(money) =
   2 + 0.1 · money`. `BASE + SURCHARGE = 2` is exactly the `0.2` a visit calms,
   which is what makes `rot(bribe) > rot(never)` hold at **every** horizon, and
   once both worlds saturate the difference is the whole `1 + 0.1 · money`.
3. **The GUI says it.** `POLITICS_HELP.score` and the bribe button title both
   state that the report is taken back.
4. **Fixtures stamp the rot clock.** `politics_test`, `career_stats_test` and
   the `obs_test` fixtures that hand-place a report now set `reportStep` next to
   `lastVisitStep`; that is the only way a fixture can say «this landing
   reported» without running a flight.

`bribeAmount` (offer `2 ×` value), the arrest roll, the rebellion law and the
visit decay are unchanged. `rot ×` (0.7.6) deliberately does not scale the
bribe: a knob that could push the increment under the report it takes back
would reintroduce this defect.
