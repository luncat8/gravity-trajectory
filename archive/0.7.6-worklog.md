# 0.7.6 worklog — economy, gravity and ship wear

## Previous-stage review

The 0.7.2 wear law made service a substantial running cost, but shot selection
and previews still priced every report as if the device stayed healthy. Flight
end is settled at frame end, so an already-landed ship could also age during
extra world ticks. Fixed that ordering, exact-zero rounding, rival burn order
and a phantom harvest at a flight cap; a hand-flown rival no longer receives
the bot's automatic burn. Landing wear still follows the last harvest.

## Changes

- Raise the visit-profit saturation cap from 3 to 4 credits without changing
  its initial slope (`0.00625/s`). A fully ramped rebellion can reach 16.
- Price bribes at twice the collected-plus-regrown visit value at every
  corruption level; corruption and arrest rules are otherwise unchanged.
- Bias deterministic seeded gravity smoothly down in the `0.6…2.8` middle
  range, retaining the `−1…3.5` bounds and `0.05` grid. HQ, Capital and A.R.F.
  stay pinned to 1. Existing replays may no longer verify after the new field
  and prices.
- Add the career-only experimental `space wear ×0…×3` slider, default ×1;
  it affects space ageing, not launch, landing or impulse wear. A copied
  career link carries a non-default setting as `ws`. A link starts a fresh
  career; it cannot recreate changes made partway through an older run.
- Forecast condition, report failure and incremental repair cost for bot
  shots, burn continuations, the aim readout, saved shots and helper scans.
  A 0%-condition arrival must reserve a full repair before its next exit;
  special-world payouts remain available without a working observation device.
  The aim warns on exhaustion and distinguishes repair-and-launch from the
  menu's launch-only option.
- Re-price the launch list on settings changes, mark entry-only estimates when
  stay-pay is on, cancel incomplete scans when their settings or field change,
  and retain armed traces correctly across book resets without silently clearing
  a stale field. An explicit stale-list clear does not resurrect the old aim,
  even if another shot is saved; a repairable saved shot can be armed at 0%.
- Bound and scroll the flight HUD on short screens, and separate the narrow
  phone columns. This removes existing overlaps that blocked the new slider;
  opening the repair menu brings its choices into view.
- Update help, notes and fixtures, including the 48-world codec boundary and a
  bot replay fixture that matches the page's 26-world default subset.

## Measurements

`SEEDS=1-4 SECONDS=400 STATS=1 PAIRED=1 LOG=0`, two rivals per seed, on
identical page-seated maps (small exploratory comparison, eight companies):

| space wear | ×0 | ×1 | ×3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| profit per company, credits/game s | 0.023 | 0.013 | 0.006 |
| property per company | 6.29 | 4.64 | 3.65 |
| maintenance per company | 1.43 | 4.77 | 7.42 |
| maintenance / income | 11% | 36% | 57% |
| repairs per company | 1.8 | 3.6 | 4.3 |
| mean condition | 82% | 69% | 82% |
| flights / built pads | 50 / 20 | 46 / 18 | 47 / 15 |
| worn out at end | 0 / 8 | 0 / 8 | 0 / 8 |

×0 removes only space ageing; other wear remains. The higher end condition at
×3 reflects more frequent repairs, not less wear. Keep the default at ×1 until
larger paired runs support a balance decision.

Across 130,000 deterministic planet draws, `0.9…1.5` rises from 14.3% to
19.2%, while `1.8…2.5` falls from 16.5% to 11.9%; all 91 grid values occur.
Only a small number of rounded values near the bias boundaries move into or out
of the outer bands.
