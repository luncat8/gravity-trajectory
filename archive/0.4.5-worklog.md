# 0.4.5 Worklog — ceiling-limited drag and an experimental settings panel

## Review of 0.4.4

0.4.4 made the starter rail weak (EMC-1 15 kN, 91.65 px/s dry) and taught the
headless controller to plan only from live pad caps. The gate was right but the
hand-flown aim was not: the drag still spanned 30–320 px/s, so a career player
could pull a 200 px/s shot and only find out at launch that the pad fires 92.
The plan-and-launch mismatch also cost precision — one drag pixel was worth
1.21 px/s everywhere, so a ±2 px/s correction needed sub-pixel mouse movement.

The audit found three smaller problems:

- `careerPadPlate` warned below a magic 90 px/s. That is not an engine limit;
  it flagged the *intended* weak starter pad as broken, and its cache key only
  held `(planet, tier, charge)`. A refit or the new force scale changed the
  printed cap without invalidating the string.
- Nothing repaired a pad whose full battery cannot lift the loaded ship. With a
  lowered catapult force that is a real dead end for the career bot.
- The career help text still promised a plain 30–320 drag.

## Implementation

### 1. The drag range is the pad's live cap

`GravitySim.speedAt(length, ceiling)` / `gripLength(speed, ceiling)` stretch one
full `GRIP_MAX` onto `SPEED_MIN…ceiling` instead of `30…320`. Without the
argument the old map is untouched, so lab, tour, route and money are unchanged.

- `GravityScene.speedCeiling(planet)` = career ? `gravity.speedLimit(planet)`
  clamped to `[SPEED_MIN, SPEED_MAX]` : `SPEED_MAX`.
- Starter EMC-1 dry: 91.65 px/s, so 240 grip px now buy 61.65 px/s instead of
  290 — 0.26 px/s per pixel, 4.7× finer, and every reachable shot can fire.
- `aimReadout` appends `· max N` only when the ceiling binds; the grip dot
  radius uses the same fraction (`gripLen / GRIP_MAX`).
- `snapAim` floors its units at `floor(ceiling * SPEED_STEPS + 1e-9)`, so a full
  drag snaps to the pad's own cap and `canFire` accepts it (no off-by-one at
  91.65 → 92 px/s units).
- `clampArmedToPad()` re-clamps an armed shot after any slider move. The pad
  note remains the last-resort explanation when a pad drains below an already
  armed speed.

### 2. Experimental settings panel

`#experimentGroup` in `index.html`: `g field` `×0…×2` and `catapult`
`×0.25…×3`, both step `0.05`, with outputs and a `g ×… · force ×…` tag.

- `GravitySim.setScales(g, f)` multiplies `mu` inside `advance()` and the rail
  term in `speedLimit`/`canFire`; wells and posts are drawn at
  `strength * gravityScale`.
- `OrbitTech.maxSpeed/speedLimit/canLaunch` take an optional `forceScale`. The
  tech tables are never mutated; energy costs stay unscaled.
- `applyExperiment()` fans both values into `gravity`, `helperProbe` and
  `GravityBot.setScales` — the probe, both rivals and the player sim — so live
  flight, every prediction and every replay agree. `attachWorld` reapplies it
  after a field rebuild.
- Lock rule: `game.on && (career ? replay.on : replay.on || anyShots)`. A career
  run may retune the world while flying (its state is not a shot list); a
  replay always replays the recorded scales, and a flown tour/money run locks
  them as soon as a shot exists.
- Links carry `&gs=`/`&fs=` at two decimals, omitted at `×1`, validated against
  `GravityGame.EXPERIMENT`; a career link restores both before `startGame`, so
  `#game=1&seed=42&rate=1&rule=career&gs=0.8&fs=1.5` replays the same world.

### 3. 0.4.4 audit fixes

- `careerPadPlate` keys on `(planet, tier, charge, ship mass, forceScale)` and
  warns only when the pad cannot fire at all (`speedLimit < SPEED_MIN`) — the
  same rule the bot uses. The magic 90 px/s is gone.
- `careerBetweenFlights` repairs the pad under the ship when its full battery
  still cannot reach `SPEED_MIN`, before it goes shopping. A lowered force scale
  or a heavy contract can no longer dead-end a headless career.
- The career help drawer states that the drag range ends at the live cap.

## Measured results

Bench data is in `experiments/logs/0.4.5-bench.txt`. The career purse gate is
unchanged from 0.4.4: four seeds, 26/26 pads, `CAREER_COMPLETE`, final purse
9.95 / 8.72 / 6.54 / 6.35 (seeds 7 / 31 / 42 / 913). Gravity parity is exact at
`×0.5` (48 shots, worst 0.000068 px), and one `×0.5` step pulls 0.010 px against
0.020 px at `×1`.
