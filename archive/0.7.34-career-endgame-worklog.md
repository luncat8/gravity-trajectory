# Career endgame

The seed 5 recovery review identified that immediate bankruptcy locked out waiting and bribes. The follow-up sets a hard deadline for every company instead of an indefinite human recovery window. Seed 5 exploration tuning is unchanged.

- A negative purse, or a worn-out ship the company cannot afford to repair, starts a recovery window on the simulation clock. Pauses do not count; accelerated waits do. Recovery clears the timer.
- After 120 continuous game seconds, Helper warns the selected company once per episode. At 600 game seconds the company goes out of business if it has not recovered. Rivals, including bots, no longer automatically restart. Bots can retry their ordinary bribe and repair decisions during recovery.
- Liquidation clears the company's planet claims and transfers its EMC equipment to the local commercial operator. The stronger commercial rail stays fully charged, charges the commercial fee, and does not block a surviving player from building there. Capital and seized-planet rules are unchanged.

Validated with `career_wear_test`, `comms_call_test`, `politics_test`, `obs_test`, `balance_changes_test`, and `career_pause_test`. The full career bench and DOM smoke were not run.
