# 0.7.30 — Arrival-charge departure pricing, contested own-pad exemption, and unreachable contract deferral

## What changed

1. **Arrival-charge departure pricing (`gravity_bot.js`)**:
   - Added `careerArrivalCharge(career, planet, flightSeconds)` and passed `GravitySim.gameSeconds(steps)` from `careerScoreFacts` into `careerDeparture`.
   - When forecasting a flight to a remote own pad (`planet !== career.at` and `flightSeconds > 0`), the available departure charge is now estimated from the pad's current free charge plus solar gain over `GravitySim.padSeconds(flightSeconds)`, capped at `pad.battery.capacity`. Short hops no longer assume a full battery on arrival.

2. **Contested own-pad exemption (`gravity_bot.js`)**:
   - In `careerScoreFacts`, `CAREER_CONTESTED_COST` is now applied only when `!own.catapult`. A shared visit is missed harvest, not lost property, so a company is no longer penalized for returning to its own pad.

3. **Unreachable contract deferral (`gravity_bot.js`)**:
   - In `careerNextShot`, when `careerOpenFan` finds no reachable leg from the standing pad, the bot now calls `careerDeferContract` and `GravityGame.refreshBoard` so it does not re-accept and stall on the same unreachable contract.
