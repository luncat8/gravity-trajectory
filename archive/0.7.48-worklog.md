## plan review — changes made before implementing

Measured against the live tables, the draft plan had four problems; the plan
now states the settled spec:

- **Part values were a second price list** (FT-1 at 4 cr, shop 0.61; FT-2 at
  6, shop 1.21). Kits are now priced at their parts' `buildCost`, computed at
  load.
- **A lone tank or engine is no gift** (an engine needs a tank's fuel; a tank
  without an engine is dead mass). Part entries are engine + tank kits.
- **Fitting the gift would break the fair start.** A kit adds 1.5–3.9 t; the
  seat planner proves exits for the starter ship. Gifts are owned, not fitted;
  an owned part refits free, the shop's existing rule.
- **"Science unlocks IMP-2" had no target**: engines, tanks and batteries ship
  fully known, and EMC-3 costs ~28.4 cr of research at the start hull (above
  any 20 cr budget). A science gift is now one free invention on a frontier
  segment per family (EMC-2→EMC-3, beyond IMP-3 / FT-3 / BAT-5), as far out as
  its value pays: 6 cr ≈ a 93–100 kN rail, a 41 kN engine, a 194 kg tank.
- **Cash entries in the pool** spent draw slots on money and contradicted the
  draft's "1+ gifts + the rest as cash"; removed — cash is only the remainder.
- Added from the draft: the `👀 example` checkbox (a sample draw on a seed no
  company uses). Address/link keys follow the house two-letter style
  (`ga`/`gb`/`gh`), and slider moves are pending until the next start, so a
  mid-run rival and a career replay draw the running budgets.
