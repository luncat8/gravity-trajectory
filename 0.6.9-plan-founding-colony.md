# 0.6.9 plan — a landing that founds a colony

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source:
`0.2.0-draft-economy-compare.md` §5 item 3 — *"Does the cargo experiment touch
the economy? a landed container could deliver goods, seed a colony or knock a
port out; or stay a pure physics toy"* — and the decision taken in its §6: "A
landed cargo container seeds a colony on a free planet."
(`drafts/career-economy.md` §2.5.)

## problem

The decision is taken and the law is written, but nothing calls it.

- `orbit_sim.js` has the whole founding: `Sim.prototype.seedColony(planet, time)`
  → `activatePort` gives the planet a founding population, its site score, a
  year of local stock, one ship, a log line (`outpost founded at planet …`) and
  a `LOAD` event, then `refreshPartners` joins it to the network. Its only
  callers are `colony_bench` and `politics_test`.
- The trade world founds colonies on its own (`EXPAND_COST`: a full progress
  ring sends a colony ship to an empty planet); the *player* cannot.
- There is room for it, and a reason. On a 13-planet map 8 to 10 planets carry
  no colony at trade minute 0, and the trade world's own expansion still leaves
  4 to 9 of them empty at minute 2000 (seeds 1–8; 18 and 8 to 13 on 26 planets).
  `makeDelivery` draws both ends of a contract from `trade.colonies`, so those
  planets are never a delivery's origin or destination.

## 1. the rule

One function in `gravity_game.js`, beside `dropContract`:

	foundColony(career, trade)   → the colony, or null
	foundBlock(career, trade)    → '' when it may be founded, else the reason

`foundBlock` is the only place the rule lives. A founding needs all of:

- the ship **landed** (`career.at >= 0`) on a planet that is in play and has no
  colony: `trade.colonyAt(career.at) === null` (`colonyAt` returns the colony
  or `null`);
- not the Capital and not A.R.F.;
- a **loaded delivery** aboard — the open contract is a `TASK_DELIVERY` in state
  `TASK_LOADED`, not a tourist one (`task.tourist`) — of at least
  `FOUND_TONS` tons. Freight founds a colony; passengers and patients do not.

`foundColony` then calls `trade.seedColony(career.at)` — no time argument, so the
colony is founded on the trade clock (`this.t`), the clock `activatePort`
schedules its first event on — and `dropContract(career)`, which clears the
contract, empties the hold and refits the mass.

It is never automatic. `careerEndFlight` does not call it: a landing with cargo
on an empty world changes nothing until the action is taken.

## 2. the price: a decision, not a gift

A founding **costs the contract it spends**: the delivery is dropped and its
reward is lost. That is the whole price, and it is why the action is a button at
the pad (`found colony · P# · drops the contract`) with the lost reward in its
title, and why the aim readout can say so before the launch
(`+0.00 · can found a colony`) when a landing satisfies `foundBlock` apart from
`career.at`.

Because nothing happens without the button, every existing bench stays
bit-identical until a bot presses it (§4).

## 3. what it does inside the career

- **Not a pad.** A founded colony is a colonised planet, not a catapult:
  `coveredPlanets` does not count it, so the completion rule is unchanged.
- **One per planet, ever.** `seedColony` returns the existing colony when the
  planet already has one, and `foundBlock` refuses before that.
- **The world reads it.** The new port joins the network at once
  (`refreshPartners`) and `makeDelivery` can draw it as an origin or a
  destination from then on — a delivery board that grows where the player chose
  is the feature.
- **Determinism.** A founding is a function of the career's own state, so the
  same seed and the same actions found the same colonies; the benches'
  determinism checks gain the colony count at the end of a run. (A career link
  carries its seed and settings, not its shots, so there is nothing to replay.)

## 4. the bot

`careerFoundColony(career, trade)` runs in `careerBetweenFlights`, like a pad
claim, and prices a founding as

	FOUND_VALUE × site score − reward of the dropped contract

with the site score `activatePort` logs (`0…1`). The bot presses the button only
when that is positive and the trace line names it
(`founded P9 · +1.20 · dropped delivery`). No change to the search.

## numbers (starting points, a bench tunes them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FOUND_TONS` | `8` | the freight that must be aboard: a full `HOLD-8` |
| `FOUND_VALUE` | `2.0` | what a perfect site is worth to a bot, in money |

## Validation

- New `experiments/founding_test.js`: a loaded delivery on an empty in-play
  planet founds one colony, empties the hold, drops the contract and does not
  count as a pad for `complete`; a second attempt on the same planet is refused
  with its reason; a tourist or a patient load is refused; the Capital and
  A.R.F. are refused; a landing without the button founds nothing; two runs of
  the same seed and actions end with the same colonies and populations; the bot
  declines a founding worth less than the contract it drops.
- `colony_bench`, `trade_route_mask_test` unchanged (the founding path is the
  existing law, now reachable).
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`, `game_bench`: no new failure name; the
  bench log gains `foundings` per run.
- `career_gravity_bench` A/B with the bot's founding off and on, seeds 1–12 at
  1200 s: foundings, deliveries completed and purse per rival-game — a founding
  must not be free money.

## Scope boundary

No goods hauling into an existing colony (§5 item 3 names "deliver goods" as an
alternative; it needs a per-colony demand model), no knocking ports out, no
change to port growth, and no founding outside `career`.
