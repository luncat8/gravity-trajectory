# 0.6.9 plan — a landing that founds a colony

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source: the one
open decision in `0.2.0-draft-economy-compare.md` §5 item 3 — *"Does the cargo
experiment touch the economy? a landed container could deliver goods, seed a
colony or knock a port out; or stay a pure physics toy"* — and the decision taken
in §6: "A landed cargo container seeds a colony on a free planet."

`orbit_sim.js` already has the whole founding law (`seedColony` → `activatePort`:
founding pop, site score, a year of local stock, one ship, a log line, a `LOAD`
event). Nothing calls it from a landing. This plan is the call site, the state
that makes it safe, and the price that makes it a decision.

## problem

The trade world grows colonies on its own (`EXPAND_COST` ring fills, a colony
ship to the nearest empty planet), but the *player* has no way to found one, so
the one decision taken in the economy comparison — "a landed cargo container
seeds a colony" — has no code. Meanwhile a career routinely lands a full hold on
an empty world for a contract that never existed there, and nothing happens.

## 1. the call site

`careerEndFlight` (`gravity_game.js`) already knows the landing body and the step,
and it already updates the trade world through `refreshBoard(career, trade, step)`.
Add, between the settlement and the board refresh:

\tif (founded = foundedBy(career, sim, trade, step)) settleFounding(career, trade, founded, step);

`foundedBy` returns the body or `-1` and is the only place the rule lives:

- `landed` on a planet that is **in play and has no colony** (`trade.colonyAt(body)
  < 0`), and
- the ship carries a **full hold** of a goods cargo (not tourists, not patients:
  a colony is founded with freight, a `TASK_DELIVERY` of `MIGRANTS` does not
  count), and
- the planet is **not** the Capital or A.R.F., and
- the landing is clean (`END_IMPACT`, not an exit or an abort).

`settleFounding` calls `trade.seedColony(body, step / 120)` and consumes the
cargo (`career.ship.cargoTons = 0`, `refitShip`), so the goods are spent.

## 2. the price: it must be a decision, not a gift

A free colony on every empty landing would make the trade world a vending
machine, and it would break the one promise the launch book makes (a stored shot
prices the same as a fresh trace, and a founding changes the world under it).
So a founding:

- **costs the contract it interrupts.** If the hold was loaded for a delivery,
  the contract is dropped (`dropContract`) and its reward is lost — the player
  chose a colony over the payout. The arm is offered as a **button at the pad**
  (`found colony · P# · drops the contract`), never automatically, so the
  decision is the player's and the aim readout can price it (`+0.00 · founds a
  colony`).

That also keeps every existing bench bit-identical unless a bot or a helper
presses the button.

## 3. what it does inside the career

- **Covered for the win.** A founded colony is a colonised planet, but it is *not*
  a catapult: `coveredPlanets` does not count it, so the career's completion rule
  is unchanged. The colony is a trade-world change, not a pad.
- **One per planet, ever** (`colonyAt` already refuses a second), so a replaying
  career cannot farm one site twice.
- **The market reads it**: the new port joins `refreshPartners` and the visit
  profit market the next tick, so the founding is visible in the pills and in the
  rivals' next plans — that is the feature.
- **Replays**: founding is a function of `(seed, step, body)`, so it re-founds at
  the same step on re-flight. The bench's determinism check extends to include
  the colony count at the end of a run.

## 4. the bot

`careerFoundColony(career, sim, trade, step)` prices a founding as
`colonyValue(body, step) − lostContractReward − exitCost`, where `colonyValue` is
the founding's own log line's site score (`activatePort` already computes it)
times a bench-tuned coefficient. The bot presses the button only when that sum is
positive, and the trace line names it (`founded P9 · +1.20 · dropped delivery`).
No change to the search: it is a between-flight shop action, like the pad claim.

## numbers

| name | value | start from |
|---|---|---|
| `FOUND_VALUE` | `2.0` | site score × this is what a founding is worth to a bot |
| `FOUND_HOLD` | full hold | the freight that must ride along |

## Validation

- New `experiments/founding_test.js`: a full hold on an empty in-play planet
  founds one colony, spends the cargo, drops the interrupted contract and does
  not count as a pad for `complete`; a second landing on the same planet founds
  nothing; a tourist or patient load does not found; the Capital and A.R.F. do
  not found; re-flying the same shot re-founds at the same step with the same
  pop; the bot declines a founding that costs more than it is worth.
- `colony_bench`, `trade_route_mask_test` unchanged (the founding path is the
  existing law, now reachable).
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`, `game_bench`: no new failure name; the
  bench log gains `foundings` per run.

## Scope boundary

No goods hauling into an existing colony (that is a different decision — §5 item 3
names "deliver goods" as an alternative, and it would need a per-colony demand
model), no knocking ports out, no change to port growth, and no founding outside
`career` (the other rules are physics toys and stay that way).
