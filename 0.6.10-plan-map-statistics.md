# 0.6.10 plan — map statistics plots

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/rivals-competitions.md` §5.3, the section headed `## map statistics plots`
in the original notes:

	- average profit per time by players and all. useful to balance new quests
	- total property cost by player
	- total sci spend by player
	- average distance fly per time by players and all
	- total corruption sum of all planets
	think what is also interesting or useful

Two other plans read these numbers: `0.6.4-plan-competitions.md` prices its prize
pool against profit per time, and `0.6.5-plan-two-leg-quests.md` uses the bench
table to show that a helper helps. Depends on nothing unshipped.

## problem

A career run has no readout of its own economy. The scoreboard prints a purse, a
pad count and a shot count; every balance question so far — is a prize worth the
flight, does a 13-planet map regrow fast enough, is science a sink — needed its
own probe in `experiments/`. Most of what the note asks for is already counted on
the career object (`earned`, `bribeIncome`, `taxEarned`, `fuelSpent`,
`evacuations`) or on the shared market (`corruption`). Missing are five
accumulators, a sampler and somewhere to draw.

## 1. the statistics (rules layer)

Every purse movement of a career happens in `gravity_game.js`, so the counters
live there, one line at each existing debit or credit:

| new field | where | line |
|---|---|---|
| `career.visitIncome` | `careerEndFlight` | `+= endFlight(career.ledger)` — `endFlight` already returns the flight's `shotGain` |
| `career.flownPx` | `careerEndFlight` | `+= sim.flightLength` |
| `career.feesPaid` | `careerBeginFlight` | `+= fee` (commercial fee or guest tax; `taxPaid` stays the guest share) |
| `career.buildSpent` | `buildGear`, `bulkUpgrade`, `installPart` | `+=` the amount each already takes from the purse |
| `career.sciSpent` | `researchPoint` | `+= cost`, beside `career.lastResearch = cost` |

All five are created in `createCareer` and zeroed in `resetCareer`. They count
finished flights: a ship in the air adds its distance and its visits on landing.

One getter, no allocation, fills a caller-owned row:

	careerStats(out, career)
		out.income   visitIncome + earned + bribeIncome + taxEarned
		out.running  feesPaid + fuelSpent + evacuations × the evacuation price
		out.profit   income − running
		out.property buildSpent
		out.science  sciSpent
		out.flown    flownPx

`profit` is what flying earned net of what flying cost; `property` and `science`
are where it was put. That makes the purse an identity the test can assert, so no
statistic can silently miss a transaction:

	purse = starter purse + profit − property − science

`property` is the money paid for pads, pad gear and ship parts — a total spend,
the same kind of number as `science`. It is not a resale value: the free starter
kit is not in it, and a rebellion that seizes a pad does not lower it.

The two map-wide numbers need no field:

	mapStats(out, market, step)
		out.rot      Σ market.corruption[p] over the planets in play
		out.standing Σ max(0, planetProfit(market, p, step)) over the planets in play

«Per time» is per second of the game clock the game bar prints
(`step × GravitySim.DT`). «By players and all» is one row per company of
`galaxy.actors` plus an `all` row: totals are summed, the two rates are the mean
over companies.

## 2. what else is interesting (the note's last line)

Three additions, each answering a question the drafts already ask and each
arithmetic over state that exists:

| statistic | the question it answers | source |
|---|---|---|
| `standing` — visit value lying unharvested on the map | does the map regrow faster than the companies harvest it (the «13 planets regenerate 2×» rule, `drafts/flight-lab-and-modes.md` §1.4) | `mapStats`, above |
| income by source — visits / contracts / bribes / guest tax | which part of the board pays: a new quest is priced against the contract share, not the total | the four terms of `income` |
| running cost by kind — fees / fuel / evacuations | whether a flight is profitable before any reward | the three terms of `running` |

Left out, with the reason: pads over time (the scoreboard prints the count and no
open question needs its history); arrests and rebellions (events, not series —
the bench's `TOTAL` line already counts them); profit per flight (`profit /
flights`, readable off two printed numbers).

## 3. the samples

A plot needs history, and a career has no length limit, so the history is a
fixed block that thins itself:

- `galaxy.stats` holds one `Float32Array(STATS_SAMPLES)` per series
  (`STATS_SAMPLES = 256`; four per company — profit, property, science, flown —
  and two for the map), allocated when the galaxy is created or a company joins,
  never while the clock runs;
- `sampleStats(galaxy, market, step)` writes one sample every `stats.every`
  steps, starting at one per `STATS_SEC = 2` game seconds. When the block is full
  it keeps every second sample in place and doubles `stats.every`, so the whole
  run is always on the plot: a one-hour career ends at one sample per 16 s;
- it is called from `GravityBot.stepCareer`, right after `advancePolitics` — the
  one place the career clock ticks for the page and for the benches — and returns
  at once on a step that is not a sample step. The same seed therefore holds the
  same samples on the page, in a bench and in a re-run.

## 4. the plots (page)

`#statsStrip`: a `<details>` under `#scoreRows` in the scoreboard panel
(`#scoreHud`), career only, closed by default, its open state remembered in
`localStorage` (`gravity.statsOpen`, beside `gravity.lastMode`). Closed, nothing
is built.

Open, it stacks six small plots — game clock on x, one line per company in the
colour of its claim ring, a neutral line for `all` or for the map — each under a
caption that prints the latest values, so the strip reads as a table too:

	profit / time     YOU +0.42/s · R1 +0.31/s · all +0.36/s
	property          YOU 18.40 · R1 12.10
	science           YOU 6.10 · R1 0.00
	distance / time   YOU 14.2 px/s · R1 9.8 px/s · all 12.0 px/s
	rot               214
	standing value    11.30

- The two rates are plotted as the running average since the start
  (`value / seconds`) — the note's «average … per time» — and the totals raw.
- Drawing is an SVG string, one `polyline` per line, built the way the science
  tab builds its cost plot (`gui_science.js`) and written with a single
  `innerHTML` only when `galaxy.stats` gained a sample or thinned. Nothing is
  drawn from `draw`, and nothing per frame.
- Hovering a caption prints the split of §2 in its `title`
  (`visits 31.2 · contracts 8.5 · bribes 2.0 · tax 0.4` and
  `fees 9.0 · fuel 1.6 · evac 6.0`).
- Refs and the open flag (`statsStripEl`, `statsOpen`) live in `gravity_gui.js`;
  the builder sits in `gui_hud_game.js` beside the scoreboard summaries; sizes and
  labels in `gui_tables.js`.

## 5. the bench

`STATS=1` on `experiments/career_gravity_bench.js` prints, from the same two
getters, one row per rival-game, one `all` row per seed and a `TOTAL` row of
means:

	STATS seed 3 R1  · profit 0.118/s · property 14.20 · science 0.00 · flown 9.8 px/s
	STATS seed 3 all · profit 0.104/s · property 26.30 · science 0.00 · flown 9.1 px/s · rot 214 · standing 11.30
	STATS TOTAL      · profit 0.097/s · … · visits 71 % · contracts 22 % · bribes 6 % · tax 1 %

That table is the artefact a balance question reads; without `STATS=1` the bench
output is unchanged.

## Validation

- New `experiments/career_stats_test.js`, headless:
  - the purse identity of §1 holds to `1e-9` after a scripted career that flies,
    lands on a commercial and on a rival rail, builds, bulk-upgrades, refits,
    researches, buys fuel, takes a bribe and evacuates;
  - each field moves only at its own transaction (a research changes `science`
    and nothing else);
  - `rot` equals the bench's own corruption sum and `standing` equals the sum of
    the pills' values on the same step;
  - the sample block after `n` steps is identical for two runs of one seed, holds
    at most `STATS_SAMPLES` samples, and a thinned block equals every second
    sample of the unthinned one.
- `career_gravity_bench` without `STATS=1` prints byte-identical lines before and
  after the change (the counters must not move a decision).
- `dom_smoke`: the strip is absent outside career; closed it has no child; opened
  it has six plots, one line per company, and its captions carry the same numbers
  `careerStats` returns.
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`: known failure names unchanged; `node
  --check` on every touched script, `git diff --check`.

## Scope boundary

No price, rule or bot decision reads a statistic. No drawing on the game canvas
and no work in the frame loop while the strip is closed. No export, no saved
history across runs, and no strip outside `career`.
