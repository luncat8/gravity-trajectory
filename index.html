mission & guide
tourist

A tourist aboard your vessel wants to fly through each planet system. Sweep inside as many port orbits as possible and land cleanly to continue your tour.

rulesgrand tour
  • visit systems — entering inside a planet’s port orbit counts that system as visited and keeps its visited mark across hops.
  • predicted visits — updates live at up to 60 fps as your aim, gravity or port orbits change.
  • stable launch base — in all modes (planets and ports · moving), launches start from static planets.
  • hop from landed body — once landed on a planet, the next launch is only allowed from that landed planet; other planets show a lock tooltip and arrow pointing to your ship.
  • continuous flight — while flying, the trajectory continues ahead until landed. clear resets the run.
game modecompare runs
  • same world — the seed builds planets, ports and colonies; everyone opening your link flies the same system.
  • clock runs in flight only — trade, ports and moons move only while the cargo flies; after it lands or leaves, everything waits for your next launch.
  • fixed rules — gravity stays at 1, no relaunch mid-flight, a tour flight ends after 120 s or on abort; the speed slider sets the world rate until the first launch.
  • score · tour — more systems, then fewer shots, then less flight time, then purse; a full tour stops when the 26th system is entered.
  • purse & planet profit — purse starts at 3.00 and each launch costs 1.00 (refused below 1.00). Every planet displays its current visit profit (+0.25 at start, growing +0.25 per 40 s of game time since last visited up to +0.75). Entering a planet’s port orbit collects that value and resets it to +0.00 — steer through long-unvisited worlds to fund your tour and out-earn rivals.
  • rule · route — no 120 s cap (1 800 s wall), traced in per-frame slices; score: systems, route length, fewer shots, less time, purse.
  • rule · money — 10-shot contract without stopping at 26 / 26; score: highest purse, then systems, then fewer shots.
  • rivals — easy / normal (1 bot) and hard (2 bots with 1-ply lookahead) fly on the same clock and harvest from the same planets, so worlds along their trails reset to +0.00.
  • playback — − slower / + faster changes only how fast you watch; physics steps and the recorded replay stay identical. The world rate slider stays locked once a shot is fired.
  • copy replay — a link with seed, rate and every launch; opening it re-flies the run, checks the recorded result, then lets you continue.
photospostcards
  • when — entering a planet’s port orbit shows the system from space; landing shows the surface (a gas giant gets an air platform).
  • where — bottom-left, scaled to the free space; on a small screen the card becomes a 256 px overlay.
  • art — files in photos/space/ and photos/landed/, named by planet type; without art a stylised placeholder of the same type is drawn.
