# 0.5.16 plan — a rival kits itself out before its first launch

## problem

The rivals never buy their first cargo hold, so most of them cannot use the
contract board at all: a delivery needs a hold of at least 4 t (the smallest
cargo is 4 t), a medical mission at least 2 (`medicLoad` is half the hold), and
`makeMedic` refuses to even offer a mission below that. Measured in
`experiments/logs/0.5.15-helper-factors.txt`: 4 of 6 seeds at 1200 s end with
both rivals still on HOLD-0, 5 of 12 rival-games ever buy it, and one seed calls
`careerBetweenFlights` 91 times without once standing on its own HQ.

The between-flight shop (`GravityBot.prototype.careerBetweenFlights`) runs on a
landing, on a sit and on a no-shot. A rival stands on its HQ only before the
first launch — queued, not landed — so the opening purchase never happens, and
whether a company ever gets a hold depends on it happening to land back at home
later. That is why the mid run shows so few medical missions even though the
rebellions are there.

## change

In `startCareers`, right after `startCareer` resets the company onto its HQ,
run one between-flight shop:

	this.careerBetweenFlights(r.career, r.sim, 0);

so the company buys HOLD-8 at home (0.76), claims its opening pad and lands on
the same decisions a landing would make. Keep the shop's existing debits,
reserves and one-pad-per-call rule untouched; do not add a rival-specific
special case. The trace line should say what the opening shop bought, so the
decision log stays readable.

## why this order

It is the smallest change that reaches the measured cause and it uses the
existing decision path — no new scoring, no new constant. The alternative
(a second `MEDIC_SLOT`) does not address it: the offer already survives an
active contract and is rarely reachable, so more offers would not be accepted.

## validation

- `node experiments/medic_offer_probe.js` (SEEDS=1-6, 1200 s): hold capacity 8
  on every rival, offers existing in every seed that rebels, boardings up from 1.
- `career_gravity_bench` A/B against the 0.5.13 numbers in
  `experiments/logs/0.5.13-bribe-contest-ab.txt` at 2400 s / 1200 s / 400 s
  (earned, pads, seized, medic trips, patients home). Every rival opening
  changes, so all three horizons matter; log to
  `experiments/logs/0.5.16-rival-first-shop-ab.txt`.
- `career_bench` (3 known failures), `career_bot_bench` (11 known failures) must
  not gain a name; `career_guidance_test`, `politics_test`, `politics_gui_test`
  must stay green.

## watch out

- The opening shop also runs the expansion claim, which spends purse on a pad.
  If the opening becomes too strong (rivals snowball on pads), split the shop
  into "fit the ship at HQ" and "claim a pad" and call only the first one at
  start; measure before choosing.
- `careerSit`/`careerNoShot` already call the same function, so an opening call
  must not double-count `this.careerBuilds`/`careerUpgrades` in a way a bench
  reads.
- A career that starts broke (cheat runs) must be unaffected: the shop's own
  purse checks already guard it.
