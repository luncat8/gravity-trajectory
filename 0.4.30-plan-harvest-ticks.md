# 0.4.30 plan — price the stay-pay from the trace

Deferred half of the stay-pay work. The mode itself ships with 0.4.30: the
experimental `stay-pay · per tick` box (career only) makes every step a ship
stays inside a system pay what regrew during that step, on top of the entry
lump. What is *not* done is the arithmetic that prices it: every number the
page and the rivals compute from a trace — the aim line, the HUD profit, the
launch list, the helper scans and the career bot's candidate scores — still
uses the entry rule and therefore under-reports. This plan closes that gap.

## Goal

Make `estimateProfit` / `estimateProfitLog` exact under the stay-pay, so a
stored trajectory, a helper candidate and a live trace are priced the same
whether the box is on or off. Then the `≥ticks` note in the HUD and the aim
line can go, and the mode can be judged on balance instead of on a hidden
discount.

## What the mode pays today (measured)

- Every step inside pays exactly `v(DT) = cap·(1 − e^(−k·DT/cap))` ≈ `k·DT`
  = `5.21e-5` at the default `k = 0.00625`, `DT = 1/120`, because the stamp is
  always one step old; `bot_bench` 1c pins this to `4.3e-16` per step.
- A dwell of `N` steps inside a system after an entry pays `N · v(DT)` — the
  un-saturated rate, not the saturating curve an entry would pay for the same
  span. A flight can be paid by the same system several times.
- The systems the ship launches from are already inside, so the first flight
  step collects their stored value (pad time included). Stamping them at
  launch instead would delete that value from the shared market; it was tried
  and reverted because it made the mode pay *less* than the entry rule.
- Split of the lift over the 72 bench flights of `bot_bench` 1c (seed 31,
  45 s horizon, 26 planets, a deliberately stale market): `+18.23` of the
  `+18.90` comes from those first-step launch lumps and only `+0.67` from the
  dwell drip. Read that as: the dwell is small at the current `k`, and the
  launch lump dominates — both are open to a balance decision, see below.

## Investigation

1. **Dwell in the trace.** `routeStep` already knows `predInside` every step.
   Attribute the steps spent inside to the entry that opened them:
   `predLogDwell[i]` = steps inside after entry `i` (the entry step itself is
   excluded — it pays the lump). Decide and pin the two edge cases: the launch
   systems (no entry, so their dwell belongs to a synthetic log row or to a
   per-flight accumulator) and the impact step (it stamps the body).
   No allocation in the loop: one `Int32Array(PRED_LOG_MAX)` on the sim.
2. **Price it.** `estimateProfitLog(market, baseStep, planets, steps, dwell,
   count)` becomes
   `Σ [ v(at_i − stamp_i) + dwell_i · v(DT) ]`, advancing the scratch stamp to
   `at_i + dwell_i` so a later entry accrues from the last drip, not from the
   entry. `estimateProfit(sim)` passes the sim's dwell array.
3. **Carry it through the launch book.** `recordLaunch` stores `logDwell`
   (`BOOK_LOG_MAX`), `careerLogStore` copies it out of the probe, and
   `careerScoreStored` prices with it — otherwise a stored shot and a fresh
   trace disagree, which is the one promise the book makes.
4. **Prove parity.** Mirror `bot_bench` 1a: run its 208-flight parity sweep in
   tick mode as well and require the same `|estimateProfit − stepHarvest| <
   1e-12`.
5. **Plan invalidation.** The drip deliberately does not bump `market.stamp`,
   because a bump every step would leave no rival plan ever finishing. That
   leaves a rival's `lookMarket` snapshot drifting by the player's drips. Add a
   second, encounter-only stamp (entries and impacts, as today) for
   `planValid` / `tickIdle` / `tickPlayer`, and keep the drip out of it; or
   re-price a rival's candidate from a snapshot taken at job start and bound
   the error by `k · Δt`. Measure both.
6. **Log capacity.** `PRED_LOG_MAX` is 64 rows; a long career flight runs past
   it (a 102 s flight on seed 42 logged 43 rows) and the tail — the last
   encounters — is dropped, which biases the estimate the same way the entry
   rule is biased today. With dwell counts, consecutive rows for the same
   planet can be merged into one row with a longer dwell, which both shortens
   the log and removes the truncation.
7. **Then the UI.** Drop `predTickNote()` (`≥ticks`) from the HUD and the aim
   line once the estimate is exact; keep the pill highlight that lights the
   systems a flying ship is inside. Optionally show the dwell in the aim line
   (`· 412 steps inside`), which is the honest reason a slow pass pays more.
8. **Balance, last.** With the pricing exact, sweep career with the box on
   (career_bot_bench, plus a short economy run) and report income per flight,
   flights to the first pad and the broke/complete split against the entry
   rule. If the dwell turns out to be noise at `k = 0.00625`, the interesting
   knobs are a separate stay rate and whether the launch lump belongs in the
   mode at all (skipping the drip for the launch systems until they are
   re-entered keeps their stored value for the next encounter, as in the entry
   rule, and needs one per-flight snapshot array on the ledger).

## Acceptance

- `|estimateProfit − stepHarvest| < 1e-12` over the 208-flight parity sweep in
  both modes, and `bot_bench` 1c keeps its per-step invariant.
- A stored launch-book row re-prices to the same number as a fresh trace of the
  same shot in tick mode (`career_bot_bench`'s book checks, extended).
- No allocation in `routeStep`, `stepHarvest` or `draw`: the dwell accumulator
  and the book array are allocated once.
- Rival plans keep finishing: with the encounter-only stamp, the sliced and
  synchronous decisions still agree and the frame-slice budget still holds.
- Replay and cross-cadence invariance unchanged; `dom_smoke`, `game_bench`,
  `career_bench` and `career_bot_bench` unchanged except for the new checks.
- The `≥ticks` note is gone and the measured lift is reported per flight, split
  into the launch lump and the dwell drip.

## Scope boundary

This plan does not change what the stay-pay pays (that shipped in 0.4.30), does
not touch the entry rule, and does not re-price pads, contracts or the tech
tables. If the sweep in step 8 shows the dwell is immaterial, report it and
take the rate question to a balance plan rather than folding it in here.
