# 0.6.4 plan — competitions: a start you can miss

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/rivals-competitions.md` §5.2. Grows the shipped `TASK_COMPETE` slot; the
four later "competition game modes" are explicitly out of scope. The prize is
checked against `0.6.10-plan-map-statistics.md`.

## problem

A competition ships, but it is not an event. `makeCompete` offers one flight
"launched from P#, land after at least N px, within 180 s" and the slot sits there
until a shot satisfies it — nobody announces a start, nobody has to be ready, and
no rival ever enters. The draft asks for the opposite: a **countdown**, an
**arm window** in which a queued shot waits for the gun, and a **prize** that is
worth the flight compared with ordinary work.

The good news is that the two mechanisms it needs already exist under other
names: the career already queues an EMC shot that fires when the barrel lines up
(`gun · N s`), and the board already has an `ACCEPTED` state. This plan adds
three fields and one queue condition rather than a new system.

## the model

`TASK_COMPETE` gains `startsAt`, `pool` and `entered`; `career.competition`
holds the one event the career is entered in.

1. **Announce.** `makeCompete` sets `task.startsAt = step + ANNOUNCE_SEC/DT`
   (`ANNOUNCE_SEC = 45` world seconds) and `task.pool = task.reward`, the seeded
   `2…4` it draws today. The row prints `starts in 45 s` instead of a bare
   deadline.
2. **Enter** = `accept`, one company at a time as with any contract, and it costs
   the entry `COMPETE_ENTRY = 0.50` from the purse, added to `task.pool` (a
   refuse-able stake, so entering late is a real decision and a crowded race
   pays more). A competition entry does not consume the
   single `career.contract` slot; it uses `career.competition` instead, so
   deliveries and a race can overlap — that is what the draft's "timer + task"
   means in practice.
3. **Arm before the start.** While entered and `clockSteps < startsAt`, a queued
   shot is legal and **holds**: it fires at the first barrel alignment at or
   after `startsAt`. `queueTurretShot` reads the barrel angle at "now" and adds
   the wait to it; the hold is one `Math.max(game.clockSteps, startsAt)` for that
   "now", and the same in the bot's `careerPrepareFuel` forecast. Unpausing/aiming rules are unchanged: a queued
   shot already cancels on re-aim.
4. **Judge.** The existing landing test (`origin === task.planet`,
   `flightLength >= minLength`, before the deadline) gains one more condition,
   `step >= startsAt`. The stake is paid *into* the pool at accept, so a winner
   takes `pool` and a loser is simply `entry` poorer — one rule, no refund path,
   and `settleLanding` keeps its shape.
5. **Rivals enter.** The bot's `careerScoreFacts` prices a competition as
   `pool × p(win) − entry`, where `p(win)` is the share of its own best
   candidate length above `minLength` against a cheap estimate of the others'
   best (a fixed `0.5` until a bench says otherwise). That is a scoring change,
   not a search change, so it stays inside the frame slice.

## numbers (starting points, a bench tunes them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ANNOUNCE_SEC` | `45` | countdown from offer to start |
| `COMPETE_ENTRY` | `0.50` | stake, paid into the pool |
| `task.pool` | `task.reward + 0.50 × entered` | a two-company race on a `2.00` draw pays `3.00` to the winner |
| `CAREER.competeMinPx`, `competeSpanPx` | unchanged `1500…3000` | the flight must be a real route |

The pool must be checked against profit per time from
`0.6.10-plan-map-statistics.md` (its `STATS=1` bench table): a race should pay
about 1.5× what the same flight time earns on that map, or nobody enters. The
draft asks for exactly this: «prices balanced to be good profit compared to
overall profit on this map per time (see map statistics)».

## GUI

- The board row: `sport · P# · starts in N s · land ≥ N px`, and after entry
  `entered · arms at T`.
- The **flight HUD work row** (0.5.17) gains the competition as a second work
  item while entered, with the countdown and a guide arrow to its planet — the
  arrow code already exists for contract rows.
- The aim line, while the shot is held for the start, prints `armed · gun in
  N s` instead of `gun · N s`.
- A `REBELLION`-style pill is **not** wanted on the canvas; the countdown lives
  in the panels.

## Validation

- New `experiments/competition_test.js`: an offer announces a start; entering
  stakes `0.50`; a shot queued before the start leaves at the start (not at the
  barrel's first alignment); a flight that lands early does not pay; the winner
  takes the pool; a loser's stake is gone; two companies can be entered at once
  and only the first qualifying landing wins; the bot enters when its length
  clears `minLength` with margin and declines when it does not.
- `career_bench`, `career_bot_bench`, `career_guidance_test`, `politics_test`
  keep their known state; `dom_smoke` covers the held queue and the countdown
  label.
- Determinism: the same seed and shots produce the same start steps and the same
  winner (the event clock is `game.clockSteps`, never wall time).

## Scope boundary

The draft's separate competition **game modes** (`max trajectory`,
`max visits`, `land on the antigravity world`, `visit and return`) and the
variations (`from the Capital only`, `EMC only`, no impulse) are not in this
plan: they are the career rule with the board replaced, and each needs its own
rule-row decision. This plan only makes the one competition slot an event, and
leaves the offer record with the fields (`start`, `pool`, `origin rule`,
`drive rule`) that a later mode would read.
