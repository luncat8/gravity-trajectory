# 0.6.8 plan — the map generator's promises: a fair opening on every roster, and gravity up to +5

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source notes:
`drafts/flight-lab-and-modes.md` §1.4 (`career map generator improve`,
`random gravity of planets -1...+5`) and `drafts/hud-input-and-help.md` §6.2
(`calibrate gravity max and random. so from 10 free planets 2-4 are not possible
to launch until upgrade EMC`).

## what ships today

The opening is planned once for the whole run:
`GravityGame.prepareCareerStarts(galaxy, world, trade, mask, count, wanted,
sourceSim)` fills `galaxy.mapSeats`, and the page calls it with
`wanted = 1 + bot.count` before any career starts.

- Every HQ carries `g 1.00` (`HQ_GRAVITY_MAX`), like the Capital and the A.R.F.
  The seat search already traces a candidate home at that strength
  (`openingTraceHome`).
- Each company is matched to its **own** unclaimed first-flight landing at
  `g ≤ OPENING_BUILD_GRAVITY_MAX = 1.5` that an `EMC-1 + BAT-1` can leave again.
- Candidate maps are ranked by `compareOpeningGroups`, in this order: no company
  without a site; a distinct site for each; every site exitable; the weakest
  opening pays at least `OPENING_FAIR_RATIO = 0.55` of the best; then the higher
  ratio (`OPENING_RATIO_EPS = 0.02`); then the higher weakest pay; then more
  sites.
- `experiments/career_start_fairness.js` asserts buildable, exitable and in the
  band. `experiments/career_start_seat_test.js` asserts that the page seats that
  plan and pins the wells. `career_gravity_bench` starts careers the same way
  (`SEATS`, `HQWELL`).

The field itself is `planetGravity(seed, planet)`: a uniform draw on the grid
`PLANET_GRAVITY_MIN = −1 … PLANET_GRAVITY_MAX = 3.5` in steps of `0.05`
(`PLANET_GRAVITY_STEPS = 20`, 91 values).

## problem

Three measured gaps between the notes and the map.

**1. The fair opening is asserted for one roster only.** The defaults of
`career_start_fairness.js` are the default career; one knob away the promise
does not hold, and the page reaches both rows (`planetCountInput` is `4…48`, the
scoreboard `+` adds rivals up to 16):

| run | buildable | exitable | in the 0.55 band | weakest / best |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COUNT=13 SEATS=3 SEEDS=1-8` (default) | 8/8 | 8/8 | 8/8 | 64 % |
| `COUNT=26 SEATS=3 SEEDS=1-4` | 4/4 | 4/4 | 2/4 | 47 % |
| `COUNT=13 SEATS=5 SEEDS=1-4` | 0/4 | 0/4 | 0/4 | no map gives every company its own site |

**2. The blocked share is printed, never asserted.** `hq_gravity_probe.js`
sets every planet to every gravity of the field in turn and asks whether the
rail's fastest shot leaves the system (`SEEDS=12`, 13 planets; `RAIL=n` is
`EMC-n + BAT-n`, `MASS` the ship):

| rail, ship | cap | `g 1.0` | `1.5` | `2.0` | `2.5` | `3.0` | `3.5` | blocked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RAIL=1`, dry 10 t (the starter) | 91.7 px/s | 100 % | 88 % | 52 % | 18 % | 6 % | 1 % | **3.3 of 10** |
| `RAIL=1 MASS=19.5` (`HOLD-8` loaded) | 65.6 | 87 % | 44 % | 13 % | 2 % | 1 % | 0 % | 4.5 of 10 |
| `RAIL=1 MASS=38` (`HOLD-24` loaded) | 47.0 | 55 % | 18 % | 3 % | 1 % | 0 % | 0 % | 5.3 of 10 |
| `RAIL=2`, dry 10 t | 168.5 | 100 % | 100 % | 99 % | 100 % | 100 % | 96 % | 0.1 of 10 |
| `RAIL=2 MASS=38` | 86.5 | 100 % | 81 % | 43 % | 13 % | 3 % | 1 % | 3.5 of 10 |
| `RAIL=3 MASS=38` | 129.8 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 87 % | 58 % | 30 % | 1.1 of 10 |

The starter's 3.3 of 10 is inside the note's 2–4. Nothing fails if a later
change moves it.

**3. The note's field is `−1…+5`.** The ceiling is one constant, but
`planetGravity` scales its hash by the number of grid values, so moving it
changes the gravity of *every* planet on *every* seed: every career, replay and
bench baseline. A uniform draw to `+5` also moves the two numbers above: planets
too heavy for an opening build (`g > 1.5`) go from `44 %` of the grid to `58 %`,
and the starter's blocked share goes to about **5 of 10** (its reach is already
`1 %` at `g 3.5`, so every added step is blocked) — outside the 2–4 the other
note asks for.

## 1. the fair opening on every roster the page can start

No rule changes before the table exists.

- `career_start_fairness.js` gains a matrix mode (`MATRIX=1`): planet counts
  `8, 13, 20, 26, 48` × seats `2…6`, seeds 1–12, one line per cell with the four
  columns above and the reason of the first failing seed (`no distinct site`,
  `dead end`, `ratio 0.47`).
- Read the table before changing anything. Two different causes are expected
  and need different answers:
	- **not enough light planets** (`SEATS=5` on 13): a company cannot have its
	  own `g ≤ 1.5` site when fewer exist than companies. The honest contract is
	  then «every company has a site it can build on and leave; sites are
	  distinct while the map has enough of them», and the test asserts that
	  instead of failing.
	- **the search stops early** (`COUNT=26`): the ratio falls under the band
	  although fairer seatings exist. Then the bound that cut the search
	  (`OPENING_EXACT_SETS`, `OPENING_PAIR_CANDIDATES_MAX`) is raised or the
	  candidate order changed, and the cell turns green.
- The default cell must not move: seeds 1–8 keep their HQs, sites and pay to the
  printed digit, so no career baseline changes in this step.
- The plan time is printed per cell. `prepareCareerStarts` runs on the page at
  career start; a cell may not cost more than the default one does today
  (2–4 s for three seats in `node`) without a sliced search.

## 2. the blocked share as a contract

`hq_gravity_probe.js` already sweeps `PLANET_GRAVITY_MIN…MAX` and prints the
share as `x.x of 10`, so it follows the constant when the ceiling moves. What is
missing is the assertion: `career_start_fairness.js` checks the *drawn* field
(not the sweep) — over seeds 1–12, between 2 and 4 of every 10 non-HQ planets
cannot be left by the dry ship on `EMC-1 + BAT-1`.

## 3. the ceiling at +5 (last, and as a baseline break)

Decide the shape of the draw from a measurement, not from the constant:

- run §1's matrix and §2's share with `PLANET_GRAVITY_MAX = 5` as a local,
  uncommitted change;
- if the default career keeps a fair opening on every seed **and** the blocked
  share stays in 2–4, ship the uniform draw;
- otherwise keep the share of light planets and stretch only the tail: grid
  values above `3.5` are drawn with the weight that returns the blocked share to
  the band (one weight constant beside `PLANET_GRAVITY_MAX`, same hash, same
  salt). Both notes are then true at once.

Whichever shape wins ships as one commit that changes only the draw, with:

- `gravity_seed_test.js` re-pinned (it asserts that `3.5` is drawn and that the
  values sit on the `0.05` grid);
- the two literals that state the range: the top tick of the potential legend in
  `index.html` (`--z: 3.5`, `+3.5`) and the sentence «seeded from −1 to +3.5» in
  `COMMON_HELP` (`gui_tables.js`). The legend *bar* already follows the constants
  (`paintZoneLegend` in `gui_view.js`) and the lab's `g` slider already reaches
  `5`;
- every bench re-run and logged as the new baseline
  (`experiments/logs/0.6.8-gravity-max5.txt`), because the earlier reference
  numbers are then historical.

One design answer the note does not give must be written into that log: what a
`g 5` world is *for*. The table above says who can use the top of today's
field: at `g 3.5` the starter rail leaves one time in a hundred, an
`EMC-2 + BAT-2` still lifts the dry ship 96 times but a loaded `HOLD-24` once,
and an `EMC-3 + BAT-3` lifts that load 30 times. So everything above `3.5` is
freight country for `EMC-3` and up — the demand `0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md` §3
prices — and the same probe, re-run with the ceiling at 5, is the check.

## Scope boundary

No new planet kinds, no change to the orbit geometry or to the trade world's own
seed stream (the gravity draw is a separate hash). No change to what a heavy
planet *does* beyond being harder to leave. A company added in mid-career by the
scoreboard `+` is seated at random, as its note asks; this plan is about the
companies a career starts with.
