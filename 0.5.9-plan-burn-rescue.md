# 0.5.9 plan — burn rescue ring when the ballistic fan finds nothing

## Goal

When a career company with an engine and fuel has no viable ballistic plan after the normal/fallback/commercial passes, search a dense ring of launch angles with the existing one-burn controller and take the best shot that clears the fee and fuel. Stop dead turns without changing impulse policy, replay encoding, or human aiming.

## Design

### 1. Detect "no viable plan"

- After all `careerPlan` passes (normal → fallback → commercial) and the 0.5.8 burn-pricing of the shortlist, `careerNextShot` has no finite landing with a passing score.
- Trigger only when `ship.engine && ship.tank && ship.tank.fuelCap > 0 && ship.fuelKg >= 0` (same gates as `careerFlightBurn`).
- Do not trigger if a viable ballistic plan exists; do not alter the human-aiming path.

### 2. Bounded rescue ring

- Ring: 16 angles (every 22.5°) plus the contract bearing and its opposite when a contract exists → ~18 candidates. Fixed step; no dense fan.
- Speeds: the pad's launch cap (`capU`) as the primary rung; if `capU` allows, also a mid rung (`round(capU * 0.6)`) to cover shorter rescues. Dedup identical `(angleU, speedU)`.
- For each candidate, reuse `burnShotSim` bound to the world (field, mask, scales, visited, landedBody, stepLimit = `CAP_STEPS`), full tank for the search (`fuelKg = fuelCap`, restored after), and pass `fee` into `careerBurnPlan(..., rescueOnly = false)` exactly like `careerPriceBurn`.
- Cap: ≤ 18 launches × ~40 traces ≈ 720 traces per dead-end plan, once per `careerNextShot`.
- Take the best landing with finite score that clears fee + fuel and beats "do nothing" by `CAREER_BURN_PRICE_MARGIN` (0.5). Fold fuel cost into `taskPay` so `score = taskPay + padValue − fee − energy·wait·cost` holds.

### 3. Wire into launch planning

- In `careerNextShot`, on the "no viable plan" branch (after commercial), run the rescue pass; on success rebuild the plan from the winning landing (angleU/speedU, angle, speed, body, score, taskPay, padValue, fee, energy, wait, exitCost, exitSource, builds, fromBook, steps, burnGain, burnBody) exactly like 0.5.8.
- `careerBurnTop` is empty here, so `careerPriceBurn` is a no-op (already guarded).
- Decision log: `burn rescue · P{origin}→P{body} · {gain} over sit · {n} shots priced`.
- No pre-applied burn; the flight still runs its own mid-flight controller.

### 4. Bench fixture for dead-ends

- `experiments/career_gravity_bench.js`: `DEADEND=1` drains the HQ pad on a deep well so the ballistic fan gives nothing; report rescue-priced plans, burns, sits, drops, evacs.
- `experiments/career_bot_bench.js`: with engine+fuel but no viable ballistic plan, rescue produces a priced plan that lands and burns when flown; without engine/tank, no rescue.

## Scope boundary

No change to impulse policy, burn search shape, replay format, launch book, or human aiming. Rescue only when ballistic gives nothing viable; do not price the whole fan; no multi-burn planning.
