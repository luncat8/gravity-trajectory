# 0.5.11 plan — special planets: A.R.F. and Empire Capital

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Depends on no
later plan; 0.5.12 reads its `planetKind` and capital index.

## Goal

Add one A.R.F. planet and one Empire Capital to each seeded world. A.R.F. pays
no fly-visit profit, has no corruption, gets more tourist-delivery quests and
remains buildable. The Capital pays `−3` on a fly-visit, has a fixed free EMC-3
resort rail, forbids building, has no corruption and generates tourist quests
both to and from its resort base.

## Data model

`OrbitWorld.create(seed, rand)` adds a `planetKind` `Uint8Array` and the two
indices `capitalPlanet` and `arfPlanet`. Choose two distinct indices among the
first four planets with `hashBits(seed, salt, k)`, never with `rand()`: geometry
and colony placement must consume exactly the historical random stream. The
first four planets are active in every legal universe (`PLANET_UNIVERSE_MIN =
4`), and `gui_world.isProtectedPlanet` protects both specials from universe
shrink. `OrbitWorld.attach` exposes the fields; `OrbitWorld.planetKind(world,
planet)` returns `KIND_NORMAL | KIND_ARF | KIND_CAPITAL`. Replays with the same
seed select the same specials.

`GravityGame` adds `OWNER_CAPITAL = -2` and one immutable `CAPITAL_PAD` with
`CATAPULTS[2]` (EMC-3), `BATTERIES[2]`, `PANELS[2]`, full charge and no
reservation. Every actor's `launchPads[capitalPlanet]` points to this pad.

## Rules

1. **Fly-visit profit.** Introduce one special-aware `planetProfitAt(market,
   planet, at, lastStep)` used by `harvestPlanet`, `harvestStay` and
   `estimateProfitLog`; the latter must not keep using the raw
   `planetProfitAtStep`. A.R.F. returns `0`; Capital returns `−3` on each entry.
   Both still stamp `market.lastVisitStep`. `harvestStay` skips non-positive
   pay, so lingering inside the Capital does not charge `−3` every step. Bot
   scoring and the GUI profit pill read the same function.
2. **A.R.F.** No corruption or bribes, no rebellion, normal building and
   upgrades. Tourist deliveries with A.R.F. as destination are offered about
   `2×` as often as an ordinary destination.
3. **Capital.** `launchFee = 0` from `CAPITAL_PAD`; `careerBeginFlight` neither
   drains its battery nor pays a host. `padAt`, `padOwner`, `claimOwner`,
   `exitCostAt`, `railOwnerText`, `refreshNetwork` and `syncCareerNetwork` treat
   it as a fixed imperial rail. `planetBuildable`, `buildGear`, `bulkUpgrade`,
   `fitEndgame` and the bot's `careerNextPad` refuse it; upgrades are impossible
   and ownership cannot be claimed. Its fixed rail counts as covered for career
   completion, otherwise "a catapult on every active planet" is unwinnable.
   Corruption and bribes are fixed at 0.
4. **Tourist quests.** Reuse `TASK_DELIVERY` with `task.tourist = true` and
   `task.good = OrbitEcon.MIGRANTS`; no new task kind and no change to ordinary
   goods deliveries. A deterministic branch of `makeDelivery` uses
   `draw01(career, SALT_TASK)` weights: A.R.F. destinations `2×`, Capital
   resort origins and destinations each `2×`. A tourist task is to **or from**
   the Capital resort base; an A.R.F. task is biased toward it. Tons come from
   `CARGO_TONS`, reward stays positive and capped by `CAREER.deliveryMax`, and
   the board/UI label it as tourists. The bot scores the task's net value,
   including the Capital's `−3` visit.
5. **Bot.** `careerScoreFacts` sees kind-aware visit profit through
   `estimateProfit`; `careerScoreTail` sees the free Capital launch; departure
   and site choice see the zero exit cost. The bot does not attempt to build,
   claim or upgrade the Capital.

## Validation

- One distinct Capital and A.R.F. per seed, both active and protected in a
  4-planet universe; geometry and colony RNG are bit-identical to before.
- A.R.F. live and estimated fly-visit profit is `0`; Capital is `−3` once per
  entry in both harvest modes. Capital launch fee and exit cost are `0`.
- No player or bot can claim, build or upgrade the Capital; A.R.F. can be
  built normally. Career completion remains reachable.
- Tourist tasks to A.R.F. and to/from Capital appear with the documented bias
  on a fixed number of board refreshes; ordinary delivery distribution is
  unchanged.
- `SPECIAL=0` A/B leaves the old 12-seed career bench numbers unchanged;
  `dom_smoke` and all benches green.

## Scope boundary

No corruption, bribe, arrest, rebellion or medical-quest logic; those are 0.5.12
and read `planetKind`. No new task kind, no physics or replay-format change, no
change to ordinary cargo goods.
