# 0.5.11 plan — special planets: A.R.F. and Empire Capital

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone.

## Goal

Introduce two special planet types with distinct economic rules and
tourist-delivery quests: A.R.F. (zero flyby profit, no corruption, more tourist
quests) and the Empire Capital (flyby profit −3, free launch, no building,
tourist quests to/from a resort base).

## Data model

- Planet attribute `kind`: `'normal' | 'arf' | 'capital'`. Assigned
  deterministically at universe generation (one capital, one A.R.F. per galaxy,
  seeded). Stored in planet metadata and exposed via `planetKind(planet)`.
- `orbit_world.js` / `gravity_game.js` expose `planetKind`.
- Capital owns a fixed commercial rail: `OWNER_CAPITAL` with a tier-3 EMC that
  cannot be claimed or upgraded.

## Rules

1. **A.R.F.**
   - Flyby profit = 0 (override `planetProfit`).
   - Corruption fixed at 0; cannot be bribed; no rebellion.
   - Tourist-delivery tasks to A.R.F. appear about 2× as often (`makeDelivery`
     destination bias).
   - Building is allowed.
2. **Capital.**
   - Flyby profit = −3.
   - Launch is free (`launchFee = 0`) on a fixed EMC-3 rail that cannot be
     claimed or built (`padOwner` returns `OWNER_CAPITAL`; `claimOwner`/build
     refused).
   - Corruption fixed at 0; cannot be bribed.
   - Tourist-delivery tasks to/from the capital's resort base appear more often.
   - No building; existing pads stay commercial.
3. **Building.** `claimOwner`/EMC build refused on capital; A.R.F. allows normal
   building.
4. **Bot.** `careerPlan` uses kind-aware profit/fee (capital launches free,
   capital flyby −3, A.R.F. 0); tourist tasks scored by their possibly negative
   pay.

## Tourist tasks

- Reuse `TASK_DELIVERY` with a destination bias and a tourist flag for display.
- A.R.F. and capital destinations are biased upward in `makeDelivery`.
- Tourist pay may be negative on capital flybys; the bot still weighs it.

## Validation

- A.R.F. flyby yields 0 profit; capital yields −3.
- Capital launch fee 0, no build possible; A.R.F. buildable.
- Tourist tasks to A.R.F./capital appear with biased frequency.
- `dom_smoke`, benches green.

## Scope boundary

No new task kinds; reuse `TASK_DELIVERY` with a destination bias and a tourist
flag for display. Corruption/rebellion are separate (0.5.12).
