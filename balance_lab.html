the blocks

plot — the research fee surface of the selected family: the dashed box is the range science has already reached, green dots are known tiers, the white cross is your draft point, the circle on the ladder line is the rung it maps to. draft sliders — the spec you are considering. result — its fee, build cost and merit, plus the fee at par. balance sliders — the law itself: frontier, metric scale, merit curves, k, base price, build share. build cost · ladder — every rung of the table, live against shipped; the Δ column is the drift you have to write back into orbit_tech.js. copy — the same settings as text.

the case, step by step
  1. A panel is only ever felt as a wait, so “too weak” is about the step between rungs next to its price, never about the top number. The shipped SP-1..SP-4 are exactly one move of this kind: keep SP-1 plus gain ×2 on the old 1.0 / 2.4 / 4.5 / 7.5 kW ladder made today's 1.0 / 3.8 / 8.0 / 14.0 — 1.90 kWh per wait and a 24 min BAT-1 refill at SP-2, where it was 1.20 and 38.
  2. Open ladder gain and pick panel. It covers the tables the plot cannot: panel and hold are build-only, so the only price they have is the build cost — no research family, no science panel.
  3. Leave keep = SP-1 (that is the “base level same” of the request) and drag gain ×: every rung above the kept one moves by that multiple of its distance from it, so another ×2 here reads 1.0 / 6.6 / 15.0 / 27.0 kW — 3.30 kWh per wait at SP-2, a 14 min BAT-1.
  4. What to look for: value is what a rung is worth in the unit the shop prints; step for Δprice is that value over the rung under it against what it costs extra; per 1.00 is the ladder's own value curve. A ! rung buys nothing over the one under it, so it is never taken; a ↓ rung pays less per money than the rung under it — mild at the top is normal, under half (thin) is the ladder quitting there. Then build: the price is derived from the spec, so a second ×2 of gain prices SP-4 at 27.83, 3.5× the EMC-2 rail it feeds. If a pad never earns that back, drop the factor or the build share, not the gain.
  5. Ship it: copy table patch is the new const PANELS = […] with specs only, because the game prices them; copy final settings is the knob block for orbit_science.js, only if the fee moved too. Then node experiments/balance_lab_test.js, node experiments/career_bench.js — and refresh the numbers the shop quotes in README.md.
