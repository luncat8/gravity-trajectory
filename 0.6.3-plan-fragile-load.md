# 0.6.3 plan — fragile load: the quest that wants a light rail

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/career-economy.md` §2.4 (`add quest` — «fragile load - cant survive
>EMC1. available on light planets»). Depends on the delivery task and on
`careerLandingFacts`.

One reading of the note is assumed and must be confirmed before building:
«cant survive >EMC1» is taken as *any rail above `EMC-1` breaks it*, whatever the
speed. The other reading — any rail may fire it, up to the speed an `EMC-1`
could give — makes it a slower drag instead of a choice of rail.

## problem

The draft asks for one more delivery kind: cargo that cannot survive a strong
rail, offered on light planets. It is the one quest that makes a pad upgrade a
cost as well as a gain — `0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md` §3 prices the opposite
pressure, freight that only a big rail lifts — and it makes the light worlds a
career map already prefers into the right place to be.

## the model

A fragile delivery is a `TASK_DELIVERY` with one extra field, `task.fragile`; no
new kind and no new board slot, so every walker of the board (the row, the bot's
score, the aim readout, the launch book) keeps working with one `if`.

- **What breaks it.** The load is lost when the ship carrying it is fired by a
  rail above `EMC-1`: `padAt(career, planet).catapult.tier > FRAGILE_TIER` (`1`)
  for the rail that launches the shot — your own, a rival's, a rebel's, or the
  Capital's imperial `EMC-3`. The reward is void and the contract is dropped.
- **Why the tier and not the energy.** `launchEnergy = k · m · v² / efficiency`
  is *lower* on a better rail for the same shot (the slowest dry shot burns
  `0.109 kWh` on `EMC-1` and `0.095` on `EMC-2`), so an energy line would let the
  strong rail through and stop the weak one.
- **The gentle way off always exists.** The commercial rail on every planet is
  an `EMC-1`, so a company that upgraded its own pad can still fly a fragile
  load from it — by choosing the commercial rail and paying its fee
  (`setCommercial`). The pad block offers that choice on a foreign planet today;
  it must offer it on an own pad above `EMC-1` while a fragile load is aboard.
  That fee is the price of the upgrade, and it is the whole point of the quest.
- **Offered only where it can be flown.** A fragile offer is made only when its
  origin has `g ≤ FRAGILE_G` — the note's «available on light planets» — and its
  load is at most `FRAGILE_TONS`. The line is measured, not chosen: an
  `EMC-1 + BAT-1` lifts a loaded `HOLD-8` ship (19.5 t, cap 65.6 px/s) off 99 %
  of planets at `g 0.75`, 87 % at `g 1.0` and 44 % at `g 1.5`
  (`RAIL=1 MASS=19.5 SEEDS=12 node experiments/hq_gravity_probe.js`); a loaded
  `HOLD-24` (38 t) is down to 83 % at `g 0.75` already.
- **Where it is checked.** One function in `gravity_game.js`,
  `fragileBlock(career, task, planet)` → `''` or the reason. The launch reads it
  (a launch from a strong rail with the load aboard voids the contract and
  prints `fragile load lost`), `careerLandingFacts` reads it for the leg credit
  (a shot that would void the load carries none, so the aim line is honest
  before the click), and `canAccept` reads it: a fragile offer is refused while
  the rail under the ship is above `EMC-1` and the commercial one cannot lift
  the load — `fragile · no EMC-1 here can lift it` — so nobody buys a doomed job.
  The bot's acceptance goes through the same `canAccept`.

## numbers (starting points, a bench tunes them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FRAGILE_TIER` | `1` | the strongest rail the load survives |
| `FRAGILE_G` | `0.75` | offered only from a planet this light (measured above) |
| `FRAGILE_TONS` | `8` | the heaviest fragile load: a full `HOLD-8` |
| `FRAGILE_PAY` | `×1.4` | a fragile reward is `1.4 ×` an ordinary delivery of the same tons and distance |

`FRAGILE_PAY` is the only value tuned for feel. An 8 t delivery pays
`0.98…2.42`, so a fragile one pays up to `3.39`, under the ordinary
`CAREER.deliveryMax = 4.50`; the cap is not raised.

## GUI

- The board row's kind cell says `fragile` (beside `delivery`, `tourists`,
  `medical`, `sport`) and its state line gains `· EMC-1 only`, so the player
  reads the limit off the row instead of learning it by losing a load.
- With a fragile load aboard, the pad block names the rail that will fire
  (`own EMC-2 breaks it · commercial EMC-1 3.00`), and the launch button's title
  says which one is selected.
- The aim line prices the void: a shot from a strong rail shows the delivery
  credit as lost, in the warn colour the blocked-landing note uses. The launch
  stays legal (it is an unprofitable shot, not a forbidden one).
- The help drawer's career `detail` sentence gains one clause: fragile freight
  flies from `EMC-1` rails only.

## Validation

- New `experiments/fragile_test.js`: a fragile load launched from `EMC-1` is
  delivered and paid; the same shot from an own `EMC-2` voids it; the commercial
  rail on that same planet does not; the Capital's rail voids it; the offer
  never appears from a planet above `FRAGILE_G` or above `FRAGILE_TONS`;
  `accept` is refused when no `EMC-1` here can lift the load; the aim line
  carries no credit for a voiding shot.
- Flyable by construction: over seeds 1–12, every fragile offer's origin lets an
  `EMC-1 + BAT-1` lift the loaded ship out of the system.
- `career_bench` and `career_bot_bench` keep their known failures; the bench log
  gains `fragile taken / lost` counts.
- `game_bench` is untouched (the rule does not exist outside career).

## Scope boundary

No new task kind, no change to `CARGO_TONS`, no damage model beyond the one rail
test, no effect on the trade economy or the trade backdrop.
