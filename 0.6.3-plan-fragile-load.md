# 0.6.3 plan — fragile load: the quest that wants a light rail

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source note:
`drafts/career-economy.md` §2.4 (`add quest / fragile load`). Depends on the
delivery task and on `careerLandingFacts` (0.5.15).

## problem

The draft asks for one more delivery kind: cargo that **cannot survive** a hard
launch, offered on light planets. It is the cheapest way to give the pad ladder
its upper bound — today `EMC-3+` has no buyer (`0.6.2-plan-pad-fees.md` §3 buys
it from the other side, with a deep site) — and it makes the light, cheap worlds
that a career map already prefers into the *right* place to be.

## the model

A fragile delivery is a `TASK_DELIVERY` with one extra field, `task.fragile = 1`;
no new kind, no new board slot, so every existing walker (board row, bot scoring,
aim readout, book, replay) keeps working with one `if`.

- **Gate, not a chance.** A fragile offer is made only from a **light launch
  planet**: the launch site's own gravity `g ≤ FRAGILE_G` (`1.0`) — the note's
  "available on light planets". It is a property of the *origin*, so the same
  contract cannot appear from a heavy well and quietly die.
- **What breaks it.** The cargo is lost, the reward is void, and the task is
  dropped the moment the flight's **launch energy** exceeds `FRAGILE_ENERGY`:
  `OrbitTech.launchEnergy(mass, catapult, speed) > FRAGILE_ENERGY`. Energy is the
  right unit — it is what the pad already bills, it is what an `EMC-2` adds, and
  it makes `carry less / launch slower` a real decision instead of a die roll.
  Calibration: `FRAGILE_ENERGY` is set so an `EMC-1 + BAT-1` starter ship
  (`10 t` dry, `HOLD-8`, `~92 px/s`) is **under** the line with margin, while the
  same hold on an `EMC-2` at the same speed is over it. Measure with
  `experiments/build_cost_probe.js` and pin the two numbers in the bench.
- **Where it is checked.** One function in `gravity_game.js`,
  `fragileVoid(career, task, sim)`, called from the same place the landing
  settles: at launch (the energy is a property of the shot, so it can be priced
  in `careerLandingFacts` *before* the shot is fired — that is what makes the aim
  line honest) and again at landing, where a voided contract pays `0` and prints
  `fragile load lost`.
- The contract **cannot be accepted** while the ship's mass would already void it
  from the planet it stands on: `accept` is refused with `fragile · too heavy for
  this rail`, so the player never buys a doomed job. The bot's acceptance path
  reads the same refusal.

## numbers (starting points, a bench tunes them)

| name | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FRAGILE_G` | `1.0` | offer only from a planet this light |
| `FRAGILE_ENERGY` | measured | the launch energy above which the load is lost |
| `FRAGILE_PAY` | `×1.4` | a fragile reward is `1.4 ×` an ordinary delivery of the same tons and distance |

`FRAGILE_PAY` is the only value tuned for feel; it must stay under
`CAREER.deliveryMax` after the multiply (the ordinary cap is `4.50`, so the cap
becomes `6.30` for this kind — state it, do not silently raise the global cap).

## GUI

- The board row's kind cell says `fragile` (beside `delivery`, `tourists`,
  `medical`, `sport`), and the state line gains `· ≤ N kWh` so the player can read
  the limit off the row instead of learning it by losing a load.
- The aim line and the predicted profit price the void: a shot whose energy is
  over the line shows the delivery as `−` rather than `+`, in the same warn colour
  the blocked-landing note uses, and the launch button keeps working (it is a
  legal, unprofitable shot).
- The help drawer's career `detail` sentence gains one clause: fragile freight is
  launcher-limited.

## Validation

- The calibration probe prints, for both rails, the energy of the starter shot
  and of a `HOLD-24` shot, so `FRAGILE_ENERGY` is a visible gap between two
  measured numbers and never a guess in the constant.
- A new `experiments/fragile_test.js`: an `EMC-1` starter ship carries a fragile
  load and is paid; the same ship on `EMC-2` at the same speed voids it; a
  heavier hold voids it on `EMC-1`; the offer never appears from a `g > 1`
  planet; `accept` is refused when already doomed; the aim line prices the void
  and the landing pays `0`.
- `career_bench` and `career_bot_bench` keep their known failures; the bench log
  gains `fragile taken / lost` counts.
- `game_bench` is untouched (the rule does not exist outside career).

## Scope boundary

No new task kind, no change to `CARGO_TONS`, no damage model beyond the one
energy test, no effect on the trade economy or the trade backdrop.
