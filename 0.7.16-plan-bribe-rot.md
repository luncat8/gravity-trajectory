# 0.7.16 plan — the bribe must rot a world more than never visiting it

Standalone spec: a fork can rebuild this from this file alone. Source:
`drafts/politics-quests.md` §3.1 (bribe formula, «suggested `1 + 0.1 × money`»,
one bribe per visit) and §3.2 (bribes are for a world the player did not report).

## the defect

`market.corruption` is fed by one passive term, and that term read the **visit
value clock**:

	advancePolitics: rate = passiveCorruptionRate(planetProfit(p, runStep))
	                 planetProfit → planetProfitAtStep(market.lastVisitStep[p], …)

A reported landing stamps `lastVisitStep`, and the report also calms the world
(`−0.2` per visit). So the bribe — which necessarily lands *first*, because the
window only opens on a visit — was paid for a world whose rot clock had just
been restarted and whose rot had just been cooled. Measured at the base commit
(`c09b2be`) with one normal planet (not the Capital, A.R.F. or the HQ) aged
`a0` by the world's own politics clock, then left alone / visited / visited and
bribed, and read `T` later (mean of seeds `1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11`, `26` planets):

| `a0` | `T` | never visited | visit only | visit + bribe | bribe − never |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `120 s` | `300 s` | `17.43` | `6.64` | `7.89` | **−9.55** |
| `120 s` | `900 s` | `70.54` | `47.90` | `49.15` | **−21.39** |
| `300 s` | `300 s` | `46.26` | `21.31` | `22.80` | **−23.47** |
| `600 s` | `300 s` | `76.84` | — | `63.60` | **−13.23** |
| `600 s` | `900 s` | `76.84` | — | `77.85` | **+1.01** |
| `240 s` | `300 s` | `35.86` | `14.77` | `16.20` | **−19.67** |

Paying the rulers left the world *cleaner* than staying away, for as long as the
restarted clock kept it under `+1.00` visit profit — five to twenty points on
the fixtures above, and the one positive row (`+1.01`) is smaller than the
`1 + 0.1 · money` the bribe is supposed to add. That is the opposite of the
note: a bribe is a political act bought at a price, and the price of silence
must not be a calmer world.

## the law

Three clauses, one clock.

1. ·**the rot has its own clock.** `market.reportStep` is what passive rot reads
  (`planetProfitAt(market, p, runStep, market.reportStep[p])`). The value clock
  `lastVisitStep` keeps pricing what the next visitor collects and what the
  bribe pays for; the two only part when a bribe buys a report back.
  `market.reportPrev` keeps the report standing **one landing deep**, so the
  bribe can restore it without any extra history. A ship sitting on the world
  ages both clocks at half speed, as it always aged the one.
2. ·**a bribe takes the report back.** In `acceptBribe`:
  `reportStep[p] = reportPrev[p]` — the landing the rulers were paid to keep
  quiet never reaches the Capital, so the world rots on from the step standing
  before it, exactly as if nobody had come. An unreported visit (quiet planet,
  dead device) stamped no report, so the assignment is a no-op there and the
  bribe stands on the last real report. A bribe is not a visit.
3. ·**the rulers add their own increment.** `bribeCorruption(money) =
  CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE + CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SCALE · money +
  CORRUPTION_BRIBE_SURCHARGE` with `SURCHARGE = 1`, so a bribe costs
  `2 + 0.1 · money` rot instead of `1 + 0.1 · money`.

`CORRUPTION_BRIBE_BASE + SURCHARGE = 2` is exactly the `0.2` a visit calms,
which is what makes the two comparisons below hold at **every** horizon rather
than on average:

	rot(bribed world)  − rot(never visited)  ≥  0.2 + 0.1 · money  >  0
	rot(visit only)    − rot(never visited)  <  0

and once both worlds saturate, the difference is the whole increment, un-capped:

	rot(bribed) − rot(never) = 1 + 0.1 · money  at C = 100

Measured after the change (`a0 = 240 s`, `T = 300 s`, the same six seeds):

| arm | mean rot |
|---|---:|
| never visited | `35.86` |
| visit only | `14.77` |
| visit + bribe | `38.09` |

`bribe − never = +2.23` — the `0.2` the plain visit calms plus
`2 + 0.1 · 0.48` — at the horizon where the base commit measured `−19.67`.

## what it changes elsewhere

- `bribeAmount` is untouched: the offer is still `2 ×` the visit value, and a
  reported landing still collects what it collected. Only rot moved.
- The bot's `careerBribeOffer` (cash only) stays right, and `careerBribe`'s
  gates are unchanged: no competitor can report *for* the briber, so a bribe
  never cleans a world its own company works. What a persona *should* weigh is
  a `0.6.6` question, recorded in the draft.
- The GUI prices the law it applies, so the bribe tooltip and the politics help
  drawer say the report is taken back and the world rots on as if never
  visited.
- `rot ×` (0.7.6) is not extended to the bribe: the increment is one-time and
  per-bribe, and a knob that scaled it could push it under the report it takes
  back, which is the defect this plan removes.

## scope boundary

No change to the passive ramp (`1.00…3.00`), to `CORRUPTION_REPORT_RATE`, to
`CORRUPTION_VISIT_DECAY`, to the arrest roll, to rebellion (§4 lets a seized
world stop rotting, which is that law's own business), or to what a visit pays.
The `0.6.8` roster-matrix work (§1–§3) is untouched by this plan and still
open.
