> **Roadmap position (End of `0.8.*` · Art-mode & instrument navigation, paired with `0.8.4` and `0.8.6`):** Placed alongside `0.8.4-plan-helper-dialog.md` (`H` radio dialog) and `0.8.6-plan-improve-sound.md` (Voronoi scale consonance, periodic pan, arrival/block cues), with clean hooks for `0.8.8-plan-space-weather.md` (weather & jammers) and hidden-map exploration (`drafts/new/adv-explorer-career.md`). `drafts/sound-and-photos.md` §7.1 (*"think how blind people could play and think

## Goal & artistic premise (not business-software accessibility)

This plan is **not** corporate WCAG compliance and does **not** rebuild the
shop, research frontier, launch-book, or contract-board tables for screen-reader
form navigation. Replacing the management GUI is a non-goal.

The goal is an **art-product experience**: making it genuinely **possible and
interesting to orient in a gravitational cluster and play a full career without
visual rendering** (with the orbital canvas shuttered/blacked out, or across an
uncharted / jammed sky). Everything outside trajectory feel is solved the same
way rival companies solve it:

1. **Management via GUI Bot Quartermaster + `H` Radio (`autohelper → fly → build → upgrade`):**
   A human-side **Quartermaster** backed directly by `GravityBot`'s existing
   between-flights logic (`bot_career_shop.js`, `bot_career_assets.js`) handles
   shop refits, HQ science, pad builds/upgrades, fuel, repairs, and routine
   contract screening. Strategic choices and turn-by-turn flow happen as short
   dialogs over **`H` radio** (`comms_copy.js`, `gui_comms.js`).
2. **Aiming with mouse or keys by sound — and a Dead-Reckoning / Jammed mode
   that forces real acoustic trajectory search:**
   In ordinary career, players naturally lean on `auto helpers` (`best profit`,
   `quest scan`) and the existing `outcome` aim chord (which maps net profit
   straight to pitch and brightness — effective as a HUD cue, too trivial when
   searching by ear *is* the game). A dedicated **Jammed-Telemetry /
   Dead-Reckoning** mode (and local weather/jammer/fog-of-war interference)
   knocks out the auto-scanner and downrange profit forecast, leaving the pilot
   with an analog **Sound-Gravity-Scope (`scope`)** that sonifies pure field
   geometry: how near the trajectory skims planets, how strongly wells bend the
   path, and whether it impacts a **heavy** or **light** world.

## 1. Radio-piloted career & GUI Bot Quartermaster (`autohelper → fly → build → upgrade`)

### 1.1 Split `GravityBot` shopping from shot firing

`GravityBot` already solves every non-aim career GUI task inside
`careerBetweenFlights(career, sim, runStep)` (`bot_career_shop.js`) — colony
founding, mandatory/preventive repair, affordable departure rails, HQ research,
ship refits (`OBS`, `hold`, `tank`, `engine`, `colonyKit`), contract
acceptance/deferral, loaded-ship pad upgrades, and remote pad claims — while
`tickCareerPlayer` (`bot_career_loop.js`) bundles that shopping pass with bot
shot selection.

Refactor the between-flights pass so human eyes-free play reuses **one** logic
path with zero duplicated heuristics and full `career_actions.js` replay parity:

- **`bot.planQuartermaster(career, sim, runStep, opts)` (read-only preview):**
  Evaluates what `careerBetweenFlights` + fuel prep (`careerPrepareFuel`) would
  do at `career.at` right now without mutating `career` or PRNG state, returning
  a bounded list of concrete action descriptors (`repair`, `build`, `buy`,
  `fit`, `research`, `accept`, `defer`, `produceFuel`, `buyFuel`, `foundColony`)
  with their exact `careerAct` payloads and costs. Moral or irreversible forks
  that belong to a human captain — **taking a ruler's bribe** (`bribeProne` is a
  bot persona roll, never forced on `YOU`) and **paid evacuation** — are surfaced
  as explicit radio choices rather than silent auto-commits.
- **`applyQuartermaster(actions)` (replay-safe execution):**
  Executes the approved descriptors through `careerAct(...)` (`gui_career.js`) so
  every purchase, build, refit, and contract action records into
  `career_actions.js` identically to manual shop clicks.
- **Two operating postures** (persisted in `localStorage` and mirrored on the
  `H` radio card):
  - **`quartermaster: advise` (default in radio/blind view):** On landing, `H`
    presents the Quartermaster's proposed bundle as a single primary CTA
    (`run quartermaster · −9.45`) beside individual override CTAs.
  - **`quartermaster: auto`:** Runs the safe bundle automatically on touchdown
    (leaving `bribe` / `evac` as radio prompts) and posts one concise receipt
    line to `H` (`repaired −0.45 · built EMC-1 on P4 −9.00 · accepted P4→P9`).

### 1.2 `H` radio decision loop (`comms_facts.js`, `comms_topics.js`, `gui_comms.js`)

Per `0.8.4-plan-helper-dialog.md`, the radio officer is **`H`** (`comms*`
namespace) and trajectory scanning is **`helper*`** (`gui_helper.js`). In
eyes-free play, `H` serves as the cockpit's unified decision desk for the
straightforward **`autohelper → fly → build → upgrade`** cycle:

1. **Touchdown & situation brief (`landing-brief` topic):**
   - Reports current world (`P#`), physical gravity class (`light` $g < 1.2$,
     `moderate` $1.2 \le g < 2.2$, `heavy` $g \ge 2.2$, `repel` $g < 0$) and
     exact $g$, visit payout earned on the flight just ended, purse, hull
     condition, fuel, and open work (`load` / `deliver` / `medics` / `sport`
     with compass bearing and coarse range when destination coordinates are
     charted).
2. **Build / upgrade / service CTAs (`COMMS_HANDLERS`):**
   - `run-quartermaster` (executes `planQuartermaster` bundle), plus existing
     verified single-step CTAs (`repair`, `build-pad`, `produce-fuel`,
     `buy-fuel`, `bribe`, `commercial`, `wait`, `evac`) and contract accept
     (`accept-best`) when `quartermaster: advise` is active.
3. **Scan & launch CTAs (standard telemetry only):**
   - When auto-helpers are online (`telemetryState.scannerOk === true`), `H`
     exposes direct radio triggers for `best profit`, `profit + EMC`,
     `quest scan`, and `plan quest` (`gui_helper.js`), then `launch` (`Enter`)
     once a shot is armed — allowing a complete career run from `H` + `Enter`
     without opening `#careerShop`, `#careerScience`, or `#careerTasks`.
   - When auto-helpers are jammed (§2), `H` replaces scan CTAs with local pad
     facts (`pad cap`, `kWh`, `hull/fuel`) and charted compass bearings to open
     contract worlds or company pads (`P9 lies ENE ~28°, far`), prompting the
     pilot to sweep with the Sound-Gravity-Scope.

### 1.3 Minimal spoken / live-region radio bridge (`#srStatus`)

Keep **one** polite live region `#srStatus` (`role="status"`,
`aria-live="polite"`, `.visuallyHidden`), fed by `srAnnounce(text)` in
`gui_hud_text.js` (throttled to `1.2 s`, deduplicated on unchanged text, zero
per-frame string building), plus an optional browser `speechSynthesis` toggle
(`radio voice`) for sighted eyes-free players running without an OS screen
reader:

- Announces only **discrete cockpit transitions**: touchdown brief, Quartermaster
  receipt, scan completion (`careerNotice`), launch-gate block reason
  (`formatLaunchReason`), turret alignment wait (`EMC turning · 3.2 s`), pause
  toggle, and armed-shot summary (**suppressing** net profit and unrevealed
  destination names whenever `!telemetryState.profitKnown`).
- Never fires continuously during drag or key-repeat aiming — continuous aiming
  belongs strictly to Web Audio (§4).

## 2. Dead-Reckoning / Jammed-Telemetry mode & Instrument Blackout

### 2.1 Storyline & mechanical rule

In standard career, `helperProfit` / `helperQuest` searches hundreds of candidate
shots automatically, and `AIM_OUTCOME` (`updateChord` in
`gravity_trajectory_sound.js`) turns net credit profit into a bright major triad.
If auto-helpers were merely hidden while `AIM_OUTCOME` stayed active, aiming by
sound would still collapse into *"sweep until the profit chord peaks"* rather
than reading space.

Introduce **Dead-Reckoning (`telemetry = jammed`)** — playable both as a
dedicated career rule/modifier (`&telemetry=jammed`) and as a dynamic local
condition driven by **hidden-map fog of war** (`adv-explorer-career.md`) or
**space weather & corrupted-planet jammers** (`0.8.8-plan-space-weather.md`):

- **Diegetic premise:** Ion storms, rebel/corrupted-world orbital jammers, or
  incomplete cluster cartography knock out long-range market telemetry and the
  brute-force trajectory computer. Pad-local instruments (rail cap, battery
  charge, hull strain, dockmaster radio `H`) still work on the ground, and the
  ship's analog **gravimeter (Sound-Gravity-Scope)** still senses mass curvature
  along ballistic probe lines, without knowing what any distant flyby will pay.
- **Single telemetry contract (`telemetryState(career, sim)` in `gui_predict.js`):**
  Returns `{ scannerOk, profitKnown, destPadKnown, fogStep, interference }`:
  - **Under `&telemetry=jammed`:**
    - `scannerOk = false`: all scan buttons in `#helperGroup` and scan shortcuts
      are disabled with reason `scanner jammed · aim by gravity scope`.
    - `profitKnown = false`: `#predProfitRow`, the money/visit-payout clauses of
      `#aimReadout`, `#srStatus`, and `aimSoundState.hasProfit` are suppressed
      (`telemetry jammed`); mechanical launch inputs (`angle`, `speed`, `cap`,
      `pad fee` at the current pad) remain visible/audible.
    - `destPadKnown`: `true` only for pads the company owns or worlds already
      personally visited/charted with pad memory; `false` otherwise (so unknown
      destinations report physical mass class via the scope, not commercial pad
      tiers).
    - Aim sound locks to **`AIM_SCOPE` (`scope`)**; `AIM_OUTCOME` is unavailable
      while `!profitKnown`.
  - **Under Hidden Map (fog of war, when present):**
    - Unrevealed bodies outside sensor range (`OBS` / planet `radar`) are masked
      out of prediction (`planetMask & chartedMask`), `scannerOk` refuses targets
      in fog, `profitKnown` drops to `false` once the predicted path exits
      charted space at `fogStep`, and `scope` sounds an uncharted-horizon cue at
      `tau_fog` (§4.3).
  - **Under `0.8.8` Space Weather / Jammers (when present):**
    - Severe local interference sets `interference ∈ (0, 1]`; above the blackout
      threshold, `profitKnown` and `scannerOk` degrade locally under the exact
      same contract, without a second code path.

### 2.2 Instrument Blackout (`blind` / no-visual-render view)

To test and showcase the art-product goal directly — orienting on the map
without visual rendering — add an **Instrument Blackout (`render: off`)** view
option alongside existing view modes (`F` cycle or dedicated cockpit shutter
toggle, URL/storage-backed):

- Skips orbital field, planet, ship, and trajectory canvas draws (`#f`, `#c`,
  `#g` painted flat dark — zero 2D/GPU field cost), leaving only the minimal
  cockpit radio faceplate (`H` comms card + compact pad/ship strip) and full
  spatial audio.
- Works in both standard radio career (`autohelper → fly → build → upgrade`) and
  Dead-Reckoning career (`scope → fly → build → upgrade`).

## 3. Aiming by sound with mouse or keys (`gui_aim.js`, `gravity_gui.js`)

Eyes-free aiming fails on two fronts today: keyboard aiming does not exist, and
mouse aiming requires clicking within `HIT_PX` of `(planetX[aimBody],
planetY[aimBody])` — impossible when the map is not rendered or the planet's
screen pixel is unknown.

### 3.1 Virtual-pad relative mouse aiming (no pixel hunting)

When landed in career (`career.at >= 0`, where `career.at` is the sole legal
launch origin) or paused in flight with an impulse engine (`isBurnPaused() &&
canImpulse()`), or whenever **Instrument Blackout** is active with a selected
launch planet:

- Pressing primary mouse anywhere on the open canvas (outside HUD controls)
  anchors a **virtual pad origin** at `(pressClientX, pressClientY)` mapped to
  `career.at` (or the ship during burn pause, or `selected` in lab/route) — no
  need to locate the planet's pixel first.
- Dragging pulls `(dx, dy)` relative to `(pressClientX, pressClientY)` through
  the existing `gripLen → speedAt → snapAim` curve, with `Shift` engaging
  `SENS_FINE` (`0.12×`) and mouse wheel nudging speed along the rail's integer
  `SPEED_STEPS` grid.
- On visual modes when clicking directly on another body in lab/route, existing
  `planetAt` / `bodyAt` selection behavior is preserved unchanged.

### 3.2 Three-gear keyboard aiming & A/B acoustic memory

When focus is not inside a text control (`INPUT`, `TEXTAREA`, `SELECT`), bind
ergonomic, deterministic keyboard controls driving the exact same `aimBody`,
`aimAngle`, `aimSpeed`, `placeGripFromSpeed()`, `burnAngle`, `burnDv`, and
`dirty = true` state as pointer drag:

| input | action | step |
|---|---|---|
| `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` | rotate `aimAngle` (or `burnAngle` when burn-paused) | `±2°` (`π / 90`) |
| `Shift + ArrowLeft/Right` | fine vernier angle trim (`fine = true`) | `±0.2°` (`π / 900`) |
| `Alt + ArrowLeft/Right` (or `[` / `]`) | coarse 24-sector compass step | `±15°` (`π / 12`) |
| `ArrowUp` / `ArrowDown` | adjust `aimSpeed` (or `burnDv`) within `[SPEED_MIN, speedCeiling]` | `±2.5%` of span |
| `Shift + ArrowUp/Down` | fine vernier speed trim | `±0.25%` (min `1` `SPEED_STEPS` unit) |
| `Alt + ArrowUp/Down` | jump speed by `±15%` (up to `speedCeiling`) | `±15%` of span |
| `M` | **mark** current `(aimAngle, aimSpeed)` into A/B acoustic memory slot | instant confirm tick |
| `R` | **recall / swap** current aim with marked A/B slot to compare two shots by ear | instant swap |
| `Tab` / `Shift + Tab` (lab / route) | cycle `selected` launch planet (`P0..Pn`) | announces `P# · g` + tone |
| `Enter` / `Escape` | existing launch (`launchShot` / `executeBurn`) and cancel/disarm | unchanged |

- **First-press auto-arm:** Pressing an aim arrow while grounded and disarmed
  arms from `career.at` (or `selected` / `P0` in lab) restoring that planet's
  last aimed `(angle, speed)` — or `(0 rad, 0.55 × speedCeiling)` on a fresh
  pad — and holds `sound.setLoop('aim')` active during key motion, settling to
  `'armed'` `250 ms` after the last key release so orientation + scope respond
  identically to a hand drag.
- **Cardinal ticks:** Crossing exact compass axes (`0°` E, `−90°` N, `180°` W,
  `+90°` S) emits a tiny dry `8 ms` detented click (distinct high tick on North
  `−90°`) so the pilot always keeps an absolute compass frame in their head.

## 4. The Sound-Gravity-Scope (`AIM_SCOPE = 'scope'`)

### 4.1 Why `flythrough` (`2..8 s` replay) does not work as a search scope

`AIM_FLYTHROUGH` (`runFlythrough` in `gravity_trajectory_sound.js`) compresses
the whole flight into a `2..8 s` sequential phrase of `predLogPlanet` plucks. As
an artistic playback after a shot is already chosen, it is pleasant; as an
**aiming search instrument**, it fails for three physical reasons:

1. **Broken motor-ear loop:** Chaotic multi-body trajectories bifurcate within a
   fraction of a degree. Waiting `2–8 s` per `0.2°` nudge makes sweeping `360° ×
   speed` impossible.
2. **Step-function blindness outside `orbitR`:** `predLogPlanet` is empty until
   the path crosses a planet's system ellipse, so sweeping across near-misses
   gives **zero gradient** telling the hand *which way* a planet lies.
3. **No mass semantics on impact:** Pentatonic planet IDs (`get_tone_pentatonic`)
   name arbitrary indices rather than whether the landing world is a **light
   `0.5g` harbor** (easy next launch) or a **`+4.2g` gravity trap** (stranded
   without a heavy rail or `IMP`).

### 4.2 Zero-allocation trajectory field analyzer (`analyzeScopePath`)

When `aimChanged(data)` fires on a predicted path (`sim.predCount >= 2`),
compute a compact physical summary in `< 0.05 ms` using static preallocated
typed arrays (zero heap allocation, striding `predX/predY` to at most
`SCOPE_SAMPLES = 192` points and using periodic `wrapDelta` when `sim.periodic`
is active per `0.8.6` §2):

- **Top `K = 3` closest non-origin wells (`scopeBody[0..2]`):**
  - `scopeRatio[k] = minSurfaceDist / orbitRadius` — continuous proximity ratio:
    `< 1.0` means actual system flyby/entry; `1.0 .. 3.5` means near-miss
    gravitational pull gradient; `> 3.5` is silent vacuum.
  - `scopeTau[k] ∈ [0, 1]` — normalized position along the trajectory where
    closest approach occurs (orders encounters early → late).
  - `scopePan[k] ∈ [-1, 1]` — local heading-relative side pan (`trajectoryPan`)
    at closest approach (port vs starboard pass).
  - `scopeG[k] = sim.strength[p]` — physical gravity $g \in [-1, 5]$ of that
    well.
- **Path curvature (`scopeBend ∈ [0, 1]`):** Normalized total heading deflection
  $\sum |\Delta \theta|$ along `predX/predY`, distinguishing a straight ballistic
  dart from a deeply bent slingshot.
- **Terminal state:**
  - `predReason` (`END_IMPACT`, `END_EXIT`, `END_TIME`, or `tracing`),
  - `destG = destination >= 0 ? sim.strength[destination] : 0`,
  - `destKind`: `light` ($0 \le g < 1.2$), `moderate` ($1.2 \le g < 2.2$),
    `heavy` ($g \ge 2.2$), `repel` ($g < 0$), or `star` (lethal lone star when
    present),
  - `fogTau ∈ [0, 1]` (`-1` when fully charted): fraction where the probe first
    leaves charted space under fog of war.

### 4.3 Three-layer real-time acoustic grammar (`< 350 ms` read time)

`AIM_SCOPE` reuses `gravity_trajectory_sound.js`'s persistent nodes (`aimOsc`,
`chordOsc[0..2]` extended with per-voice `StereoPannerNode`s built once in
`buildGraph()`, `blastFilter` noise tap, and the `POOL` pluck voices) — **zero
per-frame Web Audio node creation**:

1. **Layer 1 — 360° Compass & Rail-Tension Carrier (`0 ms` lag, continuous):**
   - `aimPan.pan = Math.cos(aimAngle)` gives West–East (`L ↔ R`) stereo bearing.
   - A dedicated `aimFilter` tilt driven by `-Math.sin(aimAngle)` brightens
     Northward (`-y`, `900 → 2200 Hz`) and warms Southward (`+y`, `900 → 380 Hz`)
     so North and South never sound identical.
   - Pitch rises smoothly across one octave with normalized rail pull
     `aimSpeed / aimCeiling`, anchored to launch-pad mass (`strength[aimBody]`).
2. **Layer 2 — Continuous 3-Well Proximity & Slingshot Field (`0 ms` lag):**
   - Voices `0..2` track the `K = 3` closest non-destination wells simultaneously
     as your hand moves:
     - **Silence in deep vacuum** (`scopeRatio > 3.5`), rising quadratically from
       `3.5 → 1.0` so sweeping *toward* an unseen planet swells smoothly in the
       ear on the side of the pass (`scopePan[k]`).
     - **System-entry lock (`scopeRatio <= 1.0`):** Crossing inside `orbitR`
       opens harmonic brightness and adds a subtle FM/tremolo shimmer scaled by
       surface closeness, unmistakably marking *"inside flyby/harvest radius"*
       versus *"near miss"*.
     - **Gravity-mass register (`scopeG[k]`):** Light wells sing in a clear upper
       register (`220–440 Hz`, sine/triangle), moderate wells in mid-band
       (`120–210 Hz`), heavy giants ($g \ge 2.2$) in deep resonant bass
       (`55–110 Hz` with slow gravitational beat), and repellers ($g < 0$) with a
       hollow detuned fifth — quantized to local Voronoi pentatonic degrees when
       `voronoi` is active so simultaneous wells remain consonant (`0.8.6` §1).
     - `scopeBend` gently opens resonance (`Q`) on `profitFilter` (repurposed as
       `scopeFilter` in `AIM_SCOPE`) so multi-well slingshots glow with richer
       overtones than straight-line shots.
3. **Layer 3 — Fast Micro-Radar Sweep & Landing Mass Signature (`~360 ms` cycle):**
   - Instead of a `2..8 s` replay, a compact **oscilloscope sweep** repeats every
     `0.42 s` (`280 ms` active trace + `140 ms` breath; phase-reset `60 ms` after
     fast motion pauses so rapid dragging never smears):
     - **`t = 0 ms`:** soft pad-trigger click.
     - **`t = scopeTau[k] × 240 ms`:** crisp micro-blips (`25 ms` decay, panned
       to `scopePan[k]`, pitched by `scopeG[k]`) for each system flyby
       (`scopeRatio <= 1.25`), turning encounter count and spacing into an
       instant rhythmic contour (*"tic – tac — LAND"*).
     - **`t = 260 ms` — Terminal fate (the decisive cue):**
       - **`END_IMPACT` on LIGHT world ($0 \le g < 1.2$):** buoyant **upward**
         dyad (`root → +7 st` woody bell in `240–480 Hz`, crisp decay) —
         immediately heard as *"safe light landing, easy escape."*
       - **`END_IMPACT` on MODERATE world ($1.2 \le g < 2.2$):** solid level lock
         tone (`140–220 Hz`, unison-fifth settle).
       - **`END_IMPACT` on HEAVY world ($g \ge 2.2 .. 5.0$):** unmistakable
         **downward** pitch-drop thud into sub-bass (`130 Hz → 52..80 Hz`,
         heavier $g$ drops lower and sustains darker) — physically felt as
         *"falling into a deep gravity well."*
       - **`END_IMPACT` on LETHAL STAR (explorer lone star):** harsh sizzle +
         alarming minor-ninth warning pulse.
       - **`END_EXIT` / `END_TIME` (no landing):** no terminal lock; a brief
         band-passed air fade (`END_EXIT` drops in pitch, `END_TIME` floats
         unresolved) so **landing vs missing is binary within `0.35 s`**.
       - **Uncharted fog crossing (`fogTau >= 0`):** from `fogTau × 240 ms`
         onward, the sweep dissolves into soft radio static, telling the pilot
         how much of the trajectory is charted before entering the unknown.

### 4.4 Audio coordination with `0.8.6-plan-improve-sound.md`

- `AIM_SCOPE` becomes selectable in `#soundAim` (`outcome | scope | flythrough |
  off`, stored under `gravity-sound-aim`) in any mode, and is **enforced**
  whenever `!telemetryState.profitKnown`.
- `0.8.6` §1–§2 owns smooth Voronoi pentatonic tuning (`Tones.tuneField`) and
  periodic `wrapDelta` pan/distance helpers; `AIM_SCOPE` consumes both directly.
- `0.8.6` §3 owns the completed-trace Voronoi arrival one-shot and the two
  `emcBeacon` block pitches (*recoverable rail/battery/purse* vs *no rail /
  `OWNER_EMPTY`*); in `AIM_SCOPE`, `emcBeacon` fires **only** when
  `telemetryState.destPadKnown` is true, staying silent on jammed/uncharted
  worlds so only physical gravity speaks.

## 6. Verification & invariants

- **No second economy or physics path:** `planQuartermaster` calls the same
  `GravityBot` valuation rules bots run; `telemetryState` gates display, helper
  access, and `aimSoundState.hasProfit` without altering `GravitySim` or
  `GravityGame` physics, RNG streams, or `career_bench` / `bot_bench` scores.
- **Deterministic replay (`career_actions_test`, `career_replay_test`):** Every
  Quartermaster action passes through `careerAct`, so compact action logs and
  full saves replay bit-for-bit across normal and Dead-Reckoning runs.
- **Zero per-frame allocation (`route_bench`, `trajectory_sound_test`):**
  `analyzeScopePath` writes only into fixed typed arrays allocated once per
  sound instance; `AIM_SCOPE` allocates zero Web Audio nodes after `buildGraph`;
  `srAnnounce` formats strings only on discrete state changes.
- **Acoustic interpretability checks (`trajectory_sound_test`):**
  1. Changing `data.profit` from `-5` to `+12` on identical geometry produces
